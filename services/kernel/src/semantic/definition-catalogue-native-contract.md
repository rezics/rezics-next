# Native catalogue work, original admission and root cuts

The four ordinary catalogue metadata reads retain their signatures. Metadata, pins, cursors and released
bodies are not native Kind identity, custody or publisher original authority. Public route shapes are unchanged.
Public publisher phases currently refuse before checkout because the installed Core command profile
cannot admit authorization/receipt-bearing publication. The selected receipt-free mechanical profile also
refuses: its genuine pre-checkout lifetime and committed result resolver are not installed. This is a missing operational operand, not successful publication.

## Receiving boundaries

The actual Accounts `collection-kind-work-admission.ts` exports two-argument refusing
`admitCollectionKindPreparationStep` and `readCollectionKindWorkDefinition`. It has no
`bindCollectionKindPreparationStep` or same-original work-bound definition. Operational transaction presence
alone does not associate the original NativeCollectionWork with the Pool, home, owner, episode and deadline.
Kind preparation/read/enrollment therefore refuse before foreign IO. They never restore an Actor or create a
second ledger. The separate Kind lock guard calls this receiving boundary; it does not call publisher admission.
Both authentic paths share a private head-then-root lock primitive. A publisher grant cannot admit Kind work.

Core's actual `COMMAND_DISPATCH_ALLOWANCE.profile` is `single-no-effect`. Its supported association excludes
this publisher's authorization, receipts, events, outbox, history and callbacks. The body callback begins after
checkout/control/admission IO and ends before the fence, COMMIT and ROLLBACK. Binding just that callback cannot
pay or authorize the inclusive lifetime. No Pool interception or Core executor edit is used here.
`CATALOGUE_PUBLISHER_MISSING_COMPOSITION` and `CATALOGUE_PUBLISHER_COMPOSITION` publish the exact missing terms;
`installed` is false. Repeated `cataloguePublisherInvocation` calls fail before IO, work construction or deadline
selection. They cannot renew a frame. `withSingleCommandAttempt` remains around the real `runCommand` call.

The other missing operands are complete fresh Access admission, the ordinary `readRelease(composed:false)`
cache/stored-body child declaration, irreversible committed dispatch resolution and finite retained-job funding.
There is no arbitrary whole-job page count. A 256-node-entry or 64-member/label body bound describes one actual
step, not the build's number of steps. DBOS does not retry a failed dispatch with a fresh allowance.

Source's actual custody exports capture, read/hold, revalidate, release and retire Library captures. They do not
export committed exact catalogue-consumer withdrawal/nonrevival acknowledgement plus same-current-owner and
restore-generation verification. Neither a Library release nor a PublicFilter observation is substituted.
`consumeCatalogueWithdrawalAcknowledgement` retains its precise unavailable boundary for nonempty cuts. No
positive Source receipt or its foreign price is fabricated. The current final CAS explicitly requires a real
empty durable cut; a historical unproven root is never treated as empty.

## Original and transition invariants

The new forward migration installs route-zero original, ticket, native-position and retirement tables. It
never backfills historical jobs as funded or settled. The prior forward migration marks old roots unproven.
A genuinely new root registers only in the build's actual creation transaction while invisible.

A public original key hashes Core's actual native receipt codec output: principal kind/id, command kind and
storage key. It retains root, phase, immutable input digest, episode, finite deadline, admitted/spent eight-axis
totals and origin. An existing key never acquires replacement tickets, input, position, allowance or deadline.
The private receiver must fund admission itself before IO. Seven disjoint one-shot components cover bootstrap,
authorization, framework, body, observations, lifecycle and cleanup. Spending checks all eight sums against the
immutable allocation and transitions all seven tickets and the owner atomically. Its returned transaction
locator is not a grant. Core must resolve its actual COMMITTED receipt before any child checkout. A rolled-back
spend cannot dispatch; a committed spend is irreversible even if dispatch fails, expires or loses its response.

Terminalization needs the original dispatch's committed result association, not the current transaction's
precommit locator. Its private missing receiver refuses that dependent transition. Terminal originals retain
input, deadline, totals and result identity permanently, have spent equal to admitted, and carry no executable
balance. No SQL deletes an original or changes spent back to admitted.

Head locking precedes root locking, including first and late enrollment. Close increments generation once and
captures the last durable consumer sequence. The activation retains expected head, previous root, generation,
cut and monotone native position. Staging retains at most 100 exact captures, including negative/preparing or
missing-current consumers. A retained page is not overwritten on replay. The cut, page and cursor commit or roll
back together. Final publication rechecks fresh Global `definitions:activate` authority, the exact current head,
ready target, protocol, closed generation/cut, staged position and absence of unresolved consumers/frontier.

## Literal prices and scheduling composition

`CATALOGUE_PUBLISHER_OPERATIONS` is the closed catalogue of actual SQL literals, parameter maxima and all eight
axis declarations. Unknown identities, wrong arities and overlong representations fail before dispatch.
`CATALOGUE_NATIVE_OPERATIONS` ties each native SQL literal to its specific bound and named parameter maxima.
Native reader literals have an exact allowlist, digest-key batches at most 100 and range limits at most 101.
There is no generic SQL footprint fallback. `CATALOGUE_LEDGER_OPERATIONS` covers original/ticket/position and
retirement queries. `CATALOGUE_PUBLISHER_CONTROLS` and `CATALOGUE_NATIVE_CONTROLS` separately declare pure bounded
representation work. The retained `CATALOGUE_BODY_COST.control` is compatibility data; operational call sites use
their specific control IDs.

Bytes add the actual SQL UTF-8 text, each parameter representation and six bytes of parameter framing before
IO. Buffers are binary, JSON objects use their actual JSON text, and digest arrays use PostgreSQL quoted-array
text. Outputs include bytea's two hex bytes per octet and scalar framing. Artifact storage enforces 4 MiB;
Release children inherit that original bound, declarations 64 KiB, node/task keys 256 KiB, build status uses
bounded projections. Consumer capture maxima are 8192 + 8192 + 65536 = 81920 octets. Staging charges both the old
and new captures: 100 * 2 * 81920 bytes plus the returned cursor. Frontier reading charges retained and current
hex captures: 100 * 4 * 81920 plus 100 * 512 scalar bytes. New reverse/FK indexes are included in native points.

Rows count addressed reads/writes, duplicate/failure and FK reverse probes. Points count bounded leading-index,
unique/FK and maintenance probes; MVCC versions and tree height remain engine work. Empty probes are charged.
For example ticket spending reads one original and seven tickets, then writes seven tickets and one original.
One terminal-ticket cleanup reads one original and seven tickets, deletes seven tickets and includes seven FK
reverse checks. These are one-statement components, not whole cleanup-job allocations.

`CATALOGUE_PUBLISHER_PATHS` exposes immutable fixed and repeated children for the executable artifact/build,
missing/refused and node/release/declaration alternatives. `CATALOGUE_CHILD_COMPOSITION` exposes native child
sequences. Neither is a whole-original allowance. Missing authorization, inclusive executor, receipt, Source,
release receiving and retained scheduling terms remain typed operands rather than zero-price children.
Every dispatched owned operation is charged cumulatively, including empty/failure/savepoint-rollback branches.
A callback returning after the original deadline fails without refund; escaped bindings are removed.

Retirement first locks the activated intent and actual previous root in head-first order. A current head or
unproven/open root cannot retire. A nonempty old cut or any frontier/current consumer retains a precise Source
liability; pending and missing consumers are included. The consumer-deletion statement is retained but is not
dispatched without the absent current-owner/nonrevival proof. Empty same-owner cuts independently advance.
Point terminal-ticket cleanup also covers artifact originals without an enrollment row and requires a separately
funded authentic repair association. Tickets are visited by an indexed original-key cursor. A nonterminal original stops with a native-settlement
liability. Deletion and cursor progress commit together: interruption rolls both back; a lost response resumes
the committed cursor, and an already-cleaned empty component still advances. No original can be admitted after
retirement starts, under the same root lock. Positions are removed in pages of at most 100 only after terminal
settlement; reverse-reference probes run before the cursor reaches terminal. Audit records and original spent
keys remain counted liabilities. Terminal cleanup never refunds work or claims Source/native records deleted.

This finite component inventory does not assert finite whole-job admission. A future compatible receiver must
account for all cohome old/new captures, live allocations, native receipts and permanent tombstones before
allocation and compose the actual retained schedule. Its missing funding/association is published explicitly.

## Mechanical receiving representation

The first selected composition is receipt-free `step` and `activation-step`.
`CATALOGUE_MECHANICAL_COMPOSITION` describes that selection separately from public
command composition. It is unavailable until genuine Core lifetime/result and
Semantic finite-original admission are installed; the ordinary noEffect and
Source-fence profiles remain inapplicable. Accounts admission and Source withdrawal
remain separate unavailable positive transitions in this base. No missing producer
has been replaced with caller proof or fabricated zero work.

`definition-catalogue-mechanical.ts` supplies canonical bounded input/result bytes.
The input binds root, phase, committed build transaction or activation identity,
episode, actual retained position and immutable deadline. Selection hashes the
native job/position, excluding deadline and episode: changing either conflicts
with the same key and a different immutable input digest. Selection is data, not
proof of a committed job, funding, currentness or authorization. The private
publisher body validates that selection against its bound root/input/deadline
and prepares bounded result bytes; Core's missing same-transaction association
must still store and authenticate them before any positive recovery is enabled.

Mechanical build work now returns no catalogue progress event, matching the
receipt-free selection. Public progress events retain their separate unsupported
command profile. Activation wakeups register one nonretrying mechanical step for
the exact intent; they cannot perform final publication or renew funding.

Input is at most 1024 canonical bytes. The result is at most 65536 bytes: the
8192-byte bounded refusal can expand to at most 49152 JSON bytes; the closed pin,
six bigint counters, state/phase, nullable digest and envelope fit the remainder.
Counters are canonical nonnegative PostgreSQL bigint strings. One build result
retains the selected steps position or advances it once; foreign roots/phases,
extra fields, different intent, active-with-pending, noncanonical bytes and
oversized results refuse. The two pure representation components explicitly
price their bounded input scans/copies and result encoding before IO; they are
additive body costs and do not price a whole original or prove commit.

Decision: Restore only catalogue-owned prerequisites from the named retained source and install its previously absent schema together in one newly timestamped forward-only migration; base migrations remain unchanged.
Decision: Mechanical result identity excludes the deadline and episode while its immutable input digest includes them, so changed replay input cannot select another payer at the same native job position.
