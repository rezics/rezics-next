## Outcome

Advance REZICS by best practice, with the manager exercising the maintainer's
judgment. This phase centres on the product people use:

- **Main site.** The user-facing and management surfaces are both excellent to
  use, built on the qualified backend APIs.
- **Accounts site.** A simple account centre in the spirit of Google Account,
  with an admin panel designed to be genuinely good to use.
- **Backend changes** that these surfaces or product quality need, made in the
  APIs rather than worked around in clients.
- **Code over documents.** Whatever code can express moves into code, and the
  documents it replaces are deleted.

How the frontend should look and work is not fixed in advance. The manager
researches, decides, builds, uses the result in a real browser and revises,
throughout the Goal.

## Completion

The Goal is open-ended: there is no fixed case list to finish. The manager sets
and revises milestones, and reports each one to the maintainer with what
works, how it was verified and what comes next. A milestone counts only when it
is merged, its checks pass and its flows work in a real browser against the
local stack. The Goal ends when the maintainer stops it.

## Constraints

The manager's authority and the maintainer's standing directions are in the
[manager charter](docs/goals/manager.md). Other documentation records practice
to consult, not rules. The manager runs workers through the
[Goal program](docs/goals/README.md), and workers follow the
[worker protocol](docs/goals/worker.md).
