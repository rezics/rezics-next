# Discovery, context and Space experience

## Common entry

Discovery leads to one Work/Main Version entry with explicit variants/releases.
Users can read, classify, rate and discuss without first choosing an external
edition. Show actual selected language/contribution and Realm perspective when
they affect interpretation. Preserve specific-version links and citations.

## Queries

Text, graph relations, classification, rating and time controls configure the
same Fluree query capability. An empty Filter adds no hidden document defaults.
Explain direct, inferred and retrieval-only match reasons. Show partial/estimated
counts honestly and offer restart for expired cursors without losing query state.

## Space and scope

Zone navigation/presentation and Realm governance/publication can differ. A fixed
Realm site visibly retains its scope through search, links and empty states.
Sparse local results cannot silently switch to Global. Changing the displayed
perspective does not copy content, merge scores or cast judgments.

## Interaction and accessibility

Ordinary controls prioritize common tasks, with direct advanced entry for exact
context/selection/rules. Saved advanced descriptors survive simple edits. Lists,
graphs and Blocks share keyboard/navigation, loading, cancellation and safe
unavailable states. Scope labels and consent cannot be hidden as implementation
detail. Verify actual changed components under the rendered-review boundary.
