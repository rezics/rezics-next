**4. Visibility and access**

Public content and public profile fields can be seen by eligible viewers and copied by people outside REZICS. General-eligible public representations may appear in search engines. Changing visibility cannot recall every external copy.

Private drafts and reading records remain private unless you deliberately use an available sharing feature. Delegating control of an Agent or Realm does not share your private reading history.

Realm moderators receive information needed for their responsibilities. They do not receive unrestricted access to your account, private library or confidential safety reports.

Authorized personnel and service providers may access private information when necessary to operate the service, resolve your support request, investigate a concrete security or safety issue, or comply with law. Such access is limited to the relevant purpose.

**5. AI**

We do not train AI models on private drafts or reading records by default, or permit a provider to do so on our behalf by default.

Using information to answer a particular request is different from using it to train a model. Before an optional AI feature sends private information to an external provider, we identify the provider and explain the information sent, the purpose and relevant retention.

Any training programme involving private drafts or reading records requires a separate, specific, optional opt-in. Accepting the Terms or using an AI feature is not that opt-in.
