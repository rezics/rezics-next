**1. Respect for copyright**

REZICS respects copyright and responds to sufficiently complete notices under the US Digital Millennium Copyright Act. Copyright complaints may concern books, chapters, translations, artwork, software, games, reviews or other protected expression.

An open-source platform does not make all hosted material open source. Attribution, noncommercial use or availability elsewhere does not by itself establish permission. Some uses may be lawful because of a licence, public-domain status, fair use or another legal exception.

This process concerns copyright. Report threats, private-information exposure, impersonation, child exploitation and non-consensual intimate imagery through their relevant safety routes; copyright ownership is not required to seek safety assistance.

**3. Sending a takedown notice**

Provide a written notice containing:

1. Your physical or electronic signature, as the owner or a person authorized to act for the owner of the allegedly infringed exclusive right.
2. Identification of the copyrighted work, or a representative list if multiple works at one online site are involved.
3. Identification of the allegedly infringing material and information reasonably sufficient for us to locate it. Give URLs and, where useful, the chapter, edition, attachment or revision.
4. Information reasonably sufficient to contact you, including your name, address, telephone number and email address if available.
5. This statement, or an equivalent statement: “I have a good-faith belief that use of the material in the manner complained of is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.”
6. This statement, or an equivalent statement: “The information in this notification is accurate, and under penalty of perjury, I am authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right that is allegedly infringed.”

Consider applicable licences and legal exceptions before submitting. Registration numbers and links to an authorized original can help but are not universally required notice elements.

Do not include unrelated sensitive information or attach suspected illegal imagery. We may request clarification where information is insufficient.

These notice requirements follow [17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3)](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap5.html#512).

**4. Our response**

We review notices for the required information and act expeditiously to remove or disable access to material covered by a valid notice. We take reasonable steps to notify the affected uploader and explain the counter-notice process.

We address the identified material and its controlled delivery paths, including relevant attachments, revisions, previews and caches. We do not automatically remove every discussion, review or translation of a work merely because one item infringes.

When a report is incomplete but provides enough information to identify a potential claim, we take reasonable steps to seek the missing information as appropriate.

We may also act on actual knowledge or other legally relevant information without waiting for a formal notice.

**5. Sending a counter-notice**

If your material was removed or disabled through this process because of mistake or misidentification, you may send a written counter-notice to our designated agent containing:

1. Your physical or electronic signature.
2. Identification of the removed or disabled material and its location before removal.
3. A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the material was removed or disabled because of mistake or misidentification.
4. Your name, address and telephone number.
5. A statement that you consent to the jurisdiction of the US Federal District Court for the judicial district in which your address is located; or, if your address is outside the United States, any judicial district in which REZICS Inc may be found.
6. A statement that you will accept service of process from the person who submitted the original notification or that person’s agent.

An email address and case reference help us communicate with you.

A counter-notice is a legal statement and may lead to litigation. You may seek legal help, but retaining a lawyer is not a condition of using this process.

**6. Forwarding and restoration**

We promptly provide a copy of a valid counter-notice to the original notifier and explain that we will restore the material in accordance with the statutory process.

Unless our designated agent first receives notice that the original notifier has filed an action seeking a court order restraining the relevant infringement, we restore the material no earlier than 10 and no later than 14 business days after receiving the valid counter-notice. These are business days, not calendar days. [17 U.S.C. §512(g)](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap5.html#512).

A separate, valid safety, legal or contractual restriction may still prevent restoration. We identify that separate reason and its review route; we do not describe an unrelated restriction as a successful copyright claim.

An ordinary moderation appeal is not a statutory counter-notice. A DMCA counter-notice does not reverse an NCII or child-safety restriction.

**7. Repeat infringement**

REZICS adopts and reasonably implements a policy of terminating, in appropriate circumstances, subscribers and account holders who repeatedly infringe copyright.

We record relevant notices, outcomes, retractions, counter-notices, court information and circumvention of prior restrictions. We assess the reliability of claims, repeated conduct, severity and the affected person’s response. A court judgment is not required before we may act.

We do not treat every allegation as a proven infringement. Invalid, withdrawn or successfully challenged claims are corrected in the account’s enforcement history as appropriate.

We do not promise a fixed number of warnings before termination. Deliberate or serious infringement may justify earlier action. Creating another Account, Agent or application to continue infringement is prohibited.

We accommodate and do not interfere with applicable standard technical measures within the meaning of Section 512(i).

**8. Privacy, misuse and other rights**

We may forward a notice, including contact information, to the affected uploader, and a counter-notice to the original notifier. We do not routinely publish notices or counter-notices in a public repository.

If disclosure creates a safety concern, contact us before submitting additional personal information so we can discuss lawful options. We cannot promise secrecy for information the process requires us to forward.

Knowingly materially misrepresenting infringement or mistaken removal may create liability under Section 512(f). The process must not be used to suppress criticism or assert rights you do not hold.

Retention follows our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md), including justified preservation for disputes. Court orders and applicable non-US copyright procedures remain available.
