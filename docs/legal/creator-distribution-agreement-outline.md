**2. Rights and ownership**

The creator retains ownership and grants REZICS a nonexclusive licence, for the agreed term and territories, to host, technically format, market using approved materials, sell and deliver the listed works and agreed updates.

REZICS may authorize infrastructure and transaction providers only as needed for that distribution. The agreement must identify any resale rights required by an approved merchant-of-record arrangement.

Translations, adaptations, substantial edits, new editions, sublicensing outside distribution and AI training require separate permission. Ordinary distribution permission does not grant those rights.

The creator confirms control of the necessary text, artwork, code, music, translation, performer, trademark and other rights. Any third-party licence obligations must be disclosed.

**3. Eligible products**

No sexually explicit goods and no works rated `r18` or `r18g` may be sold. A relabelled product, hidden file or linked patch may not be used to evade this restriction.

Products must not contain malware, unlawful content or undisclosed material limitations. Samples, descriptions, screenshots, AI disclosures and compatibility information must be accurate.

**4. Prices and revenue split**

The proposed split is:

Creator payment = 90% of Net Receipts.  
REZICS share = 10% of Net Receipts.

**Net Receipts** means customer amounts actually received for the covered transaction, less:

- Sales tax, VAT, GST and similar transaction taxes collected and remitted.
- Actual, documented payment-processing and approved merchant-of-record fees.
- Refunds, reversals and chargebacks.
- Only those additional transaction-specific currency-conversion or dispute fees expressly listed in the signed schedule.

Each deduction must be visible in the statement. No item may be deducted twice. Discounts already reflected in the amount paid are not a second deduction.

Hosting, salaries, marketing, general overhead and REZICS’s own income taxes are not additional deductions.

Example: a customer pays $12, including $2 transaction tax. Actual fees are $0.50 and a $1 refund is issued. Net Receipts are $8.50: the creator receives $7.65 and REZICS receives $0.85.

Creator-specific tax withholding, if legally required, is shown separately against the creator’s payment and supported by the required records.

**6. Checkout, taxes and payment approval**

Sales begin only after the payment provider approves REZICS’s actual distribution model and the required customer terms are ready.

The planned implementation uses one US Stripe account and hosted Checkout, with Stripe Managed Payments only if approved and appropriate for the arrangement.

Managed Payments is a merchant-of-record product; its availability does not automatically approve REZICS’s creator-distribution model, and it does not support Connect marketplace integrations. [Stripe Managed Payments documentation](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/managed-payments).

**7. Customer rights and DRM-free delivery**

Customers receive the licence stated before purchase, normally a continuing personal-use licence to the purchased edition. DRM-free delivery does not by itself permit redistribution or resale.

No REZICS launcher or DRM activation is required for the initial products. Any external runtime or account dependency of a game must be disclosed before purchase and expressly approved for distribution.

REZICS provides receipts, delivery and the agreed re-download and update service. Existing customer licences survive ordinary delisting or termination, subject to law and the agreed refund treatment.

**8. Privacy and promotion**

Creators receive information needed for accounting and agreed support, ordinarily aggregated sales information. Purchasing a work does not authorize disclosure of a customer’s private reading history, library or unrelated activity to its creator.

Customer email marketing requires a separate lawful basis. Promotional claims, discounts, review incentives and use of creator likenesses follow the signed schedule and platform rules.
