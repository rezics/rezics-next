**1. Our prohibition**

For this policy, a child is anyone under 18. A lower local age of consent does not change this rule.

REZICS prohibits:

- Child sexual abuse material and links, solicitations or instructions intended to obtain or distribute it.
- Grooming, sexual solicitation of children, child trafficking and sextortion.
- Sexualized depictions of minors in text, drawings, animation, synthetic media or other forms.
- Sexual deepfakes of children and material presenting an apparently underage person as an object of sexual exploitation.
- Encouraging, organizing, praising or facilitating child sexual abuse.

Calling a depicted child a fictional adult or assigning an implausible age does not make exploitative material acceptable.

Non-graphic prevention, survivor support, medical discussion and reporting are permitted when they do not sexualize children, expose victims or reproduce prohibited material.

These rules apply in private spaces as well as public content, and to automated actors as well as people.

**2. Anyone can seek help**

You can report without an account, even if you are below our registration age or your account is suspended. Include the URL or identifier, what concerns you, and a safe contact method if you want a response.

We do not punish someone merely for being exploited or asking for help. We distinguish a victim’s report from malicious distribution. This does not mean we can host prohibited images as evidence for a public discussion.

We do not require a child to obtain an alleged abuser’s permission or notify an unsafe adult before reporting.

**3. Our response**

We promptly assess reports and restrict access to apparent exploitation. Depending on the facts, we may remove content, suspend accounts, revoke credentials, prevent contact and preserve relevant records.

When we obtain actual knowledge of facts or circumstances indicating an apparent violation covered by US provider-reporting law, we report to NCMEC’s CyberTipline as soon as reasonably possible. Covered matters include specified child sexual abuse material offences, child sex trafficking and online enticement. We do not wait for a court judgment or completion of an account appeal. [18 U.S.C. §2258A](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2258A).

We also make other legally required reports and may contact appropriate emergency or law-enforcement authorities when necessary and lawful.

Our platform prohibition is broader than some criminal definitions. A policy violation is not automatically a determination that a crime occurred.

**4. Evidence handling**

We preserve information required by Section 2258A for one year after submission of the CyberTipline report, including reasonably accessible qualifying contextual material. Additional lawful preservation requirements may apply.

Preserved material is held securely with access limited to necessary personnel. It is not placed in ordinary support attachments, public audit logs, general-purpose AI systems or volunteer moderation channels.

Account deletion does not destroy records subject to required preservation. Once lawful retention ends, we remove information according to the applicable retention schedule.

**5. Media admission**

Image and cover uploads are available at launch. Uploads, imports, replacements, avatars and covers pass the same layered controls:

- Cloudflare CSAM scanning checks images as they are served and blocks matches to known child sexual abuse material.
- REZICS applies for Microsoft PhotoDNA and adds upload matching once approval and a working integration exist. PhotoDNA is not a launch prerequisite.
- A locally run open-source classifier screens new images and holds those likely to be sexually explicit for human review before they are shown.
- New accounts have upload rate limits.
- Anyone can report an image, with or without an account.

During a scanner or classifier outage, new uploads are held for review rather than published unscreened. Cloudflare scanning acts when images are served; it does not screen an upload before it is stored.

Imported covers display with attribution to their source and are removed on a valid notice. Non-consensual intimate imagery is removed within 48 hours of a valid request under the [NCII Takedown Policy](ncii-takedown-policy.md). Copyright notices, including notices about covers, go to our designated agent under the [Copyright and DMCA Policy](copyright-and-dmca-policy.md).

**6. Underage accounts and privacy**

Minimum account ages are 13 generally, 14 in South Korea and 16 in the EEA, subject to stricter applicable requirements.

If we learn that an account is below its applicable minimum, we restrict access and address deletion or other legally required steps. Safety support and required evidence preservation continue.

A minimum-age statement does not remove obligations arising from actual knowledge of under-13 personal-information collection. [FTC COPPA guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions).
