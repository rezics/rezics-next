This policy applies to content and conduct across REZICS, including profiles, Agents, Realms, catalogue contributions, private workspaces, links, imports and automated activity.

**1. Protect people**

The following are prohibited:

- Child sexual exploitation or abuse, grooming, sexual solicitation of children, trafficking, sextortion, and content that sexually exploits or sexualizes minors.
- Non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual deepfakes of identifiable people without consent, voyeuristic intimate imagery, and threats to distribute such material.
- Credible threats, incitement or planning of violence, targeted encouragement of self-harm, and coordination of real-world abuse.
- Stalking, targeted harassment, doxxing, identity theft, coercion and disclosure of another person’s private information without a lawful basis.
- Attacks or dehumanizing abuse targeting people because of protected characteristics.
- Fraud, phishing, malware distribution, unauthorized access and deliberate interference with another person’s accounts or systems.

A fictional, artistic, historical or educational context matters when assessing content. It does not excuse exploitation, a credible threat or a prohibited disclosure of intimate imagery.

Non-graphic discussion of abuse, prevention, recovery, health or law is permitted when it does not promote abuse, expose victims or reproduce prohibited material.

**2. Sexual and violent content**

Sexually explicit images are prohibited, including photographs, drawings, animation, screenshots and AI-generated images. This includes depictions of sexual acts and sexually explicit presentation of nudity.

An `r18` label does not create an exception. Sexually explicit imagery cannot be placed in an avatar, cover, attachment, revision, private draft or linked preview.

Otherwise lawful mature writing and grotesque fictional content may be available only through the applicable rating, age, market and opt-in controls. Content that exploits real victims, encourages real violence or violates another rule remains prohibited regardless of rating.

Our store does not sell sexually explicit goods or works rated `r18` or `r18g`.

**3. Respect rights and permissions**

Submit only material you may lawfully use. Do not pass off another person’s work as yours, publish unauthorized translations, remove required attribution or misrepresent an import’s licence.

Do not bypass age gates, access controls, revocations or another person’s block. Access to a resource does not authorize every action on it.

A public catalogue entry is not permission to distribute the underlying book, game, image or translation. A private unmatched library record must not be used to create an unauthorized public catalogue entry.

**4. Be honest about identity and participation**

Pen names and clearly identified fictional personas are permitted. Impersonation, false organizational authority and misleading claims of official endorsement are not.

Do not fabricate reviews, votes, endorsements, readership, creator credentials or community participation. Disclose material commercial relationships, including paid promotion, affiliate links and review copies where relevant.

Follow our [AI Policy](ai-policy.md). Do not hide automated actors or falsely label generated material as human-created or human-reviewed.

**5. Promotion and platform integrity**

Relevant creator announcements and genuine recommendations are welcome where Realm rules allow them. Unsolicited bulk promotion, repetitive commercial replies, deceptive links and scams are prohibited.

Do not evade limits through multiple Accounts, Agents or tokens; manipulate reputation; farm contribution recognition; submit malicious reports; or use the service to coordinate abuse elsewhere.

Good-faith mistakes, unsuccessful reports and legitimate disagreement are not abuse merely because a decision goes against the reporter.

**6. How to report**

Use [REZICS TO FILL: public reporting form] or email [REZICS TO FILL: monitored safety email]. Reporting is available without an account and after suspension.

Include the URL or resource identifier, a brief explanation, relevant context and a safe way to contact you if you want a response. You may report in any language; we may use translation and will seek clarification when necessary.

For a notice of allegedly illegal content, identify its location precisely, explain the alleged illegality and provide a statement that your information is accurate and complete to the best of your knowledge. Provide your name and email where legally required; child-exploitation reporting must not be blocked by unnecessary identity requirements.

Use the dedicated processes for [copyright](copyright-and-dmca-policy.md), [intimate imagery](ncii-takedown-policy.md) and [child safety](child-safety-policy.md). We route misdirected reports rather than requiring you to understand our internal organization.

Do not download, re-upload or circulate suspected illegal imagery to make a report.

If someone faces immediate danger, contact local emergency services. A REZICS report is not a substitute for emergency assistance.

**7. How we respond**

We prioritize child exploitation, non-consensual intimate imagery and credible threats. We assess urgency, available evidence, context, recurrence and the possible harm of acting or failing to act.

We may preserve restricted evidence, remove content, prevent interaction, change ratings, require corrections, limit visibility or capabilities, suspend access, revoke credentials or terminate accounts. Serious cases may require immediate action without a warning.

Realm moderators enforce their Realm’s rules within their authority. Platform staff handle platform enforcement and sensitive legal or safety cases. A Realm cannot authorize content prohibited by platform policy.

We may use automated signals to identify spam, prohibited imagery or suspicious activity. Signals can be wrong. An AI detector or classifier score is not conclusive evidence of a violation.

Where applicable, our explanation identifies the affected content or account, material facts, policy or legal basis, scope, duration, relevant automation and review options. We may withhold details where disclosure would expose a victim, facilitate abuse or violate law.

Reporting parties receive an acknowledgement and appropriate outcome information when we have a safe contact route. We do not disclose private sanctions or evidence unnecessarily.

**8. Appeals and correction**

You may appeal a moderation decision, including a decision not to act on your report, without charge for at least six months after being notified.

Use [REZICS TO FILL: account-free appeal form] or [REZICS TO FILL: appeal email]. Include the case reference, what you dispute and any additional context. A lawyer is not required.

A human reviews the appeal. We use a different reviewer where staffing permits and disclose when the review is reconsideration by the original decision-maker. We do not promise independent review unless it is actually available.

We communicate the result and correct mistaken restrictions, including related ratings, visibility and account consequences where appropriate. An appeal does not require restoration while an urgent safety issue remains unresolved.

Special statutory processes, including DMCA counter-notices, remain available. Nothing here removes access to courts, regulators or applicable external dispute-resolution procedures.
