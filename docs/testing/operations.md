# Deployment recovery and workload acceptance

The OPS01–OPS16 scenarios and required results are
[typed case declarations](../../scripts/qa/cases/operations.ts); the
[coverage declarations](../../scripts/qa/coverage/ops-capacity.ts) and
[qualification record](../plan/README.md#current-state) name actual evidence.
The case page remains the stable source identity in QA records.

A small fixture restore or sanitized graph copy does not prove production
physical destruction. Report suppression, retention and destroyed bytes
separately; OPS10 needs a production storage/media campaign. A derived cost
bound or bounded probe does not prove deployment capacity. Qualify OPS05 only
for the measured host, fixture, workload and recovery scope. Keep failed
attempts and incomplete evidence visible.

The [pending editorial-protection subcases](../../scripts/qa/cases/editorial-protection.ts)
extend OPS03/12 and OPS05/06. An earlier restore or load pass does not
qualify those additional records. Operators follow
[deployment](../operations/deployment.md), [recovery](../operations/recovery.md)
and [erasure](../operations/erasure.md) for the live sequence.
