# Demand study

Product manager, 2026-09-30. Operating procedure for [kill criterion 1](goal.md#kill-criteria): by
31 October 2026, study 20 target readers' actual tools, inventories and weekly repeated work, and
obtain three commitments from people who will maintain knowledge or a wiki. The maintainer runs it
personally, at zero budget. Thresholds are fixed before the first session; change them only in a
dated note that says why.

## Purpose and decision

The question: do target readers and editors already have a concrete recurring problem that the
initial task solves? That task is continuity across related works, editions and languages, with the
franchise wiki as flagship ([goal](goal.md#mission), [wiki+](goal.md#wiki-the-flagship)). The study
watches what people do and never asks whether they would like REZICS. Twenty sessions show a strong
signal or its absence, not a rate, and a self-selected sample overstates it. The launch proceeds as
planned only if the Proceed and Flagship rows both hold; the maintainer adopts the consequences in
`goal.md`.

| Evidence after 20 sessions (only scope T1 to T3 counts) | Consequence |
| --- | --- |
| **Proceed:** 12 or more confirmed problems, across 3 or more segments and 2 or more languages | The task stands. Findings order the first-launch journeys. |
| **Narrow:** 8 to 11 confirmed, or 12 or more in fewer than 3 segments or one language | Narrow the task to the chore named in at least half the confirmed sessions; if none is, treat as Change. |
| **Change:** 7 or fewer confirmed | Change the task before enlarging the engine. Take the most frequent observed chore, in scope or not, and study it in 10 sessions first. |
| **Flagship:** 3 or more commitments, from 3 people and 2 or more Works or franchises | Wiki+ stays the first-launch flagship. With 1 or 2, criterion 1 fails on supply: launch on the library task and keep wiki+ a plan. With 0, wiki+ leaves the first launch. |

## Screener and quota

| 2. Which sites, apps or files hold what you read, own or plan to read? Roughly how many entries (under 50, 50 to 200, 200 to 1,000, over 1,000)? | Tools, inventory size. |

| 5. Do you edit or run a wiki, database or shared list? Where, in what role, with how many editors? | Maintainers. |
| 6. Are you 18 or over, able to share your screen for 30 minutes, not known personally to the maintainer, and not working for a tracker or wiki host? | Eligibility. |

Languages (of the reading or list, not the UI): English 8 to 10, Chinese 4 or more (outside mainland
China, which is out of scope), Japanese 3 or more, other 2 or more (German, Spanish, French, Korean;
Korean is a probe, as its demand is [under-evidenced](markets-and-growth.md#launch-sequence-over-one-graph)).
Against friends-and-family bias, select on recent behaviour and draw lots among qualified people for
an open cell. Exclude anyone the maintainer knows, has messaged or who follows the maintainer's
accounts. Referrals must be strangers. At most 4 per community and 3 from those accounts. Keep 4 sessions for
people who report no recent switch: contentment is evidence.

## Recruitment

Everywhere: ask moderators first, post once, offer no payment, remind once at most, never DM first,
never criticise another platform, skip communities that exist to share unlicensed copies, and remove
the post when the cell is full. Have a native reader check non-English posts. Log
channel, date, permission, replies, qualified and booked; never names.

| Channel | Norm to respect |
| --- | --- |
| Reddit (r/LightNovels, r/visualnovels, tracker subreddits), forums (NovelUpdates, MangaUpdates, VNDB, Bahamut), Discord servers for trackers, VNs and wikis | Modmail or an admin first. Obey self-promotion and survey rules. Use the survey or off-topic board or channel. On Discord, join under your real name and leave if refused. Answer in public, in the thread's language. |
| Wiki hubs (wiki.gg, Miraheze, Weird Gloop), wikis' own contact pages | Write to the admin by the public route and ask them to relay. Never edit articles. Never urge anyone to leave a host. |

## Session protocol

| Min (30, hard stop; their own video tool) | Step | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0-5 | Consent, warm-up | Read the consent wording (last section) and record the choices. "Tell me about the last thing you read, played or edited." |
| 5-15 | Watch | "Share your screen and open the tool you used most recently. Use it as you normally would." Open each tool they mention. |
| 15-22 | Two weeks | "Scroll back over two weeks. What did you do more than once? Show me the last time." Per chore: trigger, steps, tools crossed, minutes, what went wrong, what they did about it. |
| 22-27 | Inventory | Size, how entries arrive, fields relied on (edition, language, translator, volume, status), three entries hard to keep right, any export held (headers and row count only). Probe once: "Does anything here exist as another medium? Show me how you follow it." |
| 27-30 | Switching | "If this went away next month, what would you lose or redo?" "What have you tried, and why did you go back?" Ask if a short unnamed quote is allowed. |
| Throughout | Rules | No pitch before observation: name no REZICS feature or competitor; if asked, "I will tell you at the end, so I do not steer what you show me." Ask about the past ("last time"), not the future ("would you"); hypotheticals are opinion, not evidence. Talk under a quarter of the time. Do not fix, teach or ask how they obtained texts. Adult titles may be hidden; record counts, not titles. |

## The commitment conversation

| Step (after minute 30; wiki, database or list editors only; only on a yes to 10 more minutes) | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Their home and AI | Host, role, editors, why they stay or leave, what they would never give up (history, credit, licence, control, address). Then how they and their editors feel about AI-assisted edits. Record it before describing anything. |
| The plan | Say: "Everything I describe now is a plan. None of it is live." Then, as [wiki+](goal.md#wiki-the-flagship) describes it: a Work page joins facts, ratings, reviews, your library, discussion and the wiki's summary. A Work can have a franchise wiki, browsed only up to each reader's position. The holder of a text runs an open-source local toolkit with their own agent. It extracts characters, aliases, relationships, events and places, with locators and short quotations, and submits a reviewable proposal. REZICS never receives the full text. The holder chooses the model, and a strict mode keeps it local. The toolkit circumvents no DRM. |
| The ask | "When a test version exists, would you maintain knowledge about one named Work on REZICS?" It is not a request to move an existing wiki; that waits for proven preservation. No money, exclusivity, date or contract; they may withdraw any time. Ask what would make them refuse. |

A **commitment** counts only if, within 48 hours, the person writes back in their own words with five
things: (1) a named Work or franchise and their role; (2) a named action (edit, review, or run the
toolkit on a text they lawfully hold) for at least 2 hours in the first month of a test; (3) their
authority to act; (4) their must-haves; (5) a channel they answer within a week. Not counted: "sounds great", "keep me posted",
"I might", a friend's promise, anyone the maintainer knows, or silence after one reminder. A
commitment shows supply, not reader demand. With their leave, keep the handle and reply (Retention).

## Recording and synthesis

| Column | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `id`, `date`, `segment`, `channel`, `lang`, `region`, `tools`, `inventory` | Code S01 to S20; pilot P01, P02 (count them only if the protocol did not change). Broad region only. Every tool shown and its role; size bucket, format, how entries arrive, fields relied on. |
| chore tab, `loss`, `moved`, `cross`, `ai_view`, `surprise` | One row per chore: trigger, steps, tools crossed, minutes a week, times seen in 14 days, shown or told, workaround, pain in a few plain words. Top three losses on switching; switches, exports, tried-and-returned in 12 months; cross-media chore (none, unprompted, after probe); view of AI-assisted edits; anything that contradicts the plan. |
| `problem`, `scope`, `commit`, `rights`, `consent` | Confirmed, partial or none; scope T1 next-release continuity, T2 library portability, T3 knowledge upkeep, T4 other (name it); commitment none, interested or written; edit rights, holds text or none; recording, quote and keep-contact flags; delete-by date. |

**Confirmed** needs all four: the chore recurred at least twice in 14 days; it crosses two or more tools
or is copied or typed by hand; it costs at least 30 minutes a week, or a loss in the last 3 months (a
release, money, data); and the maintainer saw it on screen. Told but not shown, or seen once, is partial. Scope T1 to T3 is inside the task; T4 is not.

1. Fill each row within 24 hours. At session 10, check quota gaps and channel yield; change channels
   and wording, never thresholds. With under 2 commitments by session 14, spend the rest on maintainers.
2. After session 20, count confirmed by segment, language and scope. Recode five random rows a week
   later, or have an agent recode them from coded rows only; if two codes differ, recode all. Fewer
   than 20 sessions is a recruitment finding, not a pass.
3. Apply the decision table, list contradicting sessions beside it and add a dated `Result` section
   here (counts, paraphrases, decision). The maintainer updates `goal.md`. `cross` is a baseline for
   [criterion 6](goal.md#kill-criteria), which needs 200 people in the product; it cannot pass it.

## Privacy

Consent, read aloud and sent with the booking: "I run REZICS, an unreleased project. This is research,
not a sales call, and you need not sign up for anything. I will ask you to share your screen and show
how you keep track of what you read, play or edit. You may skip anything or stop at any time. Please
do not show passwords. I will note tools, counts and steps under a code number, not your name, and
not your titles or reading history. I will not record audio unless you say yes now, and never video
or screenshots. I keep your contact handle only to arrange this. I will delete it and my raw notes by
14 November 2026, or sooner if you ask, unless you ask me to stay in touch. I may quote one short
sentence without your name only if you say yes at the end. Is it OK to go on?"

| Item | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Recorded | The coded sheet and short notes. Audio only on an opt-in yes, to check notes, deleted within 7 days of coding. Never video, screenshots, passwords, full lists, adult titles or names of unlicensed sources. |
| Retention | Unselected screener answers, handles, raw notes and audio: deleted by 14 November 2026. Someone who commits and agrees to be kept: handle and written commitment until the pilot invitation or 31 December 2027, whichever is first. |
| Location | Outside the repository, including `.temp/`, on an encrypted disk. Recordings and raw notes never go to a cloud or AI tool. The repository holds only aggregate counts and consented paraphrases: no names, handles, emails, screenshots, or cells small enough to identify one person. |
| Basis | Privacy law applies from the first person ([safety and legal readiness](../operations/trust-and-safety.md#safety-and-legal-readiness)). This keeps the minimum and asks consent. Counsel has not reviewed it. |
