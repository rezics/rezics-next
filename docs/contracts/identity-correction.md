# Identity correction, merge and split

## Meaning and authority

A merge is an evidenced identity-resolution decision, not an automatic rewrite
of every incoming edge. Preserve source identities, exact revisions, original
targets and provenance. Equal names, bytes or external identifiers are candidates,
not proof. Accounts/participation-controlled Agents require their dedicated
control/recovery process; catalog correction cannot transfer their authority.

## Protocol

Discover candidates and authorized impact, record evidence, obtain required
independent approvals, stage correspondence changes and activate a versioned
resolution under expected identity/control heads. Reject self/cyclic resolution.
Preserve incoming original references while ordinary navigation can follow the
reviewed current resolution with bounded chains and an explicit reason.

Native editorial fields, grants, votes, subscriptions and Main Versions each have
their own conflict/transfer policy. Do not blindly pool them. Source rebind/import
jobs carry old target/binding epochs and fail after a conflicting activation.
Large reconciliation pages retain approval/authority basis and stale-worker fences.

## Split and recovery

Undo/split is a new assignment case. If later edits combine evidence from both
referents, expose ambiguity instead of inventing an automatic inverse. Retain the
original decision and consumed approvals. Reversal cannot restore private or
erased payloads. Qualify concurrent corrections, stale sources, deep resolution
chains, partial reconciliation and restore/replay.
