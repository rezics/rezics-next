# Realm-scoped sites, selections and replies

## Site and context

General REZICS and a fixed-Realm site use the same frontend and APIs with explicit
site configuration. Presentation, publication and governance contexts remain
separate. A fixed site carries its Realm boundary through SSR, browser navigation,
direct APIs, Search, downloads, shared links and caches. General fallback cannot
replace an empty or inaccessible local result.

## Reply identity and multiple Realm acceptance

A reply has one utterance/content identity and exact original target. Publishing
it into another eligible Realm creates an independent acceptance/placement, not
another author identity or copied vote population. Validate destination root and
parent, selected content version, participation/review and current disclosure.

Different Realms can accept different revisions or reject the reply independently.
Root readability alone does not authorize every reply or reveal private counts.
Thread deletion/reorganization preserves other authors' original references.

## Query and lifecycle

Store Realm/parent connection facts and bounded inverse/query projections. Bind
cursor to context/selection/security generation and batch authority/hydration.
Expose more/partial/unavailable outcomes without unbounded fill-page scans.
Search text, snippets, media, counts and ranking use the same accepted selection.
Rebuild stages, catches up and switches a fenced generation.

Qualify a shared Main Version and reply across two Realms, fixed-site boundary
attempts, sparse pages, private parents, new drafts, revocation and replay.
