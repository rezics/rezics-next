# Editorial protection and reviewed corrections

Status: Work-title and Content-draft slices exist; general selection, inherited
scope and cross-owner protocols remain prospective. [Pending subcases](../../scripts/qa/cases/editorial-protection.ts)
retain unqualified owner-boundary, recovery and workload obligations.

## Independent state and authority

Access admits an actor/action; the target owner guards its state in the effect
transaction. This avoids a second lock authority while contributions, source
observations and challenges continue. Editorial control, modification protection,
contextual acceptance, evidence quality and freshness are separate. A reviewed
correction changes adoption without unlocking it. Sealed identities/releases need
successors; protection cannot block authorized [erasure](../operations/erasure.md).

## Targets, contexts and effective scope

A target uses owner, stable component/occurrence and context, never current literal
or array position. Global and Realm selections keep separate authority. A following
selection needs an exact pinned revision; inherited scope must guard new members.
The current Work profile covers one title slot; cross-owner effects need per-owner
outcomes.

## Record contracts

[Protection](../../services/main/src/modules/protection/schema.ts) and
[correction](../../services/main/src/modules/correction/schema.ts) bind immutable
predecessors, heads, rule/evidence basis, candidate digest and one-use application.
Absent head is explicit CAS, never unknown or lost restore state. Private controller
identity stays in Access; public history may cite opaque proof.

## Operations and state transitions

Same-byte human confirmation advances control. Source apply requires trusted
binding and eligible open basis; otherwise retain the observation. Proposal changes
no adoption or verdict. Independent review binds exact proposal, effect, heads,
rule and evidence; first-profile approval/application is one owner commit and a
proposal has one terminal decision. Relaxation/source return needs separate
authority. Uncertain effects resolve by receipt.

## Transaction and admission protocol

Prepare candidates outside the writer; guard actual mutation footprint and absent
as well as present heads in the owner transaction. Client actor kind and target
lists cannot establish authority. Receipts decide replay and uncertain outcomes.

## Capacity, introduction and recovery

First scalar profile admits one target, 32 evidence/rating references and 50 history
entries per page. These are unqualified ceilings; incomplete manifests are rejected.
Old writers fail closed. Restore decision/application, authority and erasure coverage
before routing; a new epoch cannot reconstruct lost protection. Qualify skew, writer
occupancy and fan-out separately from full-corpus capacity.

## Shared collaboration lifecycle

Decision 19, product manager under maintainer delegation, 2026-09-29.
Catalogue corrections, wiki edits and translations share a review lifecycle at
GitHub's level: exact candidates, stewardship, required reviewers, and changed
candidates invalidating approval. Actionable requests differ from discussions;
subscriptions and an inbox distinguish Read, Saved and Done.

The reason is accountable decisions and a reliable return path, not merely
alternative revisions. [GitHub protected reviews](https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/configuring-branches-and-merges-in-your-repository/managing-protected-branches/about-protected-branches)
and its [notification inbox](https://docs.github.com/en/subscriptions-and-notifications/how-tos/viewing-and-triaging-notifications/managing-notifications-from-your-inbox)
are the precedents. The installed correction schema above already owns exact
candidate binding; this decision extends that lifecycle across contribution kinds.
