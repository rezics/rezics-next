# Relationship graph API and views

## Meaning and presentation

A relation occurrence identifies participants and roles; an independently
evidenced claim is a [Statement](classification.md). Repeated associations
remain separate. Reachability alone proves neither causality, identity,
chronology nor permission. Layout positions and display groups are view state,
not graph truth; a graph Block carries a declarative view, never executable
code. Resource summaries follow [name/avatar resolution](presentation.md#resource-summaries).
Character, credit, causal/background and package graphs can share a representation
without collapsing their domain meanings. Imported and AI candidate assertions
retain their evidence and acceptance; exact history follows current disclosure.
Domain commands revise or retire relations under expected heads and role constraints.

The deployed [direct relation query](../../services/main/src/modules/graph-query/schema.ts)
requires an anchor, exact definition and roles, and has a bounded page and
continuation. [Layout schema](../../services/main/src/modules/graph-layout/schema.ts)
keeps positions outside relation truth. [Graph acceptance](../testing/relationship-graph.md)
records the qualified direct-read and layout scenarios.

## Further contract

General multi-hop traversal and graph aggregation remain prospective. They
need admitted selective seeds, explicit grain and equivalence rules, exact
supporting occurrence references, truthful frontier/completeness, match reasons
and bounded hydration. Full-text seeds and property paths may compose within
Jena; unanchored closure and application-side N+1 traversal are not admitted.
Collapsing a view must count a resource once across supporting paths. Role and
trait conditions must bind the same participant in a
compatible occurrence scope. A concept type adds no Tag wrapper or semantic
edge from display grouping.

Context selection must keep speaker, exact definitions, semantic qualifiers
and acceptance scopes separate, even when labels or Contexts match. Personal,
Realm and Global views may compare interpretations but must not relabel or
merge judgments without an admitted definition relationship; spelling or
`skos:broader` alone does not supply one. Private nodes,
intermediates, counts, summaries and hidden paths cannot be inferred from a
public frontier. Future tests must cover dense hubs, disputed causality,
repeated participants, cancellation and export before those views qualify.
