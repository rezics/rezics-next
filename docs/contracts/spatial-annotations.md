# Spatial annotations and world views

Spatial views are a deferred extension to shared publications and discussion.
Ordinary Web/list reading must remain available without a game client or spatial
renderer. Activation requires verified world adapters and an operating policy.

The future model must distinguish canonical generation recipes, independently
maintained world instances, reset epochs, coordinate spaces, frames and anchors.
Equal seeds, server addresses or coordinates cannot establish shared instance
identity or authority. Reset, fork, restore and upgrade need distinct continuity
decisions. Annotations must retain original references and report unresolved or
drifting anchors without silently relocating discussion.

Before implementation, elect typed selectors with explicit CRS, dimensions,
units, uncertainty and time; separate generated-feature applicability from
observed instance changes. Preserve exact content selection, current disclosure
and accessible list reading. Proximity grants no access, and publishing an anchor
does not publish movement history. Device/camera position remains ephemeral;
Earth rollout needs sensitive-location policy. Compatibility needs scoped evidence.

Activation also needs bounded spatial queries with derived geometry indexes,
verified adapter/frame assurance, accessible renderer fallbacks with density and
occlusion limits, reset/privacy conformance, and platform-rights review.
Publications cannot carry executable overlays. Coordinate storage alone does not
qualify GeoSPARQL. No spatial profile or client is currently active.
