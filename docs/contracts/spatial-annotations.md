# Spatial annotations and world views

## Product and activation

Spatial views attach shared publications/content to qualified world or geographic
anchors. They reuse Account/Access, Main Version, Realm, Collections, classification
and discussion. They are an extension after the first product foundation, with
verified game/world adapters and explicit operating policy. Ordinary Web/list
reading remains available without a game client or spatial renderer.

## Identity and applicability

A WorldRecipe identifies canonical generation inputs under an adapter revision.
A WorldInstance identifies an independently maintained save/server. An InstanceEpoch
identifies reset/continuity state. SpatialSpace identifies a coordinate namespace;
Frame identifies axes/units/origin/transform semantics; Anchor identifies a location
or shape within an exact frame/epoch. A recipe digest, seed, server address or
matching coordinates does not prove shared instance identity or control.

Recipe-based annotations describe applicable generated features; instance-specific
annotations describe observed player changes. Compatibility is an evidenced scoped
relationship, not automatic equivalence. Reset, fork, restore and upgrade have
different operations. Preserve original references and report drift/unresolved
anchors rather than silently moving comments to another world.

## Coordinates, content and queries

Selectors declare point/area/volume/path or admitted extension, coordinate reference
system, dimensionality, units, uncertainty and temporal validity. Minecraft
dimension is part of the frame; Earth uses explicit geodetic interpretation and
distance/altitude policy. Geometry is not interchangeable with a place identity.

A publication-to-anchor binding pins exact content selection and anchor meaning.
The same discussion appears through map and ordinary views, without copied bodies
or votes. Fluree graph queries combine context, classification, readable content
and an admitted spatial source/operator. Bound viewport/radius, layers, candidates,
result bytes and continuation; geometry indexes remain derived from authoritative
typed facts. Do not claim full GeoSPARQL merely from coordinate storage.

## Privacy, clients and operations

Proximity grants no access. Private coordinates, worlds, counts and cached geometry
follow current disclosure. Device/camera position is ephemeral and requires the
relevant user action; publishing an anchor does not publish movement history.
Provide manual/coarse browsing and explicit uncertainty. Sensitive-location and
abuse policies are elected before Earth rollout.

Adapters report exact game/build/loader/world/frame assurance. Renderer modes are
declarative capabilities with accessible text/list fallback, density/occlusion
budgets and viewer control. Publications cannot carry arbitrary executable overlays.
Paid integrations require their actual platform/rights review before activation;
no revenue or compatibility promise follows from this architecture.

Qualify world reset, equal seeds/different instances, dimension collisions,
unavailable frame transforms, private anchors, drift, erasure and restore. Basis:
[Web Annotation](https://www.w3.org/TR/annotation-model/),
[GeoSPARQL](https://docs.ogc.org/is/22-047r1/22-047r1.html) and
[GeoJSON](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc7946).
