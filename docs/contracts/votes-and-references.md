# Votes, references and community accountability

The implemented governance poll has a frozen charter, electorate, allocation
plan, source entitlement and ballot identity. The [vote schema](../../services/main/src/modules/vote/schema.ts),
[commands](../../services/main/src/modules/vote/commands.ts) and
[GOV11–23 cases](../../scripts/qa/cases/governance-and-delivery.ts) carry
conservation, current Access admission, one-hop proxies, independent approvals,
expected revisions, retries and tally recovery. An approved resolution executes
only the body's admitted [governance capability](governance-rules.md).

## Extension boundaries

Live proxy rerouting and full liquid delegation are not selected poll profiles.
They need explicit replacement/recount, cycle, topic, precedence, concentration,
abstention and provenance policies, followed by workload qualification. Access
inheritance cannot activate either profile. The [research basis](../research/access-depth-representation-and-voting.md)
records the choice to separate institutional representation from voting weight.

Ordinary ratings, reactions, favorites, follows and private progress have their
own populations and disclosure policies. Their references should preserve
resource, revision and occurrence grain without transferring ownership or access.
Aggregate counters are projections; they cannot become another authoritative
ballot or favorite store. The [interaction/cache design](../implementation/interactions-and-cache.md)
covers the separate rollout. Those feature profiles need tests for retry,
withdrawal, privacy and restore before this page can be retired. Withdrawn or
private contributions must not reappear through facets or reconstructed counts;
source aggregate statistics must not become native ballots.
