# Rights declarations and license offerings

A declaration records target, exact instrument/version, declaring Agent, time,
scope, provenance and authority evidence. Unknown historical grantor or time
stays unknown. The record establishes neither Access authority nor legal
clearance; imported license text is not consent from a native Agent.

The [offering owner](../../services/main/src/modules/rights/offering.ts) records
declarations, one open offering per target/instrument key, recognition changes
and immutable history through authorized, revision-checked commands. Management
reads expose all open states. Ending an offering cannot rewrite its declaration
or allow recognition to reopen it. The [rights owner schema](../../services/main/src/modules/rights/schema.ts)
records material- and use-specific bases and obligations apart from Access.

The [source reuse boundary](source-lifecycle.md#basis-for-acquisition-and-reuse)
and [dated legal/provider evidence](../research/source-data-rights.md) explain
why corporate status, wiki purpose, NC/ShareAlike labels and fair use require
material- and use-specific analysis. Exports preserve exact asset/contribution/
occurrence scope, territory/time, attribution and uncertainty without inventing
broader permission. The release agreement remains a publication artifact outside this checkout.
