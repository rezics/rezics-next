## Outcome

Reading in order is REZICS's core promise, and the second milestone review
(`.temp/manager/milestone-review-2.md`, screenshots in
`.temp/manager/milestone-2/`) found it broken. Findings 1, 3, 10, 20 and 26:

- **P0 · order.** 雨夜书店's Contents lists 第二章, 第一章, 第三章; "Start
  reading" opens chapter 2; chapter 1 has an active Previous; Continue says
  "Next: 第二章". The reader follows the composition correctly; the seed built
  it wrong. `scripts/dev/seed/progress.ts` first inserts `[parent as ch1, ch2]`,
  then removes the parent and appends ch1 and ch3 last. Make the seed produce
  1, 2, 3 on a clean stack **and** repair an existing one (a move operation
  under the same seed flow, or remove and reinsert at the right position).
  Make the shared stack's serial read 1, 2, 3 without a reset.
- **P1 · single-text Works.** Pride and Prejudice has no "Start reading", and
  Contents says "No contents yet. No chapters have been arranged for this
  Work's Main Version", yet search finds its text and a shelf marks it Read.
  A Work whose Main Version is one text reads as one text: a one-entry
  Contents and a Start reading that opens it. If the classics have no
  readable text on the stack, have the seed publish it from the locked Open
  Library or Gutenberg fixtures you find in `tests/fixtures`, and say in the
  handoff what you used.
- **P2 · words.** The Contents empty state and History use model words ("Main
  Version", "Who acted and earlier text stay private"). Say what a reader
  needs: "This book is read as one text · Start reading"; move the privacy
  note to a help tip.
- **P1 · chapter Works.** `plan.ts` still creates `serial-ch*` Works, and the
  Fiction moderation queue shows "雨夜书店 · 第一章 雨夜" as its own Work with a
  monogram. Where the model needs a Work per chapter, present it as a chapter
  of its book everywhere a reader or moderator meets it (title "第一章 雨夜 ·
  雨夜书店", the book's cover, a link into the reader), and keep chapter
  Works out of catalogue lists, search results and shelves. If that needs a
  Main read, add it in Main.
- **P2 · phone reader.** On a phone the site header, search and bottom tab
  bar stay on screen while reading; hide them on scroll down and bring them
  back on scroll up or tap, as reading apps do.

Add browser tests: chapter 1 has no Previous, Start reading and Continue open
the right chapter, and a single-text Work opens its text.
