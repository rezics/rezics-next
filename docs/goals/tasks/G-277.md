## Outcome

Three gaps left by today's community work:

1. **Per-language Main selections.** The Work page says "Main Version in
   <language>", and REZICS is bilingual from the start, but the graph has one
   Main selection head per Work, so a maintainer cannot choose the English
   text and the Chinese text independently (G-265 implemented maintainer
   selection on the single head). Implement language-specific selection heads
   with coordinated graph writes, recovery, readers (Work read, contents,
   discovery) and migration of existing single heads, keeping exact-revision
   reads and history intact.
2. **Realm reply placement.** `RealmReplyPlacedEvent` is emitted but has no
   relay reader (see the `pendingReaders` allowance in
   `tests/qa/unit/outbox-event-handlers.test.ts`; remove it when you add the
   reader). Member replies in a Realm stay unplaced without review authority;
   make placement work for members within the Realm's review policy (Realm
   moderators review; policies may allow direct placement for members in good
   standing), consistent with Realm submissions (G-257).
3. **Exact Realm member counts.** The Realm public profile (G-249) publishes
   only estimated/unknown counts; the directory (G-271) cannot offer exact
   counts and scans at most 128 public Realms (422 above). Publish exact counts
   where the Realm's policy allows, and add a directory index so the scan
   limit does not bound the product.

Owner schema first, then commands, then reads; tests for stale heads,
concurrent selections per language, recovery, disclosure and erasure.
