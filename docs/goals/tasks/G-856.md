## Outcome

One suite proves M6's launch scope (light novels and visual novels over one catalogue and
library, with franchise wikis) end to end, each journey once through the API or MCP and once
in a browser on a phone and a desktop:

1. Wiki+: the toolkit's `units` on the Pride and Prejudice fixture, a scripted model-free
   extraction following `SKILL.md`, `wiki_candidates` and `wiki_validate` over MCP, a
   `wiki-bundle` proposal, review, apply, the Zone read at two positions, the Work hub's
   wiki section, and a later-chapter bundle that adds without deleting. The wiki contract's
   first proof names two language editions: admit one public-domain translation of a fixture
   book (for example a Project Gutenberg translation; record it in `fixtures.lock.json`), seed
   it as a realization (G-833), and show its extraction matching the same characters.
2. VN discovery by one usable release, and the LN next volume in the reader's language.
3. Library: import from each supported tool, apply, export, interrupt, resume, re-import.
4. Two-device progress (G-834's 409 with both states) and the hub's primary action.

The hard cases from GOAL.md's M6 exit are covered where their capability is in launch
scope: ambiguous editions, mixed formats in one attempt, non-UI languages (a `th` title and
an Arabic name rendered right to left), a 1,000-chapter inventory, a steward whose review
authority is revoked mid-review, interrupted exports and two-device progress. Expired loans
and the frozen-engine expansion run are recorded as deferred (G-859, G-863), not faked.
