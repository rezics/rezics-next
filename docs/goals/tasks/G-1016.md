per item in Query, search result cards and Discover, while membership,
disclosure and budget failures stay authoritative (`f4c51ba0b` on main). G-1012

   builds cancelled and restarted repeatedly

   trigger that supersedes a running build on every change, a budget that

   or resume from its checkpoint under steady writes, and show it with numbers
   from the shared stack and a test.
