## Outcome

Every page of `apps/about` reads as if written natively in 繁體中文, 简体中文,
日本語, 한국어, Deutsch, Français and Español, not translated: persuasive,
idiomatic, with each market's own vocabulary for its reading culture (for
example 輕小說 / 轻小说 / ライトノベル / 라이트 노벨, 追更, 積読, 웹소설). Keep the
English meaning and every availability label exact; never add claims. Localise
the per-market messages from
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/research/R28-fundraising-marketing.md`
where a page addresses a market. Product names stay as defined (Realm is
"Realm" in English and the plain word for a community elsewhere, as
`docs/plan/frontend.md` records).

Adjust the claim paths above to where G-480 and G-481 actually put the
catalogues before you start, and say so in the handoff.

## Checks

The about catalog test (no missing key in any locale), `task about:check`,
`task about:build`, `task about:e2e`; view the home page and two product pages
in each locale at 390 px and 1440 px and fix overflow or awkward line breaks
(CJK line breaking, German compounds).
