## Outcome

G-1029 made Discover's public ranking bootstrap and heal (`263499f79` on
main), and its tests pass on clean data. On the shared stack the ranking still
never activates: `/health/discovery-ready` stays `unavailable` with
`generation: null`, and Main logs `discovery refresh deferred` on every poll,
thrown from `readRealmPolicy(...)` → `WorkReadMissing('Realm is unavailable')`
inside `admitDiscoveryBasis` (`modules/discovery/source.ts:95`) via
`refresh.ts:86`. One realm-scoped basis whose Realm no longer has a policy
(left, deleted, Zone-only or a fixture leftover) retries forever and blocks
every other basis, including the public one.

Isolate failures per basis: a basis whose Realm is missing or no longer
eligible is retired (or skipped and reported) and never blocks other bases;
transient failures retry with backoff without starving the rest; the public
ranking builds and activates whatever any one Realm does. Expose retired or
blocked bases in `/health/discovery-ready`. Integration test with a deleted
Realm basis beside a healthy public basis. Then confirm on the shared stack
(curl `/health/discovery-ready` and `GET /v1/discovery/sections` on
`http://localhost:3001` after your change is live; Main hot-reloads from the
main checkout only after the manager merges, so say what awaits the merge).
