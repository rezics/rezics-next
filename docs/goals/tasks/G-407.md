## Outcome

On the shared stack, `GET /v1/me/continue?actingSubject=<Daniel>` answers `422
work_read_budget_exceeded` (seen in `.temp/manager/seed-g405.log`: "Home
Continue and new activity … Work read exceeds its budget"). It appeared once
G-387 began seeding the public-domain classics as real Books with many chapters
(Pride and Prejudice and others, next to 雨夜书店). Continue is the first thing
Home shows a returning reader, so it must answer for any library, whatever
the book lengths.

Find where the cost grows with chapter count (per-occurrence reads, whole
composition pages, per-book content probes) and make Continue's cost depend
only on the number of books shown:
- read each book's next unread chapter from the reader's progress and the
  composition by position;
- batch across books;
- never page a whole composition.

Keep its output (the next chapter, chapters left, the package update case)
and its privacy rules. Add a budget test with at least one 60-chapter Book
among the reader's current reads, as G-383 did for the feed
(`tests/qa/integration/feed-read-budget.test.ts`).

## Verify

On the shared stack, Daniel's Continue answers 200 within budget and names the
right next chapter for 雨夜书店 and for a classic he is reading. Use only the
backend; no web dev servers.
