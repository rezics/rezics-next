## Outcome

`task dev:seed` on the shared stack (main at `bffc378e` or later, relay caught
up) completes every step but exits 2 because its public-read checks report:

```
Readers also enjoyed: No co-readers for lumen-fabric after a complete generation
Mods discovery refresh: Discovery refresh (0/4 genre queries current) did not finish within 60 seconds
Home feed: no community Realm discussions
Readers also enjoyed: no co-readers for pride, serial, lumen-fabric
```

The last two have failed on every seed since G-425 and G-412's predecessors;
the first two appeared after G-425 made "Readers also enjoyed" type-aware
(books only suggest books) and after the Mods browse changes.

For each, find whether the product is wrong or the check's expectation is
stale, and fix the right one:

- A reader who opens Pride and Prejudice after the seed should see "Readers
  also enjoyed" with other books. If the seed's co-readers don't produce that
  for books, fix the seed or the generation; if a mod (lumen-fabric) should
  only list mods and the seed gives it no mod co-readers, give it some.
- Discovery refresh for the Mods genre queries should complete on a fresh
  seed within the check's bound, or the check should wait on the real
  completion signal instead of a fixed timeout.
- Signed out, Home · All should include discussions from community Realms the
  seed creates. If the ranking or the feed projection hides them, that's a
  product bug.

Don't paper over a failure by deleting its check. Keep the seed's total time
near its current 166 s.

## Verify

The shared stack runs the main checkout's code, so a Main fix won't show there
until merged. Reproduce and verify on your worktree's own stack (the QA
harness or `goalctl slot`), seeding it with your branch until the seed exits
0, and add integration tests for what you fixed in Main. Then look at Home
signed out and Pride and Prejudice's Work page in a real browser (BrowserOS in
your own tab) on whichever stack runs your code. The manager reruns the
shared seed after merge.
