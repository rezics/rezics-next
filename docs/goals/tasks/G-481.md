## Outcome

G-480 built `apps/about` (Astro and React, eight locales, the Rezics UI tokens,
the feature registry in `src/features.ts`, the notify form). It works, but the
maintainer found it flat and template-like: the home hero's three book cards
mean nothing at a glance, the product lines and "Why REZICS" are plain text
lists, and the four first scenarios all read "Planned". Redesign it into a
grand, minimal site that comes alive as you scroll, and write the English copy
for every page. G-483 builds the remaining pages from your patterns; G-482
translates.

**References** (the maintainer's; you decide what to take): shopify.com, and
the Shopify Editions pages https://www.shopify.com/editions/spring2026 and
https://www.shopify.com/editions/winter2026. The Editions pages are
extraordinary but too heavy for a product page, and REZICS has no video.
Capture them with Playwright at 1440 px and 390 px while scrolling, study their
rhythm (scale of type, pinned storytelling sections where the visual changes as
text scrolls, alternating bands, restraint between moments of spectacle), and
translate the lessons into REZICS's own language. Do not copy their assets or
layouts one to one. Load the `frontend-design` skill first.

Deliver:

1. **Art direction.** Large, confident typography; generous space; a small set
   of strong section patterns (hero, pinned scroll story, bento or grid
   showcase, big statement band, comparison, call to action). Stay within the
   Rezics brand: the logo red never carries text, ink blue carries coloured
   text; you may add deep ink and paper tones for bands and typographic cover
   palettes. Light and dark themes both designed, not inverted.
2. **Motion system.** Scroll-driven reveals, pinned sections and a few
   signature animations. Prefer native CSS scroll-driven animations
   (`animation-timeline: view()` / `scroll()`) and View Transitions, with
   progressive enhancement where unsupported; use the `motion` library (MIT)
   in React islands only where CSS cannot do it. No scroll hijacking, no layout
   shift, 60 fps on a mid-range phone, and `prefers-reduced-motion` gives a
   calm static version. Record any new library in
   `docs/development/toolchain.md`.
3. **Visuals without video.** Animated illustrations built from real Rezics UI
   components, SVG and typographic covers (never copyrighted art) that each say
   one thing within three seconds. For example: one light-novel series whose
   volumes flip between 日本語, 繁體中文 and English while a note says "Vol. 7
   in 繁體中文 · out Friday"; a Work's wiki growing entity by entity (characters,
   places, relations) as you scroll, each with a chapter citation; a reader's
   library importing from another site and resolving the right editions;
   a Realm conversation beside its maintained knowledge. The home hero must make
   "one story, every language and edition, yours to keep" obvious without
   reading small labels.
4. **Pages you build.** The home page (hero, the four first scenarios as a
   living showcase, product lines as a rich grid or bento, why REZICS as big
   statements, the direction at a glance, call to action) and three flagship
   pages that set the patterns for the rest: `/light-novels`, `/wikis` and
   `/agents` (add it: official and bring-your-own agents, Jev-based spam and
   advertisement review, auto-tagging, relation maintenance, normalisation,
   the wiki builder, disclosure and consent).
5. **Copy for every page**, English only, in the typed catalogs: `/`, `/reading`,
   `/light-novels`, `/serial-fiction`, `/acgn`, `/wikis`, `/agents`,
   `/communities`, `/distribution`, `/developers`, `/trust`, `/roadmap`. Sell the
   future with conviction, in concrete scenes; the availability label on each
   feature carries honesty, so do not hedge sentences with "we're building".
   Never invent users, partners, catalogue sizes, logos or testimonials. Update
   `src/features.ts` statuses truthfully (M4–M8 in `GOAL.md` are the plan;
   nothing product-facing is available yet except the site's own no-tracker
   policy) and phrase the roadmap so "planned" reads as momentum.
6. **Pattern notes for G-483**: a short `apps/about/PATTERNS.md` naming each
   section pattern, its component and how to use it, so G-483 can build the
   other pages consistently.
