## Outcome

On G-480's foundation, write and design every page of `apps/about` so that it
makes readers, writers, communities, creators and developers want REZICS now,
and states the direction plainly. English is the source; G-482 translates it.

**Voice.** Sell the future with conviction. Lead with what each person gets and
why it matters, in concrete scenes ("You finished volume 6 in Japanese; the
Traditional Chinese edition of volume 7 comes out Friday"), not architecture.
Do not hedge every sentence with "we're building": the availability label on
each feature (available, in development, planned, from the typed registry)
carries honesty, and the copy carries ambition. Never claim a user count,
partnership, catalogue size or ranking that does not exist.

**Pages** (each: outcome → a familiar frustration → a concrete walkthrough →
what makes REZICS different → availability → what comes next → action):

- `/` Home: "one home for stories across languages"; the four first scenarios;
  every product line; why REZICS (natively multilingual, your data is yours and
  portable, open source, the same API for people and agents); the roadmap in
  one glance; "Get notified".
- `/reading`: a library that remembers the edition you read across formats,
  imports your history from Goodreads, StoryGraph, 読書メーター and others,
  and exports everything.
- `/light-novels`: one series view across originals, translations and omnibus
  editions; what to read next in your languages.
- `/serial-fiction`: dependable drafting, scheduling, paragraph comments,
  author backup, AI-use disclosure, and a calm reader.
- `/acgn`: visual novels by usable release (language, platform, translator),
  anime and manga tracking by episode and volume.
- `/wikis`: every Work can have a wiki as complete as the Harry Potter Wiki:
  agents draft it from the text with chapter evidence, communities review it,
  readers see only what they have read; authors get a world bible with
  characters, places, timelines and maps that replaces separate worldbuilding
  tools.
- `/communities`: Realms where your identity is yours, not locked inside one
  community; clear rules in every language; fair moderation with reasons and
  appeals; recognition for helpful contributions.
- `/distribution`: publishing and selling books and games with a fair revenue
  share, multilingual storefront pages, the Work's wiki and community attached;
  honest about being new and better than the weakest stores first.
- `/agents`: agents that keep knowledge current (auto-tagging, linking
  characters to their Works, turning a post or video into a recipe, building
  wikis), spam and advertisement review, and bring-your-own agents through the
  API and MCP; disclosure and consent rules.
- `/developers`: the API is the product: every capability through HTTP, SDK and
  MCP with receipts and recovery; scoped tokens and OAuth apps.
- `/trust`: ratings and age rules, safety and reporting, AI-use labels, no
  advertising trackers, privacy, portability, open source.
- `/roadmap`: the direction and milestones (M4–M8 in `GOAL.md`) in plain
  language, with Now / Next / Later, and how to follow or contribute.

Illustrate with real product components or faithful mockups built from
`packages/ui`, typographic covers (never copyrighted cover art), and names
shown in several languages and scripts. Each product page gets a short FAQ and
a comparison section that is specific and fair ("better than X at Y").
