## Outcome

The official Mods and AI Workshop Zones (packages by G-348, approval seeded by
G-358) render generic cards because the data is not real:

1. **Mods**: `pkg.mod_resolution` is private and has no Work binding. Add a
   public, disclosure-safe binding from a mod Work to its package and a read of
   its compatibility (game, game versions, loaders, latest release, updated),
   bounded for card use, and expose it in the Zone module reads. Seed a handful
   of real-looking mods through the package flow (Minecraft mods for Fabric and
   Forge are the obvious examples; use the existing mod-provider fixtures) with
   public game and loader concepts for the filter bar.
2. **AI Workshop**: demo prompts are generic Works. Seed Skills and Prompts as
   published Hub items (`model/definitions/hub-item-v1.ts`) with revisions, and
   expose their card fields (tested models, a disclosed preview and the
   copyable prompt text) in the module reads, so Copy copies the prompt.
3. **Books people module**: the web adapter (`features/realm/adapt.ts`, held by
   G-353) must load the people read for the Books author spotlight; list the
   change it needs under PROPOSED TASKS.

After merge the manager reseeds; verify on an isolated backend
(`task dev -- --backend` in your worktree) that `/r/mods` and `/r/ai-workshop`
reach `execution.state: "package"` with real cards.
