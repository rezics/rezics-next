## Outcome

Each launch journey passes WCAG 2.2 A and AA axe checks, keyboard-only use,
visible focus, reduced motion and reflow at 200% and 400% zoom, in light and dark
themes, at phone and desktop widths, and in a CJK and a Latin locale:

- the LN/VN Work hub and edition choice;
- library import, progress on two devices and export;
- the franchise wiki Zone within the spoiler position;
- propose, review and inbox;
- sign-up with policy acceptance;
- report without an account.

The same journeys run in WebKit and Chromium mobile emulation, and on at least
one physical iPhone and one physical Android phone, including CJK IME input in
the proposal form and the review composer. The a11y harness exists
(`apps/web/tests/a11y.e2e.ts`); it covers page types, not these journeys.

## What to read

- `apps/web/tests/{a11y.e2e.ts,a11y-axe.ts,a11y-audit.ts,perf-targets.ts}` and `apps/web/playwright.config.ts` (one Desktop Chrome project today).

## Approach

- `scripts/qa/cases/launch-journeys.ts` lists the journeys, and a test fails when a journey lacks axe, keyboard, phone and locale coverage.
- Add WebKit and Chromium mobile projects to the Playwright config. Drive the physical phones through the maintainer's devices over the local network, or through BrowserOS remote debugging. If no device is available, record the blocker and ask the manager for a maintainer session, with the exact steps and a checklist.
- Defects become findings with severity and owner, and their failing tests stay in `g-743*`. Fix only trivial markup defects in the files you claim.

- Manager (2026-10-01): journeys now on main include the Work hub (G-850), Light Novels and Visual Novels Zones (G-853), franchise wiki Zone (G-849/G-920), Library import/export (G-855), tracking (G-838), reporting and case pages (G-820), staff triage (G-821), proposals and inbox (G-867/G-868), add-to-catalogue (G-843), sign-up and policy acceptance (G-736). Fix defects inside each feature as small owner changes; list larger ones as proposed tasks. task dev refuses in workers; browser runs through task goal -- test (QA slots are three — run suites sequentially).

## Do not build

Native apps, a device farm, paid testing services, visual-regression
infrastructure, or product fixes outside the claimed paths.

## Acceptance

The journey matrix passes on the pinned local stack, or each failure has a
failing test and an owner. Physical-device evidence (screenshots or recordings,
device and OS version) is attached to the handoff. The findings list records
screen-reader spot checks (VoiceOver or TalkBack on one journey each).
