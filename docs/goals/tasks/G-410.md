## Outcome

The maintainer found this gap in Storybook's `studio-work--chapters`: Studio
manages a book as one flat list of chapters with up and down arrows. Long
fiction isn't flat. Web novels come in volumes (分卷), with a prologue, extras
(番外) and author notes. Classics have Books, Parts and Letters (Pride and
Prejudice's three Volumes, Frankenstein's Letters before its chapters).

The model already allows a tree: `book-composition` has `group` and `chapter`
roles. The reader's Contents and Continue read groups. But Studio authors
only a flat list, the seed never creates a group, and parts of Main assume
chapters sit directly under the book: `structure/refresh.ts` skips anything
whose parent isn't the root.

Study how the best author back ends and readers handle structure, in a real
browser (BrowserOS `browseros` MCP) where public pages allow it:
- 起点作家后台 and 晋江's author pages, from public help pages and
  screenshots;
- Royal Road's chapter management, and AO3's multi-chapter works;
- the reader side: 起点's 目录 with its volumes, KadoKado's 目次.

Then build it end to end:

- **Main.** Groups (volume, part, extras) with titles, nested one level
  under the book, as the profile allows. Chapters are created at the end of
  a chosen group. A chapter can move between groups, and a group can be
  reordered, renamed or removed when empty. Each is a revisioned change with
  head, idempotency and history. Every read that assumes a flat book must
  handle groups: structure refresh and serial statistics, latest chapter,
  chapter counts, progress, Continue's next chapter across a volume
  boundary, and prev/next in the reader. Each read keeps a budget test with
  a multi-volume book.
- **Studio.** Chapter management as good as the best author back ends:
  - volumes shown as collapsible sections, with create, rename and reorder;
  - drag-and-drop to reorder chapters and move them between volumes, with
    keyboard alternatives;
  - each chapter's word count and state (draft, published, published with
    changes);
  - bulk select to publish or move;
  - "new chapter" at the end of a chosen volume.

  The tab row also shows stray up and down arrows at its right end (visible
  in the story); fix them.
- **Reader.** Contents grouped by volume (collapsible, the current one
  open), chapter numbering that reads naturally in each language, and
  "Volume 2 · Chapter 3" context in the reader header.
- **Seed.** Give 雨夜书店 two volumes plus a 番外. Give Pride and Prejudice
  its Volumes I–III and Frankenstein its Letters group, reusing G-387's
  public-domain chapters (move them into groups; don't republish). The seed
  must stay replayable.

Use the query and type vocabulary in `docs/contracts/queries.md`: a Work's
rdf:type is its type, never its "kind". Add English and zh-Hans strings only.

zh-Hans. Run one web dev server (`task web:dev`) only while checking, and
stop it when done.
