## Outcome

This is the page people will judge REZICS by. The maintainer asked for a
Reddit-like information-feed home, in the spirit of Reddit and the old site
(`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics`, read-only: `apps/web/features/explore/home.tsx`,
`features/content-feed/**`), designed around how people actually use REZICS.
Read `docs/plan/frontend.md` ("Principles", "Home", "Zones") and the research
behind it: `/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/manager/research-home.md`.

Build:

1. **Shell**: left navigation with followed Zones and Realms (new-activity
   dots), official Zones, Manage for moderators; header with search, the
   notification bell (unread badge from G-286's API) and the account menu; a
   `/notifications` page. Phone layout with bottom navigation.
2. **Home**: the Continue strip; `Following` and `All` tabs; visible Best /
   New / Top sort; the feed with one card anatomy for every kind (Realm ·
   person · time, content, bottom bar with vote, comments, the kind's action,
   share, menu), grouped updates, spoiler-safe chapter cards, "You're all
   caught up", a "N new posts" pill; filters in six human kinds; a right rail
   (trending in my Realms, Realms to follow, my queue). Signed out: All · Best
   with official-Zone tiles. Cold start: the interest picker and one-step
   follow (reserve the onboarding step G-292 left for it).
3. Encode `docs/experience/filter-feed-and-zone-experience.md` as stories and
   browser tests, then delete it and repair its links.

APIs: G-282 (follows, votes, feed with reasons, grouping and actions) and G-286
(notifications) are on `main`; G-302 (continue, new-since, viewer state,
onboarding interests and batch follow, preferences, mutes, trending) is in
progress — build against its contract as it lands and keep seams until then.
Covers come from `WorkCover` in `packages/ui` (G-283 is redesigning it).
Strings in your features' `messages.ts` following the per-locale layout G-288
introduced; internal links with `features/shell/localized-link.tsx`.

## Verify in a real browser

Use Claude in Chrome if available, or Playwright from `apps/web`, against the
shared stack with demo data (`bun scripts/dev/seed/cli.ts --dry-run` prints
credentials). Compare with Reddit side by side. Screenshots at 1440x900 and
390x844, light and dark, en and zh-Hans, signed out, new and returning person.
Stop dev servers before handing off.
