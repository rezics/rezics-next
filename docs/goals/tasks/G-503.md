## Outcome

Every web catalog has every English key in de, fr and es (Accounts and the catalogs not claimed
are already complete), typed complete
(`satisfies <Messages>`), and every official Zone's strings are complete in all seven non-English
locales. Expected volume after G-531: about 255 keys per locale (manage 71, communities 71,
studio 50, post-composer 32, condition-bar 18, profile 11, workPage 7, feed 3), about 765 strings;
plus Zone strings measured on 2026-09-30: `games` 16 keys missing in ja/ko/de/fr/es and 2 in
zh-Hant, `software` 10 keys missing in every locale but zh-Hans, a few in `ai-workshop` and
`mods` (some identical words may be correct; check each).

## What to read

`task i18n:check` output; each feature's English `messages.ts` with the existing de/fr/es files
for established terms; `apps/web/zones/official/books/strings.ts` (a complete Zone table).

## Approach

Translate from English. Keep `insert()`/`plural()` shapes, binding names and placeholders; keep
the distinction between completed, pending, partial, unavailable and denied outcomes, and do not
make destructive or permission wording stronger or softer. Zone strings: translate zh-Hant, ja
and ko as carefully as the European locales (short labels); zh-Hant is Taiwan usage, not a
conversion of zh-Hans. After filling a file, change `satisfies Partial<X>` to `satisfies X`; type
each Zone table so that a missing locale key is a type error.

- Studio catalogs (`apps/web/features/studio/messages/*`) are excluded here: G-591 holds them; G-599 translates Studio after it merges.

## Do not build

Do not edit English sources, components, other lanes' locale files, the checker or catalog
layout. No placeholder or copied-English entries.

## Acceptance

- `task i18n:check` reports `missing=0` for de, fr and es in every namespace, and the Zone tables
  have no untranslated key in any locale (the G-501 test reads them; until it lands, verify by
  importing each Zone's `strings(locale)`).
- `task web:typecheck` and `task accounts:typecheck` pass with the locales typed complete.
- Spot-check de and fr at phone width: Manage queue, Studio chapter list, community list, post
  composer and the Games and Software Zone pages; long German compounds wrap.

## Checks

`task i18n:check`; `task web:typecheck`; `task accounts:typecheck`; `task storybook:test` for the
Manage, Studio, communities, post-composer and official Zone stories.
