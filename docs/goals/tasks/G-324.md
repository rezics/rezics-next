## Outcome

Reviews (G-315) emit events, but the home feed does not show them. Per the
Home decisions in `docs/plan/frontend.md`, a review **with text** becomes a
feed card (a rating without text never does): reviewer, Work cover and title,
star rating, the review's opening lines with the spoiler gate honoured, helpful
count and a Read review action; grouped when one person reviews several Works
in a short window; hidden when the review is restricted. Add the item kind to
the feed contract and the interest mapping (books for book Works, and so on).
Backend only, no dev servers.
