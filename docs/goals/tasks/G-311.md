## Outcome

Display mode (system, light, dark) and "show Zone themes" are cookies on the
web app today (`rezics_theme` and the Zone "Page style" menu from G-296), so
they reset on every device. Store them on the Account with the account
language (G-288's `account-settings.ts`): a typed read and CAS write through
the Account API, exposed to the web app through a same-origin BFF read and
write, with the cookie kept as the signed-out value and as a first-paint
hint. Signing in adopts the account's values; changing either while signed in
writes both. The Accounts site's personal-info or preferences page shows them
(one line each; keep it small).
