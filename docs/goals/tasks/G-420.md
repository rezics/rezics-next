## Outcome

REZICS is a platform for reading, writing and sharing Works of every type
(books, web serials, mods, software, prompts, recipes) with communities (Realms)
and editorial Zones. The maintainer wants the best possible product experience
and asks us to learn from the best sites in each field, and to copy what is
useful. Read `GOAL.md`, `docs/plan/frontend.md` and
`docs/contracts/queries.md` first for what REZICS already decided.

Study **Goodreads** and **StoryGraph** (book page, reviews, friends' updates, lists, challenges, stats), **Letterboxd** (diary, reviews, lists, activity feed, profile), **X** and **Bluesky** (timeline comfort: spacing, type, media, threads, notifications), **Reddit** (subreddits, threads, notifications, onboarding) and **Discord** forum channels (threads). Focus on what makes a feed comfortable to read and a profile worth visiting, and on how a new person's first five minutes go (onboarding, first follows, empty states).

Study each site in a real browser: BrowserOS (`browseros` MCP; open your own
tab and leave other tabs alone), public pages only. Never sign in, create
accounts or submit forms. Where a page needs sign-in, use the site's public help
pages or published screenshots, and say so.

Write `docs/research/reference-social-reading.md` (under 400 lines):
- for each site, what it does best, with the page URL, and screenshots saved
  under `.temp/research/social-reading/` in your worktree;
- then **What REZICS should adopt**: a prioritised list of concrete changes,
  each naming the REZICS surface (Home, Work page, reader, Studio, Zone,
  Realm, Library, search, Discover, onboarding, notifications, profile,
  Manage), what to build and why, and whether Main needs new data. Mark what
  REZICS already does as well or better.

Compare with the running local REZICS (web :3000, signed out, and as Daniel
with credentials from `scripts/dev/seed/plan.ts`) wherever it helps.
