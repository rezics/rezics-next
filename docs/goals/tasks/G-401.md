## Outcome

Home is how people consume REZICS, and the plan models it on Reddit
(`docs/plan/frontend.md#home`). Until today the seed had no community to show.
G-385 added four community Realms (网文读书会, Minecraft Modding Help, Prompt
Craft, English Classics Reading Circle), 26 discussions with 58 replies and
votes, and 34 reviews. Home's Best feed now leads with community discussions.
Now make it feel like Reddit at its best.

Study Reddit's home feed, a subreddit and a comment thread in a real browser,
and the old site's feed (`../rezics`), then work on real content, as Daniel
and signed out:

- **Feed cards.** Discussion cards show the Realm, author, age, title, an
  excerpt, a vote control with score, the comment count and the one action.
  Review cards show the rating and the Work's cover. Spoilers stay veiled.
  Density and rhythm match Reddit's card view, with a compact view as an
  option if cheap.
- **Threads.** A discussion opens a Reddit-quality thread:
  - nested replies with collapse;
  - sort by best, new and top;
  - voting on replies;
  - replying inline;
  - "continue this thread" past a depth;
  - the Realm's rules and about in a side rail.

  Use Main's replies read; add a bounded read in `realm-reply` if the thread
  needs one (the current per-page policy queries could drop from about 20 to
  5, per G-383).
- **Realm discussions tab.** A subreddit-like list with sort and flair-like
  filters (question, recommendation, spoiler, solved) where Main has them.
  Don't invent data.
- **States.** The empty, loading, error and "you're all caught up" states
  follow the plan.

Follow the query vocabulary in `docs/contracts/queries.md` (maintainer
decision): no new "interest", "kind" or "category" names. Filters are Saved
Filters or Facet Conditions. G-397 is adding author follows to Main's
`feed/**`; don't edit Main's feed module, and coordinate through the manager
if you need a feed read change. Add English and zh-Hans strings only.
