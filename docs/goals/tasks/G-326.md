## Outcome

The milestone review (`.temp/manager/milestone-review.md` in the main
checkout, findings 3, 8, 21 and 25; screenshots in `.temp/manager/milestone/`)
found the official Fiction Zone — the signed-out front door and the
maintainer's KadoKado reference — is one white "Featured works" box with three
untitled pastel covers: no hero, latest chapters, rankings, editors' list,
genre chips, taglines or authors. Realm pages also have no Join/Follow and an
empty About.

1. **Content**: the Fiction official Realm and Zone get a real presentation
   (Serial preset; hero, latest chapters, day/week/month rankings, rising,
   editors' list, genre chips, reader quotes, recent decisions) and enough
   curated Works with taglines, covers and credited authors to fill it, through
   the seed's official-Realm step (`scripts/dev/seed/**`, API only). Do the
   same, lighter, for Books, Mods, AI Workshop, Software and Kitchen so no
   official Zone opens empty.
2. **Rendering**: one cover component everywhere (`WorkCover` / catalogue
   tiles; no blank pastel blocks), taglines and authors under covers, empty
   modules collapse, the redundant "Fiction · 小说 · Featured" subtitle goes.
3. **Join and About**: a Join or Follow control on every Realm page using
   G-314 (consent, invitations, self-join where settings allow) and G-282
   follows; About shows description, rules, member count and moderators
   (G-314's public roster).

Compare with KadoKado side by side (screenshots in `.temp/manager/refs/`) until
Fiction holds up on desktop and phone. Use the shared stack (web :3000); run
only your own web dev server if you need unmerged code, one at a time.
