## Outcome

M7's exit, proven on the merged main branch against the local stack, through the
API and in a real browser: propose, review, revise, decide, notify and recover
for a catalogue correction, a wiki bundle and a wiki chapter delta; changed
candidates invalidate approval; historical wiki rendering and export survive
dependency changes; a replaced or revoked assistant credential keeps the
artifacts it was authorized to make. The `product-audit` skill finds no open P0
or P1 class in contribution, review and inbox.

## Approach
