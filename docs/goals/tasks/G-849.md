## Outcome

A reader opens a franchise wiki Zone at `/r/{zone}` and finds a wiki: a home with the
franchise's Works, main characters, places, a chapter guide and a timeline; character and
place pages with an infobox from graph statements, names and aliases by language,
relationships as structured rows, appearances by chapter and evidence quotes with their
source; chapter pages listing what that chapter reveals. A position control in the Zone
frame ("Up to: Volume 1, chapter 3 — your progress") sets the `position` every read uses,
defaults as G-847 does and offers "Show everything". An empty or young wiki says what
exists and links to how holders build it with the toolkit; it never shows invented or
placeholder content. The Pride and Prejudice fixture wiki is seeded through the public
APIs, so the local stack shows a real one.

## Approach

- In G-655's detail arm, render non-Work members with `EntityPage({ frame: false, hrefFor })`, mapping links to Zone routes.
- `apps/web/zones/official/franchise-wiki/`: slots only (infobox layout, relationship rows, appearances by chapter, chapter guide, timeline list). No data access of its own; everything comes from the host's reads with `position`.
- `features/wiki/position-control.tsx`: reads the chooser list, writes `position` into the Zone's links and URL; keyboard and screen-reader accessible; a phone sheet.
- `config/zones/franchise-wiki.json`: the starter (Collections `works`, `characters`, `places`, `events`, `chapters`, their mounts and navigation labels). `franchise-wiki-step.ts` creates the fixture Zone from it through existing Space, Zone, Collection and mount APIs, records on each franchise Work a Statement linking its wiki Zone (one semantic Property definition, created as data if absent; G-850 reads it), submits a chapters 1–3 bundle as a seeded holder Agent (G-865 kind `wiki-bundle`) and applies it as a seeded steward; approve the package digest in the official Zones step.

## Do not build

filtering or membership logic (Main decides); no review UI (G-867 renders wiki proposals);
no per-franchise package; no mount management UI.
