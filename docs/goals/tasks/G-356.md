## Outcome

The maintainer asked for an admin panel that "deserves real design work and
must be good to use". G-262 built it (overview queues, users search with
operators, user pages, dangerous actions with friction, staff and roles, OAuth
clients, audit), and the milestone review judged it coherent. Take it from
good to excellent. Study the consoles operators love — Google Workspace admin,
Stripe dashboard, Linear admin, Clerk and Auth0 user management, Cloudflare
audit logs — and the research in `.temp/manager/accounts-research.md` (main
checkout), then decide and build what matters most for REZICS operators:

- the first screen answers "what needs me now" (risk signals: bursts of failed
  sign-ins, new passkey on an old account, mass consent, suspicious email
  changes after reset — note SR-1 from the security review);
- finding a person in two keystrokes and seeing their whole story on one page
  (timeline of security events, sessions grouped, apps, sanctions, notes);
- bulk actions with previews and undo windows where safe;
- an audit log people can actually query and export;
- keyboard-first operation with a command palette, and a design that stays
  calm on phone.

Account API additions go in `services/account/src/admin*.ts` with tests;
keep every dangerous action's friction and re-authentication. Strings per
locale (English and zh-Hans; other locales fall back).
