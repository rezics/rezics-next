## Outcome

The maintainer decided on 2026-09-28 how values work in REZICS
(`docs/contracts/queries.md#concepts-and-value-pages`; read it and the rest of
`queries.md` first, it is authoritative). A Concept page is what a tag page
was, and values combine as tags did, with AO3's typed tag filters as the
reference. This task builds the Concept side. G-400 landed the Facets
(`GET /v1/facets`, `model/definitions/facet-*-v1.ts`; the free Concept Facet
is labelled "Tags").

- **Concept page** (`/concepts/<id>`, locale-prefixed like every public
  page, drawn for its type):
  - label, description, and broader and narrower Concepts;
  - the Works that reach it through a Facet (the reverse query), on today's
    classified search (`public-main-classified-phrase-page-v1`, its
    `sense` input being a Concept);
  - a Condition bar that includes or excludes more values, matching all or
    any within one Facet;
  - Follow.

  Add the Main read the page needs, bounded with a budget test.
- **Follow a Concept.** Following a Concept follows its one-Condition Filter
  (the contract's Saved Filter). Add the follow kind in Main's follows
  (G-397 just added `external-author`; follow its pattern). Access migrations
  821–824 are reserved. The Home tab that a pinned follow becomes comes
  later: G-401 holds Home and the feed.
- **Chips on Works grouped by Facet.** On the Work page, accepted values show
  as chips grouped by their Facet ("Genre: Fantasy · Tags: 後宮 · …"), each
  opening its value page. Replace today's classification presentation.
- **Vocabulary.** Concept, Facet, Condition, Filter and Saved Filter; never
  "Sense", "tag" (except the Tags label), "interest" or "kind".

Add English and zh-Hans strings only.
