## Outcome

These capabilities resolve their target through G-506 `resolveTargets` instead of Work-only gates:

- **Discussion.** Replies and threads may be rooted on a release, a chapter occurrence, a character, or a Work
  without published text.
- **Collection members.** Any admitted resource can be a member, so a franchise Collection can hold series Works
  and characters. Zone populations need this (G-630).
- **Reviews and ratings.** These keep their current grains: MainVersion contexts for Works, and FixedRelease where
  an existing read serves it.

Routes change. The reads move from `/v1/works/:id/{reviews,ratings,rating-contexts,discussion}` to
`/v1/resources/:id/{…}`, and the old paths are deleted, with no aliases. The review command's `work` becomes
`target`. Storage is unchanged.

## Approach

1. **Swap the gates.** Each gate calls `resolveTargets(session, [iri], 'discussion' | 'listed' | 'review' |
   'rating')`, and `target_not_bound` maps to 422. Reviews and ratings keep their existing MainVersion or
   FixedRelease proofs.
2. **Reply roots.** A reply root must resolve for `discussion`. Its root revision must equal the resolved
   `revision`, or be a published text draft for Works that have one. Replace the `schema:CreativeWork` placement
   guard with a base-neutral existence guard.
3. **Collection members.** They authorize through the resolver. Non-Work bases need no content selection; state
   this in the collection profile, not in the structure kernel.
4. **Routes.** Move the four reads in their existing route files, and update the two web files mechanically.
5. **Guard.** Add the ast-grep rule `capability-targets-use-resolver`, with tests. It forbids `readWorkBasis(`,
   `publicWork(` and `canReadWork(` in the review, work-activity, realm-reply and collection modules.

## Do not build

- New-grain profiles or score-optional reviews (G-652).
- Library or progress changes.
- Migrations or column renames.
- Aliases for the old paths.
- Feed inclusion of non-Work discussion.
- Per-type rules.

## Acceptance

Through the API, on G-506's SAO subset:

- Discuss a chapter occurrence of `sao.web`, a character and a release. Replies on `sao.web` never appear under
  `sao.bunko`.
- A franchise Collection holds series Works and a character, in order.
- Reviewing and rating `sao.bunko` at the new paths behaves as before. A release review returns 422 until G-652.
- Anonymous replies on a private Work's occurrence are denied.
- Reintroducing `readWorkBasis` in `review/read.ts` fails the rule.
