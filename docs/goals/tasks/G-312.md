## Outcome

Frontend workers and the maintainer judge the product on the shared stack's
demo data, but `task dev:seed` (main checkout) ends with "Public API gaps":

- Work serial summary: `PUT /v1/works/{id}/metadata` 403 `authority_denied`
- Reading status: `PUT /v1/works/{id}/reader-status` 503 `dependency_unavailable`
- Chapter reading progress: `POST /v1/compositions` 403 `authority_denied`
- Realm moderation team and queue: `POST /v1/reports` 403 `governance_denied`
- Home follows and votes: `/v1/follows` 401 `account_assertion_denied` (seed
  tokens lack the `follow:*`/`feed:vote` scopes G-282 added)
- Public Work list: 409

Make every step succeed through the public APIs, using the right actor and
the grants a real deployment would give (author baselines from G-304; the
fixture operator for official Realms), never SQL. Where a gap is a real Main
defect rather than a seed mistake, fix nothing in Main: report it with the
exact request under PROPOSED TASKS. Also add what the product needs to look
alive: a standing Global rating question with ratings (Discover favorites and
stars), Realm adoptions and decisions in the official Realms (Zone modules),
chapters with reading progress (Continue strip), follows and votes (home feed),
quotable replies. Split `cli.ts` into one file per step with a single ordered
step list, so parallel work stops colliding in it. Demo sign-in credentials
must be printed by `bun scripts/dev/seed/cli.ts --dry-run`.

`tests/qa/integration/access-baseline-seed.test.ts` fails on `main` since G-290's
official-Realm seed: without an operator the seed exits 1 instead of reporting
a gap (exit 2); keep that test's guarantee (no grants for baseline members).
