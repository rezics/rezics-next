## Outcome

On the shared stack the Fiction Realm (`01a0e90b-61b5-7165-95cd-50b4e381aa20`)
answers every settings and management call with
`503 realm_management_unavailable: Realm policy publication is pending; retry`
(`modules/access/realm-management.ts:185,205`,
`realm-management-settings.ts:75`), indefinitely, while six other Realms
work (G-909). Its adoptions and quote placements fail as a consequence. A
pending state with no path to completion is a recovery defect. After this
task a Realm policy publication either completes or is recovered: the owner
that left it pending (find which delivery, outbox event or graph write it
waits for) resumes or re-drives it idempotently, an operator can see and
recover it, and management calls succeed again once it completes.

## Approach

Reproduce on the shared stack read-only first (inspect the Realm's pending
publication record and what it waits for; never write tables by hand). Find
why it stuck (a relay gap from before G-896, an interrupted Jena write, a
lost delivery) and fix the class: the publication's owner recovers pending
publications on startup and on demand, through its own command, with a test
that interrupts publication midway and proves recovery. Then recover the
Fiction Realm through that path.

## Do not build

No manual database edits; no new admin UI; no new tables unless recovery
needs a record that does not exist.

## Acceptance

Integration: a publication interrupted after its first write is completed by
recovery, and management calls succeed; the shared stack's Fiction Realm
settings return 200.
