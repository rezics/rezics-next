## Outcome

G-287 added Realm settings, but every Realm is still public with mandatory
review because `space-realm-v1` fixes both, and member search matches only
Agent identifiers. Moderators expect Reddit's and Discourse's choices:

1. **Visibility**: public, restricted (anyone reads, only approved members
   post or submit) and private (members read), enforced by every read and
   write owner that serves Realm content — directory, search, discovery,
   feed, Realm reads, replies, submissions — with disclosure tests for each.
   Changing to private must not leak what was public through caches or
   search; say how existing public copies are handled.
2. **Review mode**: mandatory review, trusted members publish directly, or
   open; enforced where submissions and replies are admitted.
3. **Member search** by display name and handle (CJK-aware, indexed, bounded),
   for the members page.

The profile change crosses owners; list every owner you touch in the handoff
and keep changes outside your claim to the minimum they need (OWNER CHANGES).
