## Outcome

The wiki position chooser ([collections at any size](../../plan/frontend.md#collections-at-any-size))
must find a chapter in a story of any length. `GET /v1/reading-positions/{work}`
takes no search parameter, so the web pages through every chapter. Add `q`
(chapter title or number, in any language the chapter carries) and `cursor`/
`limit` with the list shape `{ items, nextCursor, complete }`, filtered and
ordered in the store before paging, keeping today's disclosure and spoiler
rules and the anonymous behaviour. Cover a 1,000-chapter story, CJK and numeric
queries, and denied cases.

## Do not build

No new position semantics and no UI (G-945 consumes it).
