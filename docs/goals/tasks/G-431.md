## Outcome

The maintainer decided (docs/contracts/queries.md, "Concepts and value
pages"; docs/plan/frontend.md, Home): Home's tabs are `Following`, `All`, then
the Saved Filters a reader pins (usually one Concept) and `+`, as X pins topic
timelines and Reddit keeps custom feeds. A new person picks languages and
Concepts, which become follows and pinned tabs, and follows suggested Realms in
one step. Home is never empty.

Today onboarding still speaks the retired "interest" vocabulary
(`services/main/src/modules/onboarding-interests`, `HomeInterestKind`,
`apps/web/features/home/interest-picker.tsx`) and Home has no pinned tabs.
Concept pages and Concept follows exist (G-409), G-426 is giving Concepts a
shared scheme with localized labels and broader/narrower links, and G-427 is
moving Search, Discover, Home Filters and Concept pages onto the Query
(`POST /v1/query`, FilterDocument; G-421).

Build the whole first-minute experience, to the standard of X's, Reddit's and
Pinterest's topic onboarding and Goodreads' genre picker:

1. **Saved Filters in Main.** A reader's named Saved Filter holds a
   FilterDocument exactly as the contract says (exact DefinitionRefs and
   Context revisions; it cannot enlarge server budgets), with pin order.
   Create, rename, reorder, unpin and delete; following a Concept creates its
   one-Condition filter. Access migrations 840–844.
2. **Home tabs.** Following, All, pinned filters, `+`. A pinned tab's feed is
   Home's feed filtered by that Saved Filter through the Query, with the same
   Best/New/Top line. `+` opens a styled picker: search Concepts (localized
   labels, broader/narrower to refine), or save the current Filters as a tab.
   Tabs scroll horizontally on phones and are reorderable on desktop.
3. **Onboarding.** Signed in for the first time: content languages (from the
   reader's locale plus choices), then Concepts from the shared scheme grouped
   by Type (novels, games, software, mods, recipes…) with covers or examples
   so a choice means something, then suggested Realms to follow, in one flow
   with Skip at each step and a finished Home at the end. Retire the
   "interest" words and types throughout what you touch; keep persisted ids
   unchanged or migrate them through new revisions.
4. **Never empty.** A reader who skipped everything still gets All · Best and
   an invitation to pin a topic; a thin Following is filled with labelled
   recommendations that can be turned off (frontend.md Home).

Reuse `postRhythm` (features/feed/post-row.tsx) and `@rezics/ui` controls; no
native `<select>`.
