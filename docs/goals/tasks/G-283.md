## Outcome

**Maintainer feedback (2026-09-28):** compare our Discover story
(http://localhost:6006/?path=/story/discover-page--global-overview) with
Goodreads — ours feels wrong. The manager's diagnosis, recorded in the revised
`docs/plan/frontend.md` (read "Principles" first):

- the cover is framed inside a tall rounded card, and missing covers become a
  single-letter monogram that reads like an empty avatar;
- no author on cards; instead a type badge, "4.4 / 5 · 12 ratings",
  "Books · Global · 4 works" — internal concepts shown to readers;
- shelves mix a novel, a JavaScript runtime and a recipe;
- no primary action (want to read / shelve / rate);
- the grid canvas background makes content pages look like a design tool.

Redesign the catalogue surfaces to Goodreads/StoryGraph quality on Rezics Aura:

1. **`WorkCover` in Rezics UI**: real cover when present; otherwise a
   generated typographic cover (title and author set in the Work-title face on
   a palette derived from the Work type and a stable hash; CJK-aware; looks like
   a book, poster or package tile as appropriate), at standard sizes with the
   right aspect ratios. Stories for every type, long titles, CJK, dark mode.
2. **Cards and shelves** (Discover, Search results, home shelves): cover-first
   grid with no frame, title, author(s), a compact star rating with count in
   plain words, and one primary action (want to read / add to shelf, rate
   inline) using the collections and rating APIs. Curated shelf titles
   ("Popular in Classic Literature", genre/term shelves, "Recently added"),
   scope shown only when the reader chose a community. Density like Goodreads.
3. **Work page header**: Goodreads-style layout — large cover on the left with
   want-to-read/shelf and your rating under it; title, author(s) with links,
   rating summary with distribution, description, genres/tags; details and
   identifiers moved into a collapsed "Details"/"Cite" section. Keep scope
   switching available but not dominant.
4. **Discover and Search** consume the newer Main contracts: rating Context
   listing (no `?context=` needed for Top rated), richer discovery items
   (credits, tags, term names; G-270), typed search summaries and facets (G-269).

Use Claude in Chrome if available, or Playwright from `apps/web`, to compare
with https://www.goodreads.com and the old site's feed cards in
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics` (read-only). Another worker (G-280) is moving
routes under `app/[locale]/` and owns `apps/web/app/**`, `features/shell/**` and
`i18n/**`: do not edit those; put strings in your features' `messages.ts`, and
list any one-line route or shell change you need in the handoff. Missing API
fields → PROPOSED TASKS, not client workarounds.
