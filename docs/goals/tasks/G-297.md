## Outcome

G-286 made notifications readable (display items, redaction at read time,
grouping, unread count) but reported that the product events people expect
do not notify anyone yet: replies to my contributions or replies, decisions on
my Realm submissions, moderation outcomes on my content or reports, and changes
to my Realm roles or membership (G-287's Realm management). Add those producers:

1. **Recipient selection** that is durable and policy-aware: who is notified
   for each event kind (author of the replied-to item, submitter, reporter and
   affected author, the member whose role changed), never the actor, never
   someone who cannot see the target at delivery time, honouring notification
   preferences.
2. **Consumers** on the outbox relay with a durable cursor, idempotent per
   event and recipient, so a replay never double-notifies (see the relay-reader
   inventory test).
3. Display kinds for each new item in G-286's enum, so the web bell renders
   them without new client logic.

Coordinate with the owners you read from (member replies, Realm submissions,
reports, `modules/access/realm-management*.ts`) through their events; do not
edit them. Leave a typed hook for follow notifications, which G-282 is adding.
