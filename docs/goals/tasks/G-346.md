## Outcome

The nav's Library currently opens the viewer's profile shelves (a stopgap from
G-330). Readers need the place Goodreads calls "My Books" and StoryGraph calls
the library: study both (Goodreads' My Books, StoryGraph's library and stats)
and build `/library` for the signed-in reader on the APIs already on `main`:

- status shelves (want to read, currently reading, read) and custom shelves
  from G-285 and G-294, with counts, sorting (date added, title, author,
  rating, last read), grid and list views, and bulk move between shelves;
- currently reading with progress per Work and "continue" to the next unread
  chapter (G-302's Continue read and progress);
- read history with started and finished dates, the reader's ratings and
  reviews (G-315), editable in place;
- privacy of the library (G-294's visibility setting) visible and changeable
  here;
- empty and first-use states that lead to Discover and the home feed.

Reuse `features/catalogue` and `WorkCover` so books look the same everywhere;
strings in per-locale message files (English and zh-Hans; the translation
tasks fill the rest). Missing reads go to PROPOSED TASKS.

## Verify in a real browser

Against the shared stack as a demo reader with shelves and progress
(credentials in `scripts/dev/seed/plan.ts`). Screenshots at 1440x900 and
390x844, light and dark, en and zh-Hans, next to Goodreads' My Books. Run one
web dev server at a time, only while checking, and stop it when done.
