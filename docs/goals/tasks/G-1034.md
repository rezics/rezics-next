## Outcome

G-1026 measured a Realm's community API (profiles in the `green-5` worktree,
`.temp/work-profiles/g-1026-{before,after}.json` and `g-1026-plans/`) and
found:

1. **Best and Top read only the newest 256 threads** and rescan them for
   ranking on every request: an older thread with more votes can never reach
   Top, and at page size two Fuseki response bytes grow from about 16.8 KB to
   90.7 KB as the Realm grows from 4 to 64 threads. Keep the ranking formula
   ([Home › Best](../../plan/frontend.md#home); constants in
   `modules/feed/ranking.ts`) and replace the cohort with complete selection:
   a maintained ranking index (score inputs updated by votes, replies and time
   buckets, Top per period) that a page reads by keyset with cost bounded by the
   page, independent of the Realm's size and history. Research how Reddit and
   comparable systems maintain hot/top listings if the owner docs record no
   design, and record the decision with sources.
2. **The header still costs 17 (anonymous) and 32 (member) SQL statements**
   with 12 rules after G-1026's batching. Bring it to a small fixed bound
   independent of the number of rules, moderators and roles.

Prove both at Realm sizes of 100, 1,000 and 10,000 threads with long history,
using G-1024's work profile, and encode the bounds as profile tests.
