## Outcome
Anyone can report any admitted resource by URL or ID: signed in, signed out, or
with a suspended account. They get a private case credential and use it to read
the status, outcome and reasons and to send follow-up. The affected party uses
the same credential mechanism to appeal or send a DMCA counter-notice. The
day-one categories each reach their own process. Child exploitation, NCII and
credible threats are urgent, and their evidence is restricted. NCII fixes its
receipt time and a 48-hour removal deadline. Copyright notices enter the existing
`dmca_512` rights process. A valid counter-notice records its earliest and
latest restoration dates (10 and 14 business days). A child-exploitation report
places a preservation hold on its target and the target's author, and erasure
respects the hold. Everything works through the API.

## Approach
- Verified today: intake needs a bearer, an acting Agent, an authority and context the caller builds, and caller-built evidence (`reports.ts:46-57`, `store.ts:164-233`). No public route exists, no platform authority scope is seeded, and `reasonCode` is a free string.
- Add one public intake route. It takes:
  - a target: a `readId` resolved with `resolveTargets`, or a URL the server resolves to one through the address owner;
  - a category from one closed, versioned list in code;
  - a statement and its content language (any BCP 47 tag);
  - a contact email, required for NCII and copyright;
  - the category's declarations: the NCII statement and signature, or the §512(c)(3) elements.
- The server chooses the jurisdiction: platform for the day-one and legal categories, the Realm for Realm-rule reports. It captures evidence at the exact current head with `ownerEvidenceCapture`. `Idempotency-Key` replays return the same case.
- Seed one platform authority scope and a platform safety-specialist role in migration 930.
  - A valid bearer attaches the reporter's principal, which lets signed-in reporters list their own reports.
  - No bearer, or a rejected one such as a suspended account's, records a contact-only reporter.
- Case credential:
  - A random secret, returned once and stored only as a hash. It never appears in logs or URLs that Main logs.
  - Status reads and correspondence need it. Correspondence kinds are `message`, `appeal`, and `counter_notice` with its declarations. Correspondence keeps its original language.
  - Export one function that mints a party credential for the affected party. G-565 calls it when a decision goes against a target.
- Urgent categories set `urgent` on the case. Their evidence can be read only with the specialist grant. Realm moderators and general tools never receive it.
- NCII writes an immutable `received_at` and a process step due 48 hours later. No later step resets it.
- The copyright category opens the existing `dmca_512` complaint instead of a new flow.
- A counter-notice with complete declarations writes two process steps: restoration not before 10 business days and not after 14. A small pure business-day function in code computes them. Restoration itself is a staff decision in G-565.
- Preservation hold:
  - A governance row keyed by target and account, created on child-exploitation intake. Staff may add one to other urgent cases through G-565.
  - Account and Content erasure postpone deleting held material and record why. Everything else in the erasure proceeds.
- Governance notices to parties become mandatory. Social and subscription notifications stay optional.

## Do not build
- No second moderation module beside governance.
- No synthetic "anonymous" Agent that holds moderation rights.
- No automated triage or scoring, and no free-form categories.
- No evidence upload: never ask a victim to re-upload imagery.
- No new mail system: reuse notifications and Account mail.
- No holiday calendar service.
- No UI (G-820) and no queue or decisions (G-565).

## Acceptance
- API integration tests:
  - Submitting without an account and replaying the key after a lost response gives one case.
  - Status reads work with the credential. A wrong credential fails without revealing whether the case exists.
  - Follow-up messages are accepted.
  - A suspended account's token is accepted as a contact-only reporter.
  - An NCII case's `received_at` and deadline survive a clarification message.
  - A copyright report creates a `dmca_512` complaint. A counter-notice gets restoration dates that skip weekends.
  - After a child-exploitation report, erasing the author's account keeps the held material and records the hold.
  - A Realm moderator is denied urgent evidence; the specialist can read it.
  - Reports work on a Work, a person, a post or reply, a Realm and a media asset, and keep a content language outside the eight UI locales.
- Class guard: fail if any category lacks a process mapping, and fail if any intake or status route requires authentication.
