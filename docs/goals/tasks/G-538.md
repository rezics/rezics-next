## Outcome

Decision 4 and `docs/contracts/content-languages.md#user-submitted-translations`:
"User translations are private by default. One becomes public only on a
recorded basis: a public-domain or freely licensed source, the author's
permission, or the author's standing rights offering." Today publication
eligibility knows only `original-contribution` (the draft's author,
`eligibility.ts:161-163`) and `public-domain`. So a translator can declare their
translation an own Work, publish it as an original contribution, and link it as
a third-party translation of a protected Work, which baseline allows
(`translation-links.ts:320-330`). After this task, content whose Work is a
translation of a Work by another author cannot become public on the
`original-contribution` basis. It can on `public-domain`. Permission and
rights-offering bases do not exist yet and fail closed with a typed reason.

## What to read

The contract section above; `content-publication/eligibility.ts`,
`eligibility-admitted.ts`; `work/translation-links.ts`;
`docs/contracts/work-and-release.md#work-levels-realizations-and-versions`
(translations are realizations; the rule must key on the translation relation,
whatever its representation).

## Approach

Check both orders, in the existing admission transactions:
1. Admitting `original-contribution` fails with `translation_basis_required`
   when the target Work has a translation link or realization-of relation to a
   Work the acting Agent does not author.
2. Creating such a link from a Work with public original-contribution content
   fails the same way, unless the source is public domain.
Reuse the author-credit check that `author-baseline.ts` already uses.

## Do not build

No permission or rights-offering basis, eligibility profile v3, rights request
flow or author-consent UI. Those are creator and distribution scope, deferred.
No change to `public-domain` handling. No new tables.

## Acceptance

- API: an author publishes their own Work. A translator makes a Work, links it
  as a translation of the author's Work, then tries to publish → 409
  `translation_basis_required`. Reverse order (publish, then link) → the link is
  refused. A translation of a public-domain Work with a current assessment →
  publishes. The author's own translation of their own Work → publishes.
- Class guard (`g-538-translation-basis.test.ts`): removing either check fails it.
