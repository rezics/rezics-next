## Outcome

The maintainer: each Zone presents information the way its field's best site
does; Games follow Steam, Software the best software directories
(docs/plan/frontend.md, Zones). G-422 shipped the Games and Software Zones
with bounded facts APIs (`/v1/game-facts`, `/v1/software-facts`), Work types
through `work-type-v2`, seeded catalogues and approved packages. The manager
opened both on the shared stack (2026-09-28) and found them far from Steam
and Flathub:

- **Games** (`/r/games`): the featured capsule and the tiles crop portrait
  typographic book covers into landscape slots, so titles are cut off ("Light
  … Mira Park 朴美罗" clipped). Minecraft and Baldur's Gate 3 are credited to
  a demo person (Mira Park) instead of their developers. Every card says
  "Review summary not tracked".
- **Software** (`/r/software`): every card says "Version not tracked"; icons
  are monogram placeholders; there is an unlabeled icon button; no
  categories, no "New & updated", no alternatives.
- The Zone cards don't read the facts at all: `ZoneWork.game` and
  `ZoneWork.software` are never filled (G-422's handoff lists the fields:
  game `source`, `status`, `releaseDate`, `platforms`, `languages`, `tags`,
  `screenshots`, `review.{label,count,period}`, `modsZone`; software
  `project`, `source`, `maintainer`, `license`, `screenshots`,
  `releases.{platform,architecture,version,changes,destination}`,
  `alternatives.{work,reason,attributedTo}`).
- A game or app Work page is the generic Work page.

Make both Zones and their Work pages feel like their references, with REZICS'
honesty (REZICS counts no downloads and sells nothing; it links to where the
game or app is officially got):

1. **Cards and art.** Fill `ZoneWork.game` / `.software` from the facts reads
   in the card adapter. Landscape slots get landscape art: a screenshot or
   header image when the facts have one, else a typographic fallback designed
   for wide slots (never a cropped book cover). App tiles use square icons
   with a designed fallback. Show only what is known; a missing fact is
   absent, not "not tracked".
2. **Games like Steam.** Home: a featured capsule carousel, New & trending,
   Upcoming (release date in the future), shelves by Concept (genre) from the
   shared vocabulary, and the Mods Zone link for games that have one. Browse:
   Steam-like filters (genre Concepts, platform, release status, language)
   through the existing Zone browse. Work page: media-first header
   (screenshots gallery), release status and date, platforms, languages,
   developer and publisher, the official page or store as the primary action,
   "Mods for this game" when the Mods Zone has them, reviews and discussions.
3. **Software like Flathub.** Home: featured banner, Editor's picks with
   reasons, New & updated (from recorded releases), categories as Concepts,
   and "Alternatives to …". Work page: icon, one-line summary, screenshots,
   install per platform (the recorded destinations), license, maintainer,
   source repository, release notes by version, alternatives with the reason
   and who attributed it.
4. **Credits and seed.** Credit seeded games and apps to their developers as
   Organizations (not demo people), give them screenshots or header art
   fixtures that are licensed for the repo (or clearly labelled
   placeholders), release dates, platforms and a few upcoming titles.

Keep one visual language (the host's tokens; a Zone package changes structure,
not colours) and reuse the shared cards, carousel and browse where they fit.
