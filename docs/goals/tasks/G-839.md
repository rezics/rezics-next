## Outcome

The catalogue structure is maintainable without the API, on phone and desktop:

- An editor with edit authority on a Work (as Main's `allowedActions` or grant
  reads say) adds, reorders and removes parts with labels and inclusion; records
  Rewrite, Reboot, Sequel, SpinOff, Adaptation and correspondence with evidence;
  and adds realizations (language, translator, source or unresolved, status) and
  releases (identifiers, format, platform, territory, coverage of one or more
  realizations). Every write sends the expected head and shows Main's receipt or
  problem; a stale head offers reload without losing input.
- People without edit authority see no edit controls, never disabled fakes.
- "Propose merge" on a Work picks the duplicate by search and creates G-865's
  `merge` proposal (G-836), then links to G-867's proposal page. If G-836 or G-867
  has not merged at dispatch, leave the action unexported and list the wiring in
  the handoff instead of a placeholder that cannot act.

## Approach

- Edit routes live at `/w/{ref}/edit/parts`, `/edit/relations` and
  `/edit/editions`, outside the hub's `(work)` group, and reuse G-837's read
  components; forms post to Main through server actions and
  keep no business rules (kind lists come from G-832's lexicon read, grain
  checks from Main's problems).
- Copy in the feature's eight catalogs; relation labels only from Main.

## Do not build

- No proposal flow for structural edits by non-editors (a G-865 adapter is a
  separate decision), no merge review screen (G-867), no bulk editing.
- No client-side cycle, coverage or numbering validation beyond input shape.

## Acceptance

- Browser against the local stack, desktop and phone: as an appointed editor, add
  "22 Reverse" after New Testament 22, move it, record Genesis Testament as
  Sequel of New Testament with evidence, add a zh-Hans realization of SAO volume 1
  and an omnibus release covering volumes 1–3, and see each on G-837's pages
  after reload; a second tab's stale edit shows the conflict; a reader account
  sees no edit controls; propose merging two duplicate volume 1 records and land
  on the proposal page. Reviewed screenshots in the handoff.
- Class guard: a test fails if any edit control renders for a viewer whose
  allowed actions do not include it.
