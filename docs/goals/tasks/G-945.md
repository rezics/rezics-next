## Outcome

Read [Discover](../../plan/frontend.md#discover) and
[Collections at any size](../../plan/frontend.md#collections-at-any-size).
G-939 gave Main one query over every type with a type Facet, Discover sections
with reasons, indexed Concept search, rating populations and the list shape;
`EntityPicker` is in `@rezics/ui` (G-941). Today Discover shows shelves
hardcoded to book, guide and recipe (`features/discover/state.ts:11`), the
communities directory is a separate page with a 20-item alphabetical Topics
list and an Apply button, the rating scope is a row of up to 20 Realm pills in
UUID order, the Realm-to-submit chooser lists the first 20, and the wiki
position chooser reads ten pages.

After this task:

1. **Discover** is the one browse: search, topic chips (followed first), type
   tabs (All, Works, Communities, Sites, People, Lists, Topics), the sections
   Main returns with their reasons, and complete traversal per tab through the
   one query. No type name is hardcoded; the communities directory becomes the
   Communities tab, and its separate page, Main's deprecated `/v1/realms` list
   and vocabulary reads, and their web callers are removed.
2. **Topic picker** on `EntityPicker` over Concept search: an empty field shows
   followed and most-used topics; results show the broader Concept and a count;
   include/exclude chips apply immediately through the URL. Use it in Discover,
   the condition bar, saved filters and onboarding.
3. **Rating scope picker**: Global, the reader's communities and the most-rated
   communities first; "Other communities…" opens `EntityPicker` over the
   target's rating populations.
4. **Searchable choosers** for the Realm to submit to and the wiki position, and
   "See all" from every preview that shows a few of an unbounded list (the
   home rail's Realms to follow, a community's related communities).
5. Stories in all eight locales with thousands of items, slow and failed loads,
   and phone width; a browser test walks Discover tabs and topics, the rating
   picker past the first page, and submitting to a Realm found by search.
