## Outcome

The Manage workspace (G-299, polished by G-330) needs data Main does not expose:

1. **Team**: the members read includes active role holders who have not
   joined, marked as such, so Members lists the whole team.
2. **Audit detail**: Realm management audit items carry what changed — role,
   member, permissions gained or lost, valid until — stored in `changeRole`'s
   receipt and exposed through the management read contract.
3. **Open counts**: decided governance cases stop counting as waiting in the
   moderation read and the managed-Realm counts, while a decision can still
   be reversed (today the nav says 17 waiting while the queue shows 7).
4. **Invitations**: a list of a Realm's outgoing invitations (who, when,
   expiry, state), and notifications for received invitations with an unread
   badge.
5. **Notifications**: role changes say given or taken; names of Realms and
   roles re-resolve for notifications created before G-329; review
   notifications carry the Work so the UI can link `/w/{work}#review-{id}`.
6. **Rights complaints**: `/v1/moderation/decisions` accepts the interim and
   final restriction outcomes rights complaints need.

Backend only, no dev servers.
