## Outcome

**Product decision (manager, 2026-09-28):** an ordinary person who signs up and
verifies their email can take part in REZICS without anyone granting them
permissions by hand. Today nothing gives a new account any permission: Work
creation in dev comes from a direct SQL grant (`grantWorkCreation` in
`scripts/dev/web-auth-bootstrap.ts`, an 8-hour grant), and `POST /v1/spaces`,
`/v1/contributions`, `/v1/translation-links` and `/v1/collections` fail with
`503 dependency_unavailable` because `access.scope_gate` rows for
`space:create:root`, `contribution:create:<work>`, `translation:link:<work>`
and `collection:edit:<collection>` do not exist, and with 403 after that
because no grant exists. `/v1/agents` gives new users only `agent.control`.

Design and implement, in Access, a system-maintained **baseline member policy**:

- Every account with a verified email, acting as its own person Agent (and its
  pen names), holds baseline permissions in public scopes: create Works;
  create contributions and propose translation links on public Works; create
  and edit its own Collections/shelves; rate; personal classification; comment
  and reply; report; create Spaces subject to a quota (propose a number, for
  example 3 per account per 30 days, and reuse the existing quota owner).
- Organizations and Realms keep their own stricter policies; the baseline never
  grants moderation, adoption or Realm management.
- Suspension (Account, G-205) and erasure remove the effect immediately through
  the existing token/fence path; baseline never survives a suspended account.
- Scope gates: root scopes exist by migration; per-target gates (per Work,
  Collection, Space) are created with their target, or derived by a rule, so
  no ordinary write can fail with `scope gate is unavailable`. Existing
  targets get a bounded backfill.
- Admission, receipts and audit stay exactly as strict as today; the baseline
  is a role binding or equivalent first-class grant source, not a bypass.

Read `docs/contracts/identity-and-access.md`, `services/main/src/modules/access/**`
and how tests grant permissions today (`scripts/dev/qa-work-read.ts`,
`scripts/load/corpus.ts`). Then make `scripts/dev/seed/**` (the demo seed)
rely on the baseline instead of SQL grants, and in the handoff give the exact
change to drop `grantWorkCreation` from `scripts/dev/web-auth-bootstrap.ts`
(claimed by another task until it closes).
