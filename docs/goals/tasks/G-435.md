## Outcome

Manage (`apps/web/features/manage`) has a queue, rules, roles, members, a
log and settings, built for Realm moderation of Works and chapters. Three
kinds of work that need a moderator's decision don't reach it:

1. **Held replies.** A Realm with mandatory review cannot take replies at all
   today (noted when G-401 landed). A reply in such a Realm should be held,
   visible to its author as "Waiting for review", and appear in Manage's queue
   with the thread's context, approve / remove with a reason, and the author
   notified of the outcome.
2. **Concept proposals.** Authors and readers can propose a Concept for a
   Work (`POST /v1/tag-proposals`; "tag" is only the UI label, see
   docs/contracts/queries.md). Stewards need to see them in Manage, with the
   Work, the proposed Concept (matching an existing one in the shared scheme
   from G-426, or new with labels in both languages), the proposer and prior
   decisions, and accept (a classification decision), merge into an existing
   Concept, or reject with a reason.
3. **Reports.** Readers report posts, replies, reviews and Works; the report
   lands in that Realm's queue (or the site's for Global) with the reason and
   reporter count, and the moderator's actions include remove, lock thread,
   and mute or ban a member for a period, each recorded in the log.

Model the queue on Reddit's mod queue and Discourse's review queue: one list
with type filters and counts, keyboard triage (j/k, a approve, r remove), bulk
actions for the same decision, a reason picker from the Realm's rules, and
every action undoable from the log while it's recent. Show the acting identity
before each decision (design principle: identity before action).

G-412 (communities, running, near merge) owns `services/main/src/modules/realm-reply/**`;
read its reply model and put review logic in a new `modules/reply-review`
module. The hook in realm-reply's member policy that holds a reply instead of
refusing it waits for G-412's merge: ask the manager then and your claim is
widened to that file. G-413 added notifications; use its
producers for review outcomes.
