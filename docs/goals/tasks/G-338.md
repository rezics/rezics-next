## Outcome

Main now models authorship (G-327): Works created with `authoring: "own-work"`
get a confirmed author credit, imported Works carry retained source authors
(G-322), and chapters belong to their serial. The demo seed predates this: the
classics (Pride and Prejudice, Alice, 西游记, Jane Eyre …) read as Lin Mei's
"New work", and Lin Mei's own serial 雨夜书店 has no confirmed author.

Make the seed tell the truth through the public APIs: classics are imported
with their source authors (Jane Austen, Lewis Carroll, 吴承恩, Charlotte Brontë)
and credited that way; the demo authors' own serials are created as
`own-work` with chapters inside them (titled chapters, a few with reading
progress); pen names author their own Works. Existing shared-stack data must
converge on rerun (idempotent), and the checks step reports any Work without a
credited author. The official-Zone steps belong to G-326; leave them.
