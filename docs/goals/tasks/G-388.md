## Outcome

G-375 made a chapter Work carry `partOf { work, occurrence }` on
`GET /v1/works/:id`, and the web opens a chapter Work's address in its
Book's reader. Reads that list Works still treat chapter Works as
standalone: search results, the header typeahead, a Realm's Works tab,
Zone new-adoptions and feed lists. Readers and moderators meet fragments
such as "雨夜书店 · 第一章 雨夜" as if they were books.

- **Search and typeahead.** A chapter's text still matches, but the hit
  resolves to its Book, with the matching chapter named in the hit ("in
  第一章 雨夜"). Decide it when the index is built, as discovery does, not
  per request. Keep the search budget: G-370 set context search at 35 of
  36 calls, and `search-budget.ts` enforces it.
- **Lists.** Realm Works, Zone new-adoptions and any list that currently
  shows chapter Works list their Book once instead. The feed is held by
  G-383; name what the feed still needs under PROPOSED TASKS.
- Tests cover a chapter hit resolving to its Book, typeahead, a Realm list
  with a Book and its chapters, and the budget.

G-377 has edited `search/result-cards.ts` and `routes/search.ts` on its
branch; leave those two files alone, or coordinate through the manager if
the change must reach them.

## Verify

Against the shared stack: search `雨夜` and a sentence from chapter 2 of
雨夜书店 signed out, and show both return the Book, with the chapter named
for the second. Fiction's Works list shows 雨夜书店 once. Use only the
backend; no web dev servers.
