## Outcome

The Accounts app (`apps/accounts`, G-204) was built while the Account service
APIs (G-205) were landing in parallel, so several account-centre features show
"coming soon" placeholders although the APIs now exist. Make the account
centre complete and excellent, in the spirit of Google Account. Read
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/manager/accounts-research.md`
(read-only; the design research) and `docs/plan/frontend.md#accounts-site`.

Wire and polish, against `services/account` as it is on `main` (read its
routes, `@rezics/account/app` types and tests):

- **Security & sign-in:** passkeys (enroll, list with provider/created/last
  used, rename, remove; protection of the last method), TOTP with backup codes,
  password change with step-up re-authentication, devices/sessions (this device
  pinned, sign out one/all others), recent security activity (90 days, "Wasn't
  you?" path), and a Home security-checkup card that only appears when action
  is needed.
- **Email flows:** verification, password reset and email change with
  verification, end to end through Mailpit (`127.0.0.1:8025`) in dev.
- **Connected apps:** OAuth grants and installations including trusted
  (skip-consent) first-party apps, plain-language permissions, last used,
  revoke.
- **Personal info:** interface language stored on the account (add the field
  if missing), name, handle display if Main offers it, avatar placeholder.
- **Sign-up enumeration:** sign-up with an existing email must not reveal the
  account (require email verification before sign-in or disable auto sign-in;
  the UI already treats `token: null` as "Check your email"). Enable
  `allowPublicClientPrelogin` so `/sign-in` names the requesting app.

You may change `services/account/src/auth.ts` (Better Auth options) and add a
small settings module; keep Account's tests passing and add tests for what you
change. Every page keeps loading, empty, error, signed-out and
re-authentication states; stories for new states; Playwright e2e for passkey
enrollment (virtual authenticator), TOTP, email verification and reset.
