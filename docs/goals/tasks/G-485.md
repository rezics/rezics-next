## Outcome

G-481 built the about site's art direction and pages. The maintainer's review:
the home page reads as if REZICS were only a reading library. Reposition it to
show the whole product and its thesis (GOAL.md decisions 34–38: one platform
where any kind of story and any language gets a complete home, cheaply), and
add the Motion library for richer, still-light interaction.

**Message** (the maintainer's, rewrite in your best English; do not criticise
other platforms' incomplete indexes, just show what REZICS does):

1. **"Don't read Japanese? It doesn't matter."** REZICS takes a work's details
   in its original language (titles, synopsis, credits, tags, editions and
   releases) and presents them in English, or in any language you read.
2. **"The community around your favourite platform falls short? Not here."**
   A complete experience: discussion that respects where you are in the story,
   reviews, lists, wikis, fair moderation, your identity carried everywhere.
3. **"Bring fans together, beyond any platform."** Works from every platform
   and every language meet in one place, with the people who love them.

**Breadth.** One home for every kind of story and the things around it: web
novels and serials, light novels, books, visual novels, anime and manga, games
and software, AI prompts and recipes, wikis and worlds, communities. Show them
as a living showcase (the same work named in several scripts, a Japanese
listing turning into English and 繁體中文 in place, a Realm where fans of one
series meet from different platforms), not a list. The library is one section
among many, not the page.

**Motion.** Adopt Motion (https://motion.dev, MIT) in React islands where CSS
scroll-driven animation and view transitions cannot do the job (gestures,
spring interactions, orchestrated sequences, layout animation); keep CSS for
what it already does well. Record the dependency in
`docs/development/toolchain.md` and `docs/development/about-site.md`, update
`apps/about/PATTERNS.md` so G-483 uses the same motion vocabulary, keep
`prefers-reduced-motion` static, and stay within the budgets G-481 met (LCP
under 1 s on a throttled phone, CLS 0, JavaScript only in islands).

Also: statuses stay honest from `src/features.ts`; English copy only (G-482
translates); invented works and names only, no copyrighted art.
