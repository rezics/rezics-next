## Outcome

The management surface (`/manage/r/{realm}/…`: queue, log, members, roles
with impact preview, settings) is planned in `docs/plan/frontend.md`. Main has
the moderation queue and audit reads, submission and moderation decisions and
generic Access roles/memberships, but nothing a Realm moderator can use
directly: no Realm-scoped member list with roles, no role editing expressed in
Realm terms, impact preview only for work-create groups, no escalation and no
Realm settings beyond the public profile.

Design and implement, for moderators and owners of one Realm, in the style of
Reddit's mod tools and Discourse's staff pages:

1. **Members.** Paginated, searchable members with their Realm roles and join
   date; add or invite, remove, ban and unban with reason and duration; each
   write with receipts and an audit entry.
2. **Roles.** Realm roles as named bundles of permissions over the existing
   Access model; create, edit, assign; an **impact preview** for any role or
   assignment change (who gains or loses which abilities, counts exact up to a
   bound) computed by the same authority that enforces it.
3. **Escalation** in the moderation queue: escalate an item to owners (or a
   parent authority where one exists) with a reason; the queue shows it.
4. **Settings.** Visibility, who may submit, review requirements, and rules
   (the rule-localization semantics in `services/main/src/modules/governance/rules.ts`),
   as one typed settings read and CAS write.

Authority decisions stay in Access; do not duplicate policy in the new module.
Extend the demo seed (`scripts/dev/seed/**`, unclaimed; keep changes small) so
one demo Realm has moderators, roles and a busy queue.
