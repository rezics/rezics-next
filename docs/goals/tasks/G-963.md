## Outcome

1. **Self-leave.** A member's own `POST /v1/access/membership-changes` with
   `action: leave` answers `403 membership_denied` (G-944's live journey). A
   person may always leave their own Realm membership: authorize the leave by
   the member's own controlled Agent, end the episode, remove only the
   join-sourced follow (G-938), and keep bans and history intact. Test denied
   cases (leaving for someone else) and repeated leaves.
2. **A Zone without a Realm.** Per [Space routers](../../contracts/space.md#surfaces-and-routers)
   a Zone needs no Realm, but the public creation API cannot create one. Let a
   person create a Space with only the Zone capability (name, optional handle,
   visibility and listing), with the same authority and idempotency as Space
   creation, and make it readable through the site router's reads.
   Migrations if needed: 1025–1027.
