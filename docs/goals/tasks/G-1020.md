## Outcome

In the final browser run's screenshots (main checkout,
`apps/web/.temp/direction-9/g-1000-1791028971131/…en-phone…/phone-sidebar-pinned.png`
and the desktop `joined.png`), long community names under Pinned and
Communities and sites run past the drawer's and sidebar's edge and are cut off
mid-letter instead of ending in an ellipsis inside the padding. Make every
navigation row truncate inside its padding (the row and its text need
`min-w-0` in the flex chain), keep the full name available as the accessible
name and a tooltip, and handle CJK and RTL names. A story with a long Latin, a
long CJK and a long Arabic name on desktop and phone, run under load as G-1015
did.
