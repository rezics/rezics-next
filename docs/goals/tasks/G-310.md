## Outcome

The Zone UI (G-296, merged) renders every module in the Zones decision
(`docs/plan/frontend.md`, "Zones"), but several have no public read yet and
fall back to empty states. Add, in the read-family style with disclosure and
cost bounds:

1. **Realm → Zone**: resolve a Realm's Zone and presentation, so community
   Zones apply at `/r/{uuid}` and an official Realm's UUID redirects to its
   segment.
2. **Module reads**: reader quotes (from public replies and reviews with a
   quotable excerpt), editor lists (Collections curated by the Realm), genre
   chips (Context concepts with names per language), a Realm discussion list,
   and people (active members and featured authors).
3. **Presentation details**: banner images as media delivery URLs with
   dimensions; per-locale module titles; one metric vocabulary shared by the
   presentation schema and the rankings reads (today presentation says
   views/shelved/rating and rankings say reads/finished-chapters); the module
   source names the web uses by convention (`new-adoptions`,
   `latest-chapters`, `recently-completed`, `recent-decisions`) made explicit.
4. **One Decision** by id, so a "Why here?" stamp opens a Decision beyond the
   log's first page.
5. Module Work cards carry `primaryCredits` with names (coordinate with G-309,
   which adds names to discovery and search; reuse its resolver).
