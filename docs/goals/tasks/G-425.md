## Outcome

G-409 shipped Concept value pages and Work-page chips grouped by Facet, but
the shared seed classifies no books or serials. On the shared stack, Pride and
Prejudice and 雨夜书店 show no chips, so readers never reach a Concept page.
Also, "Readers also enjoyed" on 雨夜书店 and Pride and Prejudice lists a
Minecraft mod (Lumen Lanterns), a JavaScript runtime guide (Bun) and a
glossary prompt next to novels.

- **Genres and tags in the seed.** Through the classification APIs the seed
  already uses (G-380's global context; `realms-step.ts` classifies mods):
  - Create a small genre ConceptScheme, with broader and narrower Concepts
    in en and zh-Hans labels: 玄幻/Fantasy, 仙侠/Xianxia, 都市/Urban,
    悬疑/Mystery, 言情/Romance, 科幻/Science fiction, 恐怖/Horror,
    Classics > Gothic, Satire, Coming of age…
  - Add free tags (后宫-free examples; keep them clean).
  - Classify every seeded Book and serial with 2–4 values, accepted in
    Global and in the relevant Realm, so Concept pages, chips, the Condition
    bar and Zone browse (G-404) all have real data.
  - Keep the seed replayable and inside its time budget, and update
    `tests/qa/unit/dev-seed-plan.test.ts` if steps change.
- **Also-enjoyed stays on topic.** Co-reader and fallback candidates respect
  the Work's type: books suggest books, mods suggest mods. The row keeps an
  honest title per basis. A different-type candidate may appear only in its
  own labelled row, if at all. Add a test.

The vocabulary follows `docs/contracts/queries.md`: Concept, Facet, type;
never "kind", "Sense" or "tag" as a model term.

## Verify

After `task dev:seed` on the shared stack, 雨夜书店 and Pride and Prejudice
show genre chips that open Concept pages listing matching Works, and their
also-enjoyed rows list only books. Use curl or a short Playwright script; no
web dev server is needed.
