## Outcome

The main site ships eight UI locales with per-key English fallback. Translate
every English message of these web features into **fr** and **es**: auth,
catalogue, discover, onboarding, profile, search, settings and work-page. Each
feature keeps English in `apps/web/features/<feature>/messages.ts` and one file
per locale in `messages/<locale>.ts` (a `Partial` of the English shape);
fill the existing locale files. Other features are still being changed by
other tasks; leave them.

- Readers here are people who read books and web novels, follow communities
  and write: translate meaning in the register the best reading sites use in
  that language (Goodreads, KadoKado 角角者, Kakuyomu カクヨム, Naver Series,
  Wattpad), not word for word. Keep `REZICS`, product names, URLs and
  code-like tokens unchanged. "Realm" and "Zone" are product nouns: keep
  them in English in every locale unless the existing zh-Hans files translate
  them, and then follow zh-Hans' choice for zh-Hant.
- zh-Hant follows Taiwan usage (作品, 書架, 登入, 帳戶); never convert zh-Hans
  character by character.
- Keep every `insert()` placeholder and `plural`/select structure exactly.
- Do not change English, code or other locales.

## Checks

- `task i18n:check`: zero missing keys, extra keys and placeholder mismatches for
  `web/fr` and `web/es` in those features
- `task web:typecheck` (errors elsewhere from other tasks may exist; none may come from your files)
- no dev server or browser
