## Outcome

In the Visual Novels Zone a reader picks language, platform, completeness and official or
fan translation and gets only VNs that have one release meeting all of them; each result
says which release matched ("English · Windows · complete · fan translation by …"), and a
translated title alone never counts. A VN's page in the Zone puts availability first. In the
Light Novels Zone a reader sees series, their volumes and, when signed in, the next volume
in their chosen language with the series progress panel. Both Zones link to each other,
show the same Works with the same library state, and say plainly what they do not cover.
VNDB attribution is visible where its data appears.

## Approach

- `features/release-filter/`: a reusable control that edits one `where` release group in the browse URL state and renders the match explanation from `matchedReleases`; usable by any Zone (Books reuse it for edition format and language).
- `zones/official/visual-novels/`: slots for the browse header (the release filter), result cards with the matched release line, and the detail slot ordering availability before About (embed `EditionsPreview`); strings in eight locales.
- `zones/official/light-novels/`: slots for series cards (volumes available in the reader's language), the next-volume shelf and `SeriesProgressPanel` on series detail; strings in eight locales.
- Approve both package digests in the official Zones seed step; stories for each slot, including empty, no-match and unknown-completeness states.

- Manager (G-893 merged): every Zone card and route index item carries `inZone`; link a card inside the Zone only when `inZone` is true, otherwise to the canonical `/e/` page. Never compute membership in the client.

- Manager: the Zone data exists (G-852: config/zones/light-novels.json, visual-novels.json and the seed). Seed scripts belong to G-905; list any seed change you need under OWNER CHANGES.

## Do not build

No client-side matching or filtering of releases (Main decides); no VN or LN route tree
outside the Zone host; no new Main read; no store links, prices or availability claims
beyond recorded releases; no ACGN, anime or episode views (deferred G-861).

## Acceptance

- Browser (`apps/web/tests/g-853*.e2e.ts`, 390×844 and 1440×900, en and zh-Hant): filtering "English + Windows + complete" shows the slice's expected VNs and hides the VN whose only English Windows release is a trial; a fan translation shows its translator; a signed-in reader who read SAO volume 1 sees volume 2 as next in the LN Zone and the same status on the VN Zone and `/w/{ref}`; keyboard-only use of the filter works; axe finds no violations.
- Class guard: a component test fails if a result card renders a release line that did not come from `matchedReleases`.
