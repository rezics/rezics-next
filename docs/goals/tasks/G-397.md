## Outcome

On Goodreads you follow an author and hear when they publish. REZICS has
author pages (`/authors/open-library/<OL…A>` and `/@handle`), but follows
cover only `realm | zone | work | agent`, with REZICS IRIs as targets
(`follows/contract.ts`). An external author such as Jane Austen can't be
followed, and G-382 left a slot for it. The manager's decision is a keyed
follow kind for external authors, not a curated REZICS Agent per author.

- **Main.** Add an `external-author` follow kind, keyed by provider and key
  (`open-library` and `OL21594A`), with the same authority, idempotency and
  privacy rules as the other kinds. Access migrations 810–814 are reserved.
  Following a native author (Agent) already works; make both appear the
  same to readers.
- **Feed.** When a Work crediting a followed author (native or external) is
  added, published or gets a new chapter, it reaches the follower's
  Following feed with the reason "Because you follow <author>". Follow the
  feed's cost rules; G-383 set its budgets and tests in `feed/**`, and they
  must stay green.
- **Author page.** Add a Follow / Following button and a follower count
  (public only, like the readers total) on external and native author
  pages, with sign-in for signed-out readers through the normal
  full-document sign-in.
- **Library.** Add an "Authors you follow" section, each with its newest
  Work.

Tests cover following and unfollowing both kinds, the feed reason, privacy of
the follower count, and the budgets. Add English and zh-Hans strings only.
