## Outcome

Building the Accounts operator panel (G-262) exposed two kit-level dialog bugs
that every app inherits; the panel worked around both locally
(`apps/accounts/features/admin/**`, see `initialFocusEl` and `useDismiss`):

1. **Initial focus falls to `<body>`.** `DialogBody` wraps content in
   ScrollArea, whose viewport has `tabIndex=0` only while it overflows. Ark's
   focus trap can choose it as the initial focus, it stops being focusable a
   moment later, and keyboard focus lands on the page behind the dialog. Make
   Dialog, AlertDialog, Sheet and Drawer start on `[data-autofocus]`, else the
   first form control, else the close button, and keep the viewport out of the
   initial-focus candidates.
2. **Dialogs close unasked.** A dialog opened from a menu item, or right after
   another dialog closes, can be dismissed when the earlier layer goes down.
   Close only on Escape, an outside click (never for alert dialogs) or the
   dialog's own controls, and never while a pending change is in flight
   (an opt-in `pending` prop or similar).

Add interaction stories that fail on the old behaviour (open from a menu,
chained dialogs, overflowing body, typing at a person's pace) and pass now. Then
remove the panel's local workarounds only if they are now redundant (out of
claim: list the one-line changes under OWNER CHANGES instead of editing).
