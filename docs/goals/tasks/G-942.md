## Outcome

1. **Sign-in after a policy change.** On the shared stack an existing member
   cannot sign in: `POST /api/auth/sign-in/email` answers 403
   `{"code":"policy_acceptance_required"}` and `sign-in-flow.tsx` shows "Sign-in
   is unavailable right now". The proxy (`features/proxy/account-proxy.ts`)
   redirects only navigations on authorize paths to the policy acceptance page;
   the sign-in form's fetch never gets there, so every policy update locks out
   every existing person. After this task the sign-in flow takes the person to
   the existing acceptance page (`accept-policies.tsx`), keeps the OAuth
   continuation, and finishes sign-in after acceptance; declining leaves them
   signed out with an explanation. Audit every Account refusal code the
   Accounts app can receive (`features/api/errors.ts`) and give each a specific
   message and next step; a generic "unavailable" is only for real outages.
   Reproduce with the member in `.temp/stack/rezics-dev/web-auth/private.json`
   against the shared stack, and cover it with a story and a browser test.
2. **Styled controls.** `sign-in-flow.tsx`, `admin/staff/role-dialog.tsx` and
   `admin/users/directory-table.tsx` use native `<select>`, radio or checkbox.
   Replace them with `@rezics/ui` controls and extend `no-native-select` and
   `no-native-inputs` (with their rule tests) to `apps/accounts`.

## Do not build

No policy model or Account service changes; no new pages beyond what the
acceptance continuation needs.
