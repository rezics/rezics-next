Phase-0 hotspot extraction with **no behavior change**. `services/main/src/app.ts`
(5,487 lines, 148 routes inside `createMainApp`) and `scripts/qa/coverage.ts` were
the paused program's merge hotspots; later bundles will each claim one domain file.

1. Move route registrations into per-domain Elysia plugin modules under
   `services/main/src/routes/` (for example works, content, publication, sources,
   packages, access, me, ratings, search, spaces, addresses, classification).
   Move shared HTTP helpers (`problem`, `commandError`, search-selection guards,
   dependency types) into `routes/shared.ts` or similar. `createMainApp` composes
   plugins with `.use()` and stays the only exported factory; keep `MainApp` and
   Eden inference intact and preserve registration order where matching depends
   on it. Aim for route files under about 800 lines; `app.ts` becomes a small
   composition root.
2. Split `completeCases` in `scripts/qa/coverage.ts` into per-prefix files under
   `scripts/qa/coverage/` (one per acceptance page or ID prefix), merged by
   `coverage.ts`. Keep its exports, validation and behavior identical.
3. Add a short "where new routes and coverage declarations go" note to
   `services/main/README.md`.

Checks (all must pass, record exact commands in the handoff):
- `corepack yarn gen:check` shows `generated/openapi/main/public.json` and every
  other generated artifact unchanged (byte-identical OpenAPI proves no route drift).
- `bun node_modules/typescript/bin/tsc --project services/main/tsconfig.json`,
  biome and oxlint on the changed directories.
- `bun scripts/goal/goalctl.ts test tests/qa/unit/coverage.test.ts tests/qa/unit/rating-coverage.test.ts`
- One real integration run: `bun scripts/goal/goalctl.ts test tests/qa/integration/authenticated-api-journey.test.ts`.

Risks: circular imports between route modules and `app.ts`; lost `.onError` or
derive/decorate scope when moving into plugins (check Elysia 2 plugin scoping);
changed OpenAPI operation order. Estimate 60–90 minutes. Commit in coherent steps.
