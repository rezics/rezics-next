## Outcome

G-1063 (merged at `189cce0d1`) moved Discover reads onto
`access.discovery_entries($1)`, `discovery_terms($1)` and
`discovery_concepts($1)` over versioned rows (migrations 1043–1044). The
manager's full integration pass fails `tests/qa/integration/concept-page.test.ts`
alone and in its shard: the G-409 plan guard at 20,000 Works no longer finds a
per-Work membership probe. The plan dump (first page, one drive term, one group,
one exclusion) shows the exclusion as

`Bitmap Index Scan discovery_entry_version` → 37,066 rows →
`Bitmap Heap Scan` → 6,666 rows

instead of at most one indexed probe per drive row. Generation, storage
version and retirement are resolved through repeated `coalesce((SELECT …))`
sub-selects, so the planner cannot seek
`(generation_id, work, work_type, term)` per candidate and reads the whole
generation's version range instead. Cost again grows with the population.

Restore bounded seeks for every Discover read that G-1063 rerouted (condition
pages and continuations, payloads, term and Concept counts, sections, cards,
refresh deltas), with retained history present (several versions and retired
rows per Work). Resolve the storage generation and version once per statement
(parameters or a lateral row), and give live and versioned rows the indexes
those probes need. The concept-page assertions stay as they are, except that
the index names may change: no Seq Scan, Sort or Hash; at most two probes per
drive row; rows within the window. Add plan guards for the other rerouted
reads at 20,000 Works with history. Keep G-1063's refresh cost results (4/46
for irrelevant events, 8/126 for a rating delta, 10k rebuild ~135 s) and
re-measure them.
