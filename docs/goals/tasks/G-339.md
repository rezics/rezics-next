## Outcome

G-337 (merged) added what the Manage workspace and notifications were missing;
wire it into the web app (G-330 left typed seams and honest empty states):

- **Members** lists the whole team: members and role holders who have not
  joined (marked), plus outgoing invitations with state and expiry, cancel
  where the API allows.
- **Log** entries say what changed ("Gave Lin Mei Community moderators until
  Oct 28"), from the new audit detail.
- **Waiting counts** in the nav, rail and landing use the corrected counts.
- **Notifications** render `realm_invitation` (accept or decline in place,
  unread badge), role changes as given or taken ("You are now a moderator of
  Fiction · 小说"), and review notifications linking to
  `/w/{work}#review-{reviewId}`.
- **Rights complaints** can be decided with the interim and final restriction
  outcomes.

Follow the existing Manage patterns and per-locale message files (English and
zh-Hans; other locales fall back). Verify with Playwright against the shared
stack (web :3000) as the Fiction steward (credentials in
`scripts/dev/seed/plan.ts`); run no dev server of your own unless you need
unmerged code, and never Storybook at the same time.
