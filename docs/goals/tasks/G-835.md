## Outcome

Main answers, for one reader and one composed Work, what is finished, what is
next and why, without double counting (the drafts' `series-tracking` as a
generic Trackable read):

- A versioned policy over the exact composition revision: required parts,
  optional extras, the reader's chosen realization language, and G-833's release
  coverage. Each covered part counts once: a session on an omnibus covering
  volumes 1–3 and one on volume 1 complete three parts, not four.
- `GET /v1/me/progress-summaries/{resource}` returns four separate states:
  caught up with available material (no later part has a realization in the
  chosen language), finished the published parts, series concluded (from G-830's
  composition status), and correspondence unresolved (a session's coverage of a
  part is unknown or partial). It also returns the next part with its reason and
  the exact composition, session and release revisions used.
- A private edition preference per Work (realization or release, and language)
  with expected-version writes: `PUT /v1/me/edition-preferences/{work}`. It
  selects what "next" and the Work page's primary action use.
- The summary also names the reader's furthest completed part and, inside it,
  the furthest occurrence or locator, which G-847 uses as the default spoiler
  boundary.
- Progress never transfers between Works: a correspondence from G-831 only lets
  the client offer an explicit extra session write.

## Approach

- Compute on read from indexed owners with a declared bound (at most 256 parts
  per composition page, one batched session read, one batched coverage read);
  report continuation when a composition is larger.
- The policy version is a constant in code with a test fixture per state; a later
  policy is a new version, not an option flag.

## Do not build

- No stored roll-up table, recommendation, availability, alerts or follows.
- No percentage averaging across translations and no sum of container and
  children.
- No series ratings roll-up (separate judgments stay separate).
- No UI (G-838).

## Acceptance

- API: Index Original in zh-Hant with volumes 1–20 translated and read reports
  caught up, not finished; sessions on the Index omnibus (1–22, SS1, SS2) and on
  volume 1 complete 24 parts, not 25; New Testament "22 Reverse" is its own part; web Spider finished leaves
  book Spider unstarted and its correspondence visible; a stale preference write
  gets 409; a private part is withheld.
- Class guard: reference-result fixtures fail on double-counted omnibus coverage,
  cross-translation percentage inference and ownership counted as completion.
