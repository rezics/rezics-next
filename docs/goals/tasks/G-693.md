## Outcome

A holder's agent submits the next chapter's extraction as a delta against the
wiki's accepted records. It adds claims, amends them with evidence, and
explicitly retracts them with a reason; nothing is deleted by omission. The
delta is a `wiki-bundle` revision on G-846's adapter, so G-865's review,
G-866's notifications and G-865's revert apply unchanged.

A wiki page or Zone section read at an earlier revision set, and its export,
still render completely and exactly after later dependency changes: an entity
renamed, an entity merged (M6 G-836), a claim retracted. The one exception is
material removed for rights, which is withheld from current reads, history and
export alike, with a visible marker.

## Approach

- Extend the protocol validation in `modules/wiki/delta*.ts`: a delta names the accepted set it is based on. Retractions and amendments name the accepted claim and carry evidence. A claim absent from the delta stays accepted. An outdated base yields `stale_base`.
- In `wiki-adapter.ts`, apply and compensate the delta items through G-846's owner commands. Retraction appends an ending occurrence; it never deletes.
- History and export (`modules/wiki/history*.ts`, `modules/export/wiki*.ts`): a revision set pins claim revisions and entity IRIs. Reads resolve renames and G-836 resolutions at read time and never rewrite the pin. Rights restrictions are checked on every read path. Use Access 887 only if the pin cannot live in existing receipts.

- Manager: G-846 merged the wiki-bundle adapter on the review lifecycle's ordered multi-command applications (per-command owner authority, revelation positions written before visibility, fail closed); G-836 merged merge/unmerge (rv:mergedInto, redirect at read). Chapter deltas are another adapter on that kernel, not a parallel one. G-907's skipped delta half in tests/qa/integration/g-848-wiki-toolkit.test.ts is yours to turn on. Keep it minimal (Sol: no new lifecycle states or tables beyond what a delta needs).

## Do not build

Automated chapter detection, provider inference, a wiki workbench, prose or
BlockNote articles, family trees, maps or chronologies, a second export format,
or a separate wiki review queue.

## Acceptance

- API (`tests/qa/integration/g-693*.ts`), on G-846's fixture: chapters 1–3 are applied, then a chapter 4 delta omits one earlier claim (it stays) and retracts another explicitly (it needs review, and a revert restores it). A character is renamed and another merged. The chapters 1–3 revision set renders the same content and exports a complete manifest, and a rights-restricted quote disappears from current reads, history and export.
- Class guard: a test fails if any path removes an accepted claim without a reviewed retraction, or if a pinned historical read changes after a dependency change other than a rights restriction.
