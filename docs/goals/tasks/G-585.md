## Outcome
Searching 魔法禁书目录 finds 魔法禁書目錄, and the reverse. Searching a title in
hiragana finds its katakana form, and full-width and half-width forms match. The
matched original literal and its language are what the reader sees. Status:
`defer`, first to promote for Taiwan and zh-Hans readers. It is a
profile-versioned analyzer change with an index rebuild.

## What to read
- `infra/jena/fuseki-text.ttl` (`cjk-bigram-v1`, the bundled Lucene `CJKAnalyzer`).
- `.temp/research/R18-search.md` §1–3.
- The Lucene ICU module version that matches Jena's bundled Lucene.

## Approach
- Add a `cjk-bigram-v2` analyzer: ICU normalization and width folding, a Traditional-to-Simplified transform applied to both index and query, and hiragana-to-katakana folding, then CJK bigrams.
- Version the profile and rebuild the index. Record the jar in the toolchain.

## Do not build
No new search service, no romaji, no morphological analyzers, and no per-language fields.

## Acceptance
- Positive pair fixtures, plus negative fixtures that keep distinct titles apart.
- Rebuild readiness through G-556's generation qualification.
