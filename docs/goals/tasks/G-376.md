## Outcome

Studio's core loop is "open my Work, edit a chapter, add the next one,
publish". The second milestone review (`.temp/manager/milestone-review-2.md`,
findings 2 and 13; screenshots `st-d-work.png`, `au-d-studio.png`,
`rd-d-studio.png` in `.temp/manager/milestone-2/`) found it broken for the
flagship serial:

- **P0.** Lin Mei opens her published serial 雨夜书店 and every chapter row
  reads "A chapter this identity can't open · Draft", while readers see three
  published chapters. Find out why from the real data: the seed
  (`scripts/dev/seed/chapters-step.ts`, `home-v2.ts`, `progress.ts`) writes
  the chapters and the composition as Lin Mei's personal Agent, her pen name
  月下书生 is also credited, and Studio reads `GET /v1/works/:id/contents`
  with the switcher's `actingSubject` and a language from
  `writingLanguageOf`. Fix the cause where it lies (Main or web). When a
  chapter truly belongs to another of the person's Agents, the row says
  "Written as <Agent> · Switch" and switching opens it; a dead row is never
  the answer. Show each chapter's real state (Published, Draft, Scheduled).
- **P0.** The header says "English" for a Simplified Chinese Work. Take the
  language from the Work's Main Version.
- **P1.** The Work page shows a skeleton for 2+ s. Load the header and chapter
  list together and stream the rest.
- **P2.** Guides, prompts, recipes and mod checklists are all labelled
  "Story" in Studio. Label by the Work's type (Guide, Prompt, Skill, Recipe,
  Mod, Book), with the same words the catalogue uses.

From Lin Mei's Studio, open chapter 1, edit a sentence, save, publish the
change, and add and publish a chapter 4; then undo the demo change
(unpublish or remove chapter 4) so the shared stack returns to three
chapters. The seed files belong to G-375 (running): if the cause is in the
seed, describe the fix under PROPOSED TASKS rather than editing them.
