## Outcome

The maintainer's review: nobody labels a principle such as "open source" as
"available", and labelling everything with "in development", "up next" or
"later" is noise. The about site is published at launch, when everything in
this Goal's plan (M4–M8) works. So:

- Features delivered by launch (every M4–M8 entry) carry **no label**.
- Only features that truly come **after launch** carry one short label
  ("Later", translated in all eight locales).
- Principles and policies (open source, no advertising trackers, no training on
  private drafts, and similar) never carry a label.

Change `apps/about/src/features.ts` so the registry expresses exactly this
(for example `launch` versus `later`, keeping each entry's milestone only where
the roadmap page still needs it), make every component render a label only for
`later`, keep the roadmap page's stages as the place that shows progress, and
update the tests. Decide which entries are `later` from the owner documents that
`GOAL.md`'s decision index links (for example decision 30 defers the Stripe
Connect marketplace; decision 24 defers taste memory and the reading companion;
decision 29 defers family trees, adaptation alignment, world maps and
chronologies); list every `later` entry and its source in the handoff. Update
`docs/development/about-site.md` to record the rule and its reason.
