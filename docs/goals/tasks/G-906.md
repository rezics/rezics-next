## Outcome

G-821 (staff triage UI) found that G-565's staff reads omit data staff need
to decide (its handoff, proposed tasks 1–4 and 7). After this task:
`GET /v1/safety-cases/{id}` returns the case's process steps with their IDs —
correspondence, appeals, counter-notices and DMCA restoration steps with
`restoration_not_after` — and the recorded decision's statement of reasons;
the staff queue returns a redacted row (category, urgency, due time, a
restricted marker, no evidence) for urgent cases a non-specialist may not
open, instead of omitting them; and the queue orders urgent first on the
server with a cursor that keeps that order across pages. Reversing an NCII
appeal from Manage works because the appeal step's ID is readable.

## Approach

Extend the existing reads and their response schemas (`readSafetyCase`, the
queue read); no new routes. Restricted evidence stays visible only to
specialists exactly as today. Run `task gen` and `task web:typecheck` and fix
web fixtures the contract change breaks (worker protocol).

## Do not build

No platform decision log route, no preference storage, no UI.

## Acceptance

Integration: staff read an NCII appeal step and reverse it with its ID; a
non-specialist sees the redacted urgent row and cannot open evidence;
urgent-first order holds across two pages; the decision read returns its
statement of reasons.
