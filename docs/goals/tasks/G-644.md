## Outcome

**Non-Work resources.** A resource with no dedicated host renders at `/{locale}/e/{ref}` from `entity-page-v1`
(G-629). This covers a release, a chapter occurrence, a character, and a resource of an unregistered type. The
page shows:

- the header: name, avatar, and type label from the registry;
- the statements and relations inventories, with cursor continuation;
- the capability sections its base binds.

**Work pages.** `/w/[ref]` takes its type-specific sections (recipe, prompt, skill) from the projection's section
list. `workPageKind` (`work-page/types/kind.ts`) is deleted, so an unknown type no longer renders as a book.

**Widgets.** The ratings, reviews and discussion components take a resolved target and call G-650's
`/v1/resources/:id/...` reads. A "Discuss this chapter" or "Discuss this release" action therefore works on any
page.

**For Zones.** The components are exported as `EntityPage({ resource, locale, hrefFor, frame })` and its sections.
Zones (G-655–G-659, `/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/manager/briefs/_design-zones.md`) mount them on detail routes with `frame: false`, and use `hrefFor` to map
links to Zone routes.

## Approach

1. **Generic page.** `features/entity-page/` renders `entity-page-v1`. Each section is a small component that
   fetches its own paginated href. Unsupported sections are omitted, never faked.
2. **Route.** One generic host route `app/[locale]/e/[ref]` follows the durable-address rules. It 404s the same
   way for missing and hidden resources, and keeps the Work's `/w` host.
3. **Work page.** Replace the `TypeExperience`/`ReadSlot` dispatch in `work-views.tsx` with the projection's type
   sections. The recipe and prompt components stay as the renderers for those sections.
4. **Widgets.** Parameterize the widgets by target, and label what was rated or reviewed ("this release", "this
   chapter") from the registry.

Also remove the work-page type-label message keys that G-651 left unused.

## Do not build

- The Work hub page's section redesign; the Wiki+ owner builds that on this brief.
- Editors or infoboxes.
- Per-type page trees.
- Business logic in the browser.
- Relation sentence rendering (the lexicon owner's).

## Acceptance

**Browser, phone and desktop, in en, zh-Hant and an RTL content language**, against the seeded SAO subset:

- Open a chapter occurrence of `sao.web`, start a discussion, reload and return.
- Open a release and a character.
- Open a resource of an unregistered type and see the generic page with no book controls.
- Recipe and prompt Work pages still render their sections.

**API.** Every section fetch uses the projection's href.

**Anonymous character pages** also wait on the authority area's semantic-resource disclosure brief; the manager
adds its ID to `depends`. Until then, anonymous readers see an honest "unavailable", and granted readers see the
page.

**Class guard.** A story matrix (base × presentation) fails when an unknown type renders book controls.
