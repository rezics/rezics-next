## Outcome

Sign-up takes a birth month and the request country. G-736 makes the Accounts
Worker forward Cloudflare's `CF-IPCountry`, and Account trusts that header only
on the existing first-party proxy path; an absent country uses the strictest
rule. It
refuses people under the market minimum (13; 14 in South Korea; 16 in the EEA)
with a typed reason. It records acceptance of the exact Terms and Privacy
versions and asks again when a version changes materially. Optional mail carries a signed one-click
unsubscribe (RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`). Hard
bounces, complaints and unsubscribes suppress optional mail. An authenticated
bounce and complaint intake and an uncertain-delivery report exist for
operators. Security and recovery mail stays unaffected by unsubscribes.

## Approach

- `market-policy.ts`: a versioned, typed constant (country or region → minimum age, adult availability), with EEA membership listed explicitly. It is code, not a database table.
- 160: birth month and policy acceptance (policy id, version digest, time). 161: suppression by address and purpose, with reason and source. `policy-versions.ts` lists each policy id, effective date and the SHA-256 of its `docs/legal/*.md` source. G-736's build check keeps it equal to the published text; do not copy texts into Account.
- The unsubscribe token is an HMAC derived from `ACCOUNT_SECRET` with domain separation, binding user, purpose and issue date. GET shows confirmation and POST applies it. The digest intake refuses suppressed addresses.
- Bounce and complaint intake: `POST /api/internal/mail-events` with a shared-secret HMAC (new `ACCOUNT_MAIL_EVENTS_SECRET`, optional; without it the route is off). The payload is provider-neutral.
- `task account:mail-report` lists uncertain and suppressed counts, and never resends automatically. Write `docs/operations/email.md` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, TLS, provider wiring, suppression review).

- Manager (2026-10-01): the first launch withholds assessed r15/r18/r18g
  targets from every reader and offers no adult opt-in
  (`docs/operations/trust-and-safety.md`, G-897), so Account sends Main no
  age band and no adult flag; do not build that introspection. The birth
  month serves only the market minimum-age refusal and is not age assurance.

## Do not build

Age verification or ID checks, a consent-management platform, marketing mail,
a provider SDK, per-topic unsubscribe in email (Main owns topic preferences),
the launch-list mail of the about site (deferred G-751), or UI (G-736).

## Acceptance

- API tests: under-age sign-up in KR, the EEA and the US at each boundary month is refused with its reason; acceptance is stored with its version; a new version demands re-acceptance; the age band is `15-17` at 17 and the adult flag is false for KR and UK adults; a person without a birth month is `unknown`. A signed unsubscribe (tampered, expired and replayed variants) stops digests but not password-reset mail. A bounce event suppresses optional mail, and a forged event is refused.
- Class guard: a table-driven test over `market-policy.ts` fails when an EEA country is missing or when any market's minimum drops below the documented one.
