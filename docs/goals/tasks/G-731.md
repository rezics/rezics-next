## Outcome

The implemented sign-up requires a minimum-age declaration and policy acceptance,
without a birthday. Account trusts Cloudflare country only through its configured
ingress; an unknown country uses the strictest registration minimum. Birthday
collection belongs to Account settings and features that need age. A known age
below the market minimum holds the account and revokes its sessions. It records acceptance of the exact Terms and Privacy
versions and asks again when a version changes materially. Optional mail carries a signed one-click
unsubscribe (RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`). Hard
bounces, complaints and unsubscribes suppress optional mail. An authenticated
bounce and complaint intake and an uncertain-delivery report exist for
operators. Security and recovery mail stays unaffected by unsubscribes.

## Approach

- `market-policy.ts`: a versioned, typed constant (country or region → minimum age, adult availability), with EEA membership listed explicitly. It is code, not a database table.
- 160: registration policy version and policy acceptance (policy id, version digest, time). 161: suppression by address and purpose, with reason and source. `policy-versions.ts` lists each policy id, effective date and the SHA-256 of its `docs/legal/*.md` source. G-736's build check keeps it equal to the published text; do not copy texts into Account.
- The unsubscribe token is an HMAC derived from `ACCOUNT_SECRET` with domain separation, binding user, purpose and issue date. GET shows confirmation and POST applies it. The digest intake refuses suppressed addresses.
- Bounce and complaint intake: `POST /api/internal/mail-events` with a shared-secret HMAC (new `ACCOUNT_MAIL_EVENTS_SECRET`, optional; without it the route is off). The payload is provider-neutral.
- `task account:mail-report` lists uncertain and suppressed counts, and never resends automatically. Write `docs/operations/email.md` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, TLS, provider wiring, suppression review).

- Maintainer revision (2026-10-01): replace blanket restricted-content closure
  with four independent General/R15/R18/R18G settings and contextual birth-date
  collection. Store the complete date privately; expose current qualification
  and preferences to Main without the raw birthday. Public birthday visibility
  is a separate explicit choice. Migration 164 stores current preferences and
  complete dates; live introspection, takeout and recovery include their
  respective current projections.

## Do not build

Age verification or ID checks, a consent-management platform, marketing mail,
a provider SDK, per-topic unsubscribe in email (Main owns topic preferences),
the launch-list mail of the about site (deferred G-751), or UI (G-736).

## Acceptance

- API tests: birthday-free registration requires an age declaration and refuses unavailable markets; known ages below the region minimum revoke admission; acceptance is stored with its version; a new version demands re-acceptance; the age band is `15-17` at 17 and the adult flag is false for KR and UK adults; a person without a birthday is `unknown`. A signed unsubscribe (tampered, expired and replayed variants) stops digests but not password-reset mail. A bounce event suppresses optional mail, and a forged event is refused.
- Class guard: a table-driven test over `market-policy.ts` fails when an EEA country is missing or when any market's minimum drops below the documented one.
