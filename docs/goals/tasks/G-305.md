## Outcome

Studio (G-289, merged) works for everything Main lets an author do and its
handoff lists what Main lacks. This task covers the reads and commands; the
permission baselines are a separate Access task after G-298.

1. **An Agent's works**: `GET /v1/me/agents/{agent}/works?state=&type=&limit&cursor`
   returning, per Work, id, Main Version, title, cover, types, disclosure,
   texts (contribution, language, draft head, publication and its head),
   Realm submissions with state, created and updated — including Works with no
   text yet. Only the Agent's controllers may read it. It replaces Studio's
   bounded scan.
2. **Draft heads**: `GET /v1/contributions/:c?actingSubject=` returning work,
   language, author, draft head and publication head; every `409 stale_head`
   from contribution edits and content draft saves carries the current head.
   Draft saves also return `byteDigest`.
3. **Create in the right language**: `POST /v1/works` accepts `language`
   (today the title language is hard-coded to `en` in `modules/work/activate.ts`)
   and optional localized title and description.
4. **Chapters inside a Work**: an atomic
   `POST /v1/works/:id/chapters` (`book-chapter-create-v1`: title, language,
   direction, parent, position, expected composition head) returning the
   chapter Work, occurrence, structure, composition revision and variant; and
   `GET /v1/works/:id/content-variants` with draft, publication and
   eligibility heads. Permission to call it comes from the Access task; test
   it with a fixture grant here.

Follow the read-family and command patterns; new events need relay readers.
