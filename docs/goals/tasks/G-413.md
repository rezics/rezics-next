## Outcome

`/settings` edits only the profile. Readers expect Reddit's and Goodreads'
settings, sections that each save and explain themselves:

- **Notifications.** Per type (replies, mentions, votes, new chapters from
  followed Works and authors, Realm decisions, role changes): on or off,
  in-app or email digest. The notification producers must respect these
  settings. Add the preferences in Main if missing, with tests.
- **Privacy.**
  - library and shelf visibility (G-294's setting; make it prominent);
  - profile visibility and who can follow;
  - hide reading activity from Home;
  - blocked people, whose posts and replies stop showing for you.
- **Reading.** Content languages to show, reader typography defaults (the
  reader's settings module exists: `reading-settings`), spoiler handling,
  and adult-content visibility.
- **Display.** Theme, card or compact feed default (G-401 keeps a cookie;
  make it a setting), interface language.
- **Account.** A clear link to Accounts (sign-in, security, data export,
  deletion), since those live there.

Keep each preference in Main when it must follow the person across devices;
use a cookie only for display-only choices. Access migrations 830–834 are
reserved. Use `@rezics/ui` controls, never a native select. Add English and
zh-Hans strings only. Look at how Reddit's and Goodreads' settings are
organized (BrowserOS in your own tab, or their public help pages) and don't
sign in to them.
