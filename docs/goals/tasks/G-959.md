## Outcome

The manager's unified verification needs one Playwright suite that walks what
standing direction 9 changed, on desktop (1280×860) and phone (390×844), in
English and one CJK locale, against the shared stack with a real sign-in
(`signInAtAccounts`, the member in `.temp/stack/rezics-dev/web-auth/private.json`
of the main checkout). Create every record it needs through the public API in
`beforeAll` (Spaces with and without handles, a Realm with a Zone, a Zone
without a Realm, a private Realm with request admission, an unlisted Space, a
Work with more than 20 rating populations, Concepts, a story with many
chapters), so it runs on any fresh dataset. Journeys:

1. Addresses: name and identity forms for a person, a Space (community and
   site), a Work and a Concept answer 200 at the canonical URL and one 301 from
   every other form; old `/r/{zone}` links reach `/z/`.
2. Account menu: second-level Language and Appearance with current values.
3. Sidebar and relationships: follow, set the bell level, pin, join (one
   command), leave, unfollow from the Following manager; the sidebar updates
   and never shows a Space twice.
4. Discover: type tabs, topic chips through the picker, a Communities tab with
   continuation; the rating scope picker reaches a population past the first
   page; the Realm-to-submit chooser finds a community by search; the wiki
   position chooser finds a chapter by search.
5. Visibility: an outsider sees the private join page, requests, the manager
   approves from the inbox, the requester sees the result after reload; an
   unlisted Space opens by link with `noindex` and stays out of Discover.

Each step screenshots to `.temp/direction-9/` and asserts with accessible
roles and names. Where a feature is still missing on the branch you test,
mark that step `test.fixme` with the missing piece named, never weaken it.
