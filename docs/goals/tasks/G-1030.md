## Outcome

The manager's browser run on main (`.temp/manager/direction-9-final2.log`,
main checkout, read-only: 70 of 72 passed) failed two zh-Hant desktop
journeys, both at interactions right after a page loaded:

1. **Accounts sign-in.** The suite filled the email field and pressed
   "下一步"; the page then showed the email field empty and invalid
   (`.temp/playwright/results/direction-9.e2e.ts-Directi-351fc-…/error-context.md`).
   Text typed before hydration was replaced by the controlled input's empty
   state, so a person on a slow connection loses what they typed.
2. **Discover type tabs.** A click on the Works tab right after `goto` left the
   URL without `?tab=works` for 30 s: the click was dropped
   (`direction-9.e2e.ts:568`).

Fix the class by progressive enhancement: forms work as plain HTML forms and
keep values typed before hydration (read the DOM value on hydrate or stay
uncontrolled until then), and links stay real links whose `href` carries the
full destination, so a click before hydration navigates instead of vanishing.
Audit the shared controls in `packages/ui` and the Accounts forms (sign-in,
sign-up, consent, recovery) for the same pattern and fix them. For item 2,
`apps/web/features/discover/**` belongs to G-1027 until it lands: fix the
shared link or tab primitive if that is the cause, otherwise report the exact
change under OWNER CHANGES. Tests that type and click before hydration (slow
the hydration script in the test) for both apps.
