## Outcome

In the second milestone review a new person picked "Books & web novels", "AI
skills & prompts" and 简体中文 in onboarding, and step 3 offered Books,
**Software** and AI Workshop: no Fiction, and Software though "Mods &
software" was not chosen. The home's right rail meanwhile suggested Fiction
(`.temp/manager/milestone-2/new-09-step3.png`). The first follows shape the
home, so a Chinese web-novel reader must not miss the flagship Zone.

Make `GET /v1/onboarding/suggested-follows` follow the choices:

- each chosen interest contributes its Zones and Realms, and nothing is
  suggested for an interest that was not chosen, except a clearly labelled
  "popular" fallback when the choices yield fewer than three;
- "Books & web novels" includes Fiction, and reading languages rank Realms
  whose Works are in those languages first (a zh-Hans reader sees Fiction
  before Books);
- each suggestion keeps a reason the UI can show ("Because you chose Books &
  web novels", "Popular in 简体中文").

Base the mapping on the Realms' data (types of their Works, their languages
and their official interest), not on Realm slugs, so a community Realm can be
suggested the same way. Tests cover each interest alone, combinations,
languages and the fallback. The web already renders the suggestions; do not
change web code.

## Verify

Against the shared stack, call the route signed out and as a new person
(credentials in `scripts/dev/seed/plan.ts`) for the review's choices and three
other combinations, and show the results in the handoff. Use only the
backend; no web dev servers.
