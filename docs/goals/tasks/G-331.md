## Outcome

Fix what the milestone review found in the catalogue surfaces
(`.temp/manager/milestone-review.md` in the main checkout, findings 1, 4, 12,
16, 18, 19, 20, 21, 22, 29; screenshots in `.temp/manager/milestone/`), using
what is now on `main`:

- **Search** (G-322): titles, taglines and credited names are searchable with
  title matches first; add the title typeahead to the search box (CJK-aware,
  IME-safe), hide facets with zero counts, and when nothing matches offer
  spelling-tolerant suggestions or popular Works rather than a wall of zeros.
- **Work page** (Goodreads is the bar): authors from G-327's confirmed credits
  and G-322's source author names, with a graceful state instead of "No
  confirmed credits yet"; synopsis, chapter count and last update (G-291); Read
  opens chapter 1 (or the next unread) on a finished book; model vocabulary
  (contribution and revision IDs, sequences, IRIs, "As of change N") moves
  behind Details/Cite in plain words; the zh-Hans "no Simplified Chinese
  title" note appears only when the title really is in another language.
- **Reviews** (G-315): a reviews section on the Work page — sort helpful/new,
  language and rating filters, spoiler gate, helpful votes, write and edit
  your own review with your rating — and the `#review-<id>` anchor the feed
  links to.
- **Reader**: chapter titles (G-327), CJK body line height 1.8 for `zh` and
  `ja`, and the site chrome hides while reading on phones.
- **Discover**: never four identical "being prepared" boxes — fall back to
  recently added and top-rated rows, hide shelves that cannot fill.
- **Covers**: one `WorkCover` style across Discover, search, Work pages and
  feeds (the Zone renderer is G-326's).

Compare with Goodreads and with the review's screenshots before and after.
Use the shared stack for data; run only your own web dev server, one at a time.
