## Outcome

G-1014 made each structure write cost O(1) and cut the large G954 case's API
setup from 122 s to 47 s, but the case ("API-built 10503-occurrence story
traverses the old ceiling, seeks distant multilingual chapters and resolves
saved positions") still takes 257 s, so about 210 s is spent reading. Long web
novels with thousands of chapters are ordinary on REZICS, and
[collections at any size](../../plan/frontend.md#collections-at-any-size)
plus [perf by algorithm](../../storage/workload-budgets.md) ask that a page,
seek or saved-position resolve cost in proportion to the page, not the story.

Measure per-request latency and store work for chooser pages, distant seeks
(chapter number and CJK title search) and saved-position resolution at 100,
1,000 and 10,000 chapters on one stack; find any read whose work grows with the
story (full traversal, per-request ordinal recomputation, unindexed search, a
cursor that rescans from the start) and fix it. Report numbers before and
after and the test's new duration; the test keeps every assertion.
