## Outcome

G-965 (commit `a73e7b56` in this worktree) delivers `new-work` notifications
for followed Concepts and saved views at levels All and Highlights. The inbox
renders them as unavailable and settings have no digest choice for them.

1. Main's notification display resolves a `new-work` notice to its Work and the
   matched topic or saved view (current disclosure checked), and the inbox shows
   "New in {topic}: {Work}" linking canonically through the address module.
2. Settings let the reader choose inbox, push or email digest for these notices
   as for other topics, with labels in all eight locales.
3. Unit tests for the display mapping; stories for the inbox item and the
   setting.
