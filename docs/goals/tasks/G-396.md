## Outcome

G-378 completed the catalogues, but readers of zh-Hant, ja, ko, de, fr and es
still meet English in two places, and today's merges added new English and
zh-Hans strings.

- **Navigation.** `apps/web/features/shell/navigation.ts` hardcodes Home,
  Discover, Create and Library with `localeText({ en, 'zh-Hans' })`. Give
  every item all eight locales. G-353 noted "Notifications" truncating in the
  phone tab bar at 390 px (German "Benachrichtigungen" too). Use short
  tab-bar labels where a locale's word is long, keeping the full word for
  screen readers.
- **Zone module headings.** `realm/realm-page.tsx` uses
  `titles?.[locale] ?? module.title`, so any locale without a title in the
  Zone's presentation shows the English module title. Fall back to a
  localized default per module kind from the zones messages. Also give the
  official Zones' seeded presentations
  (`scripts/dev/seed/official-plan.ts`) titles in all eight locales, so a
  reseed shows them.
- **New strings.** `task i18n:check` reports missing keys in all six
  locales: author 47, catalogue 18, studio 11, workPage 9, feed 4. Translate
  them. Also re-translate keys whose English changed (G-375:
  `noContents`, `noContentsBody`, `noVersionsBody`, `historyOrder`; G-376:
  `typeDocument`, `typeDocumentHelp` ("A guide"), `chapterHidden`).

Keep the product words the plan fixes (`docs/plan/frontend.md`, Languages
and themes): "Realm" only in English, and each locale's plain word for a
community. Use natural, short UI language. zh-Hans is a good second source.
G-392, G-394 and G-395 are adding English and zh-Hans strings in Studio,
Work page and Manage while you work. Don't wait for them; a later pass
fills theirs.
