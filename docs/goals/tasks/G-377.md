## Outcome

"The cover is the card" and "every card shows the author and one primary
action" are the plan's principles (`docs/plan/frontend.md`). The second
milestone review (`.temp/manager/milestone-review-2.md`, screenshots in
`.temp/manager/milestone-2/`) found a reader can't recognise a book across
surfaces. Findings 8, 14, 15, 16, 18, 21, 23, 25, 27, 28 and part of 12:

- **P1 · covers (N8).** "Bilingual book club discussion prompt" is a teal
  cloth book under "More by", a yellow arc in Studio and AI Workshop, and a
  blue arc on the profile; 雨夜书店 is red cloth on its page and blank
  charcoal in Studio. Derive one generated cover per Work (type, a hash of
  its id, title and author) in one place that both `WorkCover` and the Zone
  SDK's `workCoverProps` use; Zone themes may tint it but not change it.
  Studio (G-376) and the Work page (G-375) are held by other tasks: export
  the component so they render the same, and list their call sites under
  PROPOSED TASKS if you can't reach them.
- **P2 · Manage queue (N28).** Thumbnails are monograms ("雨", "BJ") and Keep
  and Remove each carry an unexplained empty circle. Use the generated cover
  and label or remove the circle.
- **P2 · initials (N25).** One `initials` helper
  (`packages/ui/src/components/avatar-initials.tsx`) everywhere, including
  the Work page's "About the author", which shows a generic person icon.
- **P2 · Zone cards (N15, N16).** The "Why X is here" stamp and chart rank
  badges sit on covers and hide titles. Move the stamp beside the title with
  a visible "Why it's here" on hover and focus; keep badges out of the title
  area. Recipes offer "Start reading"; use each type's verb (Open recipe,
  Copy prompt, Install, Read).
- **P2 · home (N14, N18).** A shared list becomes a card with only a title;
  show its first three covers and count. A curated pick says "New work" days
  after it was added; show one reason, and "New" only within a freshness window.
- **P2 · search (N23).** Typeahead suggestions show only cover and title; add
  the author and type and highlight the matched part.
- **P2 · profiles (N21, N27).** "Title shown in another language" appears
  under the English *Pride and Prejudice* in the English UI; compare the
  title's language with the UI's. Every row carries a full-width primary
  "Want to read" pill, including the viewer's own Works; use a secondary
  style and hide it on your own.
- **P2 · console (N12).** Library logs "Invalid hook call"; every `/r/*` page
  logs a server "unique key" warning (G-368 couldn't find the source). Fix both.

Add strings in English (`messages.ts`) and zh-Hans only; G-378 owns the other
six locale files and a follow-up fills your new keys. After changing an
official package, run `task zones:digest -- <slug>` and say in the handoff
which digests need approval on the shared stack.
