## Outcome
Platform staff can read one platform queue through the API. They filter it by
urgency, category, language and due time, claim a case, decide it, and later
reconsider it on appeal. A decision records an immutable statement of reasons:
rule and revision, facts, scope, duration, whether automation was involved, and
the appeal route. The affected party receives that statement through G-564's
party credential and a mandatory notice.
- A restriction can remove content or media, or limit a person's participation
  platform-wide until an expiry.
- Deciding an NCII case also removes every known identical copy through G-571's
  copy index.
- Staff can add a preservation hold (G-564's mechanism) to any urgent case.
- Overdue NCII and DMCA steps are listed. DMCA restoration happens only as a
  staff decision inside the window G-564 recorded.
- **The business-operation contract (G-511, moved to M5).** A decision is the
  first resumable multi-effect operation. The API answers `accepted` at once and
  reports each target's effect as `confirmed`, `pending`, `uncertain` or
  `failed`, with its receipt. Replaying the key resumes the remaining effects;
  it never repeats a confirmed one. The case reports completion only when every
  effect is confirmed.

## Approach
- Extend `decide`; do not fork it. Store reasons with the decision row.
- Participation restriction reuses the existing `participation` and `capability` effect labels with an expiry. Main write admission refuses a restricted principal. Appeals and case correspondence stay open to them because they go through G-564's credential, not the principal.
- Copy closure calls G-571's primitive, `suppressIdenticalCopies(originalDigest)`.
- Operation contract:
  - Put one small typed outcome in `modules/operation/outcome.ts`: `accepted`, per-item states and receipts, `continuation`, and a terminal `completed`, `partial` or `cancelled`. Include a conformance test helper that M6's import and wiki intake reuse.
  - The decision stores its planned effects; each effect applies idempotently by (decision, ordinal). The case read returns the outcome.
  - Cancellation stops future effects. Reversal is a new decision, never an undo.
- A misrating or prohibited-imagery decision may write a `platform` suitability assessment through G-509's command. Use the platform authority seeded in G-564, which is the M5 proposal G-509 anticipates.
- Account suspension stays an Accounts admin action. Staff cite the case id in its reason note.

- From G-571's review: an upheld appeal lifts the identical-copy suppression marker for that image; media erasure respects preservation holds (G-564).

## Do not build
- No time-boxed undo; `goal/g-435`'s `report-undo.ts` is obsolete. Reversal is a new decision.
- No concept or tag proposals and no migration 856 scopes (`realm:adopt`, `statement:write`).
- No Main-to-Account suspension call and no automated legal adjudication.
- No perceptual-hash matching.
- No job runner, lease system, generic `/v1/operations` endpoint or event stream. The generic status read is deferred G-541.
- Keep the branch's `decider()` change out; it blocks appeals by sanctioned users.
- No UI (G-821). Channel reach of restrictions is G-542's work.

## Acceptance
- API integration tests:
  - A claim race between two staff gives one owner.
  - Deciding with reasons: the party reads the statement through its credential.
  - An appeal leads to a reversal that restores the content.
  - A participation restriction blocks a reply until it expires; an appeal still goes through.
  - An NCII decision suppresses the original and two identical uploads, and a later identical upload stays suppressed.
  - An overdue NCII step is listed after the clock advances; a retry does not reset it.
  - DMCA restoration is refused before the recorded earliest date.
  - Interrupt a multi-target decision after its first effect: the outcome is `partial` with receipts, and replaying the key completes it without repeating the first effect.
- Class guard: a table test fails if any restriction kind lacks an owner effect. The operation state-machine test rejects `completed` without a receipt for every effect, and rejects cancellation presented as undo.
