## Outcome

`docs/research/reference-webnovel.md` compares REZICS with 起点, 晋江,
KadoKado and Royal Road. What keeps serial readers coming back is missing:

1. **Paragraph comments (本章说 / 段评).** A reader selects a paragraph and
   comments on it; each paragraph shows a quiet count; opening it shows the
   thread beside the text on desktop and in a sheet on phones. Comments bind to
   the chapter's exact text revision and a paragraph selector, so a later
   revision shows them against the paragraph they were about or marks them as
   about an earlier text. Readers can hide them. Spoilers past a reader's
   position stay hidden. Moderation follows the Realm's existing reply review.
2. **The author's note (作家的话).** An optional note at the chapter's end,
   written in Studio's chapter editor beside the text, published with the
   chapter and versioned with it, visually separate from the story.
3. **The next update.** Authors set a publishing schedule for a chapter
   (publish at a time, cancel before it), and a leave notice (请假条) with an
   end date. Readers see "Next chapter: Friday" or "On break until Oct 12" on
   the Work page, in the reader's end-of-chapter panel and in Continue. The
   manager decided (2026-09-28): a scheduled chapter is the author's signed
   publication intent with a not-before time, released by Main at that time;
   the acting Agent and its authority are checked when scheduling and again at
   release, and a release that no longer holds authority fails visibly to the
   author instead of publishing.

G-410 is restructuring contents (volumes, parts, extras) and the reader's
contents; this task starts after it merges and builds on its reader.
