## Outcome

A new person who signs in today lands on `/identity` with no Agent, and the
header cannot show who they are. G-284 added an idempotent
`POST /v1/me/onboarding` (bearer token, `X-Session-Key` UUID, body
`{"profile":"person-onboarding-v1"}`, retry on 202) and chosen `@handles`
(availability, 30-day rename cooldown, 90-day redirects of retired handles).
Make the first minutes feel like signing up to GitHub or Reddit:

1. After token exchange the callback calls onboarding; a first-time person
   goes to a short welcome step to choose a handle (prefilled with the
   suggestion, live availability, clear rules) and confirm the display name,
   then lands on home. Returning people go straight to where they were.
   The interest picker comes later with the home feed; leave a step for it.
2. The avatar menu and header show the acting Agent's name, handle and
   avatar, switch between the person, pen names and organizations, and link to
   "Manage your REZICS Account" on the Accounts origin.
3. A small settings page for the profile: display name, handle change (with
   cooldown and redirect explained), avatar. Rework or retire `/identity`.
4. Encode the per-tab actor choice, consent and recovery states from
   `docs/experience/identity-and-access-experience.md` as stories and browser
   tests, then delete that page and repair its links.

Read `docs/plan/frontend.md` first. Strings go in your features' `messages.ts`
(English and `zh-Hans`); G-288 is moving catalogs to per-locale files and its
handoff recipe applies to you when it lands. Build internal links with
`features/shell/localized-link.tsx`; the shell itself belongs to G-288 until it
closes, so list any one-line shell change you need in the handoff.

## Verify in a real browser

Against the shared stack: sign up a new person on the Accounts origin, choose
a handle, land home, reload, sign out and back in, change the handle and follow
the old profile URL. Screenshots at 1440x900 and 390x844, light and dark, en
and zh-Hans. Stop dev servers before handing off.
