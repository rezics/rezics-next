## Outcome

Translate every English message of these web features into **zh-Hant** and **ja**:
home, shell, feed, manage, realm and zones. Each feature keeps English in
`apps/web/features/<feature>/messages.ts` and one file per locale in
`messages/<locale>.ts` (a `Partial` of the English shape); fill the existing
locale files.

- Readers are people who read books and web novels, follow communities, write
  and moderate: translate meaning in the register the best reading and
  community sites use in that language (Goodreads, KadoKado 角角者, Kakuyomu,
  Naver Series, Reddit), not word for word. Keep `REZICS`, product names, URLs
  and code-like tokens unchanged.
- **"Realm"**: per `docs/plan/frontend.md` ("Languages and themes"), every
  non-English locale uses its plain word for a community (社群 in zh-Hant,
  コミュニティ, 커뮤니티, Community, communauté, comunidad); "Zone" stays
  "Zone". Moderation terms follow Reddit's localized mod tools where they exist.
- zh-Hant follows Taiwan usage; never convert zh-Hans character by character.
- Keep every `insert()` placeholder and `plural`/select structure exactly.
- Do not change English, code or other locales.
