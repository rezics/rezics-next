## Outcome

On `/w/{ref}` a reader recognizes the Work, sees their edition, status and progress, and
has one primary action (Continue, Start or Choose release) inside the first phone
viewport. The overview follows `docs/plan/frontend.md#work-page`: identity and next action;
About (spoiler-safe synopsis, essential facts, tags, content warnings, full facts and a
"Propose correction" slot on expansion); your edition and availability; parts and
connections; explore the wiki; ratings and reviews; discussion and communities; lists and
discovery. Sections a type does not bind are omitted; the rest keep stable anchors, and an
"On this page" control replaces overflowing tabs on phones. Every section still loads and
fails on its own.

## Approach

- Recompose `work-frame.tsx` and `work-views.tsx` into the eight sections; move Also enjoyed into Lists and discovery after reviews; keep one Suspense region per section and the existing failure isolation (`region.tsx`, `failure.ts`).
- Phone: a thumbnail beside the title, identity and the primary action first, a sticky action only after the original scrolls away, "On this page" as a compact menu. Desktop: a narrow cover and action rail with a readable main column.
- Explore the wiki: main characters from the Work's relations section read with `position` (G-847), links to the wiki Zone's characters, chapter guide and timeline, and a shortcut near the header. With no wiki, one line says so and links to how holders build one.
- Ratings name their question, population, scale and count; if G-652 has not merged, the review form keeps today's rating requirement and says so; nothing pretends a score is optional.
- Leave the "Propose correction" slot as an exported placeholder that G-867 fills.
