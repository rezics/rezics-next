## Outcome

The Goal asks for an Accounts site like Google Account, with a well-designed
admin panel. Accounts already has what Google Account covers:
- home, personal info, security (with activity, passkeys, two-step and
  secure-account), data and privacy, and connected apps;
- sign-in, sign-up, consent and recovery;
- an admin panel with an overview, users, staff, OAuth clients and an audit
  log.

G-356 redesigned the admin and G-373 polished both. Nobody has compared
the whole site with its references since.

Compare Accounts, page by page, with Google Account and the Google Admin
console (or Okta's and Microsoft Entra's admin centres). Use public help
pages, their published screenshots, and a real browser (BrowserOS
`browseros` MCP) where pages are public. Don't sign in to any real Google or
third-party account. Then close the gaps that matter most to people:
- the home page's "what needs attention" and privacy/security checkup flow;
- clear device and session management with sign-out everywhere;
- data export and account deletion as guided flows;
- the admin panel's search, filters, bulk actions and user detail, with an
  audit trail for each action;
- empty, loading and error states;
- phone layouts.

Fix what is broken or unclear. Where Account's API lacks something a page
needs, add it in `services/account` with tests. Keep security flows as they
are: step-up, consent fences and refresh coordination (G-384). The eight
locales exist for Accounts; keep them complete (`task i18n:check`).
