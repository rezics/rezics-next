## Outcome

The milestone review (`.temp/manager/milestone-review.md`, findings 4, 12, 13
and 14) found:

- every Work page says "No confirmed credits yet", even for Pride and Prejudice
  and Lin Mei's own serial, so authors appear nowhere (cards, Work pages,
  profiles that call authors "Reader");
- every chapter reads "Untitled chapter" in the reader and Contents although
  its text begins with its real title;
- a serial's chapters are separate top-level Works in Studio, on shelves
  (Want to read holds a chapter) and in the feed; Studio lists imported classics
  as the Agent's "Published" Works.

Fix in Main:

1. **Credits**: a Work created by an Agent carries a confirmed author credit
   for that Agent from creation; imported Works carry their source authors'
   credits (coordinate with G-322, which is adding Open Library author names);
   explain any credit that still needs confirmation and expose it as such
   rather than as "no author".
2. **Chapter titles**: chapters keep the title given at creation (G-305's
   chapter create) and Contents, reader and feed return it; legacy chapters
   without one derive it from the first heading of their text.
3. **Serial as one Work**: chapter Works are children of their Work in every
   inventory — the Agent works read, shelves (shelving a chapter shelves its
   Work), profile works and discovery — and the Agent works read separates
   authored from imported or curated Works.

Backend only, no dev servers; report any web changes needed as PROPOSED TASKS.
