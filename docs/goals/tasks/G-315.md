## Outcome

Goodreads-quality Work pages (the maintainer's reference) are built around
reader reviews, and Zone reader quotes need a public review source (G-310).
Main has ratings (global and per Realm Context) and replies, but no reviews.
Add them:

1. A reader's review of a Work: rating (linked to their rating observation in
   the chosen Context), text in a language, spoiler flag, optional reading
   dates from their shelf; create, edit with CAS, delete; one per reader and
   Context.
2. Per-Work review list, sort `helpful` and `new`, filter by language and
   rating, cursor pages; the viewer's own review first when present; spoiler
   text withheld unless asked for.
3. "Helpful" votes (one per person, not on one's own review) and report
   through the existing report owner.
4. A public quotable-excerpt read for Zone reader quotes (the Realm's
   Context reviews with an excerpt).
5. Events and relay readers so feed, notifications and rankings can use
   reviews (feed cards for reviews with text; ratings without text never
   become cards — see `docs/plan/frontend.md`, "Home").

Disclosure, moderation and cost bounds as in the other read families.
Backend only: no web, Storybook or Accounts dev servers.
