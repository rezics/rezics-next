## Outcome

The maintainer's reference for REZICS's Work page is Goodreads' book page
(https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/127278133-glorious-exploits). Of it they
named the "Readers also enjoyed" row as beautiful and worth copying
(`.temp/manager/refs/goodreads-readers-also-enjoyed.png`): four to six large
covers with soft shadows, title, author and "★ 4.26 · 41.9k" under each,
paged by a round arrow button and dots.

Study the reference page in a real browser (and StoryGraph's book page for
contrast), then bring REZICS's Work page to that level:

- **Readers also enjoyed.** Render G-381's `GET /v1/works/:id/also-enjoyed`
  as that row. Title it honestly by basis: "Readers also enjoyed" when the
  cards are `co-readers`, "Similar books" for `similar`, "More from
  <Realm>" for `realm`. Keep the page's generated-cover rules
  (`CatalogueCover` by Work id). Make the row usable by keyboard and on a
  phone with a swipeable row, and hide it when empty.
- **Stats strip.** Goodreads shows ratings, reviews, currently reading and
  want to read under the rating. Show the numbers Main can give: rating
  count and mean, review count, readers currently reading, readers who want
  to read. Count only public libraries, the rule for author pages and
  also-enjoyed. If Main lacks a bounded read for shelf counts per Work, add
  one (`work/read-stats*.ts`) with a statement-budget test.
- **Genres.** Show the Work's accepted classifications as chips (as
  Goodreads shows genres) that link to Discover filtered by that term, the
  way the Mods Zone chips now do.
- **First screen.** Compare REZICS's first screen with Goodreads' at
  1440x900 and 390x844: cover size, title, author, rating, primary action
  and description. Adjust hierarchy and spacing where REZICS is weaker.
  G-391 holds `work-frame.tsx` and `author.tsx` (cover by Work id,
  author links); don't edit them.

Add English and zh-Hans strings only.
