## Outcome

Moderators and Realm owners need a workspace as good as the best moderation
tools (Reddit's mod queue and mod log, Discourse's review queue and staff
pages, GitHub's org settings, Linear's keyboard-first triage). Read
`docs/plan/frontend.md` ("Principles", "Main site routes": `/manage`,
`/manage/r/{realm}/…`). Main now has what it needs:

- queue and audit reads: `GET /v1/realms/:realm/moderation`, `/audit`
  (`routes/management-reads.ts`); submission decisions
  (`routes/realm-submissions.ts`), moderation decisions (`routes/reports.ts`),
  reply reviews (`routes/realm-replies.ts`);
- Realm management (G-287, `routes/realm-admin.ts`): members with consent,
  bans and audit; roles and `role-impact` previews; `role-changes`;
  `escalations`; CAS settings with localized rules.

Build the management surface:

1. **`/manage`**: the Realms I moderate with queue counts, escalations and
   recent activity.
2. **Queue**: one triage list across submissions, reports, replies and
   corrections, with the content shown in context, keyboard triage (j/k,
   approve, reject with reason, escalate), bulk actions where the API allows,
   an undo window where it is safe, and honest states for stale or conflicting
   decisions (another moderator got there first).
3. **Log**: the public decisions and the private audit log, filterable.
4. **Members and roles**: member search, add, ban with duration and reason;
   roles as permission bundles in plain words, and the **impact preview**
   before any change ("12 members gain: remove replies").
5. **Settings and rules**: Realm settings and localized rules; encode the
   semantics in `docs/experience/realm-rule-localization-authoring.md`
   (approved meaning versus translation status and fallback) as stories and
   tests, then delete that page and repair its links.

Visibility and review modes are being added by G-298; build their controls
against its contract when it lands, and keep them hidden until then.
Missing APIs go to PROPOSED TASKS. Strings in your feature's `messages.ts`
(English and `zh-Hans`; follow the per-locale layout if G-288 has landed);
internal links with `features/shell/localized-link.tsx`.
