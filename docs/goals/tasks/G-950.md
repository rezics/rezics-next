built by one module, `apps/web/features/address/` (from G-943, in this
worktree). Main's summaries will carry each item's canonical address (G-937,
merging soon; read its contract with `git show goal/g-937:<path>`); until then
the builders take the address shape when present and fall back to the identity
form from an id.

Replace every hand-built entity path (`/r/`, `/z/`, `/w/`, `/e/`, `/a/`, `/@`,
`/concepts/`) in your claimed files, fixtures and stories included, with the
module's builders, so that a link is canonical (name form when Main gives one)
and never needs a redirect. Do not change behaviour beyond links.
