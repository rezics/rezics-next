## Outcome

On the shared stack, `/health/rating-ready` answers 503 for most of the day.
The manager's evidence (Access database of `rezics-dev`, 2026-10-04):

- The global rating Context generation (`access.discovery_generation`, scope
  `global`, Context `…01a0e915-84fb…`) rebuilds all 2,632 Works from scratch
  on every source change: `changed_works` is always null. One full build took
  78 minutes (07:43 → 09:01 UTC). Builds at sequences 7504–7510 and 7603–7607
  were cancelled (`manager-cancelled`) every ~10 minutes when the source moved
  again, so the projection was current only for 30 minutes.
- `discoveryChanges` (`modules/discovery/changes.ts`) returns null, and so
  forces a full snapshot, whenever the interval holds any event outside five
  Work-local actions. Follows, joins, ratings, Zone, theme and structure
  commands all rebuild every Discover and rating scope, even though most
  change nothing that a scope ranks.
- All 114 refresh scopes take turns one batch (~24 Works) per 100 ms tick, so
  a full rebuild runs at about 30 Works a minute per scope and grows with the
  catalogue × scope count.
- 8,155 generation rows are kept, 6,137 of them incomplete (cancelled or
  expired), with their entries.

Make refresh cost scale with the change. Classify every event type by its
effect on each projection family (irrelevant: advance the position only;
Work-local: incremental delta on those Works, including a rating changing its
Work's aggregate; scope-wide: a rebuild, which must be rare and named). Do not
cancel a running build because the source moved: finish at its pinned
position, then apply the delta. A full rebuild of the 10,000-Work corpus must
finish in minutes, and stale scopes that readers use (global, rating) must not
wait behind idle Realm scopes. Keep a bounded retention of failed, cancelled
and expired generations and their entries.

Prove it with cost assertions on the multi-scale corpus (G-1024 work profile):
refresh work per irrelevant or Work-local event is constant across 1k and 10k
Works and across scope count; a full rebuild of 10k Works fits a stated
budget; `rating-ready` and `discovery-ready` recover within a stated time after
a rating write and after a follow. Update the cost model and decisions in
`modules/discovery/README.md` and `docs/storage/workload-budgets.md` where they
apply. Say how the shared stack behaves after merge.
