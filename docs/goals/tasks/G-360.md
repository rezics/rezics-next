## Outcome

Translate every English message of the Zones feature (`apps/web/features/zones/messages.ts`)
and of the official Zone packages (`apps/web/zones/official/<slug>/`, if they
keep message files) into **ko** and **de**, filling the per-locale files.

best reading and community sites in that language; "Realm" as the plain word
for a community per `docs/plan/frontend.md`; "Zone" stays "Zone"; zh-Hant in
Taiwan usage; placeholders and plural structures exact; change nothing else.
