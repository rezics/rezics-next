## Outcome

G-1043 made Home's followed-target and admitted-score indexes become ready on
their own and report readiness and lag (merged on main; its notes are under
`.temp/goal-orchestration/runs/G-1043/` in the main checkout). It proposed the
web side: `apps/web/features/feed/feed-list.tsx` should

1. distinguish an empty feed from one whose projection is still building
   (an honest "catching up" state, not "You're all caught up"),
2. suppress items above the reader's watermark consistently while the index
   lags, without duplicates or gaps across pages, and
3. refresh once the projection catches up, without losing scroll position or
   the reader's place.

Follow the API's readiness fields as served (do not infer from timing), in
every locale, with stories for each state in a Latin and a CJK locale, run
under load as G-1015 did (20 other story files, `--maxWorkers=2`, three passes).
