## Outcome

Zone pages (see `docs/plan/frontend.md`, "Zones", and
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/manager/research-zones.md`
sections 1, 5 and 8) and the home page's right rail need module reads that
Main lacks, and cover cards need what KadoKado and Royal Road show on every
serial:

1. **Rankings** `GET /v1/realms/{realm}/rankings?metric=&interval=day|week|month`
   with metrics from signals Main actually has (reads and finished chapters
   from progress, shelving once G-285's status shelves land, ratings, follows
   when G-282 lands); a bounded projection updated from the outbox, at most a
   fixed page, disclosure-safe. A REZICS-wide variant serves "trending" on the
   home rail. Say which metrics you left out and why.
2. **Zone module reads** for what a Zone page shows beyond rankings: latest
   chapter updates, newly adopted Works, recently completed Works, rising
   (growth against a baseline), and a recent-decisions summary, each a small
   cursor read in the read-family style.
3. **Serial Work summary**: a one-line tagline (the hook shown under covers,
   separate from the description, with a length limit and per-language values),
   completion status (ongoing, completed, hiatus), chapter and word counts and
   last update, returned wherever Work summaries are (discovery, search,
   feed, rankings). Coordinate the write with the Work metadata command rather
   than adding a parallel one.

Follow the read-family template (`services/main/src/modules/work/read-contract.ts`)
and the relay-reader inventory test for new events. Extend the demo seed
(`scripts/dev/seed/**`, unclaimed; keep changes small) with taglines and
reading activity so rankings are not empty.
