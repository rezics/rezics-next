## Outcome

Manage is REZICS's moderator surface, and the Goal asks for excellent
management surfaces. The second review rated it ready for reports, members,
roles and the log. Two things still fall short:

- **Chapter Works in the queue.** A report on a chapter shows "雨夜书店 ·
  第一章 雨夜" as its own Work, with a monogram. G-375 added `partOf { work,
  occurrence }` to `GET /v1/works/:id`. Present a chapter as "第一章 雨夜 ·
  雨夜书店" with the Book's cover, and link it into the reader at that
  chapter, everywhere in the queue and the log.
- **Every submission kind.** G-354 added Work submissions, publication
  submissions and tag proposals to Main; G-353 made Manage typecheck against
  them. Moderators should review each kind in context:
  - the Work's cover, author, type and hook;
  - for a mod: game, version and loader;
  - for a prompt or skill: its preview;
  - for a chapter: its text, with spoilers flagged;
  - the submitter and their history in this Realm;
  - the Realm rule it may break;
  - one-key decisions with a reason.

  Study Reddit's mod queue and mod log (public docs and screenshots are
  enough) and Discourse's review queue, and take what fits: bulk actions,
  filters by kind and reason, a sticky reason picker, keyboard shortcuts
  shown on the buttons, and undo within a short window where Main allows it.

Keep Manage's existing decisions, roles, members and log working. G-393
holds `members.stories.tsx`; don't edit it. Add English and zh-Hans strings
only. If a Main read is missing or needs per-item calls, add the batched read
in `management-reads` with a statement-budget test.
