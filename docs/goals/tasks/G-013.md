Only the exact bounded registry/manifest grammar already admitted by the Cargo
profiles may be extended here. Existing-lock input is immutable evidence of
caller intent, not a trusted report that an artifact was installed or verified.
Yanked eligibility must be tied to an exact retained lock identity, not merely
to the presence of any lockfile bytes or a matching package name.
