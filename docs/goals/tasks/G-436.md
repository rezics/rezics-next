## Outcome

G-412 shipped community creation, the `/r` directory and a post composer
that attaches a Work. Its handoff and the manager's browser pass
(2026-09-28, as Daniel Chen) left these gaps between REZICS and Reddit:

1. **Posts without a Work.** Every post must be about a Work today, and
   replies store plain text. A community needs text posts ("What should I read
   after 雨夜书店?"), link posts and image posts, with an optional Work
   attachment. Add the content contract in Main (and Content if the body lives
   there) and the composer's post types, keeping Home's feed post/Work split
   (a post with no Work shows no attachment).
2. **Rich text.** Posts and replies accept Markdown-level formatting (bold,
   italic, links, lists, quotes, spoilers, inline code), rendered safely
   (sanitized; no raw HTML), with a preview tab and the same editor for
   replies. Spoilers stay hidden until clicked, as Reddit's `>!…!<`.
3. **Growing sort** on the directory: communities gaining members and posts
   this week, from a bounded projection.
4. **A community's first minutes.** Creation lands on the new community with
   a setup checklist (rules, topics, icon and banner, first post, invite
   link) that disappears when done, not on an empty Manage queue.
5. **Topics from the shared vocabulary.** The create form's topic picker
   offers only the pre-G-426 English-only proposition ("Mystery · 悬疑"),
   not the current Concept ("悬疑"). Read topics from the shared scheme
   (`/v1/classification-vocabulary`, localized labels).
6. **Styled uploads.** The icon and banner fields are native file inputs;
   use a styled drop zone with a preview and crop hint, as `@rezics/ui`
   controls elsewhere.
7. **Profile posts and comments** tabs on a person's profile, like Reddit's
   overview: their public posts and replies, newest first, respecting
   G-413's privacy settings.
8. **Blocked people** (G-413's remaining item): a reader who blocked someone
   doesn't see that person's replies in threads (collapsed with "Blocked
   user"), with tests.

G-435 (Manage, running) owns `realm-reply/member-policy.ts` and a new
`reply-review` module for held replies; don't edit those. G-431 owns Home.
