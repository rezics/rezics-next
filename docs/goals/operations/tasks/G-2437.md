### Program decision, 2026-10-14 04:22 UTC

Binding ruling from `mail:da502f3e-f0d4-43e7-9cf3-1059322bb6c5`, answering
Operations' `mail:7f9ca394` and API's `mail:f0fb6a3d`:

- An adapter may declare a producer build that is measured separately. That
  build runs once per source key, before the physical cut, under its own
  deadline, which the adapter declares and the evidence records. It runs
  under its producer lease, and it is neither admission wait nor test time.
- For an adapter with such a build, `SOURCE_PREPARATION_MS` (600 s) starts at
  the physical cut, after the build, and covers cut, seal and backup
  verification. Restore, startup and readiness keep their own 600 s bound
  unchanged.
- An adapter without a declared build behaves exactly as today, with one
  budget from `adapter.build`, proved by an unchanged test.
- A failed or overrun build seals nothing and cleans its staging. Custody,
  source keys, integrity, verification and admission accounting are unchanged.
  This adds no second lifecycle, raises no budget and does not reclassify
  active builds.

Operations briefs and lands this support, then mails API the accepted main
commit. G-2427 resumes after that; G-2365 stays held until the complete source
lands. This handoff proves the clock contract only, not a physical source or
an API row.
