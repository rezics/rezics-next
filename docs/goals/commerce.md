# Commerce

## Outcome

Buyers discover, purchase and use the exact offered benefit. Creators manage offers and historical sales, and can
distinguish attributed earnings from an actually attested external payment.

## Done when

- Subject entry, offer, hosted Checkout, private order and owned-benefit navigation, and protected use form a complete
  ordinary API, native-storage and reviewed-page journey. The first benefit is an exact authored Content edition:
  `content.edition`, `edition:read`, revision ID and SHA256. Content owns genuine sale authority and final protected
  bytes; Accounts owns current benefit admission. Historical subject-only orders remain unknown until explicitly
  authorized mapping.
- A one-time, account-bound, quantity-one purchase captures one fixed-version benefit and immutable accepted terms.
  Repricing and delisting do not rewrite a sale. Signed provider evidence, buyer-home orders, entitlement fulfilment,
  exact replay, reconciliation, refunds/disputes, terminal fencing, and financial erasure/custody meet the native
  Commerce contract. A redirect, Library fact or historical receipt never authorizes present use; incomplete evidence
  never grants or restores access.
- Creator offers, sales and statements distinguish historical payment, present entitlement, provider bank payout,
  attributed earnings, reserves/payability and actual external payment attestation. Unknown financial meaning stays
  visible, with recorded units, currency, exponent, sign and tax evidence retaining their captured meaning.
- The linked [Library Goal](library.md) completes the personal Library, common Collections, profiles, labels, copies,
  all admitted progress modes, follows, publication, filtering, imports and lifecycle without deferral or weakened
  native and page acceptance. Private state, payment and source mappings confer no publication or protected-use
  authority.
- Owner, other-account, anonymous, suspended/revoked/terminal and repair personas have their specified outcomes
  through real Auth/Access, Kernel and durable work. Current authority precedes receipt lookup; exact retry retains
  original operands, key and credential. Bounded native work, currentness, lifecycle and custody meet their owners'
  contracts.
- All scoped outcomes are promoted to main with checks bound to that revision, complete native outcomes and reviewed
  served pages. Specified, implemented, exposed, tested and promoted remain separate states; source review, typing,
  stories or an integrated task alone do not discharge native, browser, growth or promotion obligations.

## Scope

Direct licensed sales on one US Stripe account through hosted Checkout/Managed Payments. Commerce owns native offers,
fixed-version benefits, orders, payment evidence, entitlements, sales, statements and their journey pages, together
with the linked Library Goal under this same manager. No processor catalogue, bundles, gifts, subscriptions,
consumables, points/wallet/tokens, market/exchange or new ledger. Creator payouts remain outside Stripe.
[Commerce modules](../commerce-modules.md) owns future scope. Dynamic definitions retain adopted Source requirements.

## Ownership

The commerce manager owns `services/kernel/{src,db,test}/{commerce,library}/**` and the corresponding journey pages.
Web owns the shell/navigation, shared list/card patterns, Storybook organization and review; existing producers remain
handoffs. Semantic owns definitions, identity and filter meaning; Search owns catalogue and maxima; Content owns
publication, protected bytes and Rating; Accounts owns Auth, Access, grants, lifecycle and scopes; Performance owns
bounds/accounting; Kernel owns shared runtime. No authority is duplicated.
