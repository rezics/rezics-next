## Outcome

Fix findings F5 and F6 of the independent review
(`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/research/showcase-review-report.md`;
read both there first). The first slide is the page's largest image and today
waits for hydration and two client requests, and every image is uncacheable.

1. **F5.** Above-the-fold showcase art (the first slide and the Work header)
   is in the server-rendered HTML as a real `<picture>`/`<img>` whose
   metadata (NSFW, conceal, the reader's preferences) the server resolved, so
   the preload is used and LCP does not wait for the client. A masked image is
   rendered masked on the server; nothing unmasked is ever sent and then
   hidden.
2. **F6.** Public showcase art (public, unsuppressed, active, cleared) is
   cacheable: the BFF stops forcing `no-store` for it and passes Main's
   validators, and Main answers `If-None-Match` with 304. Private or
   reader-dependent media keeps `private, no-store`. Public art URLs carry no
   reader identity (drop the `actingSubject`/avatar query G-1143 added, now
   unnecessary after G-1142), and a signed-in reader's request for public art
   costs no Account introspection.

Read `docs/contracts/media.md#image-presentation` and keep its masking rules
exactly. Smallest change; no CDN, no new image service.
