## Outcome

Zone moderators can give a slide its own campaign art through the API, as
[showcase art](../../../contracts/media.md#showcase-art) allows. Today
`zone/campaign-art.ts` accepts only a public `publication-item` Use whose
target is the Zone's Realm, but no route creates one: `POST /v1/media/publications`
requires a current Work (`assertCurrentWork`) and `POST /v1/media/uses` makes
`document-image` Uses. So a link slide cannot have art, and v1 banners never
could either.

1. A route that turns an uploaded, screened asset into a campaign art Use for
   a Realm, under the authority that may write that Realm's Zone configuration:
   role (background landscape or portrait, logo with language, tone and
   anchor, cutout), crop and focal area, with the same admission as Work
   showcase art (exact ratio, minimum sizes, alpha) and the same typed
   refusals. Reuse G-1110's admission code rather than copy it.
2. It requests renditions, and `campaign-art.ts` accepts these Uses (and
   still reads any existing Realm publication-item Use).
3. A rate-limit family for the route (`g-543-rate-limit.test.ts` guards it).

Smallest change: no new store, no editor UI, no change to Work showcase art.
