## Outcome

The Zone hero becomes the media showcase that
[decision 54](../../../product/decisions.md#decision-54) describes; read
[Showcase carousel](../../../contracts/presentation.md#showcase-carousel) first.
This task is the whole frontend renderer, fed by today's data; art, slides and
editors arrive from later tasks through the view model you define here.

1. **Carousel primitive** (`packages/ui/src/components/carousel.tsx`, SharkUI's
   Ark carousel). Server HTML must not hide the first slide from assistive
   technology; hidden slides are `inert` too. Autoplay pauses on hover, focus,
   a hidden tab and an off-screen carousel, and stops for good after the first
   manual move; export Ark's autoplay trigger; let an item opt out of the forced
   `object-cover` image rule. Build the showcase on this primitive unless you
   find it cannot meet the decision, and then say why in the handoff.
2. **View model.** Add a showcase slide type to `packages/zone-sdk` beside
   `ZoneBanner`: target href, optional Work, kicker, title, tagline, art
   (landscape, portrait, cutout, each an image with `srcset` candidates and an
   optional focal area; logo with tone, anchor and language), optional trailer
   link. Replace `HeroSlotProps.banners` with slides and update every official
   package hero slot (books, fiction, games, software, mods, ai-workshop). Package code changes their
   digests; the shared stack re-approves them with `task dev:refresh` at merge.
3. **Stage** in `apps/web/features/showcase/`: one breakpoint table that yields
   the CSS, the `<picture>` `<source media>` sets and the first slide's preload
   (`<link rel=preload imagesrcset media>`; React does not preload an image
   inside `<picture>`, and slides after the first are lazy or low priority).
   Compact portrait windows get a 3:4 card with the next peeking; other windows
   16:9, capped by `svh`; wide fine-pointer screens add the coming-slides list.
   On a window with two viewport segments, keep a slide in one segment.
4. **Slides.** Art slides: background cropped around its focal area, scrim,
   logo or live title, tagline, the Work's own action verb and "Why here?"
   (reuse `PickSlide`'s parts). Cover-composed slides when a Work has no art.
   Anything not made for the frame (a cover, a poster) is shown whole on an
   ambient backdrop of itself. Title effect presets (plain, outline, gradient,
   glow) tuned per script; take the preset as a prop for now. A cutout is drawn
   in a stage layer outside the track. Pointer parallax on hover devices and
   scroll-driven parallax where supported; none with reduced motion.
5. **Rotation** as the decision states, with a pause control and per-slide
   progress; slide labels and live-region behaviour per the WAI-ARIA carousel
   pattern.
6. **Trailer facade.** A "Watch trailer" action opens a dialog that loads a
   privacy-enhanced embed (YouTube no-cookie, Bilibili's player) only then;
   other links open externally. Add the needed `frame-src` to the Zone CSP
   (`apps/web/features/zones/csp.ts`).
7. **Today's data.** Adapt the hero (`realm/modules.ts`, `adapt.ts`): banners
   become art slides with their one image as landscape art (shown whole on an
   ambient backdrop, since it was not authored for the frame), picks become
   cover-composed slides. New strings in all eight locales.
8. **Stories** with generated fixture art (a text-free landscape and portrait
   background, transparent logos in two tones and languages, a cutout; small
   files under `apps/web/features/showcase/`): phone 390x844, fold cover
   412x960, tablet 820x1180 and 1180x820, fold inner 984x1092, phone landscape
   844x390, desktop 1280x860 and 1920x1080; en, zh-Hant, ja and ar; reduced
   motion; a Work with no art, with art but no portrait, with a logo only in
   another language, and with a trailer. Add the viewports to Storybook.

Not in scope: hosted or autoplaying video, backend or API changes, editors, a
general layout engine, per-slide CSS or new dependencies beyond what Ark gives.
