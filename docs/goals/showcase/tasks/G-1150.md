## Outcome

Fix findings F1, F2, F3 and F7 of the independent review
(`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/research/showcase-review-report.md`;
read each finding there first), and make renditions show their source's labels.

1. **F1.** `GET /v1/zones/:id/presentation` (public) returns only slides a
   reader may see now: inside their schedule and, for Work slides, a Work the
   reader can read; other slides and their campaign art are absent, without
   saying why. The Realm showcase editor reads its slides through an
   editor-scoped read that requires the authority to edit the Zone
   (`showcase-zone-editor/server.ts:91` uses the public read today).
2. **F2.** Campaign art bytes are delivered only while a slide of a readable
   Zone currently references the Use (and its schedule is current); removing a
   slide, ending its schedule or making the Zone private stops delivery. Keep
   one bounded check per request.
3. **F3.** The campaign art batch goes through the disclosure inventory
   (`discloseInventory`), as `POST /v1/resources/showcase` already does, so
   held art is not advertised and the slide falls back to the Work's art.
4. **F7.** A Zone configuration save requests renditions only for newly
   referenced publication-item Uses, after the commit and best effort; a
   storage failure never fails the save or leaves the admission claimed.
5. **Rendition labels** per the [media contract](../../../contracts/media.md#showcase-art):
   `metadata()` shows a rendition's source NSFW and age assessment as derived
   until the rendition is labelled itself; update the README line that says
   labels never inherit.

Smallest change; do not touch rendition ladders or web rendering (G-1151).
