## Outcome

An independent review of everything the showcase Goal merged, before its
acceptance: `git log --grep 'G-11\(08\|09\|10\|11\|23\|24\|25\|40\|42\|43\)\|Goal: showcase' main`
(about 40 commits since `932ada68c`), against
[decision 54](../../../product/decisions.md#decision-54). Read-only: change
no tracked file; write the report to `.temp/showcase-review/report.md` in your
worktree and summarise it in the handoff.

Review for, in order:

1. **Disclosure and authority.** A rendition, showcase art Use, logo,
   campaign art or trailer must never reach a reader who cannot read its
   source, after suppression, erasure, a private Work or a hidden Zone; the
   campaign art batch read (`zone/campaign-art.ts`) bypasses the single-item
   disclosure adapter and needs a verdict. The `media.campaign` Access mapping
   and the public-read-without-Agent change in `routes/media.ts` and
   `routes/resources.ts` (G-1142).
2. **Correctness** of crops (oriented pixels, `xywh=percent:`, renditions,
   the `object-view-box` fallback), v1 to v2 reading, schedule handling,
   logo choice by language and tone, and the fallback chain.
3. **Cost.** Bounded queries per batch read; no per-target round trips; the
   rendition worker's limits; first-slide preload and lazy loading.
4. **Accessibility** of the carousel against the WAI-ARIA pattern and WCAG
   2.2.2: server-rendered visibility, rotation control, labels, inert slides,
   reduced motion, the trailer dialog.
5. **Over-design**: abstractions, options or layers the decision does not need.

Each finding states file and line, the failure scenario and a severity; prefer
few, verified findings over many guesses. Do not fix anything.

## Checks

Run what proves a finding (unit tests, a read against the shared stack at
`http://localhost:3001` and `:3000`), never a heavy QA run.
