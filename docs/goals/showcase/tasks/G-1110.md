## Outcome

A Work carries the [showcase art](../../../contracts/media.md#showcase-art)
that every Zone, the Work page and lists reuse: background landscape (16:9),
background portrait (3:4), logos and a cutout, plus a trailer link.

1. **Selections.** Extend the avatar selection slots (`selection_slot`,
   `selection_revision`, `use`; `store.ts` hardcodes `avatar` today) with the
   showcase roles, under the same authority, expected-head concurrency,
   idempotency and removal semantics as the Work's cover
   (`PUT /v1/resources/{work}/avatar`). A logo slot is keyed by its language
   (BCP 47 or `zxx`) and tone (`dark` or `light`) and carries an anchor
   (`start-bottom`, `center-top`, `center-middle`, `center-bottom`). Each
   selection is a Use with its crop and an optional focal area
   (`xywh=percent:`).
2. **Admission.** Backgrounds must crop to their exact ratio in oriented
   pixels, at least 1280x720 (landscape) and 960x1280 (portrait); logos and
   cutouts must have an alpha channel. Refusals are typed.
3. **Renditions.** Selecting showcase art requests its renditions with the
   function from G-1109.
4. **Trailer.** A link on the Work with the same authority, normalized to a
   canonical watch URL for YouTube and Bilibili (other https links are kept as
   plain links). Use the smallest owner beside the selections; there is no Work
   link model in the graph to reuse.
5. **Read.** One batch read for up to 64 targets and a context: for each, the
   readable showcase art with its `srcset` candidates, focal areas, logo
   language, tone and anchor, and the trailer; the requested context's slot wins
   over the default, as for avatars; hidden or erased art is absent without
   revealing why. Bounded queries, no per-target round trips.
6. Update `services/main/src/modules/media/README.md` and the showcase art
   section of `docs/contracts/media.md` only where code now settles a detail.

Smallest change. Do not add a showcase document store, a new service, editor
UI, Zone slides (G-1111) or changes to Work or Zone module reads.
