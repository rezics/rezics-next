## Outcome

A Zone's moderators arrange its
[showcase](../../../contracts/presentation.md#showcase-carousel) in a new
"Showcase" section of Realm management (`/manage/r/<realm>/showcase`).

1. **Slides.** Up to six, in order: pick a Work with the searchable picker the
   product already uses, or enter a link; optional kicker and title with their
   translations; a schedule. Reorder by drag and by keyboard. The editor
   recommends five or fewer and marks the first slide as the one most readers
   act on.
2. **Campaign art** overrides the Work's own art for this Zone: reuse the
   background, logo and cutout controls from `apps/web/features/showcase-editor/`
   (G-1123); uploads become the Realm's publication items as banners were.
   Show which art a slide uses (its own, the Work's, or the composed cover
   slide).
3. **Title effect** (`tokens.titleEffect`) with a live sample.
4. **Preview** with the real stage at its three shapes and rotation off.
5. Save with `PUT /v1/zones/:id/configuration` (`zone-presentation-v2`) with
   the expected revision; refusals and conflicts in words. Eight UI locales.

Not in scope: a general presentation editor (modules, navigation, tokens other
than the title effect), new APIs.

## Read

The decision above; `services/main/src/modules/zone/presentation-format.ts`
(v2); `apps/web/features/manage/` (settings section as the pattern);
`apps/web/features/showcase/` and `showcase-editor/`.
