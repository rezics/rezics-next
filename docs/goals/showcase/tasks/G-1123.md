## Outcome

People who may select a Work's cover edit its
[showcase art](../../../contracts/media.md#showcase-art) in a new "Showcase"
section of the Work's edit pages (`/w/<ref>/edit/showcase`, beside editions,
parts and relations), seeing exactly what readers will see.

1. **Backgrounds.** Upload or replace the landscape (16:9) and portrait (3:4)
   art; frame it to its exact ratio and mark the focal area that must survive
   narrower frames. The crop is saved as the Use's `xywh=percent:` fragment
   (Main keeps the original and renders widths), not as browser-cropped bytes.
   Say plainly when portrait art is missing and what phones will show instead.
2. **Logos.** Add a logo per language or as language-neutral, in a dark and/or
   light tone, at one of four anchors; refuse a file without transparency
   before upload and explain why. Show which reader languages get which logo
   and which fall back to the styled live title.
3. **Cutout** with transparency, and the **trailer link** (YouTube, Bilibili or
   any https link; show how it will open).
4. **Preview.** The real showcase stage from `apps/web/features/showcase/` at
   the compact portrait card, 16:9 and the wide stage with its list, in the
   reader language of choice and in RTL, with live title, tagline and actions
   on top. Every change previews before it is saved.
5. Saves go through `PUT /v1/resources/{work}/showcase/art` and `/trailer`
   with the expected selection, idempotency keys and typed refusals shown in
   words; concurrent edits surface as a conflict to reload, never a silent
   overwrite. Uploads reuse the media reserve, bytes and screening protocol
   (`apps/web/features/communities/images.ts`); screening holds are explained.
   All strings in the eight UI locales.

Not in scope: Zone slides (G-1124), the Work page header (G-1125), new APIs.
If an API is missing or awkward for this editor, stop and propose the change.
