## Outcome

1. **Work page header.** When a Work has showcase art, its page opens with it,
   as store pages do: the background for the window's shape behind the header
   (scrim, focal area kept), the logo or the styled title, credits and stats
   as live text, and "Watch trailer" through the facade from
   `apps/web/features/showcase/`. Without art the header stays as it is. One
   batch read (`POST /v1/resources/showcase`) for the page, no extra round trip
   per layer. Respect reduced motion; no rotation here. Build the art header in
   `apps/web/features/showcase/work-header*` with its strings in the showcase
   messages (the Work page's own messages are held by post-layers' G-1081);
   `work-header.tsx` and `work-frame.tsx` only place it.
2. **Dev seeds.** The shared stack seeds showcase art for a few Works of
   different kinds (a book, a game, a piece of software): landscape and
   portrait backgrounds, logos in two languages and tones, one cutout, one
   trailer, and one Work left without art, using the fixture art under
   `apps/web/features/showcase/`. One official Zone gets v2 slides mixing these
   Works, a link slide with campaign art and a scheduled slide. Seeds write
   through the API, idempotently.
