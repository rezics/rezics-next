## Outcome

Launch's first public scope includes a Bangumi-like catalogue. Kernel has
landed the data, as data and reviewed reads, with no new handler:
- G-1435 (855cb79e1, 1c4db396d):
  - a format concept scheme: TV, movie, OVA, ONA, special, manga, one-shot,
    plus unclassified; classified like genres and readable through facet
    `concept`;
  - declared episode and volume counts as literal Statements
    (`/v1/resources/{id}/statements`);
  - `completionStatus` adds upcoming and cancelled;
  - the Bangumi importer writes all of these through the API.
- G-1437 (6d92f2faa): the reviewed template `work-publication-years`, which
  lists Works by first-publication year (fromYear/toYear, newest first, with
  a cursor).
- Credits (studio, director, artist) come with kernel's G-1436, not yet
  landed. Leave a place for them; don't build them.

Build two official Zones on the existing official-package pattern. Follow
`apps/web/zones/official/visual-novels` and how the seed creates and approves
the light-novel and visual-novel Zones (`scripts/dev/seed/ln-vn-zones-step.ts`,
`official-zones-step.ts`, `official-plan.ts`):
- `/z/anime`: browse anime Works with filters for format (TV, movie, OVA, ONA,
  special) and status, and a year view newest first. Each card shows title,
  format, year, episode count and status.
- `/z/manga`: the same for manga and one-shots, with volume count instead of
  episodes.
- A Work's page inside each Zone leads with a compact fact panel: format,
  year, episode or volume count, and status. It's a package slot over the
  Statements and facets the Work page already reads. No new API, and nothing
  computed that the data doesn't say.
- A year view or filter that has no data for a Work says so; it never
  guesses. Season isn't stored: show only the year.

Seed data through the API, with the importer or the same writes it makes, as
a new seed step `anime-manga-zones-step.ts` with its test:
- enough anime and manga that a filter's results run past page one;
- mixed formats, including unclassified;
- Japanese and English titles;
- one long series (1,000+ episodes);
- an upcoming and a cancelled status.

Strings come in all eight locales, with non-English marked for native review.
Credit the data source in each Zone's footer, as the VN Zone does.

Paired journeys:
- API: `tests/qa/integration/anime-manga-zones.test.ts`. Each Zone's filters
  return the right Works through the public reads, including the second
  page, and an unclassified or yearless Work shows up only where it should.
- Browser: `apps/web/tests/anime-manga-zones.e2e.ts`, at 390 px and 1280 px,
  in English and Japanese. Filter anime by TV and a year, open the long
  series, see its fact panel, and do the same in manga.

## Do not

No new API, handler or template. No domain tables, no scraping, no change to
kernel's definitions or importer. No brand names or logos of other sites. If
something the Zones need is missing from the reads, report it under PROPOSED
TASKS.
