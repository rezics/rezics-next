## Outcome

G-1399 proved keyed Zone routes at 16 and 200 mounts. It stopped at 200
because native publication refused a navigation whose reverse dependency
footprint exceeded 256 (`zone-route-keyed.test.ts:25`). That cap was a
physical safety guard, not a Zone limit. Kernel's 74298b7cf exempts the
unchanged canonical Zone identity, and it is serving at a2153ac04 (native
5692ff6a9bb2).

Raise the larger size to 1,000 mounts, as the test's own comment says, and
prove through the API at 1,000:
- publish succeeds;
- a retry with the same Idempotency-Key returns the same publication and
  writes nothing new;
- a route to a private mount and a route to a missing key answer identically
  to a reader who may not see the private one;
- the first, middle, last and absent keys cost the same at 16 and at 1,000.

Update the test's comment to say why 1,000 now works.

## Do not

Change no product source, and keep the 16-mount case. Don't relax any cost or
privacy assertion, and don't seed with raw graph writes; fixtures write
through the API. If publication at 1,000 is refused, don't lower the size:
report the response, the QA ID and the footprint under BLOCKERS. Launch
routes that to kernel.
