## Outcome

The launch surfaces people use most work on a phone and by keyboard alone, at
the quality comparable products set (launch GOAL.md: "on phones and desktops",
"real devices early"):
- Home;
- a Realm's front page, a thread and the composer;
- joining a Realm;
- a Zone's home and browse pages;
- the app shell (navigation, search, notifications, account menu).

Walk each one at 390 px with touch emulation and at 1280 px with keyboard only.
Check signed out and signed in, in English and one CJK locale. Fix what you
find in the claimed features:
- touch targets under 24 px;
- content that overflows or is clipped;
- focus that is lost, trapped or invisible;
- controls that are unreachable without a pointer;
- dialogs that do not return focus;
- layouts that break with long names or CJK text.

Record the walk as `apps/web/tests/launch-phone-keyboard.e2e.ts` on a QA stack
(`task goal -- test --heavy`; a worktree dev server cannot sign in), with
screenshots you have looked at.

## Do not

Do not redesign surfaces, change Main, or touch `features/library/**`,
`features/tracking/**` or `features/work-page/**` (live tasks hold them).
List problems you see there as proposed tasks.

## Checks

```sh
task goal -- test --heavy apps/web/tests/launch-phone-keyboard.e2e.ts
bun node_modules/typescript/bin/tsc --project apps/web/tsconfig.json --noEmit
```

In the handoff, give a table of each problem: surface, viewport, what failed,
the fix, and the screenshot path.
