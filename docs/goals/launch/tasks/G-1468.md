## Outcome

Trust-ops decided (2026-10-08) that realm-admin `realmSettings.visibility`
(public, restricted or private) is authoritative for who may read and join a
Realm. Access's `spaceVisibility` (public or private) is its projection:
private exactly when the Realm is private. G-1457 made the create form offer
the three levels with this help:
- Public: anyone reads and joins.
- Restricted: anyone reads; the founder decides who joins and posts.
- Private: members only.

Manage's Access panel (`apps/web/features/manage/settings-access.tsx`) is
still bound to `spaceVisibility`, can't express Restricted, and its Private
help differs.

The panel edits `realmSettings.visibility` through the existing realm-admin
settings route and command keys, with the same three words and the same help
as the create form, reusing its strings rather than copying them. The panel
doesn't offer `spaceVisibility`: show it read-only or not at all. A Realm
created as Restricted opens in Manage as Restricted. A stale settings write
says so and keeps the choice, as the panel's other settings do.

Strings come in all eight locales, with non-English marked for native review.
Add a unit test that each level round-trips, and update the panel's story at
390 px and 1280 px.
