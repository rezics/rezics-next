   shape from the poster's side.
   - The contribution is recorded under the retained canonical key.
   - The response is the ordinary 201, or 200 on replay, with the poster's own
     Person, reply id and placement.

   - 202 only for a genuinely in-flight operation, with the same body in both
     cases.

     retained.

     exists.

   with disclosure ids `path:realm`, body `about` and `actingSubject`.
