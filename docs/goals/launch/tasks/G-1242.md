## Outcome

A writer marks a post or reply as containing spoilers, and every reader sees
the warning, in every language, because it is data. Today the composer writes
the choice as an English title prefix (`apps/web/features/post-composer/api.ts:64`,
`Spoilers: `), and `features/feed/discussion.ts:12` (`announcesSpoilers`)
guesses it back by regex. A post in Japanese, or a title that starts with that
word, gets the wrong answer (review finding FE-12).

The kernel Goal landed the contract on `main` (K2, 7adef77bc, regenerated
e79a7a829): an optional boolean `spoiler` on `POST /v1/content-publications`,
on reply drafts (member replies and Realm replies), and on reply and Post
reads. Omitting it keeps the existing declaration; `false` clears it. Graph
Posts store `rv:spoiler`; replies keep it in their exact Content revision body
(`services/main/src/modules/realm-reply/content-store.ts`).

## Work

1. The composer sends `spoiler` and leaves the title as the writer typed it.
2. Realm thread and feed responses carry `spoiler`
   (`realm-reply/thread-read.ts`, `thread-contract.ts`), read from the exact
   revision the response already uses. No extra read per item.
3. Feed cards and the Realm thread view show the warning from the field, with
   the localized label the UI already has for review spoilers, and hide the
   body until the reader chooses to reveal it, as reviews do.
4. Delete `announcesSpoilers` and its regex. Search `apps/web/features` for any
   other place where policy lives in a title or label text, and list it in the
   handoff (reviews already carry a boolean; leave them).

No data migration: the product is unreleased, and seeded posts are written
fresh. If `scripts/dev/seed/**` writes a "Spoilers:" title, list it under
OWNER CHANGES rather than editing seeds outside the claim.

## Do not

Do not change `realm-reply/content-store.ts`, Post schemas or
`content-publication/**` (kernel's). Do not add a spoiler scope or position;
it may come later as another optional field.
