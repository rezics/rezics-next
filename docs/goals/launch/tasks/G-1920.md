## Kernel's writer audit (2026-10-11 22:31 UTC), staged at `.temp/ref/kernel-writer-audit.md`

- **No hole from the ordinary writer.** Allocation is in the same Fuseki
  transaction.
- **Slim commands omit the physical RDF batch by design.** The batch is
  reconstructed from receipt custody (\`receipt-custody.ts:868-881,1039-1064\`).
  A consumer that reads delivered batches without custody sees a slim batch as
  missing.
- **The likely cause for Zone browse.** It reads SQL-delivered batches and
  requires the exact next header (\`zone-browse/store.ts:151,188-196\`), so it
  waits for ever on a slim command's batch. Kernel fixed the same omission in
  the Content rebuild preflight by passing \`{ ownerOutbox: env.receiptCustody }\`
  (\`content-publication/rebuild-preflight.ts:42\`).
- **So step 2 must also:**
  - make the Zone browse reader consult receipt custody, so that a slim
    command's batch reconstructable from custody counts as present;
  - include a slim command's custody-reconstructed batch in the regression
    tests, as the late or hole case.
- **The fault log carries:**
  - the requested sequence, the projection checkpoint, the relay epoch and the
    routing epoch;
  - the retention floor, or its explicit absence;
  - the minimum retained, newest committed and newest delivered sequences;
  - the acknowledged relay checkpoint;
  - whether the custody lookup was supplied and found.
