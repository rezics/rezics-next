## Outcome

G-1336 shipped the browser recipe editor. Its screenshots (QA
20261007t235932-0fe6ca) show the editor and the Work page rendering the same
recipe in two ways:
- The editor's list and its preview, which says "How the recipe reads on its
  page", show "1 1/2 cups flour"; the Work page shows "1 ½ cups flour". The
  cook typed "1½".
- The preview lists each step's linked ingredients under the step; the Work
  page doesn't.
- Prep, cook and total time inputs show a bare number with no unit.

One meaning, one representation:
- The preview renders through the same component as the Work page
  (`work-page/types/recipe.tsx`), fed the editor's current state. Don't keep a
  second renderer.
- A quantity is formatted by one shared formatter, used by the editor list,
  the preview and the Work page.
- If the Composition read returns each step's ingredient references, the Work
  page shows them under the step, as the preview does now. If it doesn't,
  the preview stops showing them and you report the gap as a proposed task;
  don't add an API.
- Time inputs show their unit (minutes) visibly and in their accessible name,
  in all eight locales (non-English marked for native review).

## Do not

Change no API and no write path; the store and saves stay as G-1336 left them.
In the journey, only update text expectations that change because of the
shared format. Keep every step and check; the land review compares them.
