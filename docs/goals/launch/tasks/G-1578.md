## Outcome

`/manage/r/{realm}/roles` builds each role's holders from the first page of
`GET /v1/realms/{realm}/members` (20 rows), so in a larger Realm a role shows
no holders or only some. Nothing reads "holders of role X", and nothing caps
roles per Realm. Trust-ops (owner of access) approved this contract; launch
builds it and trust-ops reviews the diff before it lands.

1. **Cap.** At most 32 roles per Realm, as a named constant. Creating a 33rd is
   refused with `RealmAdminLimit`. A Realm already above the cap keeps its roles
   and can't add more. The roles read reports the cap, so the UI can say it.
2. **Holders of a role.** `GET /v1/realms/{realm}/members?role=<id>`:
   - keyset by member id, with the existing `after`/`limit` page shape and the
     existing max limit;
   - one indexed query whose cost depends on `limit`, not Realm size. Add an
     index on the role-assignment table by (realm, role, member) or
     equivalent in migration 1781, and assert the plan or the statement count
     in the test;
   - an unknown role gives an empty page, never a 404 that would tell a
     non-admin roles apart from missing ones;
   - the same visibility rules as the members read: a private, banned or
     hidden member is left out exactly as there.
3. **Preview.** `GET /v1/realms/{realm}/roles` gives each role its first 3
   holders (read 4, and set `moreHolders`):
   - one statement for all roles (lateral or window), bounded by 32 × 4 rows;
   - ordered by member id, so it's deterministic;
   - same visibility rules, and no per-role round trips.
4. **Authority.**
   - The read never reveals a holder to a caller who can't already see that
     member, and holding a role never grants a read by itself.
   - Don't touch grant or permission writes.
5. **Web.**
   - Role cards show the preview from the roles read; drop the page's
     derivation from the members page.
   - "Show all" links to Members filtered by that role, which pages through
     the role filter.
   - Show the cap when a Realm reaches it.
   - Strings in all eight locales, with non-English marked for native review.

Tests in `services/main/tests/realm-role-holders.test.ts`:
- a Realm with 25 members and 3 roles: the preview is right, with `moreHolders`
  true and false;
- the role filter pages across 2 pages;
- the cap refuses the 33rd role;
- a hidden member is left out;
- the statement count stays constant as members grow from 25 to 250.
