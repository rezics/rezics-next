## Outcome

Trust-ops' G-1438 landed the sanction appeal, behind the platform group
`platform:realm-appeals`:
- `POST` and `GET /v1/realms/{realm}/member-receipts/{receiptId}/appeal`
  (`services/main/src/routes/realm-appeals.ts`);
- only the sanctioned person may appeal;
- one open appeal per receipt;
- the member's read never carries the decider's identity;
- the resolution is readable by the member and the Realm's moderators.

Launch builds the member's side in the browser. A member banned from a Realm,
when they open that Realm:
- sees that they are banned, until when or permanently, and the reason as
  recorded;
- can appeal once with a short statement, and sees that it was received;
- later sees the resolution: upheld or reversed, and when.

A second appeal on the same receipt isn't offered. A member who isn't
banned sees nothing of this. Moderators' identities never show.

Find how the member's own ban receipt reaches the page from what the API
already answers. If no read gives a member their own ban receipt and reason,
report the missing read under PROPOSED TASKS with its method, path and
privacy rule, and build the rest against it. Don't change services/.

Strings come in all eight locales, with non-English marked for native review.

Add the journey `apps/web/tests/realm-appeal.e2e.ts`, at 390 px and 1280 px:
- the journey grants `platform:realm-appeals` in its setup;
- a moderator bans a member with a reason;
- the member sees the ban and the reason, appeals, and sees it received;
- a second appeal isn't offered;
- after a resolution through the API, the member sees it.

## Do not

No change to services/ or to the governance case store. No moderator
identity on the member's side.
