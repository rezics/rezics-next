## Outcome

A spoiler is the author's declared `spoiler` field (kernel K2; launch G-1242,
merged 07058a496). G-1242 moved the composer, Realm threads, feed cards and the
discussion list to it. Four surfaces still miss it (G-1242's handoff):

1. **Home feed.** `feedPost` never carries `spoiler`, so a Home card does not
   warn. Copy the boolean in `services/main/src/modules/feed/read.ts` and
   `contract.ts` from the exact body `replyExcerpt` already loads (no extra
   read per item), then remove the runtime cast in
   `apps/web/features/feed/card.tsx`.
2. **Profile contributions** render the excerpt in full: carry `spoiler`
   through `readProfileContributions` in `realm-reply/thread-read.ts` and the
   contributions schema in `routes/realm-reply-threads.ts`, and veil the body
   in `apps/web/features/profile/contributions.tsx` as feed cards do.
3. **Reply composer** cannot mark a reply although reply drafts accept
   `spoiler`: add the same control the post composer has to
   `apps/web/features/feed/reply-composer.tsx`.
4. **Policy in titles.** `scripts/dev/seed/community-plan.ts` still writes a
   `【剧透】` title prefix and its rule tells writers to do so
   (`community-plan.test.ts` expects it); moderator copy teaches "No spoilers in
   titles" (`apps/web/features/manage/fixtures.ts`,
   `manage/rules.stories.tsx`). Seeds set `spoiler: true` with the title as the
   author would write it; the rule text asks writers to mark spoilers, not to
   prefix titles.

## Do not

Do not add a spoiler scope or position, change Post schemas or
`content-publication/**` (kernel's), or detect spoilers from text anywhere.
Every operation keeps its `exposure` declaration.
