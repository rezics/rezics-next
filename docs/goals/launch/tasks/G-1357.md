## Outcome

The non-English strings in these four catalogs read as a native speaker would
write them in de, es, fr, ja, ko, zh-Hans and zh-Hant, not as translations:
- `manage/messages`: moderation decisions and reasons. Mind the `rsn*` keys,
  `reasonKeepTitle` and the duration statements, which must keep their exact
  meaning: a restriction lasts until an authorized moderator changes or
  reverses the decision.
- `catalogue/messages`: shelf and rating words, including `ratingProcessing`
  and `refresh`.
- `showcase-editor/messages` and `showcase-zone-editor/messages`.

The English catalog (`messages.ts` in each feature) is the source of meaning.
Keep every key, placeholder and plural form exactly; change only the wording.
Follow each language's conventions for UI copy: formal or informal register
as the existing catalogs already use it, Japanese and Chinese punctuation,
German capitalization. Remove any "native review" markers you resolve.

## Do not

Do not change English strings, keys, code, or any other feature's catalog.

## Checks

```sh
task i18n:check
task goal -- test apps/web/i18n/catalogs.test.ts apps/web/tests/manage-reasons.test.ts
```

In the handoff, list per language the strings you changed most and why (one
line each), so a native reviewer can spot-check.
