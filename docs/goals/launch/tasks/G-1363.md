## Outcome

A moderation outcome notifies every reporter and party, however many there
are. Trust-ops' G-1353 audit (`.temp/trust-ops/moderation-salvage-findings.md`)
found that `notification-producers/producer.ts` selects the moderation audience
with a limit of 257 and throws above 256. The rollback leaves the source
cursor where it was, so a case with more than 256 reporters plus parties blocks
that producer forever. Disjoint reporters and parties can also pass 256 between
them. The related caps on represented parties, Realm roles and contribution
authors are reachable too.

Page the audience through the existing relationship paging. Commit each bounded
batch with its durable frontier, the same way the broadcast producer does since
G-1230, and advance the event only when the audience is exhausted.

Not this: no truncation, no raised cap, no new queue. The independent
mandatory-party notice jobs already page correctly; leave them.

Counterexamples:
- 600 reporters plus parties are all notified exactly once across ticks;
- a restart mid-audience resumes without duplicates or gaps;
- other producers keep flowing while one large audience pages.
