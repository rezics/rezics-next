## Outcome

Inventory item L21, the recipes kind. It follows the approved pin list (program,
2026-10-11) and lands the fixture with its first journey. Kernel's G-1883 pinned
the texts, on main at `cb94f029c`, in
`tests/fixtures/gutenberg/recipe-extract-pins.json`:
- **The Boston Cooking-School Cook Book** (Gutenberg 65061, the 1910 printing):
  - 20 recipes, 111 ingredient lines, 47 fractions, 2 ranges;
  - one two-stage case, `boston-puff-paste-flour-dough-and-rolling` (flour
    for the dough and for rolling).
- **Artusi, La scienza in cucina** (Gutenberg 59047, Italian): 10 recipes, 62
  ingredient lines.

Each pin gives line ranges into the pulled text (`scripts/fixtures/pull.ts`).

Read `.temp/ref/journey-map.md` first. Copy the books corpus:
- `scripts/load/launch-corpus.ts` (pinned corpus recipe, built once through
  the API, backed up and restored with `scripts/load/corpus-backup-driver.ts`);
- `tests/qa/integration/launch-real-books.test.ts`;
- `apps/web/tests/launch-real-books.e2e.ts` and its preparation.

1. **Build** the 30 recipes once, through the product's recipe import
   (`POST /v1/recipes/{id}/file-imports`, Schema.org).
   - Each extract becomes one Schema.org Recipe: the title as name, each
     ingredient line verbatim as `recipeIngredient`, the method lines as
     instructions, and `inLanguage` (en or it).
   - Let the product parse quantities; the test parses nothing.
   - Add it to `launch-corpus.ts` as a recipes kind next to books.
   - State the build and restore times. Restore, startup and readiness stay
     under 10 minutes.
2. **API proof** (`launch-real-recipes.test.ts`, on the restored corpus):
   - all 30 recipes import, with 111 and 62 ingredient lines kept in order,
     duplicates included;
   - the 47 fractions and 2 ranges are read as quantities;
   - the two-stage puff paste keeps flour as two lines in their stages;
   - the Italian recipes keep `it`;
   - the recipes Zone's browse and search return them, complete across pages.
3. **Browser proof** (`launch-real-recipes.e2e.ts`):
   - search the recipes Zone and open the puff paste: both flour lines,
     the fractions as written, the steps;
   - open an Artusi recipe and see it in Italian under an English UI;
   - browse the Zone across pages.
4. **Registry rows** for recipes, "Work hub and edition choice" and "Catalogue
   browse and search in its Zone": report which of your tests prove each. If a
