## Outcome

The notification a member receives when a Realm bans them names the moderator
and leaves out the reason. The appeal contract (trust-ops' G-1438) withholds the
decider from the member everywhere, so naming them in the notification is a
leak through another channel (trust-ops, 2026-10-09).

The ban notification (and the unban one, if it exists) carries:
- the Realm;
- the action;
- the recorded reason;
- the end, or that it is permanent;
- a link to that Realm's page, where the member can appeal.

It carries no moderator identity: no name, handle, IRI or avatar, in the
stored payload or in any rendered form (web, mail or push). Find where the
producer reads the moderator. If it reads it from a trust-ops module (access
or realm-admin), say which, so trust-ops can stop exposing it at the source.

Add `services/main/tests/ban-notification-privacy.test.ts`:
- produce a ban notification and scan the stored payload and every rendered
  form for the moderator's identity; none appears;
- the reason and the end or permanence appear;
- an unrelated notification type that legitimately names an actor is
  unaffected.

Strings come in all eight locales, with non-English marked for native review.

## Do not

Don't change the appeal or realm-admin contracts, and don't drop the reason.
