## Outcome

Launch's phone and keyboard walk (G-1305, `apps/web/tests/launch-phone-keyboard.e2e.ts`)
could not cover the Realm join dialog or a Zone's own home and browse pages:
- the QA stack's seeded Realm doesn't offer joining;
- the Zone seeds (`g-838-catalogue.ts`, `g-853-records.ts`) fail on a fresh QA
  stack with `platform_closed`, since platform gates (T5) closed groups their
  actors use.

1. Open the closed groups those seeds use for their own actors through the
   shared helper `tests/qa/fixtures/platform-grant.ts` (as G-1264 did for
   other seeds).
2. Add `apps/web/tests/launch-walk-fixtures.ts`: a Realm that offers joining
   and an installed Zone with home and browse pages, created through the API.
3. Extend the walk to cover the join dialog and the Zone's home and browse at
   390 px touch and 1280 px keyboard, signed in and out.
