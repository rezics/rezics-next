## Outcome

A moderator deciding on a reported item in a Realm's moderation queue picks a
structured reason, and can add a note, for every decision, including "keep".
Trust-ops' G-1270 (bounded enforcement notices) made the reports contract
require structured reasons for decisions that notify, and the web adapter
refuses incomplete drafts (its compatibility edit is in
`apps/web/features/manage/commands.ts`). Today the dialog
(`command-dialog.tsx`, `queue-detail.tsx`) supplies only a free reason and
note, so those decisions cannot be sent.

Read the reports contract's reason schema from the generated SDK. Offer its
reasons in the decision dialog in the moderator's UI language. Make "keep" a
first-class decision with its own reasons. Show the reason the affected person
will see before sending. Moderation must be fast and keyboard-first: comparable
tools (Reddit and Discord mod queues) take one or two keystrokes per decision.
Follow their need, not their layout.
