## Outcome

In the franchise wiki journey (`apps/web/tests/g-856-wiki.e2e.ts`, QA
20261008t035012-3331c2), signed-in character rows show the generic
"Relationship" where the reviewed label is "Sister" ("姉妹" in Japanese)
(`apps/web/tests/journeys/franchise-wiki.ts:334`). The wiki seed stores the
relation presentations as drafts, and the signed-in page renders the
participants with an empty label.

A relation's label is its reviewed presentation, in the reader's language
with the usual fallback. A draft is never shown as reviewed. Find why the
reviewed label isn't selected for a signed-in reader: whether the read picks
the draft, misses the reviewed one, or the seed never reviews it. Fix it at
its source:
- If the API read is wrong, fix `relations.ts`, with a unit test.
- If the seed never creates a reviewed presentation, the seed must review it
  through the API. `apps/web/tests/g-856-wiki-seed.ts` is unclaimed; list it
  under OWNER CHANGES.
The anonymous reader and the signed-in reader see the same reviewed label.

## Do not

Don't edit the journey's assertions, and don't show drafts as reviewed.
