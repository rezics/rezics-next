## Outcome

A Zone's `defaultRealm` must be in the Zone's own Space
(`services/main/src/modules/zone/configuration.ts`, the ASK on
`rv:space ${head.space}`). Launch and trust-ops agreed (2026-10-08) how a
Zone attaches a Realm from another Space. Build the first slice, the
attachment itself:
- Attaching needs `zone.edit` on `zone:edit:<zone>`, as today, and
  `realm.attach` on `realm:attach:<realm>`, held by the Realm's stewards the
  same way the existing realm-admin permissions are. A Realm in the Zone's
  own Space keeps today's rule.
- The `realm.attach` check runs inside the same register and claim admission
  that `zone.edit` already uses (configuration.ts, `access.register` and
  `access.claim`), so revocation and expiry are judged in one place. If the
  access API can't judge two authorities in one admission without changing
  `services/main/src/modules/access/` beyond the baseline hunk below, stop
  and report what it lacks under BLOCKERS.
- The attachment is a revocable grant recorded on the Zone, never a copy of
  authority. Either side withdraws it at once: a steward revokes the Realm's
  grant, or a Zone editor removes the Realm. After either, every read of the
  Zone (configuration, published site, routes) behaves as if no Realm were
  attached, with no stale link. A publish binds no attachment whose grant is
  gone.
- A Realm the actor can't see is refused exactly as a missing one ("default
  Realm is unavailable"). A Realm someone else owns is never disclosed by the
  refusal.
- Reads stay bounded: honouring the grant costs a constant number of checks
  per Zone read, whatever the number of Zones or attachments.

Trust-ops loans launch one hunk in `services/main/src/modules/access/baseline.ts`,
which adds `realm.attach` (scope `realm:attach:<realm>`) beside the
realm-admin permissions. Change nothing else in that file. Put the hunk's exact
diff in the handoff under OWNER CHANGES; trust-ops reviews it before landing.

Tests, with data written through the API (unit, plus integration on the real
stack):
- attaching across Spaces with both authorities succeeds;
- attaching without `realm.attach`, and attaching an invisible Realm, are
  refused with the same answer as a missing Realm;
- revocation by a steward and removal by a Zone editor each end the
  attachment on every read;
- a grant revoked or expired between register and claim is refused;
- a publish after revocation binds no attachment;
- the Zone read's Realm check costs the same with 1 and with 50 attached
  Zones.
