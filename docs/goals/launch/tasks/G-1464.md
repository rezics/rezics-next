## Outcome

Trust-ops' G-1454 traced main's `NestedPoolCheckoutError` worker faults to
`services/main/src/modules/library/backfill.ts:117`. QA
20261008t092836-2d8d73 showed 98 of them in two minutes, and they may be why
a published recipe page's "Explore the wiki" failed to load.
`prepareLibraryShelves` (started from `services/main/src/index.ts:632`) holds
a long-lived `content.connect()`. The pool's nested-checkout guard attributes
that hold to the async context it was opened in, the content projection
worker's, so any other checkout in that context looks nested and faults.

Isolate that long-lived connection in its own `AsyncResource`, the way
`services/main/src/modules/notification/realtime.ts:55` does for its
listener. Keep the pool size, the guard and the backfill's behaviour as they
are: the advisory lock, the deadline, `logWorkerFault` on a row error, and
the release on every path.

Add `services/main/tests/library-backfill-checkout.test.ts`. While the
backfill holds its connection, a checkout from the context that started it
is not reported as nested, and a real nested checkout inside the backfill's
own resource still is.

## Do not

Don't change the guard, the pool size or `index.ts`. The content service's
own long-lived checkouts (`services/content/src/projection-cursor.ts:34` and
`core.ts:374`) are kernel's; report under PROPOSED TASKS if you see the same
pattern there.
