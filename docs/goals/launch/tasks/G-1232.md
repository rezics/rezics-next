## Outcome

Reading a person's public shelves and writing reading progress cost the same
for a person with 30 books and one with 300,000. Today:

- public shelf totals traverse every status row, check publication and hydrate
  cards on the first page (`library/public.ts:32–46,52–53,68`; exact totals
  required by `routes/library.ts:60–67`);
- every public read hashes the person's whole library as a fence
  (`library/status.ts:304–314`, called at `public.ts:16,25`);
- the visible-page refill is unbounded (`library/shelf-page.ts:62–79`);
- every progress write and replay computes `MAX(updated_at)` over the person's
  rows for that Structure with no supporting index (`progress/store.ts:66–72,115,168`),
  and the latest-state read uses `DISTINCT ON` over all matching states
  (`library/status.ts:292–296`).

Evidence: `/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/goal-launch/scale-showcase-evidence.md`, "Task C" items 3 and 4. Binding criterion: bounded
neighbourhood per operation; `LIMIT` or a timeout that bounds output but not
work is not a fix.

## Decisions already made

The maintainer's default (2026-10-07) lets community surfaces lag when they
show their freshness. So a public shelf total may be maintained
asynchronously and lag, or be shown as partial ("N+") while a resumable count
continues; it must never count a hidden or unpublished Work in a way that
reveals it. Choose the smaller of the two and say why. Replace the whole-library
fence with a per-person revision. Index progress as
`(principal_issuer, principal_subject, structure, updated_at DESC, occurrence DESC, selection_key)`
or equivalent and express latest and maximum as top-one seeks; the batch latest
read becomes bounded per-Structure lateral seeks.

Do not build a new progress owner, a latest-state pipeline, an analytics
rebuild, a count table for every shelf kind unless the chosen design needs it,
SQL `COUNT(*)` over the shelf, or larger timeouts.
