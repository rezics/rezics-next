## Outcome

Kernel's recipe publication without notes (its G-1802 specification, implemented
by G-1809, now on main) provides the generic, explicit publication of a selected
Structure; see `services/main/src/routes/publication.ts` and the work
publication commits. The specification is staged at
`.temp/ref/G-1802-specification.md`. This task is its launch slices 7 and 8, as
one chain with two resumes, landing once.

The decided behaviour (launch and program, decision 7), staged at
`.temp/ref/recipe-publish-decision.md`:
- **API:** two explicit steps, publishing the Structure and publishing the
  notes, each with its own exact revision and idempotency. There is no
  transaction across them.
- **GUI:** one Publish button. It runs the Structure step, then the notes step
  only when there are notes. Results read part by part:
  - "Recipe published";
  - "Notes published";
  - pending, with the durable operation shown and resumed;
  - refused, with the reason and a retry for that part only.
  The editor never says "published" for a part that is refused or pending. A
  recipe without notes publishes in one step and shows no notes line. Withdraw
  is per part. "Changes not published" appears when the draft is newer than
  the published revision.

1. **Step 1 of 2, specification slice 7: the editor.**
   - Remove mandatory-notes validation.
   - Replace every `Boolean(notes.publicationHead)` proxy with the server's
     Work publication state, read back after success and after a reload.
   - Retain the retry key and acting subject.
   - Never show a false Published state for pending, stale, revoked or partial
     outcomes, and keep the entered data on failure.
   - Proofs: the slice's tests and stories, including a no-notes story and
     server-status, retained key, queued save, session expiry and partial
     outcome. Review the story PNGs.
2. **Step 2 of 2, specification slice 8: the browser journey.** Extend
   `recipe-authoring.e2e.ts`:
   - an own Recipe with no notes, denied privately before publishing;
   - an explicit publish, then a fresh anonymous context and a reload;
   - a private draft edit, then a republish, with the public state confirmed by
     the server.
   Register RECIPE07's browser evidence in `scripts/qa/cases/recipes.ts` and
   `scripts/qa/coverage/recipe.ts`. Do not edit `launch-coverage.ts`; report
   the titles and the manager records them.

Do only step 1 now. Read `.temp/ref/journey-map.md` before step 2.
