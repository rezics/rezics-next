## Outcome

Class A (launch analysis G-1856, staged at `.temp/ref/g1856-nested-reply.md`).
A reply to a Realm-origin reply always fails.
- `mainThreadApi.reply` (`apps/web/features/feed/thread-api.ts:89`) drafts the
  child without `originRealm`, so its author provenance is null.
- The identity check requires the same origin as the parent
  (`services/main/src/modules/realm-reply/content-store.ts:259`), so the result
  is a guaranteed 403.
- It also reads the child's digest through the public
  `GET /v1/member-replies/{reply}` (line 119). Once the origin is set, that
  read would 404 before placement, the same defect G-1833 fixed in the
  first-post composer.

Implement the analysis's section 4:
1. Obtain the parent's authoritative `originRealm` before drafting and keep it
   in `ReplyProgress` across retries.
   - An independent parent keeps null.
   - Send the recorded origin through the draft helper and the withdrawal.
2. Keep `data.revisionDigest` with `data.revisionId` from the draft response,
   and remove the child public digest GET.
   - Reuse G-1833's approach, now on main: same-key draft replay when saved
     progress lacks a digest, plus a revision equality check.
3. Keep retry payloads immutable.
   - An older null-origin draft answering a Realm-origin parent needs
     withdrawal and a fresh intent; it cannot be fixed under its old key.
   - A definitive refusal before placement keeps the words and clears the
     rejected intent.
4. Tests:
   - new `apps/web/tests/thread-api.test.ts`, covering:
     - Realm-origin and independent parents;
     - an unavailable parent;
     - no child GET;
     - the digest kept across lost responses;
     - legacy progress;
     - refusal, then retry after an unban;
   - update the draft-response mocks in `document-replies.test.ts` and
     `reply-spoiler.test.ts`;
   - owner coverage for matching and mismatched parent origins in
     `services/main/tests/realm-reply-write-cost.test.ts`.

Change no Main product code. A review-revoked parent racing a placement is a
separate known gap; leave it.

QA economy (program, 2026-10-11): one `task goal -- test a b c` call for all files a step needs (one stack, not one per file); prove at the lowest tier that proves it (unit/owner needs no stack; integration only across a service boundary); rerun a file only after a change that could affect it, never "to be sure"; heavy non-test commands (web build, depcruise, type-aware lint, yarn install) one at a time and not during your own QA run.

## Refusal rules after the second land review (2026-10-11 18:00 UTC)

Every branch of `mainThreadApi.reply` follows one rule, and the worker's
handoff tables each branch:
- A **terminal refusal** clears the saved intent and keeps the words editable,
  so the next Post starts a fresh key.
- A **lost or pending response, or a 5xx**, keeps the key and the exact
  payload.
- A **409** is terminal only for `stale_head`, `content_conflict`,
  `realm_reply_stale` and `idempotency_conflict`. Busy admissions, a changed
  read basis, unknown conflicts, read-budget failures and authentication
  failures keep the intent.
- A replay revision mismatch clears the inconsistent intent without placing it.
- A read denial on the parent lookup does not reject a write key.

Legacy progress with no `revisionId` is recovered with its original
null-origin payload under its original key, then withdrawn, before a fresh
Realm intent.
