## Outcome

Class A: the launch half of the imported-cover rights obligation. Kernel's cover
spec is staged at `.temp/ref/kernel-g1800-covers-spec.md`; do its slices 9 and 10.

Kernel's G-1831 (on main) gives every Main cover read a typed `coverCredit`:
`{ kind: 'copyright', text, holder, source: { name, href } }`.
- The holder is present only when evidenced, and no licence is claimed.
- Ordinary covers carry null.
- A hidden or fallback cover carries no credit.
- The shape is in `services/main/src/modules/media/cover-credit.ts`.
- The API also marks cover-only provenance.

Program's decision (2026-10-11): an imported cover is identifying art in the
Work's avatar slot.
- Web shows the credit beside the image.
- A cover is never a showcase background, ambient copy, crop or fallback.
- A framed own-Work card in a carousel is fine.

1. **Slice 9, the credit.**
   - Carry `coverCredit` through media metadata and the selected-cover props.
   - Extend `rights-mark` with a cover-credit renderer, separate from
     fact and text obligations.
   - Render a compact, accessible credit next to every imported identifying
     cover: compact cards, headers, and server-rendered output.
   - Never put it inside an aria-hidden Work link, and never nest the source
     link inside the Work link.
   - Invent no holder or licence text. A hidden or generated fallback renders
     no mark.
   - Localize in all eight locales.
2. **Slice 10, the showcase.** Use the API's cover-only provenance marker, never
   a provider name or URL heuristic.
   - An imported cover never supplies showcase ambient art, a background or a
     preload (`showcase/slide.tsx:39–52,194–212`).
   - Independent showcase art still displays.
   - A framed own-Work cover card stays.
3. **Tests and stories,** as slices 9 and 10 list them:
   - metadata round-trip;
   - known and unknown holder;
   - every locale, RTL and mixed scripts, long names;
   - a valid source link, no fake licence;
   - hidden and generated fallback;
   - the named case "imported cover never supplies showcase ambient art or
     background preload".
   Add cover cases to the catalogue and rights-mark stories, and review the
   changed stories' screenshots.

The Zone site cover type (`packages/zone-sdk`) is not in this task; launch's
games chain carries it. Change no Main code.
