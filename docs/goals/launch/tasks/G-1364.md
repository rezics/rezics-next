## Outcome

Following a topic (Concept) gives it a Home tab with that topic's public items,
newest first, seek-paged, as before saved views closed. The approved launch
matrix keeps saved filters closed, but promised a public server-selected read
for this. Kernel's C5 delivered it: the template
`https://rezics.com/query/followed-concept-feed`, revision 1, with a `roots`
parameter, served through `POST /v1/query` (top-level `limit`/`cursor`,
default 20 and max 64, `template-query-v1`).

Build the tab from the follows list plus that template read. The saved-filter
tabs stay closed (G-1236's gate). A followed topic shows its tab for signed-in
people without any platform grant.

Do not reopen saved filters, fake a tab from another operation, or change the
API.
