## Outcome

Class A (program, 2026-10-11). A returning member signing in on a second device
was sent to onboarding instead of the page they asked for. In
`apps/web/app/auth/callback/route.ts:55-63`:
- the callback selects the session Agent;
- when that selection fails transiently and `ensureOnboarding` answers
  `unavailable`, `onboardingDestination(unavailable, selected=false)` treats
  the member as new and redirects to `/onboarding`.

"Unknown" is being decided as "new".

Fix: when the onboarding outcome is `unavailable`, redirect to `next`, whether
or not the Agent selection succeeded. The requested page already handles a
missing Agent, and onboarding is offered again when the state is known.
`name-required` still goes to onboarding. An `active` first visit still goes to
welcome and onboarding as today.

Prove it in `apps/web/tests/onboarding-flow.test.ts` with `onboardingDestination`
and the callback's destination choice:
- for a returning member and for a new member, with `active` (first visit and
  later), `name-required` and `unavailable`;
- with the Agent selection succeeding and failing.

Change nothing else.
