## Outcome

Read-only, about 30 minutes; change no code. The program (2026-10-12 03:53 UTC)
classes the missing "retire or delete a Realm" command from two facts. Main has
no such command. A Zone can be retired and recovered; a Realm cannot. Answer
both with file and line:

1. **Can a platform operator today take a whole Realm out of public reach**,
   through a moderation decision, a rights or notice effect, a platform grant
   or closure, or Access (scope gates, representation)?
   - Name the exact operation and what it changes.
   - Say what stays visible afterwards: the Realm's home, its threads, its
     replies, its members list, its Zones attached to it, search and feed
     entries, and links into it.
   - Give each one's answer for an anonymous reader and for a member.
2. **What happens to a Realm when its owner (the Person or Account that
   created it, or its only steward) is erased** through the erasure lanes?
   - Does the Realm stay public, become ownerless, transfer, or disappear?
   - What happens to other members' replies inside it?
   - Name the erasure journal kinds and owners involved.

End with one line: "platform can take a Realm down: yes/no (operation)", and one
line: "owner erasure leaves the Realm: …". The program decides A or B from them.
