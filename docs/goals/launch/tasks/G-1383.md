## Outcome

Some integration fixtures write raw graph facts and then create populated
lists through the API. The raw write invalidates native membership
completion, so the populated list is refused. Kernel's read-only audit found
three such boundaries. G-1380 (23d7ea59f) and kernel's 4c98d4bf7 fixed two
others the same way.
- `feed-home.test.ts`: raw Book at 558, then a 2-entry list at 589, then an
  append at 648/649.
- `library-status.test.ts`: final raw observation repair at 411, then a
  chapter at 436, then an anthology insert at 520 on the populated root.
- `g-937-support.ts`: `addressFixture.work` raw `catalogueVisible` at 61,
  then the g-937 Zone mounts at 94/98.

After the last raw write and before the API list writes, await
`normalizeStoredMembership(stack.env)` and assert it reports complete. Keep
every assertion and the native invalidation. No runtime producer change and
no blanket raw-update wrapper.

Also take `feed-home.test.ts` to the end. Its later step, a reader recording
progress on a later chapter, gets 404 `occurrence_unavailable` because the
seed leaves that chapter Work private. The product is right: a reader can't
record progress on a chapter they can't read. Seed the chapter readable the
way an author really publishes it, through the API. If the API has no way to
do that, name the defect under BLOCKERS.

Never weaken an assertion, and never add retries or reloads.
