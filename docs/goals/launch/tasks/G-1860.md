## Outcome

Class A (program, 2026-10-11). After a Realm reply's placement succeeds, the
composer can still show a generic failure with "Try again" while the reply
projection catches up. A retry then succeeds idempotently, but the person was
told their post failed when it had not.

G-1833 (on main) made the composer keep the draft's `revisionDigest` and
recover refused attempts. Build on that:
1. When the placement call answered success (its receipt is held), any later
   read of the placed reply that is not yet visible means "posted, appearing
   shortly", not a failure.
   - Keep the person's words cleared as for success.
   - Offer no "Try again".
   - Poll or refresh the discussion within the existing bounded read; no new
     endpoint.
2. A placement that did not succeed keeps today's behaviour: retry with the
   same key, or a fresh intent after a terminal refusal.
3. Localized messages in all eight locales. Adapter or component tests:
   - success, then a projection that is not yet visible, shows the message,
     then the reply;
   - a placement that failed still offers a retry.
   Add a story for the new state.

Change no Main code.
