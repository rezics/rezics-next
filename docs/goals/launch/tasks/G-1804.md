## Land review 1 (2026-10-11 21:15 UTC): fix all four

`site-route.tsx` and `site-pages.tsx` are unclaimed and shared with G-1808, which
also changes them (the Zone discussion entry). Edit them under `--allow-scope`.
Whichever task lands second rebases and keeps both changes. Then:
1. **P1, the Showcase on published home pages.** `site-route.tsx:207` bypasses
   `SiteHomeRoute` whenever a published document exists, and
   `ZoneHomeDocument`'s `DocumentBody` renders Showcase extensions as fallback
   text. A published home page, and its preview, must render the Showcase and
   modules as `SiteHomeRoute` does.
2. **P1, preview for a new Zone.** `manage/z/[space]/preview/page.tsx:44`
   rejects an absent presentation, which is the normal state of a new Zone. Use
   `DEFAULT_ZONE_PRESENTATION`, as the showcase-editor preview does. The
   browser fixture must cover a Zone with no saved presentation, without
   installing a baseline first.
3. **P2, saved navigation in preview.** Preview takes the saved presentation's
   navigation, not the public read's.
4. **P2, unsaved edits when storage fails.** The draft provider keeps draft
   values itself, not only in `sessionStorage`. A storage failure or full quota
   must not lose edits on a section change. Otherwise, add a save/discard guard.
Add a test for each, and rerun `zone-site-authoring.e2e.ts` once with the unit
tests and stories. Review the story PNGs and the journey screenshots.

## Land review 2 (2026-10-11 22:20 UTC): a third blocking review ends the fix rounds

   \`complete=false\` and the continuation cursor from \`loadZoneNavigation\`.
   Follow the continuation, or show that the result is incomplete; never
   silently drop mounts.
