## Outcome

The first main-wide browser run (program, 2026-10-11) stopped in preparation:
the scoped-subjects seed's `POST /v1/semantic/changes` (journey
`…:scoped:v2:resource:match`, from
`tests/qa/integration/scoped-subjects-support.ts:83`) answers
`403 platform_closed`. The route is declared `public`, so the refusal comes
from a template-level platform gate (kernel's gates on query, graph, export and
generic edits, `requireSelectedPlatformCapability`). Because of this one step,
341 of 346 browser files never ran. The log is staged at
`.temp/ref/e2e-scoped-subjects-seed.log`.

Decide and fix, in this order:
1. Name the platform group and operation that refuses the seed's change, and
   what the scoped-subjects journey (`scoped-subjects-journey.e2e.ts`) proves
   for a person.
2. If the journey proves a first-public-scope function, such as a Zone or
   catalogue page reading its own continuity, the seeding principal receives
   that group's grant the way other seeds do: through the API, with the
   recorded platform grant fixture (`tests/qa/fixtures/platform-grant.ts`,
   `grantRecordedPlatformUse`). Never open the group for everyone, and never
   write with SQL.
3. If the journey proves a capability that is closed until later, do not grant
   anything. Keep the journey, skip it while its group is closed with the
   reason in the skip, and keep the seed from running in the launch plan.
4. Run the journey's preparation and the journey itself.

Put the decision and the group name first in your RESULT.

## Decisions since this brief (manager, 2026-10-11)

- The journey proves first-scope functions: catalogue ratings, saved reading positions, and continuity-aware catalogue and Zone reads.
- Its seed writes fixture data through operations in three closed groups: `events` (a `schema:Event` subject), the lexicon's `platform-admin`, and the Zone's `executable-themes` (a first-party theme activation).
- The seed's own principals receive those grants through the API with an administrator session (`grantPlatformUse`). No group opens for anyone else, and the fixture's QA stack keeps groups closed so the grants are proved.
- This task lands the preparation fix. With it, the browser tier can prepare.
- The journey still fails after preparation: Work reads answer 503 (`DisclosureUnavailable` in `main.route.work_read_unavailable`). That is a separate defect, analysed in its own task. This task does not have to make the journey pass.
