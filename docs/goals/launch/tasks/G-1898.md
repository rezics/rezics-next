## Outcome

A visual review of stories that four landed launch tasks changed without
seeing them rendered, because workers had no story screenshots until 20:00 UTC:
- the composer's "posted, appearing shortly" state;
- the Work and Entity discussion spoiler veil;
- the Realm roles list after saving;
- recipe exchange (export, import and their refusals).

1. Run in one call:
   `task goal -- test apps/web/features/post-composer/composer.stories.tsx apps/web/features/work-page/discussion.stories.tsx apps/web/features/manage/roles.stories.tsx apps/web/features/recipe-editor/recipe-exchange.stories.tsx`.
   It leaves one PNG per story under `.artifacts/qa/<run>/story-screenshots/`.
2. Look at every PNG. Check:
   - clipping or overflow;
   - text that overlaps or is cut at 390 px;
   - a veil or status that is unreadable or has poor contrast;
   - controls without visible focus or labels;
   - layout that breaks in CJK or RTL where a story renders them;
   - anything that looks unfinished next to its neighbours.
   If these stories lack a CJK or RTL locale or a 390 px variant for the changed
   state, add one to the story file.
3. Fix what is wrong in the component's own file, one commit per fix, then
   rerun only the changed story files once. If a fix is larger than about 30
   lines or needs another feature's file, report it under PROPOSED TASKS
   instead.
4. In your handoff, list every PNG you looked at and what you found in each:
   fine, or the defect and its fix.

QA economy (program, 2026-10-11): every stack you start makes the whole host wait. Stack startups take turns across the host (about half a minute each), and runs queue behind them.
- One `task goal -- test a b c` call for all the files a step needs: one stack, not one per file.
- Prove it at the lowest tier that can: unit and owner tests need no stack; integration only when the proof crosses a service boundary.
- Rerun a file only after a change that could affect it, never "to be sure".
- Run heavy non-test commands (web build, depcruise, type-aware lint, yarn install) one at a time, and never during your own QA run.
- Browser journeys: read `.temp/ref/journey-map.md` first. It names the sign-in and seed helpers, the closest existing journey, the selectors, the routes and how to read a failure. If what you need is not in it, say so in your handoff instead of searching widely.
- Fixtures grant access only through `grantFixtureAuthority` (services/main/src/modules/access/fixture-authority.ts). A new file that writes the access tables directly fails the repository guard (scripts/static/access-fixture-writes.test.ts), comments included.
- Stories: `task goal -- test <story file>` saves one PNG per story at `.artifacts/qa/<run>/story-screenshots/…`. Review your changed stories there by eye, and name in your handoff the PNGs you looked at. No screenshot helpers in stories, and no "partial, needs visual review" handoffs.
