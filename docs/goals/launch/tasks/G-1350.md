## Outcome

When a read fails, the person is told the truth. Today a Realm's discussion
list says "Couldn't load the discussions. Check your connection, then try
again" even when the server failed with a 500. Their connection was fine, and
retrying won't help (G-1305's walk,
`.temp/worktrees/g-1305/.artifacts/qa/20261007t083537-de11f1`).

`features/feed/types.ts` already classifies read failures (`ReadFailure`,
`failureOf`, `settle`, now with `closed`). Use that classification in the
error states:
- **offline or network failure:** "check your connection";
- **server error:** "Something went wrong on our side", with a retry and a
  short reference if the response carries one;
- **closed:** absent, per G-1236;
- **not found and denied:** their own wording.

Fix the Realm states first, then every other surface that hard-codes the
connection sentence. Search `apps/web/features` and list any you leave in the
handoff. Strings go in the eight locales; mark non-English for native review.
