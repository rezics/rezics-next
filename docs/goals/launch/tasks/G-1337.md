## Outcome

The Bangumi-like launch kind's core job works in the browser. A viewer marks
"watched episode 7" (or "read chapter 1000"), sees where they are in a series
with its specials, and resumes the same episode on another device.

Kernel's K8a (G-1245) made episodes, chapters and specials Structure
occurrences: a series Structure with main episodes plus a specials group, items
typed `schema:Episode` with `episodeNumber`. Chapter and episode progress
(GET/PUT) and episode resume are proven at the API. The tracking panel
(`series-progress-panel.tsx`, `series-states.tsx`) predates that. Make it show
the series structure and let the viewer:
- mark the next episode watched in one tap;
- jump to any episode by number;
- tell specials and main episodes apart;
- see "continue from episode 8".

A second device shows the same position after reload. Comparable product:
Bangumi's collection progress grid. Follow the need, not its layout. It must
work on a phone.

## Do not

No anime, manga or game progress stores, no API change; hand off if the API
lacks something. Edition paging (G-1292) is merged; keep it.
