## Outcome

Since trust-ops' T5, closed platform groups refuse callers without a grant
(403 `platform_closed`). Browser-journey seeds that use a closed group as
their own fixture principal must open it for that principal, the way
`apps/web/tests/platform-grant.ts` (G-1256) opens saved views for the web
member. G-1256 found three:

1. `apps/web/tests/g-849-records.ts:288` (also 290, 294, 297): theme writes
   need `platform:use:executable-themes` for the seed's own principals.
2. `apps/web/tests/g-530-seed.ts:71` (also 75, 79, 80), spawned from
   `g-530-m4.e2e.ts:48`: discovery generation needs `platform:platform-admin`
   for the seed author.
3. `apps/web/tests/library-import.e2e.ts:28` POSTs the catalogue-import route
   and expects 401; the exposure gate now answers 403 `platform_closed` first.
   Decide from the approved matrix (catalogue import is closed at launch)
   whether the assertion becomes "closed for an ungranted caller" or the test
   grants the importer group and keeps checking authentication.

Extend `platform-grant.ts` so a seed can open any group for a named principal.
Line numbers may have moved; find each call by content.

## Do not

Do not grant groups to everyone, change product code or the matrix, or delete
assertions. The QA stack's missing first platform administrator is the
program's harness to fix; if the helper cannot grant without it, say so with
the exact error.
