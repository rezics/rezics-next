## Outcome

L18, class A (program, 2026-10-12 05:22 UTC). The maintainer accepts the
launch by hand at 15:10 UTC. A navigation entry, button or link that leads to
a platform-closed operation, for a person without the grant, fails that
acceptance.

**Step 1 (now, read-only, about one hour):** sweep `apps/web`.
- Find every navigation entry (header, menus, tabs, sidebars, Zone and Realm
  navigation, home cards, empty-state calls to action) and every action
  (buttons, forms, links to editors or composers).
- Map each one to the Main operation it reads or writes. The exposures are in
  `generated/openapi/main/exposure.ts`: `public`, or `platform:<family>` for
  closed ones. The web reads platform access through its existing
  platform-access client (find it from
  `services/main/tests/platform-exposure.test.ts` and the web's
  `platform-access` usage).
- List every entry or action that is visible or enabled for a signed-out
  person, or a signed-in person without the grant, while its operation is
  `platform:*`, with file and line, the operation, and what the person sees.
- List also every entry the web hides that the matrix makes public (a hidden
  open feature is a defect too).
- Propose the fix for each, as one rule if possible: a shared gate component
  or hook keyed by the operation, never per-page booleans.

Change nothing in step 1. The manager reports the list to the program. Step 2
fixes it, by 12:00 UTC.
