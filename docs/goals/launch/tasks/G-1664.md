## Outcome

WIKI05 ("Realm media serves its accepted exact set…") fails on main with 13 >
2. At line 169 it compares every Fuseki query of one byte delivery with
`REALM_MEDIA_COST.servingGraphReads` (2, kernel's constant for the Realm's own
reads). A trace (launch analysis, 2026-10-11) counted the 13 foreground graph
reads of one delivery at this fixed topology (one asset, one attachment, one
Realm):
- 2: the Realm's own reads (`publicationRoutes()` admission/lineage ASK plus the
  exact selection query);
- 4: route `discloseInventory()` (attachment summary read, two name-owner
  probes, Work head resolution);
- 2: `disclosureContent()` (Work head resolution, erased-revision tombstone
  query);
- 5: `disclosureMedia()` (attachment summary read, two name-owner probes, Work
  head resolution, delivered target name-owner probe).

Trust-ops, which owns the disclosure and media audience reads, confirmed that 13
is the correct count of the required probes (2026-10-11).

Change the assertion to the exact itemised count:
`REALM_MEDIA_COST.servingGraphReads` plus a test-local, named breakdown of the 11
composed probes (4 + 2 + 5), checked with `toBe`, not `<=`. Add a short comment:
- the media audience read still grows with an asset's retained attachments;
- that is a known limit the owner recorded;
- this test pins only the fixed topology.

Keep the exact-set, stale URL, unaccepted Use, replaced selection and private
asset assertions unchanged. Do not change `REALM_MEDIA_COST` or any product
file.
