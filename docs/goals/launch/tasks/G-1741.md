## Outcome

Analysis only. Do not change code. Your RESULT is a specification of slices,
each fitting one implementation session.

Inventory item L17 (class A): a site author edits a Zone's pages, navigation
and theme in one journey, including:
- an unpublished preview;
- a revoked editor;
- a lost publish response;
- retirement and recovery.

A Zone without a Realm must work the same way.

What exists:
- an author writes, previews and publishes a Zone home page as a Content
  document, and a second tab keeps a stale draft
  (`apps/web/tests/zone-site-authoring.e2e.ts`);
- published-page membership is decided from the head and its pin;
- Zone routing is proven at 1,000 mounts;
- `zone-document-home.test.ts` covers Realm-less binding, the lost publish
  acknowledgement and revocation after the Content pin.

The coverage registry (`scripts/qa/cases/launch-coverage.ts`) still lacks:
- the API proof for "zone / Navigation and theme";
- the browser proofs for "Navigation and theme", "A Zone without a Realm" and
  "Publish after editor revocation".

Specify:
1. What a person cannot yet do: compare the Zone editor
   (`apps/web/features/zone-editor/**`) and the Zone configuration operations
   with the outcome above, step by step.
2. Theme: which theme editing is in the first public scope and which is closed.
   The `/v1/themes` routes are `platform:executable-themes`, a closed group. Say
   what a site author edits (tokens or a first-party theme choice) without
   opening that group, or that the scope needs a decision.
3. The slices in order, each with files, the change and the tests (API and
   browser) that prove it, filling the registry rows above.
4. Anything another Goal must provide, with the exact ask.
