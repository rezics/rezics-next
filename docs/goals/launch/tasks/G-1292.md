## Outcome

A reader choosing which edition or release they own or read can reach all of
them, not just the first twenty. `apps/web/features/tracking/api.ts`
(`editions`, around line 97) reads 20 realizations and 20 releases, then turns
their cursors into a Boolean `more`. `tracking-sheet.tsx` (around line 88) only
says "more editions", with no way to see them (launch product audit item 11).
VNDB and Bangumi users routinely pick one release among dozens.

Keep the two `nextCursor`s from the API (which already pages) and let the
sheet load the next page of each on demand ("show more editions"). Also offer
the search or filter the sheet already uses where present. Loaded pages keep
their order and are never duplicated.

## Do not

Do not raise the page size, fetch every page up front, or change the API.
