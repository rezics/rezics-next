## Outcome

G-1439 landed cross-Space attachment through the API, approved by
trust-ops:
- a Zone editor sets another Space's Realm as `defaultRealm` when the actor
  holds both `zone.edit` and `realm.attach`;
- a current steward withdraws it with
  `POST /v1/zones/{id}/realm-attachment-withdrawals`;
- an official Zone takes only its own Space's Realm.

A steward can withdraw only an attachment they can find, and today nothing
lists them. No browser surface does either side.

1. **A steward's list.** A read of the Zones attached to a Realm, for
   people who hold `realm.attach` on it, and no one else. Anyone else gets
   the same answer as a missing Realm.
   - Paged by keyset with a constant cost per page, whatever the number of
     attachments; nothing scans all Zones.
   - Each row gives the Zone's name and address, when it was attached, and a
     withdraw action.
   - It declares its `exposure` and its rate-limit family beside its
     neighbours. Send the method and path for trust-ops' review in the
     handoff.
   - If keyset paging needs an index that doesn't exist, report it under
     PROPOSED TASKS with its write cost. Don't add a migration.
2. **Manage, Realm side.** The list, with withdraw, in the Realm's Manage
   area.
3. **Zone editor side**, beside G-1461's navigation editing. The author
   picks a Realm by its handle to attach it. The steward check happens
   server-side; a refusal reads as "not available", with no further detail.
   The editor shows the attached Realm and a remove action.

Strings come in all eight locales, with non-English marked for native
review.

Tests:
- Unit and integration, beside the existing attachment tests: the list's
  disclosure (a steward sees it; a non-steward and an anonymous reader get
  the missing answer), paging past page one, and equal cost at 1 and 50
  attachments.
- Journey `apps/web/tests/zone-realm-attachment.e2e.ts`, at 390 px and
  1280 px: an editor who is also a steward of a Realm in another Space
  attaches it; the steward's list shows the Zone; the steward withdraws it;
  the Zone's editor no longer shows it.
