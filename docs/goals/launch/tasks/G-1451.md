## Outcome

The program's bisect (G-1428) shows
`services/main/tests/g-1014-structure-cost.test.ts` "G1014: ordinal
aggregation sums prior segments once per segment rather than once per
chapter" failing on main since launch's d1b5a7b63 ("Continue bounded reading
windows past hidden items and recover eligibility"). The wider hidden-item
reading window made that seek cost more than its model allows, once per
chapter instead of once per segment. That's an algorithm regression: fix the
algorithm, not the budget.

and restore per-segment cost while keeping what d1b5a7b63 fixed. That is: a
window continues past hidden items, eligibility recovers, and no ordinal
counts a placement the reader can't see. Check G-1426's `around` and
`firstSeen` lookups (merged before this) for the same pattern.
