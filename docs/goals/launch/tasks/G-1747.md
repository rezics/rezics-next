  recipes, VN, Bangumi anime, manga and games):

    `scripts/load/corpus-backup-driver.ts`;

  - never production sources (external condition C-24);

    editions of one Work, mixed formats, non-UI languages, thousand-chapter or
    thousand-episode inventories.

Keep it to sizes and slice lists; no code and no full contracts.
