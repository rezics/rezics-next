## Outcome

Launch's G-1365 (75474eed6, d1b5a7b63) bounded private resume with a
prepared series order index. Kernel's G-1300 found that it regressed the
original G847 continuity test (`tests/qa/integration/g-847-reading-position.test.ts`,
signed `position=mine`): expected `[early, unchanged]`, got `[unchanged]`.

The fixture is a series of volume Works. Each volume has a book Composition
of chapters, and the reader's position is a chapter inside volume 1. The
chooser reads only the series' prepared index, so a position on a nested
part returns `ReadingResumeUnavailable`, and everything introduced before it
disappears.

A position anywhere in the series' continuity must resolve:
- to the volume's place in the series order and the part's place inside that
  volume;
- for resume, for progress, and for spoiler-safe disclosure (introduced at or
  before the position is visible; after it is not).

Asymptotic bound: each request touches a bounded neighbourhood. That means
the part's enclosing member found through a bounded ancestor read, that
member's entry in the prepared series index, and the part's ordinal from
that volume's own order. Never walk the series or every volume. A nesting
deeper than the supported bound is an explicit, typed refusal, not
"unavailable" read as completion.

One meaning, one representation: compare positions with one continuity key
(series order, then order within the member) used by resume, progress and
disclosure alike.
