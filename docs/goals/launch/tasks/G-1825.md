## Outcome

Inventory item L21, books slice (program, 2026-10-11): real-data fixtures, built
once through the API and restored, landing with their first journey. The
sizing is staged at `.temp/ref/g-1747-sizing.md` ("L21", slices 1–2).

Kernel pinned the books records (G-1761), with hashes and licence lines in
`tests/fixtures/fixtures.lock.json`; readers are in `tests/fixtures/sources/`:
- the Open Library edition set for work OL66554W (*Pride and Prejudice*): a
  translation, two publishers, a revised or annotated edition, an ebook and an
  audiobook (CC0);
- a 250-record Open Library library slice;
- six Wikidata labels.

The non-UI-language Gutenberg texts and the Pepys serial come later (kernel's
G-1762) and are not part of this session.

1. `scripts/load/launch-corpus.ts`: build the books corpus once through Main's
   public API from the pinned records, using the existing catalogue intake,
   release and Content operations and existing seed helpers, never SQL. Then
   retain it with the shared corpus backup driver
   (`scripts/load/corpus-backup-driver.ts`, as
   `tests/qa/integration/profile-corpus-restore.test.ts` does) and restore
   isolated copies.
   - Report the source digests and the build and restore times. Restore,
     startup and readiness stay under 10 minutes.
   - Import only what the cases need, with the count beside each.
2. First journey, on a restored copy: a reader opens the OL66554W Work, sees its
   real editions and formats, chooses one edition, and the choice persists
   after a reload. Prove it with the same case in both:
   - API: `tests/qa/integration/launch-real-books.test.ts`, the exact edition
     persisted and mixed formats listed;
   - browser: `apps/web/tests/launch-real-books.e2e.ts`, by keyboard, at phone
     and desktop width.
3. Report both exact titles. The manager records them in the coverage registry:
   "books / Choose among ambiguous editions of one Work" (now gap) and the books
   mixed-formats row.

Use only the pinned files, with no network and no covers. If the product
cannot show or persist an edition choice, report the step under BLOCKERS rather
than changing product code.
