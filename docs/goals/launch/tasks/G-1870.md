## Outcome

Class A (launch and kernel agreed, 2026-10-11). The analysis is staged at
`.temp/ref/g1865-ranking-time.md`, section "A: ordinary writes interrupt
established Realm reads".

`rankings/realm-threads.ts:158` admits a first Best/Top page only under global
quiescence:
- exact global Content and Main relay positions;
- no unindexed reply anywhere in the epoch;
- no dirty job anywhere.

An ordinary reply advances Content's position, and ranking maintenance runs
asynchronously about every second. So a reader who arrives between any write
and its projection gets 503 `RealmRankingsIncomplete` in every Realm. Thread
details and continuations share the gate (`thread-store.ts:72,85`,
`thread-read.ts:355,492`).

The list of other globally gated reads the program asked for is launch's
G-1904 (read-only, separate).

The fix is one change, because placement and admission are coupled:
1. **Placement settles its own index.** Before acknowledging a placement
   (`RealmReplyStore.place` and `placeAdmitted`, `store.ts:302,378`), settle
   the exact `(realm, reply)` index delta, root aggregate, order and revision.
   - Its cost stays constant: no global drain inside a reply command, and no
     unrelated inventory read.
2. **Readers admit on their own Realm's completeness.** Best/Top and thread
   details admit on the Realm's own publication completeness, not global
   quiescence.

Kernel's constraints (`rankings/realm-threads.ts` is kernel's area; this task
edits it with `--allow-area`, and kernel reviews its diff before landing):
- Read kernel's latest change to that file first (`4634362af`, on main).
- Positions keep their comparison domain. Admit on the Realm's own completeness
  in stream positions (Main and Content scoped coverage). Never compare a
  relay-derived checkpoint with the graph diagnostic. Keep the Main and Content
  checkpoints distinct.
- The scoped fence still refuses:
  - this Realm's relevant pending effects;
  - an unindexed placement in this Realm;
  - a revision mismatch;
  - recovery.
  The refusals `realm-ranking-stream-position` and `realm-thread-continuation`
  pin stay.
- Do not just drop the checks: checkpoints advance before dirty effects finish.

Proof, in the new `realm-ranking-scoped-admission.test.ts` and the existing
tests:
- After establishment, a write to Realm B, then a read of Realm A: 200 for A,
  for Best/Top and for thread details.
- A read of B immediately after its own placement: the placed reply, or a typed
  incomplete for B only.
- One placement's statements and graph requests stay equal at two inventory
  sizes (`assertWorkCostAtScales` or the shared meter).
- Recovery and revision refusals still hold.

**Kernel's design (2026-10-11 21:49, on main):**
`docs/goals/kernel/reports/gated-reads-design.md`, family 1b, staged at
`.temp/ref/gated-reads-design.md`. It is the same rule as your four constraints,
narrowed:
- Best/Top admit on a consistent cut keyed by (data epoch, Realm, population,
  sort, period, published revision);
- thread reads admit on (Realm, root/parent, population);
- additions still awaiting projection are simply absent and never close
  admission;
- removal effects are registered at the authoritative transition, before
  acknowledgement, not discovered later through replay;
- an affected item is excluded before ordering, pagination and counts.
  Otherwise the read returns a typed incomplete for the affected scope only.
Keep population initialization, private-history admission and period
expiration. Run the report's one-line acceptance check, and name it in your
handoff.
