and a signed-in reader sees and saves only their own exact edition choice.

and paperback/ebook/audiobook choices. The manager read all three six-persona
matrices on main: visitors compare public releases but cannot persist a choice;
every signed-in persona controls only their own Person, including the operator.
Original fixture metadata retains its credited owner; no external media is added.

are `EditionsSection` and `YourEdition`. Share the smallest page/reader seam
needed for the story to run the production mapping in `books-edition-page.tsx`;
do not recreate the whole Work frame or replace readers with story doubles.

Stories show loading and populated, then empty and refusal only where an
unchanged recorded answer proves them. Cover the visitor sign-in invitation,
same-title releases distinguished by exact identity, the three formats, and a
signed-in reader's recorded saved preference and resulting progress summary.
