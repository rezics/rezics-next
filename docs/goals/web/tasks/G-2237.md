## Standing production-reader rule

a story runs the production reader with the replaying client. Where a production reader builds its own client, it takes the client as a parameter instead (the request-cached host client in production, the replaying client in a story); its behaviour in production does not change, and the failure adapter keeps the recorded problem's information. A story file holds no reader of its own, and a story shows no state its production route cannot reach from a recorded answer: a refusal or an empty state without a record is removed from the stories and named as a record gap, not drawn.
