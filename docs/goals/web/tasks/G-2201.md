## Outcome

Two tests of the web that failed in the regression run of 2026-10-13 on `main` pass (D080 and D129 in `.temp/ref/regression-web.md`), each for its cause.

1. D080: the about-discussion copy shows an internal service name. `apps/web/tests/g-900-service-names.test.ts:91` reports `web/zones/about-discussion-messages en: Content in Content language`. Reword the message in every UI locale that has it so that a reader sees no service name; the test's list of names is not changed.
2. D129: `apps/web/zones/official/ai-workshop/slots.tsx` imports outside the boundary an official Zone may use (React, the Zone SDK, the UI package, icons, its own files); `apps/web/tests/zone.test.ts:472` names it. Bring the import inside the boundary; if the Zone needs something the SDK does not offer, hand off `partial` naming it and do not widen the boundary.

Do not build: a change to either test's rule, a change under `packages/` or `services/`.
