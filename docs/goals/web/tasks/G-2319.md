## Outcome

The Manga row's visitor cell promises title, format, status and direct year
Conditions. A year supplied in the browse address remains the same API
Condition through every link, continuation and search submission. It appears
among the active Conditions and the person can remove it without losing the
other Conditions. Clearing Conditions removes it too.

Program decided this in mail:100e05b4-074e-4793-8277-74455a7dcc92.
Read `apps/web/features/zones/browse-state.ts` and its consumer
`browse-view.ts`, the six-persona matrix at
`scripts/qa/cases/outcomes/manga/catalogue-browse-and-search-in-its-zone.ts`,
and the existing API contract for `publicationYear`. Make only the smallest
shared-state/model correction: `BrowseState`, `parseBrowseState`,
`mainBrowseQuery` and `browseHref` preserve the API's admitted year value and
range (including `1995` and `1995-2001`). Keep exact values, opaque cursors,
other filters and production API validation; do not infer a year from records.
The browse model keeps the year in its form and links, counts it as an active
Condition, and supplies its removal link through the existing chosen-Condition
renderer. Use the existing localization and removal wording.

The presentation's `official: null` means the Zone declares no year control.
The page offers no new year picker or facet, but honours a year in its address.
This is shared browse-state behavior, not a new catalogue engine. Do not change
API/schema, records, routes, the catalogue year directory, frame readers,
Manga stories, or other files. Report any necessary additional claim before
editing it. G-2229 stays exited until this correction and G-2318's records are
on main; this task does not resume it.
