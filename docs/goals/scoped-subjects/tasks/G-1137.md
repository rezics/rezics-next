## Outcome

Second review, finding 16 (`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/manager/scoped-subjects/g1127-review.md`).
`GET /v1/projections` and `POST /v1/graph/queries` (profile
`statement-graph-v1`) take no reading position. Here is how that leaks: a
participant is visible at the reader's position, but their participation
Statement is revealed later. The event page still finds them through incoming
claims, so the ranking spoils the participation.

- Both operations accept an optional `position` (the same value and meaning as
  other position-aware reads: `mine` by default, `all`, or an occurrence).
- They filter through the existing `ReadingBoundary` and add no per-item
  queries. Each pays its revelation cost per batch, as other reads do.
- A claim or projection whose Statement, subject or frame is not revealed at
  that position is left out, exactly as if it were absent.
- `event-participants.tsx` passes the page's position to both calls.
- Integration test: a participation revealed at chapter 3 is absent at
  chapter 2 and present at chapter 3, for both operations.
