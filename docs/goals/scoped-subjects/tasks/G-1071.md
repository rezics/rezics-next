## Outcome

Make "X in F" readable and keep facts in one place, per
[decision 51](../../../contracts/semantic-model.md#identities-variants-and-projections)
and [decision 52](../../../contracts/context.md#scope-and-evaluator). G-1065
merged projections (`services/main/src/modules/projection/`, `covers` in
`dimension.ts`); G-1067 merged credited names and the `variant-of`,
`holds-title`, `represents` and `in-continuity` definitions.

1. **One home for facts.** A Statement write whose subject is a Projection is
   stored as a Statement about its subject with the projection's frames added to
   its applicability (refuse with a typed error if the union exceeds eight). A
   relation participation that names a Projection is refused with a typed error
   that points to applicability: projections are not identities.
2. **Frame filter.** `GET /v1/resources/{id}/statements` and
   `GET /v1/resources/{id}/relations` accept up to eight `frame` coordinates and
   return only records whose applicability covers them (`covers`; empty
   applicability covers everything), each marked with how specifically it
   matched. Without `frame` the reads are unchanged. Extend `covers` so a frame
   naming a Work is covered by applicability naming a narrative continuity the
   Work is `in-continuity` of, read once per request with a bound.
3. **Projection page.** `GET /v1/resources/{projection}/page` returns a header
   with the subject's and frames' summaries, the subject's Statements and
   relation occurrences covered by the frames (most specific first, the reader's
   position applied as on the subject's own page), and the projection's own
   ratings, reviews and discussion sections (they stay empty until scoped
   judgments land). Replace G-1065's empty `baseSections.projection`.
4. **Canonicity and seeds.** Seed a `canonicity` property definition whose value
   is a Concept from a seeded scheme (canon, legends, semi-canon, non-canon),
   applicability naming the continuity and the speaker being the authority; who
   counts is each Context's acceptance, so add no policy engine. Wire G-1067's
   `seedVariantKindConcepts` and the canonicity scheme into bootstrap and the
   dev seed.

Smallest change that satisfies the decisions. Do not build: a general
inference or rule engine, containment beyond position ⊂ Work ⊂ continuity,
caches, new storage, Context policy machinery, or UI.
