## Outcome

Express the identity outcomes of [decision 51](../../../contracts/semantic-model.md#identities-variants-and-projections)
as lexicon data on the existing relation-occurrence path, with the two small
kernel additions they need.

1. **Credited names.** A participation may record the name credited in that
   occurrence (Saber Alter credited as "Saber" in one route; Shinichi credited
   as "Edogawa Conan"): `relation-occurrence-v2` adds an optional
   language-tagged `rv:creditedName` per participation, leaving v1 untouched.
   The relation command, read, `GET /v1/resources/{id}/relations` traversal and
   export carry it; v1 occurrences read as having none.
2. **Star constraint as definition metadata.** A relation definition may declare
   `star: { leaf, hub }` naming two of its roles: a participant holds the leaf
   role in at most one active occurrence of that definition, a hub participant
   never holds the leaf role and a leaf never holds the hub role. The relation
   command enforces it under the same guarded write, with a typed refusal.
   Definition state, its change command and its read carry the field; nothing
   else in the semantic module changes.
3. **Seeded definitions** (meaning, both viewing directions, labels in the eight
   UI locales, through the existing lexicon seed):
   - `variant-of`: roles `variant` (leaf), `hub` and `kind`, whose participant
     is one of two seeded Concepts, `persona` (a transformed other self, such as
     an Alter) or `counterpart` (another universe's or timeline's individual who
     can coexist); declares `star: { leaf: variant, hub: hub }`.
   - `holds-title`: roles `holder` and `title` (an `rv:Title`), repeatable, with
     applicability for the Works or continuities in which it holds.
   - `represents`: roles `unit` (an `rv:GameUnit`) and `character`; a unit may
     represent several characters.
   - `in-continuity`: roles `work` and `continuity` (an
     `rv:NarrativeContinuity`); a Work may be in several.
   The `rv:Title`, `rv:GameUnit` and `rv:NarrativeContinuity` types are added by
   G-1065 in the type registry; if it has not merged, reference them by IRI and
   note it.

Do not build: participant class constraints, qualifier declarations, a variant
module, inference over variant links, merge or split of Characters, or UI.
