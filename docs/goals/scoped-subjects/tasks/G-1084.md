## Outcome

A RatingContext's question is authored once, in its own language, and that
authored text with its Context identity is the question's meaning (changing the
question creates another Context, per the rating owner README). People reading
in other languages need the question in their language. Treat translations as
presentation, as the relation lexicon does for definitions
([semantic model](../../../contracts/semantic-model.md#relation-lexicon),
[vocabulary labels](../../../contracts/content-languages.md#vocabulary-labels-are-graph-content)):

1. **Profile.** `rating-question-presentation-v1`, modelled on
   `definition-presentation-v1`: one current presentation per (RatingContext,
   language) with its own revision chain, the question text in that BCP 47
   language, review status (draft or reviewed), source and licence. A
   presentation revision never changes the Context, its manifest or its
   observations.
2. **Writes.** The Context's owner (the Realm's rating managers, or platform
   administrators for Global Contexts) creates or revises a presentation and
   marks it reviewed; drafting and reviewing are separate permissions as in the
   lexicon. Any BCP 47 language is accepted; the authored language cannot be
   overwritten by a presentation.
3. **Reads.** Context reads and resource rating-context listings return the
   authored question with its language plus the question selected for the
   reader's languages through the shared display-language selection
   (`services/main/src/modules/display-language/select.ts`), reporting the
   language actually used, review status and fallback provenance. Script is
   preserved (`zh-Hant` never silently becomes Simplified).

Smallest change: no new aggregation, no migration, no change to Context or
observation profiles, no UI. Do not edit `rollup-*` files (another task holds
them).
