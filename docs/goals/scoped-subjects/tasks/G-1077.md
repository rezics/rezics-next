## Outcome

People can rate, review and discuss "this character in this episode" or "this
player on this map", see honest per-frame numbers, and read a franchise in the
continuity they choose ([decisions 51–53](../../../contracts/semantic-model.md#identities-variants-and-projections)).
On `main`: `POST /v1/projections` (get-or-create) and `GET /v1/projections?subject=`;
`GET /v1/resources/{projection}/page`; `frame` filters on resource statements and
relations; projection-grain and Global target questions with accepted subject
types and frame dimensions; target aggregates with thresholds;
`POST /v1/rating-rollups`. The dev seed `scoped-subjects` (G-1076) holds the
eight acceptance cases on the shared stack.

1. **Projection page** at the ordinary entity route: a header that names the
   subject (avatar, name, link) and its frames as localized chips formatted from
   the summary's structured parts (never concatenated server strings), the
   covered facts most specific first with spoilers gated, then ratings, reviews
   and discussion for the projection.
2. **Rate in a frame.** From a Character's or Person's page, and from an
   episode, chapter, match or map, a person picks the frame (pickers limited to
   types with a frame dimension), the client gets or creates the projection and
   shows the questions that accept it; rating, reviewing and discussing work
   there and on any subject's own Global questions (Characters can be rated
   without joining a Realm). Writes follow the existing idempotency and
   revision-head pattern with retry on conflict.
3. **Per-frame numbers.** A subject's page lists its projections with their own
   figures. A Work or season view shows a subject's per-episode figures and a
   roll-up labelled by its formula ("pooled" or "average of episodes") with
   coverage; an event or match view ranks participants by the published
   weighted rating, listing only eligible ones and saying why others are not.
   Below-threshold numbers follow G-1072's honest display.
4. **Continuity switch.** On entity and wiki pages a labelled control filters
   facts and relations to a narrative continuity or Work (`frame`), off by
   default; the franchise wiki package can name a default continuity that
   readers can change, and the choice persists across its routes like the
   reading position.
5. Eight UI locales; stories for each new surface in its empty, spoiler-hidden,
   below-threshold and populated states, phone and desktop.

Reuse `packages/ui` primitives, the entity-page sections, `EntityPicker` in a
`Sheet`, the rating distribution components and the wiki `PositionBar` pattern.
Do not change APIs; list any gap under OWNER CHANGES.
