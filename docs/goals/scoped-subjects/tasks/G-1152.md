## Outcome

Main-wide regression class. Under load, Storybook stories fail when they click
a combobox or listbox option while the popup's entrance transition still has
`pointer-events: none`. Seen on: `zones/g-1061-position` "English Phone"
(showcase fixed it locally in c804afc69), `showcase-zone-editor` "Add
Campaign Art Tablet", `work-page/shelf-words` "Game", and home "Pin A Topic"
(G-1149 added a local wait).

1. Add one shared play helper under `apps/web/features/stories/` that every
   story can import, for example `chooseOption(scope, name)`. It finds the
   option by role and accessible name, waits until the option and its popup
   accept pointer events and are no longer animating, then clicks it. Give it
   a short doc comment saying why.
2. Move every story under `apps/web/features/**` that clicks an option onto
   the helper. Replace the local waits in g-1061-position and home-page.
   Stories that only *assert* options, without clicking, stay as they are.
   Showcase has agreed to the edits in `apps/web/features/zones/**` stories;
   use `task goal -- reclaim --allow-area` if the claim check stops you. Make
   story-only changes, no product code.
3. Add the ast-grep rule `scripts/static/ast-grep/rules/story-option-click.yml`
   with tests. It covers `*.stories.tsx` under `apps/web/features` and
   refuses a `userEvent.click` (or `.dblclick`) whose target is found by
   role `option`, pointing to the helper. `packages/ui` component stories
   that test the primitive itself are out of scope.

## Checks

`task ast-grep -- test` and `task ast-grep -- scan` (clean). `task web:typecheck`.
`task storybook:test` on every changed story file, three times in a row, all
passing.
