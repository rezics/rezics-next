## Outcome

Exports keep the meaning of scoped judgments outside REZICS, as
[decision 53](../../../contracts/ratings.md#scoped-targets-and-roll-ups) states.
Extend the existing export owner (`services/main/src/modules/export/`, owner
readers in `readers.ts`) and the personal library export, not a new export path:

1. **Projection member.** An owner reader exports a projection as a resource
   with `prov:specializationOf` its subject and each frame coordinate with its
   dimension, from the exact projection revision.
2. **Aggregate member.** An owner reader exports a target's rating aggregate
   per RatingContext as a `dqv:QualityMeasurement` (`dqv:computedOn` the target,
   `dqv:isMeasurementOf` the RatingContext's question as a metric), carrying
   count, histogram, display threshold and the mean only where it is displayed;
   a roll-up exports its formula, members and coverage. Mark imported or
   derived values as such; never export a withheld mean.
3. **Own ratings in the personal export.** A person's own target ratings export
   as Web Annotations with motivation `assessing`, target the rated Resource
   IRI (a projection carries its `prov:specializationOf` subject and frames),
   body a rating value with its scale and the RatingContext's question. Only the
   owning principal receives them, through the existing personal export
   authorization.

Record residuals the way the other readers do for anything that cannot be
expressed. Do not build a new route, a JSON-LD framework, content negotiation
on reads or new storage.

## Read

`services/main/src/modules/export/README.md` and `readers.ts`; the library
export bundle; the projection and rating module READMEs; Web Annotation
(https://www.w3.org/TR/annotation-model/) and DQV
(https://www.w3.org/TR/vocab-dqv/) for the terms.

## Checks

`task main:typecheck` through the slot; unit tests for the three mappings;
integration tests alone: a projection export names its subject and frames; an
aggregate below threshold exports no mean; a personal export contains the
owner's projection rating as an annotation and nobody else's; existing export
integration files still pass. `task ast-grep -- scan`.
