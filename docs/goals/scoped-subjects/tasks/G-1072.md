## Outcome

A character's page shows who it is, which versions exist and how people rate
each, without inventing numbers ([decisions 51 and 53](../../../contracts/semantic-model.md#identities-variants-and-projections)).
The APIs exist on `main`: relations carry `creditedName` and the
`variant-of`, `holds-title`, `represents` and `in-continuity` definitions
(read through `GET /v1/resources/{id}/relations` and the lexicon); target
rating aggregates return `displayThreshold`, `meanDisplay` and a null `mean`
below the threshold, with count and histogram always present.

1. **Honest scores everywhere a target aggregate shows.** When the mean is
   withheld, show the count, the histogram and how many more ratings reveal the
   mean; never show 0 or a placeholder star value. Update the e2e expectations in
   `g-841-q05-reviews` and `g-914-acceptance` that assumed a mean from one rating.
2. **Variant family.** On a Character that is a hub or a variant, a section lists
   the hub and every variant with its kind (persona or counterpart), avatar and
   its own rating figures side by side, with no family mean. A variant's page
   names its hub. Variant links respect the reader's spoiler position like other
   relations.
3. **Units and titles.** A unit (`rv:GameUnit`) page shows the Characters it
   represents; a Character lists its units with their own figures. A title
   (`rv:Title`) page lists its holders with the Works or continuities in which
   they hold it; a holder lists its titles.
4. **Credited names.** Appearance and relation rows show the credited name when
   it differs from the participant's display name ("Saber Alter as Saber").
5. All new strings in the eight UI locales; stories for each new section in its
   empty, spoiler-hidden, below-threshold and populated states, phone and
   desktop.

Use the existing entity-page sections, `RelationRows`, `RatingInline` and the
rating distribution components rather than new primitives; the design system is
in `packages/ui`. Do not build projection pages, scoped rating entry, roll-up
views or the continuity switch (a later task), and do not change APIs: list any
API gap under OWNER CHANGES.
