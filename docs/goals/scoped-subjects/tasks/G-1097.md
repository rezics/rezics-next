## Outcome

People reach and use scoped judgments from the pages they already visit
([decisions 51–53](../../../contracts/semantic-model.md#identities-variants-and-projections)).
On `main`: G-1072's identity sections and honest scores on entity pages;
G-1077's components in `apps/web/features/scoped-rating/` and the continuity
switch in `apps/web/features/wiki/continuity*`; projection pages with real
ratings, reviews and discussion sections; one slot per Work and per edition in
frames (G-1090); the caller's own target rating read (G-1096); the dev seed
`scoped-subjects` with the eight acceptance cases.

1. **One honest-score implementation.** Fold `scoped-rating/score.ts` and its
   local histogram into G-1072's components (or the reverse), so every surface
   uses one set of rules and one component.
2. **Character and Person pages:** a subject's projections with their own
   figures, "Rate in an episode / match" opening the frame picker, and the
   subject's own Global question (rateable without a Realm). Re-rating on a new
   device works: seed the rating form from the `own` field of
   `GET /v1/resources/{id}/ratings?scope=mine&context=…`, and on a 409
   `stale_head` retry once with the `currentHead` the problem body returns;
   drop the localStorage head memory G-1077 added as a workaround.
3. **Projection pages:** the projection header, covered facts most specific first
   with spoilers gated, then the projection's ratings, reviews and discussion.
4. **Event pages:** participants ranked by the published weighted rating with
   eligibility explained; a Work or season view of a subject's per-episode
   figures with the formula-labelled roll-up and coverage.
5. **Wiki and entity pages:** the continuity switch beside the reading position,
   off by default, the franchise wiki package able to name a default
   continuity, reads passing `frame`.
6. Eight locales; stories updated for the composed sections.
