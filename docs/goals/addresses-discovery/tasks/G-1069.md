## Outcome

`tests/qa/fault-recovery/g-744-safety.test.ts` SAFETY04 fails alone on main
("Expected: held, Received: cleared"). The manager bisected it to `59629091b`
(2026-10-03, "Separate image NSFW labels, concealment and age presentation"):
it passes at `59629091b^` and fails from `59629091b` on. That commit deliberately
moved image classification to the client and replaced
`media.delivery_clearance` (Content migration 741), and it updated the owner
document, `docs/operations/trust-and-safety.md`:

> A client classifier (NSFWJS) supplies NSFW presentation evidence tied to the
> exact image. It neither establishes prohibited content nor grants clearance
> or creates a review hold. … An NSFW classifier outage permits manual
> labeling and does not create a governance hold. Required safety-matching
> outages follow their own admission policy. Actual review holds and removals
> remain explicit platform decisions.

The acceptance case (`scripts/qa/cases/launch-safety.ts`, SAFETY04: "Image
scanner is unavailable → New uploads are held, not delivered…") and its test
were not updated, so they now contradict the owner document.

Align SAFETY04 with the owner document; the document is the authority, so
change neither it nor product code:

- An NSFW classifier outage leaves the presentation evidence unknown, permits
  manual labeling and creates no governance hold, and the upload is delivered
  under the viewer's presentation preferences.
- If "required safety-matching outages follow their own admission policy" is
  implemented, cover it under SAFETY04 as the scanner case that still holds.
  If it is not implemented, say so in the handoff with evidence; do not build
  it.
- Keep the attributable, recoverable parts of the case that still apply.

Update `backend-operations.ts` only as far as the case mapping requires. Do
not add new `G-NNN` mentions or new `g-NNN` files.
