## Outcome

Read `.temp/ref/G-1522.md`, finding 10.

`scripts/datasets/network.ts` and `scripts/fixtures/pull.ts` each implement
request metadata, digest-addressed blobs, pacing and Retry-After handling.

1. One paced fetch (pacing, 429 and Retry-After, bounded retries) and one
   verified immutable-byte store (write by digest, re-verify on read, refuse
   corrupted bytes) in `scripts/lib/paced-fetch.ts`. Both callers use them and
   delete their copies.
2. Keep the two retention contracts apart: dataset snapshots keep the full raw
   external capture (`scripts/datasets/snapshot.ts`); fixtures replay committed
   normalized bytes offline. Neither importer replaces the other.

Acceptance (unit, no network):
- corrupted cached bytes are refused and refetched (dataset) or reported (offline fixture replay);
- a 429 with Retry-After waits as told and stays within the retry bound;
- a restart reuses verified bytes;
- offline fixture replay makes no request.
