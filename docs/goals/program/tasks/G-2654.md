## Outcome

Structured publication (`POST /v1/work-publications`, `work-structure-publication-v1`) admits only the `rv:OriginalContribution` rights basis. As a result, the Gutenberg recipe corpus, which is public-domain text copied from Project Gutenberg, is published as "original contribution". That claims authorship we do not have.

The cause is native admission: `infra/jena/command-module/src/main/java/com/rezics/jena/CommandService.java` pins the v1 decision, source and Work validation focuses (around lines 1555, 1562 and 1616) and requires `rv:OriginalContribution` (around line 1681). G-2209 stopped on this on 2026-10-14. Its analysis is `.temp/ref/g2209-handoff.md` and `.temp/ref/prerequisite.md`, including the exact request envelope.

Deliver both:

1. **Admission:** native admission accepts a public-domain basis for structured publication when it is bound to a source observation of the copied text. Keep every fixed binding: Work, Main Version, Structure, receipt, origin, and the authority to publish. An original contribution still needs no source. A public-domain claim without a source observation is refused. Cover both in `WorkStructurePublicationGuardTest.java`.
2. **Truthful corpus:** the Gutenberg recipe import publishes its recipes as public domain, bound to their Gutenberg source. The public reader then shows the source and its licence, as G-2623 made the hubs and exports do on wave/3. The public reader is G-2623's one open surface.

Prove it through the API for every persona in `tests/qa/integration/outcomes/recipes/work-hub-and-reading.test.ts` and `tests/qa/integration/sourced-structure-publication.test.ts`. G-2209 retained two authority refusals in `c93ea6d9a5`, which is not on wave/3; cherry-pick them. Prove withdrawal and replay too. Do not weaken any binding.
