## Outcome

A read-only task, such as a review, triage or audit, writes its result to `.temp/goal/<name>.md` in its worktree and commits nothing. On 2026-10-15 the settlement closed two such tasks as verified at handoff (G-2626, a review with two verified findings, and G-2593, an eleven-task triage table) and removed their worktrees. Both results were lost, and only the handoff summaries survived on `archive/goals`.

Before any close removes a worktree (verified, cancelled or settled), copy the worker's `.temp/goal/*.md` and `.temp/goal/*.json` outputs, excluding `brief.md`, into `.temp/goal-orchestration/runs/<id>/outputs/`. Also archive them with the handoff on `archive/goals`. Links in the handoff to `.temp/goal/<name>.md` then still resolve; rewrite them to the kept copy.

Prove it with a test: a task whose worktree has `.temp/goal/review.md` and no commits closes as verified, and the review survives in both places.
