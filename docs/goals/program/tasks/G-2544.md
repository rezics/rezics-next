## Outcome

The maintainer forwarded an analysis of the Recipe model and then corrected its scope. Both are in `.temp/ref/content-model-handoff.md` in your worktree (the updated report). Read it first. The concern is **general**: distinguish a field's or relationship's **semantic role** from the **form** of its value or referenced content. Content form, derivation by a human or AI Agent, and references within that content are separate dimensions, and they apply to any field where appropriate.

- A resource's body, for example a Recipe's instructions, is a common case, not the boundary. Its form may be a structured procedure, a Post, audio, video, or a combination.
- Not every field accepts every form. Decide the admitted forms of each field from its meaning and the operations it must support: a quantity that is scaled needs an exact value, while a note may be text or audio.
- *Derivation* (organised, transcribed, summarised or structured from which source revision or fragment, by which Agent) is its own relationship. A derived result has its own identity and history, and derivation implies no equivalence or authority.
- *References within content* (a Post mentioning the tomato resource, instructions pointing at an intermediate preparation's own recipe, a resource's wiki) belong to the content model and resource relationships, never inside a domain object.
- Source wording stays with its content or source owner, linked at an exact revision.
- No universal wrapper or runtime, and no scope expansion.

Today's custom Recipe structures mix domain facts, extraction artifacts, relationships and storage, and leak Recipe semantics into shared structure and command layers. No documented mature-product research backs their selection. Recipe is the concrete case; other domains are not yet audited.

Write `docs/architecture/content-adoption.md`, a design note for the supermanager's review. It answers:

1. **The general boundary: role against form.** Across the repository's existing content and resource models (Content, Posts, compositions, works and versions, the semantic graph, wiki), which owners already let a field or relationship take a value whose form is content (and which forms), and which already express derivation and in-content references? Sample fields beyond Recipe, in at least two other domains in the repository, to test the boundary. Prefer those, schema.org (`Recipe`, `HowTo*`, `QuantitativeValue`, `PropertyValue`) and SHACL constraints. Propose no new universal runtime or composition mechanism; where something is missing, name the smallest addition and its owner. Read `docs/architecture/target-architecture.md`, `docs/contracts/semantic-model.md`, `docs/contracts/model-profiles.md` and `docs/contracts/source-lifecycle.md`.
2. **Field-by-field classification.** For every custom Recipe field and structure listed in the handoff (IngredientLine, RecipeStep, RecipeMeasure, RecipeComposition, RecipeImport and Imported*, RecipeSourceConversion, ScaledIngredient, the work page), classify each part by semantic role (domain fact, content payload, derivation, in-content reference, source or extraction metadata, storage or operation detail), and state which value forms its meaning and operations admit. Give the standard term or existing owner that fits. List where Recipe semantics enter shared layers and what would replace each.
3. **Mature products, observed.** For recipes, compare at least Mealie, Tandoor, Paprika and NYT Cooking or Allrecipes. For the same boundary in other domains, compare iFixit (repair guides) and protocols.io (experimental protocols). Record what each actually models and what its real import or export format preserves or loses: instructions as video, audio, Post or steps; linked sub-preparations; ingredient entities against occurrences; scaling; nutrition. Cite primary sources (the products' documentation, schemas, export files). Map each capability to: A, configuration of the general model; B, a new capability; C, out of scope.
4. **Behaviours that must stay.** List the current Recipe behaviours users rely on (display, scaling, nutrition aggregation, substitution, import and export, bounded work page), with concrete counterexamples that would falsify a replacement.
5. **Migration and launch.** Recipes are in the first public scope. Estimate what moving to the general model costs: code removed and added, data migration of existing Recipe content, effect on the open wave/1 Recipe rows. Recommend whether to change the model before Recipes open publicly or after, with the reason.

Write no product code. Measurements or scripts the note needs stay under `.temp/`.
