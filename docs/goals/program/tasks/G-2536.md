## Outcome

      at apps/web/zones/official/index.ts:9:29

The files are `tests/qa/integration/outcomes/realm/create-a-realm.test.ts` and `tests/qa/integration/outcomes/zone/publish-after-editor-revocation-revoked-editors.test.ts`.
