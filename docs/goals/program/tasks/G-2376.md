## Outcome

The Manga browse record's frame asks `GET /v1/addresses/resolve?scope=space&key=<the Zone Space's id>`. The production page is opened at `/z/<canonical key>` and its `resolveSite` (`apps/web/features/realm/read.ts`) asks the address by the key in the URL, so the story has no recorded answer for the request the page sends.

Everything else in the frame is right and stays: the persona fixture's Zone is its own Space and presents the Realm it attaches (the scope's `zone/attach-a-realm` row), its address carries a Zone capability only, its presentation names the Realm, and `routes?path=browse` answers 404 there. An earlier text of this brief asked for a Zone on the Realm's own Space; that was the legacy shape and is withdrawn.

What must be true afterwards:

1. `recordFrameReads` in `tests/qa/support/frame-reads.ts` records the address read with the key production's URL carries. It learns the canonical key from the API (an unrecorded read by the id it is given is fine) and records `scope=space&key=<canonical key>`. Callers are not asked to know the key.
2. The Manga browse row is recorded once with it, for all six personas. Existing samples keep their answers apart from identities; the handoff names the old and new Realm, Zone and Space identities and the canonical key, and lists every frame label with its request.
3. The handoff lists the other row tests that call `recordFrameReads` when you start; they pick the change up at their next recording and are not changed here.

Do not build: a change of the persona fixture, a Zone on the Realm's Space, a mount named `browse`, a change under `apps/web/`, any change of the row's assertions or cells.
