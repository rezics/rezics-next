## Outcome

Account allowances and scheduling constraints are internal operational details that stay out of Git. Today `scripts/goal` hard-codes two Codex accounts by name and home:

- `dispatchEngine` and `tasks.ts`;
- `solAccount` in `handover.ts`;
- `efficiency.ts`;
- `measures.ts`;
- `coordinator.ts`;
- `REVIEW_ENGINES` in `land.ts`.

A new account cannot be used without a code change, and the code states which accounts exist.

Make goalctl read its Codex accounts from an untracked machine-readable file, `.temp/goal-orchestration/accounts.json`. Each account has:

- its engine name, as used by `dispatch --engine`, `resume` and the coordinator;
- its launcher or Codex home;
- a dispatch preference weight;
- whether it may continue past its allowance.

Every place above uses the configured set:

- dispatch, resume (an attempt stays on its account), the coordinator, account selection for new work by preference and current usage, efficiency and usage readouts per account, review engines, and measures;
- with no file, there is one account, the default `codex` launcher.

Tracked code, tests and documents name no real account and no policy. Tests use synthetic accounts. Document the file's format, not its contents, in `docs/goals/manager.md` under Resources.

Prove it:

- with three configured accounts, `dispatch --engine <third>` launches through that account's launcher;
- automatic selection follows the weights;
- a resumed attempt stays on its account;
- the efficiency readout lists all three;
- removing the file falls back to the default.
