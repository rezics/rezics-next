## Outcome

The supermanager knows from measurement what bounds landed tasks an hour now, and which change removes the most of it. The staged `.temp/ref/efficiency-audit-3.txt` and `.json` read the sessions begun from 15:40 to 18:46 UTC on 2026-10-13: 160 sessions, 3,965 steps, 283.9M input tokens, 96% cached; workers by step wait 41%, read 32%, edit 11%, test 6%; 21 of 48 tasks took one turn. `.temp/ref/efficiency-audit-2-researcher.md` is the second reading. You change no file of the repository.

What the supermanager measured in the window, to test against the logs and not to take on trust: (a) 19 to 22 of 34 workers were live; the host (62 GiB, 64 cores) stood at load 70 to 80 with 20 GiB in compressed swap. (b) The test queue is the bound: the Docker VM (23.5 GiB) holds about eight QA stacks; admission (`scripts/qa/memory-admission.ts`) asks 5.38 GiB free in the VM for a starting stack and 10.38 for a run with two, while a running stack uses 1.8 to 2.3 GiB; six to twelve runs waited for memory at any time, some for over an hour. (c) Landings failed on timing alone: a guard's 30 s limit and five-second harness tests under the load. (d) A manager's turn is cut at ten minutes and killed its own landing until 18:20 UTC; four landings died that way. (e) Changed in the window: a list-only test plan takes no slot, admission serves waiters in arrival order, `vm.swappiness` 10 to 100 at 17:42 UTC.

Read in the main checkout (read only): workers `.temp/goal-orchestration/runs/<id>/attempt-<n>.json` and `land.log`, the ledger `.temp/goal-orchestration/ledger.json`, the waiter and slot records under `.temp/goal-orchestration/qa-slots/`, each worktree's check and gate evidence under `.temp/worktrees/<id>/.temp/`, and `scripts/goal/efficiency.ts`, `scripts/goal/qa-slots.ts`, `scripts/qa/memory-admission.ts`, `scripts/goal/land.ts` for what the records mean. Write `.temp/goal/audit.md` in your worktree, at most ninety lines:

1. **The three windows, counted one way.** For each of 09:30 to 12:35, 12:36 to 15:40 and 15:40 to 18:46 UTC: worker input, manager input, tasks landed, input for each landed task, live workers (median).
2. **What a waiting worker waits for.** Split the wait share's tokens and minutes into: a test run waiting for a slot or for memory, a test run running, a landing, anything else. Tokens for each wait call and the calls shorter than 300 s that remain. For the ten tasks that waited longest: minutes in the queue against minutes of tests.
3. **Stack-minutes.** Which runs held the QA stacks in the window (a worker's check, a landing's gates, the unit gate's side on `main`, a record run, the scheduled run), with stack-minutes for each kind and for each landed task. Which were repeats of a run whose inputs had not changed, such as a landing gate on the tree the worker's check had just passed. How many stacks stood warm and unused while runs waited.
4. **From handoff to landed.** For the tasks landed in the window: minutes waiting for the manager's decision, waiting for a slot, in gates; how many landings were refused and for what, timing apart from real faults.
5. **Three changes, the largest gain in landed tasks an hour first.** For each: the measure now, the expected measure after, where it is made, what it risks. Say plainly whether admission by a stack's measured use with a margin, in place of its Compose limits, is safe on the figures you find, and what the same host could run. Then what is not worth changing.

The handoff repeats sections 1 and 5 in at most forty lines.

Do not build: a change to any file of the repository, a new tool, advice without a measure.
