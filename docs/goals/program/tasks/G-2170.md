## Outcome

What a manager turn decides is done when the turn ends, and a turn's event fits its bound. On 2026-10-13 the api manager's turns left their acts to a hand in one hour, and the hand met the tool's own gaps: the first planning event carried 24 rows, the turn wrote four briefs and changed two, and was cut at the ten-minute bound with none committed; a turn decided a blocked handoff and resumed nobody; a turn accepted a `partial` handoff, and nothing ran `land --step`; two briefs stood held because a dispatch interrupted at a busy ledger had left an empty worktree and branch that refused every later dispatch.

1. **A row with a brief is covered.** `uncoveredPlanningRows` (`scripts/goal/planning.ts:108`) counts a row as covered when a brief in the Goal's `tasks/` directory names it in `rows:`, whether that brief is dispatched, queued or only written. Half of the 24 rows had such a brief.
2. **A planning event is a slice.** `planningBody` (`:136`) lists at most six rows, all of one vertical, taking verticals in the scope's order, and says how many rows wait after them. The next slice is the next event, raised as today when the uncovered set has changed. Replace the body's line about naming rows in `state.md` (the role card forbids that edit outside a checkpoint) with: a row that waits for another task gets its brief now, queued behind that task.
3. **The role card says to act** (`MANAGER_ROLE_CARD`, `scripts/goal/coordinator.ts:129`), in two short sentences added to it: carry out what the decision needs before the turn ends (`resume <id> -m` for a worker the decision gives more work, `land <id> --step` then `close` for an accepted step with nothing left, `dispatch` or `queue` for a brief the turn wrote); commit each brief when it is written, with `git commit --only <path>`.
4. **A retry is told what the cut turn left.** The retry's body (`:554`) names the brief files of the Goal that are untracked or changed in the main checkout, so that the second turn continues them and does not write them again.
5. **An interrupted dispatch leaves nothing that refuses the next.** Where `dispatch` and the queue meet a worktree that exists (`scripts/goal/tasks.ts:94`, `scripts/goal/queue.ts:68`): when the ledger has no task of that ID, the branch has no commit that `main` lacks and the worktree has no change, remove both and go on; otherwise refuse as today.
6. **Handing a running worker a changed brief is no fix round.** `resume` (`scripts/goal/tasks.ts:350`) records `fix` for every resume that follows no `partial` or `blocked` handoff, so `stop`, `reclaim` and `resume` of a worker whose brief the manager extended used up the two fix rounds before any review (G-2142, corrected by hand in the ledger). A resume whose previous attempt was ended by `stop` and wrote no handoff is recorded as `step`, and the cap does not count it.
7. **A reference to a closed task still resolves.** Closing G-2138 removed its brief and its worktree; two open briefs named them in `refs:`, and `dispatch`, `reclaim` and `resume` then stopped on `Reference does not exist` (`scripts/goal/handover.ts:28`). A reference under `docs/goals/<goal>/tasks/` or `.temp/worktrees/<id>/.temp/goal/` whose task is closed is read from the copy the close kept (`.temp/goal-orchestration/runs/<id>/worktree-temp/goal/`); any other missing reference refuses as today.
8. **A declaration the reader cannot read ends no pass.** `declarationValues` (`scripts/goal/planning.ts:21`) reads declarations without running them and threw on a note built from constants in a template; the coordinator then exited on every pass for five minutes (13:48 to 13:53 UTC, fifteen restarts) and raised no task event. The reader now evaluates templates and string sums; what it still cannot read becomes one line of the planning output (`<file>: unreadable declaration: <reason>`), the row counts as uncovered, and the pass goes on. `planning.test.ts` gets the template case, the sum case and the unreadable case.

Do not build: a longer turn bound, a second manager, a file where a manager lists rows it leaves, a change to how a task event or a mail is delivered, a change to `land`, or any change to `resume` beyond the label in step 6.

## Checks

`task goal -- check`, and the four test files once with `CLAUDECODE=1`. The tests cover: a row named by a brief that was only written is covered; a slice of six from one vertical and its count of the rest; the retry's body naming an untracked and a changed brief; an empty leftover worktree removed and a worktree with a commit or a change refused; a resume after `stop` recorded as `step` and one after a refused landing as `fix`; a reference to a closed task's brief read from the kept copy. Then run `task goal -- planning api` once on the real state and give its output in the handoff. Wait on a running command with one long call (`yield_time_ms: 300000`).
