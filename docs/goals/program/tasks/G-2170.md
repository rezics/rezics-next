## Outcome

What a manager turn decides is done when the turn ends, and a turn's event fits its bound. On 2026-10-13 the api manager's turns left their acts to a hand: the first planning event carried 24 rows, the turn wrote four briefs and changed two, and was cut at the ten-minute bound with none committed; a turn decided a blocked handoff and resumed nobody; a turn accepted a `partial` handoff, and nothing ran `land --step`; three tooling briefs and two implementation briefs that a turn wrote stood undispatched for an hour. What the hand then met in `dispatch`, the queue and `resume` is another task of this Goal.

1. **A row with a brief is covered.** `uncoveredPlanningRows` (`scripts/goal/planning.ts:108`) counts a row as covered when a brief in the Goal's `tasks/` directory names it in `rows:`, whether that brief is dispatched, queued or only written. Half of the 24 rows had such a brief.
2. **A planning event is a slice.** `planningBody` (`:136`) lists at most six rows, all of one vertical, taking verticals in the scope's order, and says how many rows wait after them. The next slice is the next event, raised as today when the uncovered set has changed. Replace the body's line about naming rows in `state.md` (the role card forbids that edit outside a checkpoint) with: a row that waits for another task gets its brief now, queued behind that task.
3. **The role card says to act** (`MANAGER_ROLE_CARD`, `scripts/goal/coordinator.ts:129`), in two short sentences added to it: carry out what the decision needs before the turn ends (`resume <id> -m` for a worker the decision gives more work, `land <id> --step` then `close` for an accepted step with nothing left, `dispatch` or `queue` for a brief the turn wrote); commit each brief when it is written, with `git commit --only <path>`.
4. **A retry is told what the cut turn left.** The retry's body (`:554`) names the brief files of the Goal that are untracked or changed in the main checkout, so that the second turn continues them and does not write them again.
5. **A declaration the reader cannot read ends no pass.** `declarationValues` (`scripts/goal/planning.ts:21`) reads declarations without running them and threw on a note built from constants in a template; the coordinator then exited on every pass for five minutes (13:48 to 13:53 UTC, fifteen restarts) and raised no task event. The reader now evaluates templates and string sums; what it still cannot read becomes one line of the planning output (`<file>: unreadable declaration: <reason>`), the row counts as uncovered, and the pass goes on. `planning.test.ts` gets the template case, the sum case and the unreadable case.

Do not build: a longer turn bound, a second manager, a file where a manager lists rows it leaves, a change to how a task event or a mail is delivered, or a change to `land`, `dispatch`, the queue or `resume`.
