## Outcome

Read `.temp/ref/G-1522.md`, findings 13 and 14 (the journal part only).

1. **Preview runner.** `web-preview.ts` and `accounts-preview.ts` share one
   build/serve/signal/exit runner. Web keeps its port release and OAuth config
   as its own adapter.
2. **Stack registry.** Dev (`stack-session.ts`) and QA (`stack-ownership.ts`)
   serialize stack options once; dev adds its accountsApp field. Each keeps its
   registry location and cleanup policy.
3. **Ports.** `scripts/dev/cli.ts` and `scripts/fixture/stack.ts` allocate a
   unique free port set through one allocator.
4. **Command journal.** The write-ahead command and replay core in
   `scripts/ops/bootstrap/journal.ts` and `scripts/datasets/import.ts` is one
   envelope with two storage adapters. Keep the dataset's per-entry persistence,
   which avoids quadratic journal writes; do not substitute the whole-file save.

Net production lines must drop (the audit puts it at about 50–80); show
`git diff --numstat main...HEAD`. Where two copies differ for no stated reason,
choose one behaviour; options are plain values, not callbacks. Keep each file's
existing style.

Acceptance:
- A failed stop leaves retryable registry records.
- The preview binds only after the port holder releases.
- Retained request bytes, idempotency keys and digests are unchanged (journal replay test over a recorded journal).
