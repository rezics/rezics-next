## Outcome

A published section list's entries are disclosed by each entry's own target. A visitor without a session reads and searches every entry whose target is public: a public Work, or a published public Post. Entries whose targets are private, unpublished or otherwise unreadable stay withheld, as do their notes. Signed-in personas keep their current reads, and each sees at least what a visitor sees.

Today the `structure-target` disclosure in `compositionTargetBatchReader` (`services/main/src/modules/composition/disclosure-read.ts`) admits nothing when there is no principal or acting subject. The `section-list-v1` profile (`services/main/src/modules/section-list/structure-profile.ts`) is the only profile using it. Give the anonymous branch the targets' public baseline, through the same owners that decide a public Work or Post. Do not add a section-list-specific disclosure rule.

Update the proofs that currently assert visitors see no section-list entries, including the integration tests under `tests/qa/integration/` that cover section-list structure, traversal, placements and search. Every persona row states what it reads: visitor and stranger see public entries only, and private entries stay hidden from everyone without a grant.

none

## Design note

Reviewed: 2026-10-17

Decision: public entries of a published section list are visible and searchable for anonymous visitors, each filtered by its own visibility. Disclosure follows the entry's target, so a section list never widens what its targets disclose, and an anonymous read never sees more than a signed-in one.

## Checks

Per-persona outcome tests for section-list reads and search, including a list that mixes public, private and unpublished targets. They fail before the change and pass after it. The integration tests named above pass. `task goal -- check`.
