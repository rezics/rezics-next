## Outcome

A task whose worker has exited is settled by the tool, with no `wait` process and no manager call. On 2026-10-13, 55 of the supermanager's 146 calls were mechanical: reading the handoff of a finished task, merging, closing, arming a wait.

- One pass, `task goal -- settle`, looks at every open task of every running Goal whose worker process is gone and does what `wait` does today (`waitFor`, `scripts/goal/tasks.ts:121-207`): it continues a capacity or transport drop, marks the task exited, and lands and closes it (`landTask`) when the handoff is `RESULT: done` and the brief has `land: auto` or its kind is outcome, surface or tooling.
- A landing takes minutes. The pass starts it as a detached process and returns. A task is never settled twice at once, also across a restart of the coordinator, and the gate lanes bound how many land together.
- What needs judgment stays open and is marked settled for that attempt, with the reason in the ledger: a handoff that is not `done` (its result and its NEXT line); a landing that stopped (the first line of the refusal); an implementation or mechanism task without `land: auto`. `status` shows the reason beside the task.
- No manager turn starts for a task the tool is still landing: the task events of `managerEvents` (`scripts/goal/manager-commands.ts:69-74`) are raised only once the task is marked settled for its last attempt, with the reason as the event's body.
- The pump loop of `coordinatorCommand` (`scripts/goal/manager-commands.ts:222-237`) runs the pass each interval for every running Goal, enrolled or not.
- A task whose last attempt committed nothing and whose earlier steps are merged closes as `verified` (`closeTask`): today a read-only step after a merged step leaves the task impossible to close.
- `wait G-NNN` prints what it prints today. When the pass has taken the task, `wait` watches and reports and does not land a second time.

`scripts/goal/manager-commands.ts` is claimed by G-2119; the program lends this task the two places named above. Change only them in that file, and nothing in `coordinator.ts`.

Do not build: fresh or bounded manager turns, mail, a change to the review or the gates, documentation.
