## Outcome

The web's surface for `realm/membership-and-admission` replays this row's record through the production readers and commands. Its worker found three things the page sends that the record cannot answer (its handoff: `.temp/ref/g2232-record-gaps.txt`, the PROPOSED TASKS paragraph), so three controls stay inactive in the stories:

1. An invitation, and the pending answer (HTTP 202) to it, with the method, path, query and body the production page sends and for the persona who sends it.
2. The withdrawal and the decision. Both POSTs are in the record, but with a body that differs from the one the production confirm step sends, so the replay does not match them.
3. The Realm header read with `language=en` as the management frame sends it when it is refused, for the member, the moderator, the stranger and the operator.

What must be true afterwards:

- Read the requests from the production web code, not from the existing test: the membership, requests and invitation pages and their commands under `apps/web/features/realm/` and `apps/web/features/manage/` (start from the readers and command clients those pages import; `grep` for `joining`, `invitations`, `requests` and the decision and withdrawal commands). Record each with the exact method, path, query, headers and body, for each persona the page lets send it, through the outcome recorder the row already uses.
- Where the API refuses a persona, the refusal is recorded as given. No request is recorded for a persona the page never lets send it; say which those are.
- Existing samples keep their answers apart from identities; no assertion and no cell changes. The handoff names old and new identities and lists every new label with its request and status, and every request those pages can send that is still not in the record.
- If a request the page sends is answered 5xx, hand off with the request and the answer; do not vary it.

Do not build: a change under `apps/web/` or `tests/qa/support/`, a new row, a change of what the row proves.
