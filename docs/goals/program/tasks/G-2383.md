## Outcome

The web's surface for `recipes/read-a-recipe` replays this row's record through the production readers. The page reads the reader's follow state of the Recipe's author or Realm through `GET /v1/me/follow-state` (client: `apps/web/features/relationships/api.ts`), and the record holds no such read, so the follow control has no recorded answer for any persona.

What must be true afterwards:

- The row's test records `GET /v1/me/follow-state` as the production page sends it: no body, no `kind` parameter, the target the page names and `actingSubject` of the persona, for the stranger, the member, the owner, the moderator and the operator. Take the target and the parameter names from the production reader and from where the Recipe page calls it; the visitor sends none and gets none.
- Existing samples keep their answers apart from identities; no assertion and no cell changes. The handoff names old and new identities (the Recipe, its Realm and the five acting Persons), lists the five new labels with their requests and statuses, and names every other read the Recipe page's production readers send that is still not in the record.
- If the read is answered 5xx for a persona, hand off with the request and the answer; do not vary it.

Do not build: a change under `apps/web/` or `tests/qa/support/`, a new row, a change of what the row proves.
