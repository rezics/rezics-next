## Outcome

The record of `visual-novels/catalogue-browse-and-search-in-its-zone` holds, for each of the six personas, the answers to the requests the production Zone frame and browse page send, so the page's story runs the production readers on a replaying fetch. Today the story builds its frame from the presentation body, because these reads are not recorded, and the empty catalogue is recorded for the member alone.

1. Each persona's proof records, under new labels, with the persona's acting subject when signed in and with no query at all for the visitor where the page sends none:
   - `GET /v1/facets` (`frame-facets`);
   - `GET /v1/realms/<the row's Realm>` (`frame-realm`);
   - `GET /v1/zones/<the row's Zone>/routes?path=browse` (`frame-route`);
   - `GET /v1/realms/<the row's Realm>/modules/browse?sort=relevance&limit=20&type=https://schema.org/VideoGame&q=<a text the fixture cannot match>` (`browse-search-empty`; the member's existing empty sample keeps its label and request).
   Read the Zone browse route and its readers under `apps/web` only to copy each request's exact spelling and parameters; change nothing under `apps/`.
2. A further read that the page's server render makes for one visit and the record lacks is recorded too under a label of its own, at most six such reads, each listed in the handoff. More than six: list them and record none of them.
3. No existing sample's label or request changes, no assertion changes, no cell changes state. Never write an answer by hand: run the row's test so that it records, and check in the record it writes.
4. `scripts/api/answer-records.guard.test.ts` passes. The handoff lists the samples added (persona, label, method, path, status), the record's bytes and samples before and after, and the recording run's seconds.

An answer of 500 or above inside a proof is handed off, never recorded. A 503 in preparation or of the catalogue's search: run once more, then hand off `partial` with the request, the answer, the host's load (`cat /proc/loadavg`) and the log path, and leave the record on `main` as it is.

Do not build: a change under `apps/` or `services/`, a change to the recorder, the record's format or the guard, another row's record, a rewrite of the recorded presentation request (the replaying fetch reads an empty query as none).
