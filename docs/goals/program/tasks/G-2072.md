## Outcome

`land`, `resume` and `dispatch` apply docs/goals/method.md ("What blocks a
landing", "API first") so that no mail and no reviewer decides them. Two ordered
steps: hand off `RESULT: partial` after step 1, which lands by itself, then you
are resumed for step 2.

Step 1, landing and the attempt cap:
- Brief frontmatter takes `kind: outcome | implementation | surface | mechanism |
  tooling` (required by `dispatch` and `reclaim`) and optional `rows: [<kind>/<slug>]`.
- `land` runs a review only when `kind` is `implementation` or `mechanism` and
  the diff changes authority, disclosure, rights, erasure or Account code: a path
  table in code beside the review, from where those owners live today, with
  `services/account/**` whole. Every other landing is the gates, the guards and
  `close … verified`.
- The review is one reviewer pass, once for a task. A finding blocks only when it
  carries a test (through the public API, under `tests/qa/integration/review/`)
  that `land` writes into the worktree, runs with `task goal -- test` and sees
  fail on the branch; that test is then committed to the branch as the fix's
  target. A finding with no test, or whose test passes or cannot run, goes to
  `recorded.json` and never blocks. A later `land` runs those committed tests and
  the gates, never the reviewer.
- A task gets two fix rounds after its first handoff. `resume` refuses the next
  unless the brief in the tree has a `## Decision` entry dated (UTC) after the
  last attempt started that names a cut, a split or a design note. Capacity and
  transport retries are not rounds. No flag bypasses it.

Step 2, dispatch:
- An `implementation` brief is refused unless every row in `rows` has a
  declaration (`loadOutcomes()` of `scripts/qa/cases/outcomes.ts`, on `main` by
  then) with no missing cell and at least one `pending` cell.
- A `mechanism` brief is refused without a non-empty `## Design note` section
  that carries `Read by program: <date>`.
- Added by program on 2026-10-13 after reading step 1: a resume of a task whose
  last step landed (state `merged`, after a `RESULT: partial` handoff) is the
  next step, not a fix round. Step 1 counts every attempt, so a three-step task
  is refused its first fix. Record on the attempt which it is, and count only
  fix rounds against the cap.
- Added by program on 2026-10-13 after reading step 2. (a) A `partial` handoff
  that leaves nothing to land (no commit ahead of `main`, clean worktree) is a
  finished step too: the api Goal's outcome tasks hand off a matrix first and
  write tests on resume, and that resume is counted as a fix today. (b) An
  `implementation` brief with no `rows` passes unchecked; refuse it and say that
  a task with no row is `tooling` or `mechanism`.

Remove what this replaces (lens passes, the confirming pass, verify rounds,
blocking by severity); do not keep both paths. Update the `land`, `resume` and
`dispatch` rows and the brief example in docs/goals/README.md to what the tool
does, shorter than today. Do not build a review cache, new severities, a switch
for the old behaviour or a new command. Two other open tasks hold small lent
hunks in `goalctl.ts` (one line in `status`; the `mail` and `coordinator`
commands and `managerEvents`): leave those regions alone.
