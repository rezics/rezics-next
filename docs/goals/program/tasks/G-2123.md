## Outcome

The host is the limit, and a wide test run starts only where the program put it. On 2026-10-13 the full regression's batches took 296 minutes beside workers, and nothing refuses a worker that starts a whole tier. The survey staged in your worktree at `.temp/ref/test-admission-facts.md` lists every way a run starts today.

- **One definition of wide**, in one function: a whole tier with no file; `--affected` without `--list`; a run of the `e2e` or `static` tier, or of a Storybook suite, with no file named; `task qa` with no file; `regress`. Named files are never wide.
- **A wide run is refused**, in one line that says what to run instead, when the caller is a worker (`GOAL_TASK_ID` is set) or the manager of a Goal other than the program. The refusal holds on every way in: `task goal -- test`, `task goal -- slot`, `task test`, `task qa`, `task storybook:test`, `task accounts:storybook:test`, `task web:e2e`, `task accounts:e2e`. A person at a terminal, with neither `GOAL_ID` nor `GOAL_TASK_ID`, is never refused.
- **The program's wide runs go one at a time** through the heavy lease that exists. One waits while a worker is live and says so; `--beside-workers` starts it anyway, and its record says beside how many.
- **A slot names its holder.** An ordinary QA slot records the Goal and the task that hold it, and `task goal -- slot --holders` prints the slots by Goal.
- **Each Goal has a share.** The slots are divided evenly among the Goals that have a live worker, at least one each. A Goal at its share waits while another Goal waits for a slot, and takes a free slot when none does. The program's landing gates and wide runs are outside the shares.

`isHeavyTest` (`scripts/goal/briefs.ts`) and `scripts/goal/goalctl.ts` are other tasks' files: call them, do not change them. The regression that is running reaches `goalctl test` with files named and `GOAL_ID=program`; it must keep running through your change.

Do not build: a schedule or a timer, a change to `regress.ts` or to its batches (another task measures and cuts the regression), a change to memory admission, the worker protocol's text.
