## Outcome

A task that is resumed hours after its dispatch works on what `main` is now, with no manager preparing its worktree by hand. On 2026-10-13 three matrix tasks were resumed for their second step on worktrees that stood at the commit of their first dispatch, behind `main` and without the tooling their orders named; one stopped as blocked in two minutes, and that resume was counted as one of its two fix rounds.

- **`resume` brings the worktree to `main` before the worker starts.** A clean branch with no commit of its own is fast-forwarded. A clean branch with commits is rebased; when the rebase stops on a conflict it is undone, the worktree is left as it was, and the resume's output and the worker's prompt name the files in conflict in one line. A worktree with uncommitted changes, or one another live task shares, is left alone, and the output says which.
- The output of `resume` says in one line what it did: the commit the worktree stood at, the commit it is at now, and how many commits it was behind.
- **A blocked handoff that changed nothing is not a fix.** A resume after a `blocked` handoff whose attempt made no commit and left the worktree clean is a step: it does not count toward the two fix rounds and needs no `## Decision`. A `blocked` handoff after commits, or with changes left in the worktree, counts as today.

`resumeTask` is in `scripts/goal/tasks.ts`; put the worktree's update in `scripts/goal/resume.ts` and call it. The landing already rebases a branch (`scripts/goal/land.ts`, not yours): read how it does it and say in the handoff if the two should share code.

Do not build: a rebase of a running task, a change to `dispatch`, to the landing or to the fix cap's number, a change to `status` or to `goalctl.ts` (other tasks hold them), a merge commit on a task's branch.

## Mechanisms

none

## Checks

`task goal -- check`, and `resume.test.ts` once with `CLAUDECODE=1`. The tests use a temporary repository and the recording launcher of `handover.test.ts`, and cover: a branch with no commits fast-forwarded; a branch with a commit rebased; a conflict undone with its files named in the output and the prompt; a dirty worktree and a shared one left alone; the one-line report; a blocked handoff with no change resumed as a step, and with a commit as a fix. `goalctl.test.ts` is no task's file: if one of its tests must change, name it in the handoff.
