## Outcome

On 2026-10-15 parked workers waited up to two hours for nothing. Measure and fix the causes below.

1. **Ends recorded up to 31 minutes late.** These turns wrote `attempt-<n>.last.md` at the times shown, but the ledger recorded `endedAt` 10:09:49 UTC for all of them:

   | Task | Attempt | Handoff written (UTC) |
   |---|---|---|
   | G-2620 | 4 | 09:38:41 |
   | G-2597 | 11 | 09:45:47 |
   | G-2606 | 6 | 09:57:33 |
   | G-2587 | 34 | 09:59:27 |
   | G-2639 | 10 | 10:05:30 |
   | G-2595, G-2598, G-2609, G-2623, G-2632, G-2634 | | similar |

   Coordinator passes (`freshCoordinatorPass` in `scripts/goal/manager-commands.ts`, about every 64 s, seen in `journalctl --user` as `run-p*.scope`) kept starting throughout. Find why `coordinateWorkerJobs` did not record those ends: an engine process that outlives its turn, a pass blocked or slow (one pass scope logged 6 min CPU and a 5.5 GB peak), a lock, or something else. Fix the cause, so that an end is recorded within one pass of the turn's final message.
2. **A wave worktree ran stale coordination code.** G-2582 (worktree on a wave/2 branch) parked as attempt 37 at 08:07 waiting for job `67e3b516`, which attempt 35 had started. The job ended at 08:09 and was reported "worker attempt was replaced or closed". The adoption rule from `e418dea7b` on main would have handed the job to attempt 37, but the job owner process runs the worktree's own `scripts/goal/job.ts`, which predates it. Job ownership, wake and report rules must run main's code for every worktree, whatever its branch.
3. **Test processes leak.** `scripts/goal` tests leave `scripts/goal/fixtures/sleep-worker.ts --child` processes and `settle.ts G-001` cases running after the test ends, reparented to the user manager. Sixteen were found, up to 16 hours old. Tests must stop every process they start, even on failure or timeout.

Prove each with a test that reproduces it, and for item 1 give the measured end-recording latency on the live coordinator after landing.
