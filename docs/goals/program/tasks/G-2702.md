## Outcome

A shared owner does its job without recognising a vertical or a source (`docs/product/platform-thesis.md`; `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md`, Publication, Structure and types, first paragraph; Acceptance evidence). The audit evidence is in `.temp/ref/` (report-1.md, report-2.md, report-4.md).

Sourced structured publication interprets Recipe inside Work publication: it requires `complete-recipe` coverage, reparses with `importRecipe`, requires `recipe-composition`, checks ingredient, step and group qualifiers and picks Gutenberg's licence by provider. Its adopted dependencies are written as `schema:recipeInstructions`, which Content and Media delivery grants read. Move correspondence verification to the profile boundary, returning a reference to the exact Source observation and Structure revision plus structured attribution, and make publication consume that with its common admission, disclosure, rights and attribution rules. Make the dependency relation owner-neutral and let the Recipe profile project its semantics. Also move the shared grant and disclosure obligations listed under the Recipe hub row (`scripts/qa/cases/outcomes/recipes/work-hub-and-reading.ts`) to their capability owners and correct its two stale prerequisites.

Likely files: `services/main/src/modules/work/{sourced-structure-publication,sourced-publication-credits,instruction-adoption,structured-publication}.ts`, `routes/publication.ts`, `content-publication/public-selection.ts`, the Recipe profile, `scripts/qa/cases/outcomes/recipes/work-hub-and-reading.ts`.

Prove it with a test that fails before and passes after, on the real composition (`composeMain`), using a consumer other than the original one. Preserve exact versions, order, unknown authorship, truthful credit, rights evidence, private, refused and revoked states, idempotent recovery and bounded cost. Do not add a second engine, a framework or a per-vertical replacement, and do not move or rename a special case. Keep provider parsing and domain arithmetic in adapters. When the repair lands, remove its paragraph from `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md` in the same change, or narrow it to what remains.

## Design note

Writers: `POST /v1/work-publications` and the adoption of dependencies. Readers: Work headers, credit reads and Content and Media delivery grants. The Recipe profile now supplies the verification it did inline, and retained links migrate to the neutral relation, so Recipe publication and delivery answer as before.

Proof: A non-Recipe structured profile (for example the section-list profile) publishes from a public-domain source with attribution, and its adopted Content is delivered; Recipe publication stays green.
