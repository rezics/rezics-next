## Outcome

A brief is right from its first line to its archive without a manager knowing the tool's history. Two defects met on 2026-10-13:

1. **The skeleton names every field and no Claude worker.** `task goal -- new` wrote `engine: claude` and left out `kind`, `rows`, `tests`, `refs`, `operations` and `land`, and a brief with no `engine:` line also went to Claude (`DEFAULT_ENGINE` in `scripts/goal/types.ts`); the maintainer keeps Claude for managers. `new` takes `--kind <kind>` (asked for when missing, with the kinds listed) and writes a skeleton that passes the brief's validation as soon as its `## Outcome` and `## Checks` are written: every field the validator knows, `engine: sol`, `land: auto` for the kinds that settle by themselves, and the comment lines of today. A brief or an environment that names no engine gets `sol`; `engine: claude` stays valid when a brief says it.
2. **Closing a task breaks no link.** The archive step (`scripts/goal/ledger.ts`, where a closed brief leaves the tree) ignores mentions under `docs/goals/`, so a state record that linked `tasks/G-NNNN.md` failed `task docs:check` for every later change until someone edited it. In the commit that removes the brief, each Markdown link to it in a file under `docs/goals/` becomes its plain text (`[G-2135](tasks/G-2135.md)` becomes `G-2135`); a link from anywhere else keeps the brief in the tree, as today.

Each step is committed when its tests pass. `scripts/goal/goalctl.test.ts` is another task's file: if one of its tests asserts the old skeleton or the old default, name it in the handoff with the line, and do not change it.

Do not build: a refusal of `engine: claude`, a change to the validator's rules, to `close` or to what the archive holds, a rewrite of text that is not a link.

## Checks

`task goal -- check`, and the test file once with `CLAUDECODE=1`. The tests use a temporary repository and cover: the skeleton of each kind validates once the two sections have text; `new` without `--kind` is refused with the kinds; a brief with no engine line resolves to `sol` and one that says `claude` keeps it; closing a task whose brief is linked from its Goal's `state.md` and from another Goal's document rewrites both links in the archiving commit and the documentation check's link test passes on the result; a link from outside `docs/goals/` keeps the brief. Wait on a running command with one long call (`yield_time_ms: 300000`).
