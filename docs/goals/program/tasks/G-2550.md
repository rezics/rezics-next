## Outcome

`docs/architecture/content-adoption.md` is decided: read its Decision section and decision 4. The vertical slice is the acceptance gate before Recipes open publicly. This step defines it API-first (docs/goals/method.md): the contract and the outcome tests that fail today, before any implementation.

1. **Contract.** Update the owning contracts to the decided boundary:
   - `docs/contracts/recipe-exchange.md` and the Recipe profile description;
   - Content's procedure block as the one structured instruction form;
   - Content's derivation record (PROV-O qualified derivation);
   - the general Resource reference with grain selectors;
   - a Recipe's instructions admitting a Post, an exact Media recording representation, a procedure, or an ordered combination;
   - local terms only for occurrence substitution, scaling policy and measurement basis/coverage.

   State each new operation's request and response at the API.
2. **Outcome row.** Declare a new outcome row, `recipes/instructions-in-any-form`, with six pending persona cells, and write its outcome test against the public API. In one recipe, cover:
   - an exact quantity with a note;
   - instructions adopted as an authored Post, then as a recording representation, then as a structured procedure, then as an ordered combination;
   - a mention of a tomato resource inside the instructions;
   - a referenced intermediate recipe at an exact revision;
   - a derivation record from the recording to the procedure, credited to an AI Agent as transformation.

   Each persona sees what its role allows: drafts private, publication pinned.
3. **Falsifiers.** Turn the note's "Behaviours a replacement must preserve" into executable checks in the existing Recipe owner and outcome tests, where they are not already covered: scaling exactness, substitution per occurrence, nutrition coverage, bounded work page and exchange loss reporting. They must pass today and keep passing.

No implementation of the new forms in this step; the new row stays pending and its tests fail for the right reason.
