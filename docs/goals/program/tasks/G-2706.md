## Outcome

A shared owner does its job without recognising a vertical or a source (`docs/product/platform-thesis.md`; `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md`, Discovery, exchange and release, second paragraph (exchange lifecycle)). The audit evidence is in `.temp/ref/` (report-3.md).

Exchange lifecycle lives in Recipe routes. Collection imports reupload and reparse files for offset continuation outside the import job store; native Structure and Content export traversal exists only for Recipe; composition export stops at 255 pins. Make collection imports use the existing upload, row and job custody, with Recipe command generation as the adapter, and lift native occurrence, Content and dependency paging into the shared export owner, migrating Recipe export and composition export together. Keep Schema.org projection separate.

Likely files: `services/main/src/routes/recipe-collections.ts`, `recipe/{collection-import,export-page}.ts`, `library-import/job-store.ts`, `export/readers.ts`, `packages/model/src/recipe-export.ts`.

Prove it with a test that fails before and passes after, on the real composition (`composeMain`), using a consumer other than the original one. Preserve exact versions, order, unknown authorship, truthful credit, rights evidence, private, refused and revoked states, idempotent recovery and bounded cost. Do not add a second engine, a framework or a per-vertical replacement, and do not move or rename a special case. Keep provider parsing and domain arithmetic in adapters. When the repair lands, remove its paragraph from `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md` in the same change, or narrow it to what remains.

## Design note

Writers: collection import and export jobs. Readers: import status and export downloads. Recipe imports and exports keep their answers because the job store and shared paging carry the same rows and pages.

Proof: A non-Recipe composition exports complete past 255 pins, and a Paprika import resumes through the job store without reupload.
