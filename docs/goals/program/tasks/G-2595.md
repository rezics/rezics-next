## Outcome

Audit what a person can learn about **closed Realms, private Realms and restricted members or content**, across every surface that can carry it: feed, search and typeahead, notifications, rankings, boards, threads and replies, media, roster and role-holders, directory, Zone discussion modules, counts, and continuations issued before a closure.

Work as an adversary of the product's disclosure rules. For each read surface, write integration probes as each of the six personas (`tests/qa/support/personas.ts`): visitor, stranger, member, owner, moderator, operator. Each probe attempts a disclosure the contracts forbid: direct ID, continuation token reuse, a revision or version selector, a search or typeahead hit, a count, a notification payload, a media byte or metadata request, an export, and a cache validator such as an ETag or 304. Tonight two real leaks were found this way: a closed Realm's roster (G-2577) and an explicit-revision read without authorization (G-2588).

For every leak, fix it at its cause in the owner module and keep the probe as a regression test. For every probe that holds, keep it as coverage. Do not weaken any existing assertion. Hand off a table: surface, probe, verdict (held or leaked), fix commit.
