## Outcome

Two landing-tool defects cost manager time today.

A landing that stops on a rebase conflict records `mergeStop.kind = task` with "Correct the brief, reclaim, then merge" (`scripts/goal/merge.ts:481`), and `land` then refuses the task. Reclaim replaces the task's claims from its brief (`scripts/goal/tasks.ts:703`). A mechanism brief's claims are empty, so `merge` then refuses scope that the landing review had admitted. G-2705, G-2699 and G-2702 needed `merge --allow-scope`. Record a distinct rebase-conflict stop and keep the claims and submission. Once the manager resolves the branch against current main, `land` accepts it again and refreshes the landing review when the head changed. Reclaim stays for intentional scope changes.

Closing a verified landing drains the Goal's manager snapshots, including failed ones, and `runManagerCommit` rethrows their stored error (`manager-commit.ts:217`, `tasks.ts:722`, `land.ts:529`). G-2327 was verified, then reported a failure because of an unrelated, obsolete G-2475 brief snapshot. Keep failed snapshots reviewable, report each under its own ID, and never fail a task's closure for them. Explicit snapshot retry stays.

Unit tests: a mechanism task with empty claims stops on a rebase conflict, the manager resolves it, and `land` merges with the admitted scope preserved; a changed head refreshes the review.
