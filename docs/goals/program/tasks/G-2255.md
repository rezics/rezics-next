## Outcome

The loans page's refusal can be shown from the record. The page's reader asks `GET /v1/me/library-loans?actingSubject=…&state=active&limit=20` (`apps/web/features/library/loans/read.ts`; read it, change nothing there). The Books loans records hold that request for the owner (`active-loans-page`) and the member (`empty-active-loans`), but the refused personas were asked without `limit=20` (`deniedLoans`, the `active-loans-refused` sample in `tests/qa/integration/outcomes/books/collection-tracking-and-progress.test.ts`), and a story replays a request by its parameters, so no story can show the visitor's 401 or the stranger's, member's, moderator's and operator's 403.

1. `active-loans-refused` is asked with the reader's own request, `limit=20` included, for every refused persona. No assertion changes, no cell changes state, and no other sample's label, request or order changes; a proof that then fails is reported, not edited.
2. The two records the test writes (`tests/qa/fixtures/outcomes/books/loans.json` and `return-an-expired-loan-expired-loans.json`) are rewritten by one admitted run of the test and checked in; never write an answer by hand. `scripts/api/answer-records.guard.test.ts` passes.
3. **The Work hub's releases, for the visitor.** The hub's reader asks `GET /v1/works/{id}/releases?limit=20` with no body (`apps/web/features/work-page/read.ts`; read it, change nothing there); `tests/qa/fixtures/outcomes/books/work-hub-and-edition-choice.json` holds that read with `limit=2` only, so both visitor stories of the hub fail to find it. Add the reader's own request to `tests/qa/integration/outcomes/books/work-hub-and-edition-choice.test.ts` for the visitor under a label a story can name, keeping the `limit=2` sample and every proof as they are, and check in the rewritten record.
4. In the handoff: the samples that changed in each record (label, persona, path, status), the run's seconds, and any other read of `read.ts` the records still do not hold.

Do not build: a change under `apps/` or `services/`, a change to the recorder, the record's format or the guard, any other row's record.
