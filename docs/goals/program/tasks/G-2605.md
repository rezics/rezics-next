## Outcome

Two cost findings apply to every request:

1. Every command carries about 24 fixed SQL statements, for baseline-member admission and Agent fences.
2. 35 readers replay a whole read at their fence, making about twice the reads they need.

The findings come from `docs/goals/api/later.md`; detail and file positions are in the archived end-of-run tables (`archive/goals:program-2026-10-06/study-2026-10-12/end-of-run/`; read them with `git show archive/goals:<path>`). Check each finding against wave/3 first, since some may already be fixed. Say so with evidence and move on.

Measure both on wave/3 with one representative command and one fenced read, counting statements and reads. Then remove the redundancy at its cause: admission and fence checks that can be batched or proven once per transaction, and fences that verify a version rather than re-reading the whole result. Keep every safety property: a fence still refuses a read whose basis moved. Add a test that pins the new statement and read counts, so they cannot regress silently. Hand off before and after counts.
