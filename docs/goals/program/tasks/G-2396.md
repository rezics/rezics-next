## Outcome

The web's surface for `realm/moderation-and-appeal` replays this row's record through the production readers (`apps/web/features/realm-appeal/read.ts`, `api.ts`, `reading.ts`, `apps/web/features/manage/safety-api.ts`, the platform gate in `apps/web/features/api/platform-access.ts`, and the Realm frame those pages sit in). The record on main was made before two API repairs and lacks states the pages reach. Both repairs are on main when this task starts: `GET /v1/me/platform-access` reports the groups the deployment opens, and the Realm's Zone read answers for every signed-in persona.

What must be true afterwards, each request taken from the production client and sent with the path, query and body production sends (not from this list; a request production does not send is not recorded, and the handoff says which listed one that was and why):

- The member's appeal page: the platform-access read that now opens `platform:realm-appeals`, the member's own ban read, the appeal's read and the appeal command with the body the production form sends, and the receipt read that follows it.
- The moderator's and the owner's management pages: the Realm header read with the `language=en` the management frame sends, the queue and its permission and supporting reads, and the moderation-context read if, and only if, the production name reader sends it for this item.
- A moderator refused in a Realm they do not moderate: the target Realm, the acting moderator and the exact production request, answered by its refusal. Another persona's 404 does not stand in for it.
- An owner's decided case: a case a moderator has decided, read as the owner's page reads it (`decisionHead` not null) with its supporting reads, the case identity and generation kept through the journey.
- The Realm frame's reads for all six personas, the visitor included, as the other Realm rows record them (`tests/qa/support/frame-reads.ts`).
- This is meant to be the row's last re-recording for the surface. Before recording, read the pages' whole production path once and compare it with the record's labels for each persona; record every read still missing and list those beyond the items above.
- No assertion and no cell changes. Existing samples keep their answers apart from identities, except where one of the two repairs changed the answer; name each such sample. No 202 exists for this row; do not invent a pending state.
- The record's reads run under their own deadline (120 s) after the proof, and the persona tests get the limit both need; the proof keeps its own deadline. Say in one comment which part is the proof's.
- A 5xx: hand off with the request and the answer, do not vary it. The log names slow requests under `[persona-request]`; quote them if a proof meets its limit, and do not run a third time.

The handoff names old and new identities and every new label with its request and status, in a file under `.temp/` of the worktree as well as in summary.

Do not build: a change under `apps/web/` or `tests/qa/support/`, a new row, a request with another persona's bearer, a join-request record.
