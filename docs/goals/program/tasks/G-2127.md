## Outcome

`task goal -- efficiency` answers for a window of a day in under ten seconds on this host. On 2026-10-13 a window of 77 minutes took 165 seconds: the two session roots hold 5,968 logs and 13 GB, and the command reads them all to find the 13 in its window.

- The logs lie under `sessions/<year>/<month>/<day>/`, named by the local time a session began, and a resumed session appends to its first file. The command opens only the logs whose day directory can hold a session that began in the window, one day either side for the time zone, and decides by each log's first record as today.
- Its figures do not change: the existing tests pass untouched, and on the real roots `--since 2026-10-13T09:30:00Z --until 2026-10-13T10:46:47Z` still prints 13 sessions, 687 steps and 57,510,393 input tokens.
- A window with no bound on one side reads what it must and says how many logs it opened.

Do not build: a cache or an index of sessions, a change to a definition or to the output, anything for another engine's logs.

## Mechanisms

none

## Checks

`task goal -- check`, and `efficiency.test.ts` once with `CLAUDECODE=1`. A new test builds a tree with day directories inside and outside a window and shows that no log outside it is opened. Then time the real call named above and give its seconds and its three figures in the handoff. Wait on a running command with one long call (`yield_time_ms: 300000`).
