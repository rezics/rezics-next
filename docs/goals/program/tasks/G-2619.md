## Outcome

`docs/goals/api/later.md` records that occurrence progress (chapters and episodes read or watched) is keyed by Account principal, while library status is keyed by Person. A person who reads with two principals, such as two sign-in methods for the same Person, sees one library but split progress. The product principle is that identity belongs to the Person, not to an account or a Realm.

Key occurrence progress by Person, consistent with library status:

- a migration moves existing progress to its Person;
- progress reads and writes resolve the acting Person;
- the two-device outcome still passes;
- a new owner test shows two principals of one Person sharing progress, and two Persons never sharing it.

State the change in the owner document. Keep every disclosure rule: a Person's progress stays private to that Person.
