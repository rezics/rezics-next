## Outcome

A worker that parks a job is woken when the job ends, resuming its whole session even when the job passed and the next step is only another check. Over 2026-10-15 04:00–16:00 UTC about 200 such green wakes cost about 120 M input tokens.

Let a worker declare an ordered sequence of commands when it parks, for example `task goal -- job --then '<command>' --then '<command>' -- <first command>`, or an equivalent the existing job protocol fits better.

- The job owner runs the commands in order, as jobs with the same admission, deadlines and logs.
- It stops at the first failure.
- It wakes the worker once: with the failing command, its exit and the first error lines of its log, or, when all pass, with one line per command.

A plain job behaves as today. Update `docs/goals/worker.md` so a worker declares its remaining checks as one sequence instead of parking for each.

Prove it with tests: an all-green sequence wakes once; a failure in the middle stops the sequence and wakes with that failure; the remaining commands do not run; a stall or timeout reports as a failure.
