## Outcome

A shared owner does its job without recognising a vertical or a source (`docs/product/platform-thesis.md`; `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md`, Discovery, exchange and release, second paragraph (release and capture export)). The audit evidence is in `.temp/ref/` (report-1.md, report-3.md).

Fixed releases and sealed exports accept only VNDB evidence shapes: external links require a `vndb:release` identifier, a VNDB appearance claim and a character capture, and raw capture export is selected as `vndb-concept-run`. Have Source verify a provider-neutral external release reference against exact frozen evidence, keep VNDB parsing in its adapter, and generalise capture export over the existing `SourceRunStore` and export planner. Migrate write validation, manifest validation, release reads, export revalidation and saved export locators together.

Likely files: `services/main/src/modules/work/fixed-release.ts`, `export/{readers,operations}.ts`, `routes/{works,exports}.ts`, the VNDB adapter.

Prove it with a test that fails before and passes after, on the real composition (`composeMain`), using a consumer other than the original one. Preserve exact versions, order, unknown authorship, truthful credit, rights evidence, private, refused and revoked states, idempotent recovery and bounded cost. Do not add a second engine, a framework or a per-vertical replacement, and do not move or rename a special case. Keep provider parsing and domain arithmetic in adapters. When the repair lands, remove its paragraph from `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md` in the same change, or narrow it to what remains.

## Design note

Writers: fixed-release creation and sealed export. Readers: release reads and export downloads. Existing VNDB links migrate to the neutral reference with the same frozen evidence, so sealed manifests verify as before.

Proof: A release link backed by another admitted provider's frozen evidence seals, reads and exports, and VNDB releases stay green.
