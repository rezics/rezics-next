## Outcome

Over 2026-10-15 11:40–23:40 UTC, at least 32 green gate passes were thrown away, at least 3.1 task-hours. The cause was that another landing advanced main during a gate: "main changed type-checked workspaces again after the retry type check; retry merge", and "does not rebase". The analysis is `.temp/ref/report.md`, section 2.

Replace serial merge-and-retry with a merge train in `scripts/goal/merge.ts` and the settlement:

- queued landings, including program's own commits to main, form an ordered train;
- each entry is gated on the cumulative tree of main plus every entry ahead of it;
- main advances in order as entries pass;
- a failing entry leaves the train, and only the entries behind it are regated.

Reuse the existing gate selection and its per-file budgets, so the train adds no gate work of its own.

Record a timestamp for every gate start and end, lock wait and train position, so landing latency can be measured end to end.

Prove it with tests where three landings that touch overlapping files land in order with no green pass discarded, a failing middle entry is removed and the entries behind it regated, and a manager commit joins the train. Then report discarded gate-seconds and p95 handoff-to-main latency over the first real landings.
