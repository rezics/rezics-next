## Outcome

The efficiency audit's measuring is a command, and its figures compare from one audit to the next. `task goal -- efficiency` prints, with no model, the measures of the Codex worker sessions begun since the last recorded audit.

- It prints tokens by the task's turn (1 to 3, 4 to 6, 7 to 12, 13 and later); by what a step did (read, wait, edit, test, think, sub-agent, commit, other); by engine and by account; worker sessions against landing reviews; the sessions past 150 steps and their share; and the ten dearest tasks, each with its turns, steps and tokens.
- The definitions are those of the Python script staged in your worktree at `.temp/ref/sessions.py`: a step is one model call and its input tokens are that call's whole context; a turn is one prompt to a session. Port them and do not improve them: audits compare only while they stay. Run the script once on your fixture logs and assert its numbers in the test; commit the expected values, not the script.
- `--since <UTC time>` and `--until <UTC time>` choose the window by a session's first timestamp. The session roots are `~/.codex/sessions` and `~/.codex-1/sessions`; `GOAL_CODEX_HOMES` overrides them for tests.
- `--record` appends the figures as one JSON line to `efficiency.jsonl` in the Goal state directory, and the next call without `--since` starts from that record's time. `--last` prints the last record beside the one before it, each figure with its change.
- Output stays under sixty lines. `--json` prints the record.

Build the fixture under `scripts/goal/fixtures/efficiency/` from the two trimmed real logs staged at `.temp/ref/session-a.jsonl` and `.temp/ref/session-b.jsonl`: keep the record shapes, cut every text to a few words.

Do not build: the three-hourly start of a researcher, a line in `status`, Claude session logs, a change to `scripts/goal/measures.ts`, documentation.
