## Outcome

Documentation has one authority, whoever wrote it. Make every document state current rules and decisions plainly, in English, once, and remove whatever is no longer true.

**Scope.** `AGENTS.md`, everything under `docs/goals/` (manager, worker, method, wave, README, each Goal's GOAL.md and state.md), `docs/architecture/`, `docs/contracts/`, `docs/plan/`, `docs/research/README.md` and `docs/development/`. Leave task briefs (`docs/goals/*/tasks/`) and archived history alone.

1. **English only.** Translate or remove every non-English instruction, quotation or note. Keep non-English text that is product data or an example of data, such as titles, names or a localized label under test.
2. **No attribution.** Remove quotations of people and markers of origin: "the maintainer said", "(maintainer, date)", "decided by program", "Program review", "proposal for program review", and dates kept only to say who decided. State the rule or decision itself. Keep a date only where the date is part of the meaning, such as a schedule or a measured baseline.
3. **No stale or contradictory content.** Where two places disagree, keep the current one and delete the other. Where a rule describes tooling, check the code and correct or remove it. Where a document duplicates what code, schemas or tests already enforce, cut it to a link. Delete superseded history, such as past runs and old measurements, unless a current rule depends on it.
4. **Style for the readers, who are GPT-6.1 Sol, GPT-6 Luna and Claude agents.** Follow OpenAI's latest-model guidance:
   - short paragraphs, one idea each, main point first;
   - plain language, with no invented compound labels and no filler;
   - lists only for parallel or ordered items, and no nested lists;
   - no emphasis through capitals or repetition;
   - one explicit statement of which instruction wins when two conflict;
   - finish reversible and authorized work before asking;
   - no warnings about hypothetical risks;
   - no tests that only mirror a small change.

Preserve every rule that is still in force. If you are unsure whether a rule is current, keep it, and list it in your handoff with the question.
