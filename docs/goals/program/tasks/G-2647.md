## Outcome

Twice on 2026-10-15 a task's commits reached main while the ledger recorded the task as `conflict`. The next `merge` then said "has no commits to merge", and `merge --landed` failed because the worktree was already removed. Program fixed the ledger by hand both times.

- **G-2636 (about 08:22 UTC):** `merge --allow-scope` refused with "type check inconclusive; not merging", yet `5e1302da7` (its rebased commit) was on main, `mergedCommit` was set, the state was `conflict` and the worktree was gone.
- **G-2641 (about 09:05 UTC):** "unit gate inconclusive: unattributed affected runner failure; not merging", yet `b7f39d7a1` was on main and the state was `conflict`.

Both tasks also had a settlement (`runs/<id>/settle.log`) that may have landed concurrently with program's hand `merge`.

Find the race or the ordering fault. Possibly a settlement fast-forwards while a hand merge's gate fails and records `conflict` over it, or a gate's inconclusive branch runs after the fast-forward. Fix it so that:

- whenever a task's commits are on main, its state is `merged` with that commit, and nothing overwrites that with `conflict`;
- a hand `merge` refuses while a settlement holds the task's landing lock;
- `merge --landed` works without a worktree when the branch, or a patch-equivalent commit, is already on main.

Reproduce both orders in tests with injected processes or hooks.
