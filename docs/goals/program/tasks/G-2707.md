## Outcome

A shared owner does its job without recognising a vertical or a source (`docs/product/platform-thesis.md`; `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md`, Erasure custody). The audit evidence is in `.temp/ref/` (report-4.md).

Source byte erasure finds imported files only under the `rezics/recipe-file` provider. Paprika and Mela collection imports keep their bytes under their own provider, so erasing a person leaves those bytes, and restore does not prove their absence. Record an owner-validated binding from Source observation to resource and revision at every import, and make destruction, the immutability guard and the restore absence proof use that one binding, replacing all three provider filters. Keep independently held or shared evidence under the existing retention rules.

Likely files: `services/content/migrations/2131_recipe_file_source_erasure.sql` (by a new migration), `services/main/src/modules/erasure/{content,reconcile}.ts`, `routes/recipes.ts`, `recipe/collection-import.ts`.

Prove it with a test that fails before and passes after, on the real composition (`composeMain`), using a consumer other than the original one. Preserve exact versions, order, unknown authorship, truthful credit, rights evidence, private, refused and revoked states, idempotent recovery and bounded cost. Do not add a second engine, a framework or a per-vertical replacement, and do not move or rename a special case. Keep provider parsing and domain arithmetic in adapters. When the repair lands, remove its paragraph from `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md` in the same change, or narrow it to what remains.

## Design note

Writers: file and collection imports record the binding; erasure destroys through it. Readers: the erasure completion proof and restore admission. Existing Recipe-file observations are backfilled into the binding, so their erasure and restore proofs stay green.

Proof: Erasing a person removes the bytes of their Paprika or Mela collection import, and restore proves those bytes stay absent; Recipe-file erasure stays green.
