## Outcome

Its reference target is a Media asset representation. The same occurrence is available on the publication revision.

Program's ruling: the owner who attached a recording must see it when previewing their own private revision, unless rights, erasure or a disclosure restriction withholds it, the same rules that govern the publication. A private revision never discloses media to anyone who cannot read that revision.
