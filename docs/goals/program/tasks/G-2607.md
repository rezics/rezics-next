## Outcome

Three findings make the platform unreliable after a restart:

1. The content projection replays for three to four minutes after every restart.
2. The rate limiter refuses requests for some seconds after a restart.
3. Model bootstrap does not wait out a Fuseki restart.

The findings come from `docs/goals/api/later.md`; detail and file positions are in the archived end-of-run tables (`archive/goals:program-2026-10-06/study-2026-10-12/end-of-run/`; read them with `git show archive/goals:<path>`). Check each finding against wave/3 first, since some may already be fixed. Say so with evidence and move on.

Fix each at its cause:

- **Projection:** resume from its persisted checkpoint rather than replaying.
- **Rate limiter:** restore or start from a state that does not refuse ordinary traffic.
- **Bootstrap:** wait for Fuseki readiness within a bounded retry.

Prove each with a restart test on a stack, measuring the time to readiness before and after.
