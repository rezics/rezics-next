## Outcome

The supermanager knows from measurement what the hours from 18:46 to 21:50 UTC on 2026-10-13 cost for each landed task, how much of that the three faults below explain, and which change gains the most landed tasks an hour now. The staged `.temp/ref/efficiency-audit-4.txt` reads the sessions begun in that window: 205 sessions, 4,981 steps, 324.6M input tokens, 96% cached; workers by step wait 42%, read 33%, edit 10%, test 6%. `.temp/ref/efficiency-audit-3-researcher.md` is the third reading; count this window the same way. What the supermanager found in the window, to test against the records and not to take on trust:

(a) Ten tasks stood in `starting` without a worker for 3 minutes to 3.6 hours (G-2254 from 17:39, G-2280, G-2276 from 19:30, G-2166, G-2289, G-2315 from 20:36, G-2323 from 20:50, G-2339, G-2287, G-2334) and were counted as live; all started between 21:25 and 21:27. The fix is `d22063edd`.
(b) Test runs: from 20:00 to 21:15 the host ran ten or eleven stacks at a load of 85 to 103 and almost every stack run failed on late answers; from 21:15 to 21:42 the slot count was 6 and the queue stalled with one stack running; since 21:42 it is 16 with a load limit on stack starts (`eb58b22b0`). Stack runs passed of those ended, from the worktrees that still exist: 20:15 to 21:15 3 of 51, 21:45 to 21:50 5 of 7.
(c) A coordinator pass took up to 234 s (fixed in `d22063edd`: 7 to 17 s), so manager turns started late.
(d) A scheduled confirmation ran from 19:47 to 21:36 on two lanes and was stopped at 25 of 60 files.

1. **The four windows, counted one way.** For 09:30 to 12:35, 12:36 to 15:40, 15:40 to 18:46 and 18:46 to 21:50 UTC: worker input, manager input, tasks landed, input for each landed task, workers with a live process (median, not the ledger's count), landings an hour.
