## Outcome

`docs/architecture/content-adoption.md` (Decision section) and `docs/contracts/content-adoption.md`, `docs/contracts/recipes.md` and `docs/contracts/recipe-exchange.md` (from G-2550) define the slice. G-2552 builds the Content procedure block, resource references and derivation record in parallel on wave/2; rebase on its pushes and use them.

Make `recipes/instructions-in-any-form` (`tests/qa/integration/outcomes/recipes/instructions-in-any-form.test.ts`, six pending cells) pass:

- A Recipe's instructions adopt an authored Post, an exact Media recording representation, a Content procedure block, or an ordered combination, through Structure's existing ordered selection at exact revisions. Remove the profile rule that forbids step targets and requires `instructionText`, and replace it with the field-specific admitted forms.
- Recipe facts use their standard terms (`recipeIngredient`, `recipeYield`, times, `nutrition`, `HowTo*` for projection), constrained by SHACL in the Recipe profile. Local terms remain only for occurrence substitution, scaling policy and measurement basis/coverage.
- Exchange maps the new forms explicitly and reports loss or unsupported forms, never silent flattening.
- The falsifiers G-2550 listed stay green: exact scaling, per-occurrence substitution, nutrition coverage and basis, the bounded work page, exchange preservation and refusal, support withdrawal and publication privacy.

Remove Recipe-specific branches from shared layers (Structure format, change and consistency, measure commands, the general Composition request schemas) only where the new boundary replaces them, each with its test. When the row passes, promote its six cells.
