## Outcome

`task goal -- status` and `task goal -- usage` print the measures of
docs/goals/method.md ("Measures") for each running Goal, from the ledger and the
workers' session logs, so a checkpoint reads them instead of computing them.

`scripts/goal/measures.ts` (new):
- attempts by class, from the ledger: first attempt; step resume (after a
  `RESULT: partial` handoff); fix round (after a `done` handoff, a refused
  landing or a gate failure); capacity or transport retry. First-attempt share is
  first attempts over first attempts plus fix rounds, for today (UTC) and for the
  Goal's run;
- attempts for each landed task: the median and the largest with its task;
- tokens in and out for each Goal, counted by growth inside each session: a
  resumed Codex session's log carries a running total, so never sum
  `turn.completed` across resumes
  (`git show archive/goals:program-2026-10-06/study-2026-10-12/tools/efficiency.py`
  has the counting that was checked against `~/.codex*/sessions`);
- landings refused, by what refused them (outcome test, gate, finding with a
  failing test, anything else).

`status` prints one line for each running Goal, such as `measures api:
first-attempt 71% (today 12 of 17); attempts/landed median 1, max 3 (G-NNNN);
tokens 41M in, 0.6M out`, and a line starting `STOP` for a Goal whose share today
is under 50% over at least 10 attempts. `usage` carries the same figures as JSON
under `measures`. Tokens for each proved row are printed as `n/a`: the count of
proved rows is another task's.

The hooks are one import and one call in `status` and one in `usage` in
`scripts/goal/goalctl.ts`, lent to this task; touch nothing else there. Do not
build a dashboard, a history store, charts or a dispatch refusal.
