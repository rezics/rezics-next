## Outcome

A woken worker resumes its whole session. Over 2026-10-15 04:00–16:00 UTC, waking parked jobs cost 346 M input tokens in the ten dearest tasks, 335 M of it re-sent context, and prompts reached 308,000–329,000 tokens. Sol compacts only at 400,000 (`CODEX_COMPACT_TOKENS` in `scripts/goal/launch.ts`).

Make the compaction limit a field of the layered goalctl settings (`.goalctl/settings.schema.json`), with one value per model family, and set Sol's project default to 160,000 in `.goalctl/settings.json`, keeping Luna's 200,000. `GOAL_CODEX_COMPACT_TOKENS` keeps overriding both. Every launch, resume and wake reads the setting, including those the resident coordinator starts, without a coordinator restart. Update the comment in `launch.ts` to state the current rule and its measurement plan, not history.
