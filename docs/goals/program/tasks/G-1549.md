## Outcome

The duplicate-code report found two exact copies:

1. **Outbound address policy (security).** `connected-apps/protocol.ts:371-409`
   and `web-publication/transport.ts:22-60` each refuse non-public and
   special-purpose IP blocks before an outbound fetch. Keep one in
   `services/main/src/infrastructure/outbound-address.ts` and have both use it.
   If the copies differ, keep the stricter block list and say what changed.
2. **Child-process deadline.** `media-rendition/transform.ts:174-184` and
   `media-screen/classifier.ts:37-43` each kill a child when its deadline or
   abort fires. Keep one in `services/main/src/infrastructure/child-deadline.ts`.

Production lines must drop (show `git diff --numstat main...HEAD`).

Acceptance (unit):
- loopback, link-local, private, CGNAT, unique-local IPv6, IPv4-mapped IPv6 and
  a DNS name resolving to one of them are refused; a public address passes;
- a child that ignores SIGTERM is killed at the deadline, and an abort kills it at once.
