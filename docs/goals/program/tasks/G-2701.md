## Outcome

A shared owner does its job without recognising a vertical or a source (`docs/product/platform-thesis.md`; `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md`, Source adoption and credit, second paragraph). The audit evidence is in `.temp/ref/` (report-1.md, report-2.md, report-3.md).

Credit is split by provider and role. External credit adoption accepts only Open Library author occurrences, enforced by a SQL constraint and SHACL shape; a separate native Agent owner repeats admission and revision; public and discovery readers recognise each representation and filter roles. Extend the native credit owner so a participant is either a native Agent or a provider-qualified external reference, with admitted role vocabulary, keeping source-reported and human-confirmed status, order and evidence apart. Migrate Source adoption, catalogue writes, schemas, public and discovery queries, and retirement and recovery together. Keep external references distinct from resolved Agent identities and never fabricate a Person.

Likely files: `services/main/src/modules/source/{author-credit,reconcile-author-credit}.ts`, `work/{author-credit,read-contract}.ts`, `profiles/credits.ts`, `query/templates/work-credits.rq`, `discovery/credits.ts`, `model/definitions/{work-author-credit-v1,native-agent-credit-v1}.ttl`, a migration replacing the Open Library-only constraint.

Prove it with a test that fails before and passes after, on the real composition (`composeMain`), using a consumer other than the original one. Preserve exact versions, order, unknown authorship, truthful credit, rights evidence, private, refused and revoked states, idempotent recovery and bounded cost. Do not add a second engine, a framework or a per-vertical replacement, and do not move or rename a special case. Keep provider parsing and domain arithmetic in adapters. When the repair lands, remove its paragraph from `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md` in the same change, or narrow it to what remains.
