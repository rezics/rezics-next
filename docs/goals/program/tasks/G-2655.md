## Outcome

Building G-2587's retained Realm ranking source on wave/2 made 573 publications through the API. A placement publication took a median of 962 ms and a p95 of 2.8 s. With eight concurrent publications, they queued past the native 12 s deadline and answered with an unknown outcome. Populating the public rankings took 762.8 s, discussion siblings 521.4 s and nested discussion 131.9 s. The evidence is `.temp/goal/event-source-current-handoff.md` in G-2587's worktree (`.temp/worktrees/g-2587`); copy what you need.

A write that slow is a defect: every write goes through the API.

1. Profile one placement publication and one ranking population step on a quiet stack: SQL rows, graph calls, round trips and lock waits.
2. Find what grows with the Realm, the ranking or the batch, and make each publication touch a bounded neighbourhood.
3. Make eight concurrent placements finish within the native deadline, without raising it.
4. Prove it with a growth check on two seeded ranking sizes, and give before and after numbers.
