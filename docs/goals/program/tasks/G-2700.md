## Outcome

A shared owner does its job without recognising a vertical or a source (`docs/product/platform-thesis.md`; `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md`, Source adoption and credit, third paragraph). The audit evidence is in `.temp/ref/` (report-2.md, report-3.md).

Library import matching admits only ISBN and VNDB identifiers and chooses Open Library unless VNDB applies, and its automatic rights assessment reconstructs Source authority through an Open Library join inside `rights/store.ts`. Match every admitted identifier through the generic Release identifier lookup, put identifier grain and source selection in reviewed mappings, and generalise binding lookup and locking. Have Source verify the exact admitted binding and the importer policy supply the assessment input, while Rights keeps persistence and idempotency; share assessment construction with operator imports.

Likely files: `services/main/src/modules/library-import/{match,reader-import,open-library}.ts`, `services/main/src/modules/rights/store.ts`, `routes/library-imports.ts`, `apps/web/features/library/{library-import-rows.tsx,import-api.ts}`.

Prove it with a test that fails before and passes after, on the real composition (`composeMain`), using a consumer other than the original one. Preserve exact versions, order, unknown authorship, truthful credit, rights evidence, private, refused and revoked states, idempotent recovery and bounded cost. Do not add a second engine, a framework or a per-vertical replacement, and do not move or rename a special case. Keep provider parsing and domain arithmetic in adapters. When the repair lands, remove its paragraph from `docs/architecture/shared-capabilities.md` in the same change, or narrow it to what remains.
