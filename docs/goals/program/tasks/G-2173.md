## Outcome

A brief that is ready is dispatched, queued or resumed without a hand clearing the way. On 2026-10-13 each of these four stopped a brief of the api Goal until the program came by: two briefs stood held for an hour behind an empty worktree, two fix rounds were used up before any review, three commands stopped on a reference to a closed task, and two briefs written in one planning turn could not be queued behind the brief they wait for.

1. **An interrupted dispatch leaves nothing that refuses the next.** Where `dispatch` and the queue meet a worktree that exists (`scripts/goal/tasks.ts:94`, `scripts/goal/queue.ts:68`): when the ledger has no task of that ID, the branch has no commit that `main` lacks and the worktree has no change, remove both and go on; otherwise refuse as today.
2. **Handing a running worker a changed brief is no fix round.** `resume` (`scripts/goal/tasks.ts:350`) records `fix` for every resume that follows no `partial` or `blocked` handoff, so `stop`, `reclaim` and `resume` of a worker whose brief the manager extended used up the two fix rounds before any review (G-2142, corrected by hand in the ledger). A resume whose previous attempt was ended by `stop` and wrote no handoff is recorded as `step`, and the cap does not count it.
3. **A reference to a closed task still resolves.** Closing G-2138 removed its brief and its worktree; two open briefs named them in `refs:`, and `dispatch`, `reclaim` and `resume` then stopped on `Reference does not exist` (`scripts/goal/handover.ts:28`). A reference under `docs/goals/<goal>/tasks/` or `.temp/worktrees/<id>/.temp/goal/` whose task is closed is read from the copy the close kept (`.temp/goal-orchestration/runs/<id>/worktree-temp/goal/`); any other missing reference refuses as today.
4. **A brief can wait for a queued brief.** `waitsFor` (`scripts/goal/queue.ts:39`) throws `Unknown dependency` when a dependency is itself an entry of the queue and not yet a task, so a chain written in one turn (a brief behind a queued brief of the same Goal) cannot be queued. A dependency that is a queued entry of the same Goal is known: the brief waits with `depends on <id>, which is queued`, and is dispatched after it as any other dependency. A dependency that is neither a task nor a queued entry is refused as today, and so is one of another Goal.

Do not build: a change to `land`, to the coordinator or to planning, a change to how claims are judged, or any change to `resume` beyond the label in step 2.
