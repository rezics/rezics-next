## Outcome

Anime, Manga, Visual Novels and Games: Work hubs and edition choice, catalogues, episode and chapter inventories, tracking and two-device progress.

Review every production page of this area at phone width (390 px) and desktop width (1440 px), in English and Japanese. Use the Storybook stories that replay the recorded answers (`task storybook:test` with screenshots) and the browser journeys. Look for what a careful person would notice:

- broken or cramped layout, overflow, truncation and misalignment;
- inconsistent spacing or type scale;
- missing focus rings, and keyboard traps;
- empty, loading and error states that look unfinished or leak implementation words;
- untranslated strings, and Japanese line breaking;
- contrast below WCAG AA, and images without size or alt text.

Fix each defect in the page or the shared component that causes it. Keep the design system's tokens, and do not restyle what is right. Compare before and after screenshots yourself; quality comes from looking, not from adding tests. Hand off the before and after screenshots for each fix.
