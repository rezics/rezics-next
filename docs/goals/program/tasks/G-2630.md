## Outcome

`docs/goals/api/later.md` records that contributions and submissions cannot list one Work without scanning a capped population. A scan bounded by a cap answers wrongly once the population passes the cap, and costs work proportional to the cap rather than to the Work. Every operation must touch a bounded neighbourhood.

Give the per-Work contribution and submission listing an index-backed, bounded, paged read keyed by the Work. It must keep the same disclosure rules: private drafts are visible to their authors and to authorised reviewers only. Prove it with a growth test where the total population grows 10 to 1,000 while one Work keeps 5 contributions: work per page stays flat, and the listing is complete past the old cap. Add API tests as the author, a reviewer and a stranger.
