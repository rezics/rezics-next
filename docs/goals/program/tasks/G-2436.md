## Outcome

The web's surface for `realm/roles-and-team` (web's G-2235) replays this row's record through the production role dialog in `apps/web/features/manage/members-view.tsx`. The record's owner assignment (`role-impact-preview`, `role-assignment-receipt`) targets the stranger, who is not in the recorded roster, with a `validUntil` no duration choice of the dialog yields. The dialog cannot send that request, so the surface cannot replay its Save. `.temp/ref/g2235-attempt-2.last.md` is the surface's last handoff.

What must be true afterwards, each request taken from the production client (`useRoleChange` and `apps/web/features/manage/admin-api.ts`, imported, not copied) with the path, query and body production sends:

- A whole owner Save as the dialog sends it: the target is a person in the recorded `member-roster` answer who does not hold the role; the role is one of the dialog's choices; `validUntil` is the dialog's instant plus one of `roleDurations`; the reason is non-empty. Record the role-impact preview with its actual answer (including its reason and impact digest), then the save with the retry key and generation the dialog sends, then the read that shows the change. Record the instant the dialog takes as `openedAt` under its own label, so a replay with that clock builds the same body.
- The stranger's existing assignment stays as an API outcome of the proof: keep its assertions; it is not a dialog request, and the handoff says so.
- Read the production path of the members and roles pages once and compare it with the record's labels for each persona; record every read or write still missing and list those beyond the reference.
- The reads one render sends together start together (`Promise.all`); writes and reads that need an earlier answer keep their order. A label names what the request is and is fixed where the request is written, never a counter: the web chooses a sample by its label and expects exactly one.
- No assertion and no cell changes. Existing samples keep their answers apart from identities; if recording refreshes identities, every sample changes together.
- The record's requests run under their own deadline (120 s) after the proof, and the persona tests get the limit both need; the proof keeps its own deadline. One comment says which part is the proof's. One console line per persona gives the elapsed time of the proof and of the record. The file has a 480 s wall.
- A 5xx that is the final answer: hand off with the request and the answer, do not vary it; do not run a third time.

The handoff summary names old and new identities and every new label with its request and status.

Do not build: a change under `apps/web/` or `tests/qa/support/`, a new row, a request with another persona's bearer, a read at the Account origin.
