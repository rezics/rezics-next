## Outcome

`zone/attach-a-realm` is proved for five personas and pending for the operator, and nothing of it is recorded, so the web has no answers to build its attachment surface from. It matters now: a Realm made on the web has no site until its owner creates a Zone and attaches the Realm, and the web's page for the second half (`apps/web/features/manage/zone-attachments.tsx`, `apps/web/features/zone-editor/realm-attachment.tsx` and the readers they import) has no story on recorded answers.

What must be true afterwards:

1. The row's test records, through the outcome recorder the other rows use (see a recorded row such as `tests/qa/integration/outcomes/zone/publication.test.ts`), for each of the six personas: the reads the attachment page makes before the write, the attachment write as that page sends it, and the reads the page makes after it. Take the requests from the two web files and their readers, with the query parameters and headers they send; do not invent shapes.
2. Each persona's answer is recorded as the API gives it: the refusals of the visitor, stranger, member and of each single authority, the attachment by both authorities, the moderator's two cases. The operator's cell stays pending and its request is recorded with the answer it gets today.
3. After the successful attachment the record also holds what a site page then reads: the Zone's presentation (which names the attached Realm), the address read by the Zone's canonical key, and the Realm's read of its Zone as the Realm page asks it.
4. No assertion and no cell changes. If a request the page sends is answered 5xx, hand off with the request and the answer; do not vary it.
5. The handoff lists every label with its request and status, and every request the two web files can send that is still not in the record.

Do not build: a change under `apps/web/` or `tests/qa/support/`, a new row, a change of what the row proves.
