## Outcome

G-1494 (landed 3b05173f6) made the QA stack open every platform group (`REZICS_PLATFORM_OPEN_GROUPS=*`) and made an anonymous call to a closed operation answer 401 before any grant check. Two follow-ups it found:

1. `tests/qa/integration/realm-sanction-appeal.test.ts` (and check `realm-member-ban.test.ts`): an anonymous POST `/v1/realms/{realm}/member-receipts/{id}/appeal` on an open stack reaches the handler and answers 401 `account_assertion_denied` where the test expects 403 `platform_closed`. Do NOT weaken the test: split it. (a) A closed-gate case (construct the app with a closed gate, as G-1494 did for access-platform.integration.test.ts) proves anonymous => 401 `unauthorized`... follow the landed rule exactly (missing bearer on a closed operation = 401 before any grant lookup; authenticated without grant = 403 `platform_closed`; member-ban and appeal reads keep the identical 404 `appeal_unavailable`). (b) The open-stack case expects what the handler really answers for an anonymous caller. Keep every other assertion.
2. `tests/qa/integration/g-724-bootstrap.test.ts` fails with "Bootstrap did not receive a platform grant page" (QA 20261008t233603-58c81f-r1): the bootstrap reads GET /v1/access/grants, which is public, so opening groups does not change that body. Find what the test and `scripts/ops/bootstrap/platform-grant.ts` expect (the production bootstrap grants the first platform administrator through the API) and why the page is missing; classify as fixture drift against the landed grant contract or a real bootstrap defect, fix at the right layer with a falsifier. Redaction in platform-grant.ts was fixed by G-1456: keep it.
