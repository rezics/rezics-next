## Outcome

Since G-1235, platform administration consists of ordinary Access grants:
`platform:grant` plus `platform:use:platform-admin` and the resource grants.
Migration 1291 dropped the `access.platform_administrator` singleton. The
first-installation path still has to produce that state, and production must
not open without it.

1. **First designation.** `PLATFORM_FIRST_ADMIN_ACCOUNT`, consumed by Main's
   startup when no platform governance exists, gives that active principal:
   - a permanent `platform:grant`;
   - `platform:use:platform-admin`;
   - the administrator resource grants;
   - one audit receipt, committed atomically.

   Check what G-1235 left in the startup path (`services/main/src/index.ts`
   and `access/platform-*.ts`) and complete it.
   - Unknown or inactive subjects fail without designating anyone.
   - Once any permanent `platform:grant` holder exists, the setting is ignored
     and a log line says so.
2. **Further holders** come only through `platform:grant`, the ordinary
   grant path within the assignment ceiling. The record asks for at least two
   holders (§9). The last permanent holder cannot be removed; migration 1290
   already guarantees that.
3. **Verification refuses to open production without governance.**
   - Bootstrap verification (`scripts/ops/bootstrap`) fails unless at least one
     active principal holds a permanent `platform:grant`. It warns when fewer
     than two do.
   - `ops:env-check` states the rule.
   - Production readiness or opening never passes without that holder.
     Startup itself still runs, because the first operator must sign in first.
4. **Docs.** Rewrite the operator-and-authority part of
   `docs/operations/production-install.md` and the bootstrap paragraph of
   `docs/operations/deployment.md` to match. Delete the line "There is no
   additional administrator assignment path."

## Do not build

- No new administrator table.
- No second designation path.
- No Account-side administrator changes.
