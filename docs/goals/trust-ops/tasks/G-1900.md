Add to services/account/src/recovery-coverage.ts one explicit, versioned, reviewed exclusion list of usage-only columns/tables (reason beside each entry: monotone usage or counter telemetry that carries no credential, grant, scope, session-validity or policy meaning). Start with `rezics_account_grant.last_used_at` and only those rows/columns the measurement proves changed AND are pure telemetry (e.g. rate-limit counters). The digest excludes them for C0, C1 and the restored Account alike (same function), so base verification and the C0/C1 comparison stay consistent.

Do not exclude anything that the measurement did not show, and nothing that could hide a revocation.
