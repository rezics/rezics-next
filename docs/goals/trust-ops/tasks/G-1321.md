## Outcome

Re-verify and fix the backchannel review's follow-up: /revoke-sessions can delete the first
session's authored token links before later provider hooks snapshot their
OAuth families. Snapshot/retire every affected session/family under the
existing revocation fences before destructive link cleanup. Cover revoke-all,
revoke-other and single-session behavior; more than 100 sessions must finish
through bounded pages, never silently stop at the provider sample. Preserve
the newly disabled backchannel dispatch and the intentional survival of
consented third-party offline access after ordinary sign-out. Only session-bound
first-party families retire here. If current introspection already
closes the exact impact, prove it and keep the smallest necessary correction.

No new revocation service, global session scan, provider dependency edit or
unbounded all-user transaction. Selected first-party claims/refresh must stay revoked; unrelated connected-app
consents must remain intact.
