## Outcome

Account recovery's social factor and email protections cannot be turned against
the person they protect.

1. **Guardian consent lifecycle.** Today a signed-in user who types another
   person's email makes that person their guardian at once
   (`recovery-claim.ts`, around lines 42–60). That then blocks that person from
   deleting their own account (`auth.ts`, around line 181), and they can neither
   refuse nor leave. Make it a lifecycle: invite, then accept, then the person
   may withdraw at any time. An invitation expires.
   - Only an accepted guardian approves a recovery.
   - Only an accepted guardian who has not been used up blocks their own
     account deletion, and they see why and can withdraw first.
   - An invitation that was never accepted, has been withdrawn or has expired
     blocks nothing.
2. **No email-existence signal.** Enrollment answers the same way whether or
   not the invited email belongs to an account (today 200 versus 403, around
   `recovery-claim.ts:55`). Delivery tells the invitee, not the inviter.
3. **Turnstile before target budgets.** Email enrollment and reset currently
   spend the 3-per-300-second target budget for the caller-supplied email
   before Turnstile is verified (`app.ts`, around lines 169–182; `auth.ts`,
   around lines 348–352). Three invalid requests lock a victim out of password
   reset. Verify Turnstile first, then charge the target budget.
   - Keep a per-caller budget for failed CAPTCHA attempts.
4. **Accounts UI.** It shows a pending invitation, lets the invitee accept or
   decline it, and lets an accepted guardian withdraw. It follows the Google
   Account style the site already uses.
   T3 built the recovery claim flow but not enrollment: the owner also needs to
   set up recovery (choose a guardian, get a code) and renew the code in
   Accounts, as part of this same lifecycle.

Re-verify IAM-08, verify/06 #3 and the enrollment-existence spot check in
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/arch-review/README.md` (lines 162 and 248–249) before you fix them.

5. **Regression.** `services/account/tests/g-543-turnstile-browser.test.ts`
   ("offline enrollment works at localhost…") fails on main: the sign-up page
   offers no `Name` textbox within 30 s. Find whether the sign-up form or the
   test drifted, fix the right one, and keep the test exercising the real
   Turnstile flow.

## Do not build

- No silent guardian assignment as a fallback.
- No second recovery mechanism.
- No change to how factors are revoked; T3 owns that and this builds on it.
