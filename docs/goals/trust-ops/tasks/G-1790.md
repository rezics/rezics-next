## Manager decision (2026-10-12), after attempt 2: the D3 aggregate ceiling needs a shared preflight budget

D3 is an aggregate bound: ONE request may not exceed 64 graph revisions, 64 indexed units, 256 targets and the 8 MiB metadata ceiling in TOTAL, refused before any destructive work.
