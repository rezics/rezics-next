### F3 — Medium: unreviewed claimant correspondence permanently vetoes restoration

**Evidence.** A copyright reporter credential can append `claimant_action` using self-authored court, case number and document digest, without contact ownership verification or an adjudicating decision (`services/main/src/modules/public-report/contract.ts:65-69`; `services/main/src/modules/public-report/store.ts:269-282`). Its immutable process step has no decision_id (`services/main/src/modules/public-report/store.ts:290-298`; `services/main/migrations/access/061_moderation_decision.sql:167-169`). Restoration rejects any such case step matching the restriction **or having NULL decision_id** (`services/main/src/modules/rights/counter-notice.ts:168-177`). Restore/reverse and resumed effects invoke this gate; scheduled restoration also treats claimant action as a stay (`services/main/src/modules/governance/store.ts:684-698,1183-1196,1509-1512`).

**Exploit, inference.** A malicious claimant enters invented court details and a valid-format digest. After a real moderator restriction, the assertion blocks automatic restoration and explicit moderator reversal. NULL also matches later restrictions in that case; the gate consults no reviewed rejection or override. The attacker still needs a real staff restriction to cause the initial takedown. The defect is the persistent veto over recovery, not automatic takedown from intake.

**Smallest fix and acceptance.** Keep the assertion as intake bound to the exact complaint/restriction. Existing authorized review must establish or reject the stay, with an appended immutable resolution that restoration recognizes. Fabricated filing followed by restriction/counter-notice: an authorized moderator can reject it and restore after applicable gates. A genuine accepted filing stays only its answered restriction; later restrictions remain independently adjudicable. Cover pre-decision intake and a filing during pending restoration.


### F4 — Medium: an earlier ordinary dispute poisons later copyright counter-notice credentials

**Evidence.** Authenticated rights intake accepts ordinary_dispute and dmca_512 under the same rights_complaint kind (`services/main/src/routes/rights.ts:206-224`). Case matching ignores complaint process; process is stored per report (`services/main/src/modules/governance/store.ts:383-413`). A decision can use the later report's evidence basis (`services/main/src/modules/governance/decision-evidence.ts:21-28`), but affected notices always mint credentials against the earliest report (`services/main/src/modules/governance/notices.ts:98-121`). Public correspondence rejects counter-notice if that credential's report is not DMCA (`services/main/src/modules/public-report/store.ts:266-280`). Authenticated counter-notice resolves the same affected credential/report (`services/main/src/modules/governance/store.ts:1454-1466`), and the shared helper requires that exact report to have a DMCA complaint (`services/main/src/modules/rights/counter-notice.ts:47-55`).

**Exploit, inference.** An admitted reporter submits the ordinary dispute first. A later copyright complaint joins the case and receives a real moderator restriction based on its evidence. The affected party's issued credentials still reference the first ordinary dispute, so both counter-notice transports deny recovery. This requires the initial ordinary-report authority and a later staff restriction; it does not depend on an uncertain policy for multiple independent claimants.

**Smallest fix and acceptance.** Bind affected notice credentials to the decision's applicable complaint/evidence basis, retaining explicit report/restriction identity. Ordinary dispute first, DMCA second, restriction on DMCA evidence: the affected party's valid counter-notice succeeds through both public credential and authenticated transports. Credentials must not be redirected to another complaint by report insertion order.


Both findings live in the same files (counter-notice window, notice credentials, claimant correspondence): one session. Careful with the DMCA process timing: keep the delivered-counter-notice, not_before and undelivered-notice rules exactly; only add the adjudication of a claimant stay and the basis binding of credentials. Keep authorization: only an authorized decider may reject a stay.
