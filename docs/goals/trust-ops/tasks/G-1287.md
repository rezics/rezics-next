## Outcome

On main at 18e2983b5, `scripts/ops/tests/platform-governance.integration.test.ts`
fails reproducibly, twice in a row from a pinned worktree, through
`task goal -- test`:

- "first governance designation is atomic, …" passes;
- "revoking the designated holder ends the seeded assignment ceiling" fails,
  followed by "Unhandled error between tests … pool.emit('error') …
  Connection terminated unexpectedly".

The ceiling was linked to its `platform:grant` by migrations 1440 and 1441
(G-1257, first written as 1297/1298, then 1340/1341, then 1420/1421). The
worker's own runs passed, so the difference may be ordering or state against
current main.

Find why the connection ends. Candidates:
- a trigger or fence that calls `pg_terminate_backend`;
- an `idle_in_transaction` or `transaction_timeout` kill from the
  connection bounds;
- the test reusing a client after a revoke ends its session;
- a migration renumbered past another migration it depends on.

Fix the product if revocation terminates sessions it should not. Fix the test
if it misuses the pool. Keep the property under test: after the designated
holder is revoked, that holder can no longer grant, and the second holder
still can.
