## Outcome

Qualify the US, Taiwan, Singapore, Japan, South Korea and EU launch gates
within the maintainer's zero-budget scope decision. Read trust-and-safety.md,
the legal owner drafts and actual registration/suitability/telemetry policies.
Use current primary official sources to verify uncertain legal duties; send
no repository text to outside services. Produce one concise decision matrix:
registration, adult/image features, privacy/transfer duties, analytics,
representatives, available code evidence and exact external operator inputs.
Distinguish implemented controls, accepted risk and unmet launch conditions.
EU stays reachable; uploads are intended at launch with layered controls;
do not silently substitute a geographic exclusion or text-only launch.

Smallest output is reasons and readiness decisions, with focused tests only
where existing policy has executable acceptance. No new legal/policy engine,
paid services, policy promises beyond code, scanner or expanded data collection.
Unresolved third-party registrations/operator identity remain explicit blocks,
not a reason to stop the local review. Do not change legal facts or UI.
