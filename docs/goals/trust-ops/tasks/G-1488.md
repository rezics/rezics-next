## Outcome

Launch's sanction-appeal UX (G-1485, on landed G-1438) needs one read: a banned member cannot
reach their appeal because `GET /v1/realms/{realm}/member-receipts/{receiptId}/appeal` needs the
receipt id and nothing gives the member their own receipt. Add, in the appeal module:

- `GET /v1/realms/{realm}/member-ban?actingSubject=<agent>`, exposure `platform:realm-appeals`,
  bearer, rate-limit family read.
- Only the principal who controls `actingSubject` may call it (reuse the controller proof the
  appeal read already uses). **404 when that subject has no ban, and the identical 404 for every
  other caller** (not controller, anonymous, wrong realm, expired ban): same status, body shape
  and problem code; add a test that compares the full responses byte-for-byte except the request id.
- Body: the existing appeal reading shape of G-1438 (action ban, recorded reason, `bannedUntil` or
  permanent, `happenedAt`, the receipt id the existing appeal routes take, the appeal with its
  state), plus `decidedAt` on a decided appeal. Never the decider, and the rationale follows the
  disclosure rule already implemented in `readRealmSanctionAppeal` (private disclosure => outcome only).
- An expired ban (`expires_at` passed) counts as no ban for this read; the latest active ban
  receipt wins if there are several.

Do not change the appeal routes, the governance case model or any migration. Run `task gen`.

## Checks

Real-PostgreSQL integration through real HTTP: member sees own ban with and without an appeal and
after a decision (all three disclosure values), another member / a moderator / anonymous get the
identical 404, expired ban is 404, decider identity absent from the whole JSON. Show the controller
guard and the 404-equality test fail with the check removed (state the mutation and QA id). Update
`platform-exposure.test.ts` counts and the operation review. `task goal -- test`, lint, AST, types. No `--heavy`.
