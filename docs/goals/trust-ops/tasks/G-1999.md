### F5 — Medium: public case status exposes private targets from another report

**Evidence.** Anonymous intake is supported (`services/main/src/routes/public-reports.ts:15-18,28-35`) and reports share an open case by target/component/context/scope/kind (`services/main/src/modules/public-report/store.ts:111-118`; `services/main/migrations/access/060_governance_case.sql:32-35`). Authenticated reports may include independently authorized secondary evidence, including private Content (`services/main/src/routes/reports.ts:47-59`; `services/main/src/modules/governance/store.ts:350-370`; `services/main/src/modules/governance/evidence.ts:206-215`). Decision target evidence membership is case-wide (`services/main/src/modules/governance/decision-evidence.ts:44-60`).

Status validates the individual report credential (`services/main/src/modules/public-report/store.ts:170-177`), then returns every effect of the case decision with target resource, receipt, continuation and error, unfiltered by that party's evidence entitlement (`218-239`). `operationOutcome` preserves those items (`services/main/src/modules/operation/outcome.ts:85-90`). Reasons have a disclosure guard, but this projection does not (`services/main/src/modules/public-report/store.ts:199-200`).

**Exploit, inference.** A private report on public A includes private B; staff decides both cited targets. An anonymous caller reports A into the same open case and uses its own credential to learn B's native ID and effect receipts. No stolen credential or moderator privilege is needed. This is identity/progress disclosure, not proof of B's body disclosure.

**Smallest fix and acceptance.** Retain full case-wide operation progress for staff; project only party-entitled targets and avoid concealed counts. Two-target private report A/B, staff decision, anonymous report on A: its status contains no B identifier, receipt, continuation or concealed target count. Staff retains full progress; affected parties receive their specific entitled projection.
