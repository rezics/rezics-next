## Outcome

Re-verify and fix the program's live QA defect: discovery refresh purge holds an
Access client then a derived-generation helper checks another out of the same
pool. With the T6 detector it throws every tick (312k error lines in22minutes).
Pass the held client into the exact lifecycle operation and keep its writes in
the caller's transaction. Audit same-class helper calls in these two files;
standalone callers still get the existing bounded transaction. Reproduce with
nested checkout configured to throw and a one-client pool; rollback/retry must
preserve retirement/generation/lease fences and not spin logs.

No larger pool, disabled detector, generic transaction framework, new service
or altered lifecycle/authority semantics. Paths are currently unclaimed.
