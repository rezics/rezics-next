## Outcome

Two guard repairs in trust-ops' area.

1. **List convention.** `scripts/static/list-convention.test.ts` is red. Every
   collection read answers `complete: boolean`, and `items` where the body is
   a list. Follow the shape of a passing read. Fix:
   - `GET /v1/access/grants`, both its `platform-grants-v1` and
     `work-create-agent-grants-v1` shapes (`routes/access-authority.ts`);
   - `GET /v1/me/managed-realms` (`routes/managed-realms.ts`, and
     `management-reads.ts` if the shape lives there).

   Update callers you find in tests. A web caller in `apps/**` is launch's
   area: list it in the handoff and do not edit it. Run `task gen` and commit
   the regenerated OpenAPI. `GET /v1/public-reports/{caseId}` is G-1275's.
2. **Admin-predicate tests.** Since G-1235, `platformAdministratorAction`
   checks syntax only. Authority comes from the exact `platform:resource:
   <action>` grant on the target scope (`platformAdministratorTargetAllowed`)
   plus target authorization at register, read, replay and claim. Migration
   1290 seeds those grants only for the scopes the old closed list allowed.

   Move the negative assertions in `scoped-judgments.test.ts` and
   `rating-question-presentation.test.ts` from the predicate to admission:
   with the seeded grants, a rating-context or semantic question-presentation
   target is refused. Do not weaken them into asserting nothing.

3. **Account recovery test applies every relay migration.**
   `services/account/tests/account-access-recovery.integration.test.ts`, OPS03
   and IAM10 ("two-owner deletion cut rejects either missing WAL frontier"),
   fails with `column "stream_scope" of relation "checkpoint" does not
   exist`. The test applies a hard-coded subset of relay migrations (around
   lines 224 and 341: 001–006, then 010–013) and misses kernel's relay
   `020_main_stream_scope.sql`. Apply every relay migration in order, through
   `scripts/lib/migration-order.ts`, as the erasure graph-purge test does by
   looping over the directory. Then the next relay migration cannot break it.
