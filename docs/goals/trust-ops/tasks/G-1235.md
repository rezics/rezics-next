## Outcome

Gradual opening (maintainer, 2026-10-07: "所有需要打磨的，都可以暫時不開放，然後我們逐步完善，逐步開放.").
Every operation that is not public is closed behind a platform permission. Design:
§9, "平台權限與逐步開放", in
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/arch-review-2/target-architecture.md`.

1. **Exposure beside the route.** Every Main operation declares `exposure` in
   its route module's `openApiOperations` entry: `'public'` or
   `'platform:<group>'`.
   - `openApiOperations` becomes complete: every served route has an entry,
     including health, MCP, the OAuth protected-resource metadata and the
     WebSocket upgrades.
   - A static check fails when a route has no entry. At runtime, a missing
     declaration is closed.
2. **The gate.** A closed operation admits only a principal (directly or
   through a group) that holds the Access grant `platform:use:<group>` or
   `platform:use:<operationId>`. Otherwise it answers one problem type, a 403
   `platform_closed`, before the handler runs.
   - The resource authority check still runs afterwards; both must pass.
   - Bounded: the gate compares the principal's few platform grants with the
     operation's group, cached by grant generation. Its cost never grows with
     users, operations or resources.
3. **Template- and command-bound gates.** A generic operation must not reach a
   closed capability. Bind the gate to the template, profile or command the
   server selects, never to a field the client declares. The operations are
   listed in the approved matrix, §2.5: `/v1/query` and the other query
   operations, `/v1/exports` source kinds, generic semantic, content and
   relation edits, Person-owned adoptable contexts, and non-first-party Zone
   blocks.
   - Put the binding where the selection happens. If that is in another Goal's
     area (kernel's `query/**`, `semantic/**`; launch's `zone/**`), add the one
     function those sites call to `access/exposure*.ts`, and list each call
     site in the handoff as a request for its owner.
4. **Platform grants.**
   - `platform:use:*` and `platform:grant` are ordinary Access grants, with
     episode, expiry and receipt.
   - `platform:grant` can grant and revoke any `platform:*` permission within
     the existing assignment ceiling.
   - The last holder of `platform:grant` cannot be removed.
   - Replace the hard-coded action list in `access/platform-administrator.ts`
     and the `access.platform_administrator` singleton (migration 983) with
     grants: `platform:use:platform-admin` plus the resource permissions it
     listed. Add a migration that gives the current singleton holder
     `platform:grant` and those grants, so existing stacks and the dev seed
     keep their administrator.
   - Update its readers to match: `rate-limit/store.ts` classification,
     `access/contexts.ts` and `scripts/qa/serialization-points.ts`. These are
     owner changes; check `task goal -- owner` first.
5. **The exposure summary**, which the web reads to hide closed sections as a
   class. The shape is fixed, because launch builds against it now:

   ```text
   GET /v1/me/platform-access        exposure: public; works without a bearer
   200 { "groups": string[], "operations": string[], "generation": string }
       cache-control: private, no-store
   ```

   - `groups` lists the closed groups the viewer holds `platform:use` for.
   - `operations` lists the operation IDs the viewer was granted individually,
     at most 64.
   - An anonymous viewer gets empty lists.
   - Generated OpenAPI marks every operation with `x-rezics-exposure`, and the
     SDK carries it. An operation is open to a viewer when its exposure is
     `public`, its group is in `groups`, or its ID is in `operations`.
   - The MCP `tools/list` omits operations closed for the caller.
   - Give the new route a rate-limit class (`rate-limit/routes.ts`). If T6
     (G-1218) still holds that file, hand off with that blocker.
6. **Apply the approved launch matrix in this same change.**
   - Source: `/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/trust-ops/launch-matrix-approved.md`.
     Use Appendix A, as amended by its section "launch: answers":
     - composition stages, seals and refreshes move to `catalogue-import`;
     - `POST /v1/compositions/{id}/restorations` stays public, because it is
       a recovery path (kernel confirmed this);
     - so do composition create and change, and the reads of a composition,
       its revisions and its occurrences;
     - `POST /v1/recommendations/pages` and `/queries` go to a new group,
       `recommendations`.
   - Beside the `GET …/seals/{seal}` declaration, add a comment: it reopens if
     a public book page ever reads an imported seal.
   - Result: 517 public and 247 closed of 764 operations, plus the routes
     outside the inventory (§2.4) declared public.
   - Generate the declarations mechanically from the matrix and commit them.
     The script itself does not stay in the tree.
   - Edits to `services/main/src/routes/*.ts` are owner changes: one `exposure`
     field per entry, nothing else. The manager merges them with
     `--allow-scope`. If an entry already disagrees with the matrix, keep the
     matrix value and list the entry.
7. **Record in `docs/contracts/api.md`** how exposure works:
   - the declaration;
   - opening a group is a reviewed change to its declarations, with no
     code-path or flag change;
   - Account routes are out of scope: they are identity foundation and stay
     open; a non-foundation Account feature needs a gate before it ships.

## Do not build

- No feature-flag service.
- No operation registry runtime.
- No second policy evaluator.
- No per-resource permission fan-out.
- No change to Account routes.
- No group state stored outside the route declarations.
- Controller continuity is T2 (G-1220), which may run beside you on the
  admission and baseline files. Do not edit those files.

## Checks

- Unit tests of `access/exposure*` and `platform-*`; `task gen`;
  `task main:typecheck`; the static check fails on a deliberately undeclared
  route.
- Integration tests through `task goal -- test <files>`. They prove there is
  no lockout:
  - On a stack with zero `platform:use:*` grants, no operation in the matrix's
    lockout-foundation list (§3.1) answers `platform_closed` for an ordinary
    user, and neither does an anonymous public read.
  - A closed saved-views operation is refused without the grant, then allowed
    with it, then refused again after revocation and after expiry.
  - A `platform:grant` holder grants and revokes `platform:use:saved-views`.
  - Removing the last `platform:grant` holder is refused.
  - A grant beyond the assignment ceiling is refused.
  - A closed template reached through `POST /v1/query`, and a closed command
    reached through a generic edit, are both refused.
  - The exposure summary answers for anonymous, ordinary and granted viewers.
- The handoff lists every owner change and every call site requested from
  another Goal.
