## Decision: published-author lifecycle contract reading

Accept the wave's `author-management-outcomes.json` correction, exact
member-reply history and two withdrawal proposals as targets for separate
implementation by the existing Content, Work, qualification and Realm-reply
owners. This ruling is independent of, and does not revise or reopen, the Studio
inventory contract already adopted for G-2753.

Corrections reuse existing draft/edit, publication, qualification and placement
operations. Keep the chapter Work, Post and reply identities; bind predecessor,
exact revision/digest and expected heads to the acknowledged intent. Saving a
draft changes no public bytes. Extend the existing exact Content revision reader
to retained member-reply revisions: public bytes require a live placement for
that exact revision, while author-private history uses existing Access and
retained provenance even after placement withdrawal. Preserve immutable reply
root/parent/origin coordinates and author attribution. Unknown or unauthorized
private history remains 404; rights, restrictions, disclosure and erasure still
apply. Add no parallel history store or publication authority.

Accept `content-search-eligibility-withdrawal-v1` on
`POST /v1/content-search-eligibility` and
`realm-reply-placement-withdrawal-v1` on `POST /v1/realm-reply-placements` as
explicit union additions, retaining the proposed exact target/head receipts.
Require authenticated control and ownership of the selected qualification or
direct author placement, existing live authority and Idempotency-Key. Creation
returns 201; identical-intent retry returns the same acknowledgement with 200
and `replayed=true`; changed intent under a key and fresh stale-head attempts
return the proposed 409 conflicts. Unauthenticated and authenticated non-owner
commands refuse with 401 and 403 respectively. Realm ownership, membership,
moderation and platform roles alone confer no author mutation authority.

Qualification withdrawal ends only its selected support, preserving the Content
publication head, exact history and independent publication/adoption support.
Placement withdrawal ends only the selected Realm/reply placement, preserving
other publishers' placements and other authors' replies. Structure occurrence
removal, Work publication withdrawal, qualification withdrawal and placement
withdrawal remain distinct effects; none substitutes for another or erases
independently authorized bytes.

This ruling releases no production or baseline/fixed execution under the outcome
brief. Preserve genuine stopped-corpus/restore prerequisites, the original
product baseline, G-2768's registry ownership and all twelve independent persona
proof requirements. Contract acceptance, historical setup failures and static
checks establish no proved outcome or GUI acceptance.
