## Outcome

Every address a chapter had while it was a Work keeps working with a real
HTTP 301 to the chapter's place in its Book's reader, per
[Posts, texts and Works](../../../contracts/work-and-release.md#posts-texts-and-works)
(chapter IRIs did not change when chapters became `rv:Post`).

Today `/en/w/<chapter-id>` reaches the reader only through a redirect issued
inside the streamed page (`apps/web/features/work-page/read.ts`), so it answers
200 first. An old chapter alias does not resolve at all: Main's
`modules/address/resolution.ts` returns `unavailable` when a `work`-scope
address's holder summary is not of type `work` (a Post's summary comes from the
Post adapter in `modules/media/summary.ts`).

1. **Main.** A `work`-scope address (ID or alias) whose holder is a Post
   resolves as moved, with canonical = the Post's reader place:
   `/w/<book>/read/<post>` for its one active Book placement, the Book's
   Contents when the Book places it more than once, and the first Book by
   stable order when several Books place it (as `post-read-v2` already orders
   placements). An unreadable Book or Post stays unavailable.
2. **Web.** The address edge and redirect (`apps/web/features/address/`) answer
   that resolution with HTTP 301 before rendering, in every locale. Remove
   the in-page chapter redirect once the edge covers it.
3. Retired aliases keep their existing behaviour.

Smallest change. Do not build: new address scopes, alias migration of chapter
Works to a new scope, or client-side guesses.

## Read

`modules/address/resolution.ts` (≈290–345), `modules/media/summary.ts` (Post
adapter), `apps/web/features/address/edge.ts` and `redirect.ts`, `modules/post/read.ts`.
