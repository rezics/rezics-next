## Outcome

People see chapters as Posts of their Book everywhere, and nothing a reader
relies on changes, per
[Posts, texts and Works](../../../contracts/work-and-release.md#posts-texts-and-works).

1. **Durable addresses.** Every address a chapter Work had before the
   migration (`/w/<ref>` with its ID or alias, in every locale) still opens
   that chapter in its Book's reader with a permanent redirect, or the Book's
   Contents when it has no place now. Serve it from Main: `GET /v1/posts/{id}`
   (`post-read-v1`, `modules/post/read.ts`) exists; add its active Book
   placements (Book and occurrence, bounded, as a new profile version if the
   shape changes), not client guesses. Chapter IRIs did not change in the
   migration, so an old `/w/<id>` resolves to a Post.
2. **Studio.** The chapter list and editor address, title and label chapters
   as Posts; nothing says "Work" for a chapter.
3. **Reader and Work page.** Chapter headings, "Chapter N · title", previous
   and next, and Contents read the Post and its occurrence.
4. **Manage and search.** Queue subjects and labels name a chapter as a Post of
   its Book; search results show the matched chapter's title and link to its
   place in the Book.
5. **Feed cards** keep "Chapter 212 · its title" with the Book attached; if the
   card's web file lies in another Goal's area, propose the change instead.

Use the existing components and design tokens. Do not build new pages for
Posts, a Post feed, or Post-level ratings.

Generated artifacts (`generated/**`, `packages/model/src/generated/**`) are
regenerated with `task gen` and allowed at merge; do not hand-edit them.
