## Outcome

A regression from the chapter→Post change (main-wide holder's bisect: passes at
58dee1a1a, fails from b2c8bc0cd/e22b0f36f): `tests/qa/integration/g-542-endpoints.test.ts`
≈188 restricts a Book's `title` component (owner graph, revision = the Book's
`rv:head`) and expects the public content phrase search for its chapter text to
return 0 results; it returns 2. Evidence:
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/verify/.artifacts/qa/20261005t021508-e5ff27/logs/integration-r3.log`.

Before b2c8bc0cd a chapter MatchUnit carried `rv:searchResultWork` (its Book),
so the hit was the Book's and the Book's disclosure restrictions applied. Now
`content-publication/search.ts` (≈218) binds the parent Book through
`postBookPlacement` + `publicWork` in an OPTIONAL, and `disclosure/search.ts`
gates the Post's name/body but not the placing Book's restricted components.

Restore the rule the test states: a chapter Post's hit is withheld when its
Book's title (or any component the Book's disclosure fence withholds) is
restricted, and a Post with no readable Book placement is not returned as a
Book's chapter. Keep the existing assertion unchanged; fix the code, not the test.
Prefer the smallest change (gate on the placing Book in the disclosure selection)
over re-adding `searchResultWork` writes, unless the relay/rebuild genuinely need
it; explain the choice in the handoff.
