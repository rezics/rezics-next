## Outcome

A Post that evidence identifies as a Work of its own becomes one without moving
anything, as
[Posts, texts and Works](../../../contracts/work-and-release.md#posts-texts-and-works)
decides: the link "adds a Work without moving the Post's text, custody,
discussion or occurrences", only on evidence (separate authorship, standing
alone under its own title, separate translation, publication or citation).

1. **Command.** Identify a Post as a Work: the Post, one evidence kind from
   that list with an optional source (URL or citation), and either a new Work
   (title per language and Work type) or an existing Work. Compose existing
   admitted commands, in the way `bootstrapAdmittedStructureOwner` composes two
   (deterministic sub-operation keys, replay-safe, no compensating deletion):
   the Work creation command (new Work), a Book-profile Structure for its Main
   Version whose single chapter occurrence places the same Post, and a part-of
   relation occurrence from the Work to the Book whose Structure placed the Post
   (the seeded lexicon definition `composition-part`, roles `whole` and `part`,
   in `scripts/dev/seed/relation-lexicon-data.ts`; add no new definition).
   Record the evidence on the identification receipt and the relation.
2. **Authority.** The Post's custody holder or a maintainer of the Book that
   places it may identify; anyone else uses the existing correction or proposal
   path. No new governance.
3. **Reads.** An identification read for a Post names the Works it realizes
   (your own route; `modules/post/read.ts` belongs to the surfaces task); the identified Work's
   page and reader read its text through its composition, so ratings, reviews,
   search and follows apply to the Work as to any other. A small "also a Work"
   link component for the reader, which the surfaces task places.
4. **Undo.** Retiring the relation and the Work uses their existing commands;
   the Post and its occurrences are never touched.

Smallest change that satisfies the decision. Do not build: automatic
identification, a review queue, text copies, a new relation mechanism or
changes to Work creation itself.

Generated artifacts (`generated/**`, `packages/model/src/generated/**`) are
regenerated with `task gen` and allowed at merge; do not hand-edit them.

## Read

The decision above and its Work-level rule;
`services/main/src/modules/structure/README.md` (bootstrap composition);
`modules/relation/README.md`; the Post read and custody from G-1079;

chapter identified as a new Work by its writer (searchable, ratable, readable,
the Book unchanged); a chapter identified as an existing Work; a stranger refused;
replay after a lost response; undo leaves the Post and Book intact. A story for
the link component. `task ast-grep -- scan`; `task gen` with regenerated
claimed paths committed.
