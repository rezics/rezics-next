## Outcome

Implement [Posts, texts and Works](../../../contracts/work-and-release.md#posts-texts-and-works)
in the backend: a chapter is an `rv:Post` placed in its Book's Structure, and no
command mints a chapter Work. Today `structure/change.ts` (`intent.newWork`,
≈1155–1330) writes a `schema:CreativeWork` with a MetadataOnly Main Version,
`schema:isPartOf` the Book, a maintainer set and two revision anchors per Studio
chapter, because `content-publication/draft.ts` (≈53–66) admits Content only on
a Work with a Main Version and a Book chapter occurrence needs a target. The
Content service itself accepts any resource (replies and Organizations already
hold Content). Progress, reading position, notifications, feed cards, rankings
and the reader URL are already keyed on the occurrence.

1. **The Post.** One kernel type, `rv:Post`, in the current graph: label(s) per
   language, the acting Agent as `rv:publisher`, `rv:head` with a revision
   anchor, and the existing protection and erasure predicates. Add its native
   shape the way other profiles are added (`model/definitions/post-v1.ts` plus
   its accepted profile).
2. **Creation.** `POST /v1/works/:id/chapters` and its composition change create
   a Post and place it as the chapter occurrence. Receipts and the outbox event
   name the Post; replaying an old receipt that names `rv:chapterWork` still
   resolves.
3. **Content, custody, rights, comments.** Content draft, publish and
   search-eligibility admit a Post. Custody reuses the per-resource maintainer
   set and author proof keyed by the Post IRI, so a co-writer controls only
   their own chapter and a Book maintainer transfer behaves as today. Rights
   assessments and Realm submission of a chapter publication accept a Post.
   Readers may comment on a public Post's text (today Studio chapter text needs
   an explicit grant because `publicWork` fails for chapter Works: fix it).
4. **Migration.** Existing chapter Works (a CreativeWork with `schema:isPartOf`
   a Book, a MetadataOnly Main Version and a Book chapter placement) become
   Posts **under the same IRIs**, so Content variants, progress, comments,
   Access rows and rights keep their keys. Their Main Version and `isPartOf`
   leave the current graph; history graphs are untouched. Follow the resumable,
   bounded startup backfill pattern (`library/backfill.ts`); it must be
   idempotent and finish on the dev and QA backups within the data-preparation
   budget. Seeds create Posts through the same route.
5. **Readers.** Every reader of chapter text or authority reads the Post:
   work-contents, continue, Studio chapters and works, serial projection, Zone
   modules, feed source, Realm submission, content search and its relay and
   rebuild, the search index rewrites in `work/select-main.ts`,
   `work/select-realm.ts` and `work/search-*`, translation and source-rights
   climbs (`work/translation-links.ts`: Post → occurrence → Book), library,
   also-enjoyed, management context, discovery, progress visibility and
   disclosure. Map a Post to its Book through its active Book placement only.
   Then delete what only existed for chapter Works: the 13 `isPartOf` and 7
   chapter-placement hide filters, `isPartOf` as chapter ancestry, and the
   chapter→Book folding that has nothing left to fold. A Work placed in a Book
   (an anthology entry) is a Work and stays visible in the catalogue.
6. **Contract.** Search's `matchedChapter.work` becomes the Post and its Book;
   update the claimed web consumers minimally so the web compiles and the
   reader, Studio editor and Work page keep working. Visual and copy changes
   belong to the next task.

Discovery (agreed with the addresses-discovery manager, who must stay green):
give `studio.chapter.create` and any new post action an explicit classification
in `discovery/effects.ts` (`'work'` on the owning Book, or `'irrelevant'` if
Discover no longer reads it), never unclassified, because an unknown action
rebuilds every Discover and rating scope. Chapters never appear as Works in
Discover, and chapter feed cards keep Book + occurrence. Cost assertions stay
unchanged and are re-measured: 4 graph calls / 46 SQL statements per irrelevant
event, 8 / 126 per Work-local delta, constant at 1k and 10k Works.

Smallest change that satisfies the decision. Do not build: a generic resource
abstraction, a Post service or store, renamed Access tables or stored scope
strings, author's notes, Work identification of a Post, Realm discussions as
Posts, or UI beyond what keeps the web compiling. Do not touch
`services/main/src/routes/work-reads.ts`, `realm-reply/root.ts`,
`review/read.ts` or `outbox/relay.ts` (another Goal's open task); if one
must change, stop and tell the manager.
