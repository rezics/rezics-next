## Outcome

`access.scope_gate` rows fence authority. Closing a scope or bumping its
`authority_epoch` must exclude every command admitted under the old epoch.

Admission's register, claim and seal steps take the gate FOR UPDATE for every
action except `rating.observation.set`
(`services/main/src/modules/access/admission.ts:157-163`, used at about 946, 1397
and 1714). About 25 other sites also take a gate FOR UPDATE (find them with
`git grep -nE "scope_gate[^;]*FOR UPDATE"`). Some keys are per object, such as
`publication:select:<version>`, and those are fine. Others are constant and
shared by unrelated targets:
- `'work:create:root'`, which every Realm join or leave, join request, grant,
  role, representation, invitation and `work.create` takes up to three times;
- `access:representation-topology`;
- `space:create:root`, `semantic:create:root`, `relation:create:root`;
- `verification:*:global`, `classification:define:global`;
- `context:create:*`, `projection:create:root`.

So these unrelated commands run one at a time, and FOR SHARE readers
(`admission.ts:816`, `role-proof.ts:32`, `private-recipient-proof.ts:41/81`)
queue behind them.

Classify every FOR UPDATE site by its key (constant or per object) and by what
the exclusive lock actually protects:
- **Changing the gate itself** (closing it, bumping the epoch,
  `group_generation`): keep FOR UPDATE.
- **Only checking the gate is open at an epoch:** take a shared lock, as the
  rating path already does. Closing still waits for those commands and still
  excludes new ones.
- **Mutual exclusion between peers that the gate happened to provide**
  (uniqueness, inventory moves, per-Realm membership counts): replace it with a
  lock or constraint on that object, as ratings do with their (Context, target)
  gate.

Write the classification as a short table in the handoff. Record the rule in
a comment at `observationGateLock`, renamed to say what it now decides.

G-1165 moved join-time graph reads out of the membership transactions. One
case remains in your claim: `AccessRealmJoining.policyFor`
(`access/realm-management-joining.ts`) still reads the graph inside its scoped
read transaction. Move that read before the transaction, the way G-1165 did
for `selfJoin`.

Keep the authority semantics exactly: revocation, closing, epoch races and
recovery fences behave as before. Do not change policy evaluation, add tables
for locking, or change response shapes.
