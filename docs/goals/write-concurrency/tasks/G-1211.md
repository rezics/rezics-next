## Outcome

Main-wide pass 3 (QA `20261005t231747-99cfe8`, project f10):
`tests/qa/fault-recovery/rating-daily.test.ts` is flaky. On current main it
passed once and failed once in two runs alone. The failure is in
`tests/qa/support/rating-aggregate.ts:320`: "Expected 457n, received 458n"
(and 453 vs 454 in an earlier pass).

The helper counts every Access state row before and after out-of-band head
writes, and adds `logged(n)` for the change rows those writes append to
`access.discovery_source_change` and `access.also_enjoyed_source_change`.
Since G-1179, G-1183 and G-1197, background workers fold and prune those logs
on a timer, and record keyed discovery changes. The count therefore depends on
when a fold or prune happens, or on extra keyed rows.

Make the coverage deterministic without weakening it:
- exclude the background-folded change-log tables from the count and digest
  comparison, and assert their contribution separately;
- or quiesce the fold and purge workers for the measured window, if the test
  stack allows it without product changes.

The recovery property must stay asserted: a lost or rolled-back head changes
the digest, and restoration returns it.
