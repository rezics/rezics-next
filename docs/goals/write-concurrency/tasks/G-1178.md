## Outcome

Main-wide regression: since about 00:16 on 2026-10-05, the public ranking on
the shared stack has stayed stale. `/health/discovery-ready` reports
`sequenceLag` 2090 with the active head at checkpoint 7660. Every rebuild (91
so far) fails with `source-invalid` at batch 7841–7856.

The cause:
- `runBatch` (`services/main/src/modules/recommendation/ranking.ts:~396-409`)
  requires a native `work` IRI on every `rating.observation.set` receipt.
- Since G-1084 (`1dc8fa391`), the relay also emits target-form observation
  receipts: `target` set, `work` and `main` absent. The emitter's validator
  (`outbox/relay.ts:1461-1464`) treats that form as valid.
- On the shared stack, the first such receipt is relay sequence 7847.

Accept the target form as the emitter defines it:
- **When `target` is a Work** in the ranking's population, the observation
  counts as a Work rating does.
- **Otherwise** (a character, a person or another non-Work target), the event
  is skipped for the Work ranking, without failing.

Decide by reading the target's kind the way the rest of the ranking resolves
Works, not by guessing from the IRI.

Fix the class: one unrecognized event must not stall a builder silently
forever.
- Surface a repeated build failure and its reason in the ranking health that
  `/health/discovery-ready` reads (`discovery/public-ranking.ts`), so an
  operator sees `source-invalid at <sequence>` rather than only a growing lag.
- Keep the failure strict for genuinely malformed receipts.

No new options, tables or workers.
