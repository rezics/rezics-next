## Outcome

Review finding P2-5 (G-1195): GOAL.md promises "waits are bounded and lag is observable", but the notification producer log and the review-rank and editorial sequencers stall behind any open writer (up to 5 min on bounded pools) and nothing measures it.

Report, through the existing worker telemetry (`@rezics/observability/runtime`, `recordWorkerOutcome` / `withWorkerTelemetry`), for each horizon consumer: the age of its oldest pending row and the age of the oldest open writing transaction (`pg_stat_activity` where `backend_xid` is set). Expose the same numbers on the existing operator health surface if one fits; do not add a new endpoint family. Put the lag queries in a small `services/main/src/modules/horizon/` module and read the review-rank and editorial pending rows from there; do not edit `editorial-review/**` or `review/**` (other tasks hold them).

Smallest change; no new services or options. Read the finding in full in .temp/research/write-concurrency-review.md (main checkout) first.
