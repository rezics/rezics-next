## Outcome

`FeedStore.vote` (`services/main/src/modules/feed/store.ts:340-380`) takes the
singleton `access.feed_checkpoint` FOR UPDATE (`:351`) and rewrites its
`revision` (`:366`) on every vote. As a result:

- all votes on the platform run one at a time;
- the feed watermark PUT (`personal.ts:195`, FOR SHARE) queues behind them;
- projection refresh, which holds the same row while it applies a batch, blocks
  every voter (2 s `lock_timeout`, then failure);
- every vote changes the global `revision` that paging frames are pinned to
  (`frame.ts:148`, `read.ts:484`, `new-since.ts:56`, `store.ts:202`), so one vote
  anywhere invalidates every open feed page with "Feed changed".

The vote trigger also updates a per-Realm `access.realm_thread_state` row
(migration 1038 around lines 118–133), so all votes in one popular Realm share
that row.

Make a vote lock only what it changes: its own vote row and the target item.
Check the epoch with a non-exclusive read. Paging frames must stay valid across
votes on other posts, and must behave as the feed owner documents
(`feed/timeline.md`, `docs/contracts` feed sections) when the ranking of
already-served items changes. Choose the smallest mechanism; candidates are a
frame pinned to the projection sequence plus keyset positions, with score
changes tolerated as the contract allows. Record the rule where the feed owner
keeps it.

Move per-Realm thread counters off the vote path, or make them commutative so
votes in one Realm do not serialize. The projection refresh keeps its own
exclusive section, but voters must not wait on it.

Keep `access.append_notification_producer_event` calls as they are: G-1164
changes that function's internals, not its signature.

Do not redesign ranking, add caches or add new services.
