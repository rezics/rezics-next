Review, read-only, the product changes this Goal merged since `f08e85295`. They
are commits whose messages name G-1162 to G-1193, except G-1187 and G-1188,
which are still running; `git log f08e85295..HEAD` lists them. Read
[GOAL.md](../GOAL.md) and the research note

Look for defects a reviewer would block on, in this order:

1. **Lost or duplicated events.** For every ordered consumer that changed
   (notification producer log, Content post-commit sequencer, editorial and
   review sequencers, source change logs and folds, discovery keyed changes,
   co-reader schedule), can an event be skipped, delivered twice without
   idempotency, or numbered out of commit order? Consider restore with a new
   epoch, a crash between commit and numbering, concurrent sequencers, and a
   long-running writer.

   without locks (G-1168), the media cache headers (G-1181 and the manager's
   VIEW07 fix), and G-1184's 404 rule.
3. **New serialization or unbounded cost** introduced while removing the old
   one: a hot per-Realm or per-target row, a scan that grows with history, or
   a log that is never pruned.
4. **Migrations on populated data.** Backfills against immutability triggers
   (1171 was one), long locks during migration, and renumbered files.

Write the report to `.temp/research/write-concurrency-review.md` in your
worktree. Give each finding a severity (P0 / P1 / P2), the file and line, a
concrete failure scenario and the smallest fix. List what you checked and
