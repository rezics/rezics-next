## Outcome

Launch's decision (2026-10-11, K38): a game Work carries (1) its platforms as multi-valued Statements, each a Concept of ONE shared platform concept scheme, and (2) ONE first-release date: the earliest public release on any platform, as a Statement of the same date shape as the first-publication date (`schema:datePublished` bound by the property definition `statement-first-publication-date-v1`; the year template `work-publication-years` already reads it, so the games Zone browses by year through the anime and manga path). Per-platform dates, regions and editions stay out. Play status is the existing collection status plus progress; build no game store.

Follow what the anime and manga facts task did and reuse its pieces: the format concept scheme is a concept scheme classified with `rv:classifiedAs` Statements plus acceptance exactly like genres; the importer is `scripts/datasets/bangumi.ts` (it already reads `subject_platforms` and maps subject type plus platform in `bangumiFormat`; `WorkFormatKey` and `formatKeys` are the closed list for formats); the seed data is `scripts/dev/seed/relation-lexicon-data.ts`; the unit and isolated integration tests are `services/main/tests/anime-manga-facts.test.ts` and `tests/qa/integration/anime-manga-facts.test.ts` (copy their structure).

Do:
1. A platform concept scheme as seed data with the Bangumi game platforms captured from the real archive records (read the distinct `platform` values of subject type 4 in the captured fixtures; unknown stays unclassified, never guessed).
2. The importer maps a type-4 subject's platform token(s) to platform Concepts (many allowed; a duplicate token is one) and writes them through the API, and writes the first-release date as the earliest of the subject's own release dates in the same `datePublished` Statement shape as anime and manga.
3. Tests: unit test of the mapping from captured real Bangumi game records (including a multi-platform record and a record with no platform); isolated integration `game-facts`: import two games through the API, read the platforms through `facet-concept` and the date through `GET /v1/resources/{id}/statements`, and read the year through the year template.

Do not build: a new Work type, a handler, a new read or facet, per-platform dates, region or edition models, any web UI, a game store.
