## Outcome

Program decided: class A, one session, owner kernel. Decision 7 (docs/contracts/content-languages.md, "Withdraw is per part") says a cook publishes a Recipe's Structure and its notes (the optional Text, Content) as two explicit parts and withdraws per part. The Structure part has its command (`POST /v1/work-publications` with `operation: withdraw`, `services/main/src/modules/work/structured-publication.ts`); the NOTES part has none: `services/main/src/modules/content-publication/publish.ts` only publishes (a `ContentPublicationDecision`, `POST /v1/content-publications` in `routes/content.ts`) and `content-publication/README.md` says the withdrawal lifecycle "remains open". There is NO Text withdrawal in contribution-publication or content-publication today; the precedents are the Work structure withdraw (head swap to Withdrawn, receipt, settlement) and the replacement path (a new published revision removes the old MatchUnit and body from public search).

Build the author's own withdrawal of a published Content revision, in this one session, designing from the precedents and writing the decisions into the contract:

1. API: `POST /v1/content-publications` gains `operation: 'withdraw'` (or a sibling route if the existing body union cannot carry it cleanly; justify) for an exact `(resource, variant, expected publication head)` with idempotency, admitted by the owner (the author's `content.publish`-equivalent authority; a non-owner and an unrelated principal answer hidden == absent), returning the same receipt/pending-operation shape as publish (200/201/202), settled by the same Content owner delivery.
2. Effect: the exact published revision stops being public everywhere publication made it public: public read (`public-domain-read.ts`, resource and revision reads), public Content phrase search (the MatchUnit and body leave the index through the SAME projection relay and eligibility exclusion path, as a replacement does; counts, facets and continuation follow; a withdrawn decision is an exclusion), recommendation/feed surfaces that read the same eligibility. The author's own draft read is unaffected (the draft head and revisions stay). Re-publishing a new revision afterwards works and restores eligibility (the head moves from Withdrawn).
3. Safety ordering: withdrawal vs an erasure or Rights hold in flight (the stronger exclusion wins, never un-hides); restore replays it (the decision is retained like publication); a withdraw of an already-withdrawn head is an idempotent replay or a typed stale-head, not a second decision.
4. Tests: Main tests for the command, the receipt and idempotency, hidden == absent for non-owners; ONE integration file through the API (named cases): publish a Recipe's Structure and its notes, withdraw ONLY the notes: the Structure stays published and readable, the notes read and phrase search answer absent, the author's draft remains, re-publish restores; withdrawing the Structure leaves the notes published. Fixtures grant access through `grantFixtureAuthority`.
5. Docs: `docs/contracts/content-publication.md` (create or extend the nearest owner contract) states the operation, its exact-revision rule, the exclusion semantics and the replay/restore behaviour; `task gen` for the OpenAPI document.
