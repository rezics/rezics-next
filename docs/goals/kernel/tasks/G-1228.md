## Outcome

Contract C3, first end-to-end path. Record §10 (`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/arch-review-2/target-architecture.md`,
"現存的全站串行正確性依賴"; read §1's asymptotic criterion and §10 only) lists
places where correctness depends on one site-wide order: `DATASET rv:sequence`,
the site-wide ModelComponent generation guard, before/after graph positions,
the gap-free outbox position and cursors bound to the global sequence. A
read-only scout inventoried every producer and consumer with line evidence:
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/kernel/scouts/K4.md`. Read it first.

This slice converts three paths and leaves the rest working as they are:

1. **Work reads.** The Work header and editions continuation, including the
   page hydration in `work/read-session.ts`, bind to dependency tokens of the
   selected component heads and disclosure state instead of the global
   sequence. Both metadata writers advance an editions collection revision, so
   an insertion invalidates a cursor even though no returned row changed.
2. **Context reads.** Context revision reads and interpretation bind to a
   token over the selected heads, including absent higher-priority selection
   slots, the fallback head, the pinned chain (at most 8) and current
   disclosure, instead of before/after global positions.
3. **Main RDF → durable relay.** Positions carry one fixed stream scope,
   persisted with positions, retained batches and checkpoints. Sequences are
   compared only within a scope, and the relay's correctness basis is its own
   stream, not the DATASET high-water (a scope field over the old basis is not
   enough). Upgrade retained batches and checkpoints into the scope with a
   relay migration from your range. Zero-event progress, deduplication,
   crash-after-delivery replay and missing-batch detection keep working.

Acceptance: an unrelated write never invalidates a Work page, an editions
cursor or a Context read, while a relevant insertion, a change of a selected
shape, revocation and erasure still do. Equal sequences in two streams are
never compared. Keep the bounded call, byte and deadline checks
(`read-session.ts:246`) and the fresh authority and disclosure rechecks; the
trust-ops Goal owns admission (C1), so do not change how admission decides,
only what basis a read binds.

Not to build: no sharding, distributed sequencer, consistency framework,
token registry, configurable stream topology, graph-copy snapshot or retained
transaction service. Do not declare `rv:sequence` diagnostic-only site-wide
and do not drop it from shapes: other consumers (rule and staging guards,
search, ranking, notifications, recovery; see the scout's list) still rely on
it and convert in later slices. Do not touch other Work collections.

`services/main/src/modules/media/summary.ts` lies in the trust-ops Goal's
area and is lent for this task only: change which basis its read binds and
nothing about which media a reader may see; put any visibility or disclosure
need in the handoff.

The Java files are shared roots: keep changes there to the invariant and
policy checks this slice needs, and say exactly what changed so the next
Java task can rebase on it.
