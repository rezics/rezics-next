## Outcome

Contract C4, first slice: one representation for a proposition. Read
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/arch-review-2/target-architecture.md`
§6 "陳述與溯源（C3）" and the read-only scout with line evidence,
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/kernel/scouts/K6.md`, first.
Trust-ops' T1 (C1 first half) landed at e64b08472: admission judges published
policy at register, retry and claim; build on it, do not change it.

1. **Catalogue import writes Statement plus acceptance.** Catalogue
   classifications stop requiring `sense/expectedSenseHead` and stop writing
   `ClassificationApplication` plus `ClassificationDecision`; they take direct
   Concept and exact-definition inputs and create Statement, revision,
   DecisionSlot and acceptance decision inside the existing bounded bulk
   transaction, keeping admission-derived identities, manifests, CAS guards and
   receipt replay. Source staging's source-only `rdf:Statement` evidence stays
   as it is.
2. **The Statement migration goes**: `statement/migrate-v1.ts`, its conversion
   helper, the cutover profile, and the three operations (GET pending, POST
   cutover, POST migration in `routes/contexts.ts`). Readers that branch on the
   cutover (classification writer and resolver, both searches, feed source,
   onboarding interests, Discovery) become Statement-only; the old decision
   writer retires. Restore (`reconcile-restored.ts`) recreates Statements, not
   Applications/Decisions. Remove the three operations' rate-limit and
   OpenAPI entries; if `rate-limit/routes.ts` is claimed by another task when
   you get there, list the exact lines under OWNER CHANGES.
3. **Subject reads seek.** `statement/subject-read.ts:287` aggregates,
   GROUP BY/ORDER BY and then truncates. Replace it with one reference-only
   seek index in Access PostgreSQL keyed `(dataEpoch, subject, predicate,
   meaningKey, statementId)` (migration from your range): seek a page of
   candidates first, then hydrate and resolve acceptance only for that bounded
   batch. Missing index coverage answers `unavailable`, never a fallback scan.
   Framed reads keep their specificity order.
4. **PROV export.** The existing Statement export adds speaker →
   `prov:wasAttributedTo`, operation → `prov:Activity`, revision →
   `prov:wasGeneratedBy`, the revision's public `recordedBy` →
   `prov:wasAssociatedWith`. No private principal, no inferred
   `prov:actedOnBehalfOf`.

Speakers stay separate: two speakers with the same meaning are two
Statements. The acceptance reader (`statement/read.ts:301`) already bounds
targets and refuses fallback; extend it to exact DecisionTargets only as far as
item 1 needs.

Populated state: retained catalogue decisions exist in the shared stack.
Convert them once with an idempotent, receipt-backed step that preserves
provenance and revisions and rebuilds the seek index, run on an isolated
populated copy in your tests; no permanent dual read, no startup backfill.
State in the handoff what the shared stack needs after merge.

Not to build: no Claim, Application or Perspective family, no nanopub storage,
no RDF 1.2 triple terms, no acceptance engine, exporter framework, registry or
generic index layer. Do not start folding verification's `rv:Claim`; it needs
its own lossless mapping later.
