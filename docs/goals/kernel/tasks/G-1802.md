## Outcome

Launch API decision de499 (kernel brief state): publication of a no-notes Recipe. Authored own-work stays private absent selected public text or `catalogueVisible`; the Recipe reader uses Work disclosure and then the selected Structure. A cook can author a Recipe with ingredients and steps and no prose notes, and today it cannot be published as a public Recipe page, because public disclosure is keyed to selected public text. Establish the exact current behaviour (the Work disclosure rule in `services/main/src/modules/disclosure`, the recipe read routes in `services/main/src/routes/recipes.ts`, `modules/recipe/work-page.ts`, publication of Works and the Content publication owner, `catalogueVisible`) with a reproducing test design (a cook's own Recipe without notes: who can read it, what is public). Then specify the smallest GENERIC structured-publication rule (not Recipe-special-cased: one rule that says a Work whose public content is its structured Structure is publishable, usable by other structure-only kinds), the owner decision it needs (list it as a question for Program, Launch and Trust-ops: it widens what becomes public), the API (an explicit publish step with its receipts and the rights and safety gates it must pass), tests, and what Launch's recipe editor must call.
