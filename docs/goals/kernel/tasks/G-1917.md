## Outcome

Launch's L21 recipes proof (30 pinned cookbook recipes through the product's Schema.org import; the pinned Boston Cooking-School Cook Book has 47 fractions and 2 ranges) found that `services/main/src/modules/recipe/importer.ts` (functions `exactAmount`, `exactStructuredAmount`, `ingredient`, about lines 33 and 60) accepts only ASCII "1", "1/2" and "1 1/2": a Unicode vulgar fraction ("½", "1½", "⅔", "¾", "⅛"...) or a range ("2 to 3", "2–3", "1-2") keeps its text but gets NO amount and `parseStatus: 'unparsed'`, so the line neither scales nor converts. Program classed it A (2026-10-12).

Do: (1) parse Unicode vulgar fractions U+00BC to U+00BE and U+2150 to U+215E (and the fraction slash U+2044 form "1⁄2"), mixed numbers with or without a space ("1½", "1 ½", "2 1/2"), and ASCII fractions as today, into the EXACT amount `{ numerator, denominator }` (reduced), keeping `amountLexical` VERBATIM and the same bounds (denominator at most 1e12); (2) RANGES: "2 to 3", "2-3", "2–3" (en dash), "1½ to 2": check the qualifier shape of the ingredient line in the structure and model definitions (`model/definitions`, the structure occurrence qualifier `amount`/`amountMax` or any range field; read how `parseStatus` and `scaling` use them) and if a range field exists, fill it with the exact lower and upper amounts; if the model has NO range representation, implement fractions and mixed numbers only, leave ranges at `unparsed` with their text, and say exactly what a range needs (a model change is class B and goes to Program), do not invent a field; (3) the unit text after the amount keeps working (`1½ cups flour`, `2 to 3 tablespoons butter`); an amount that cannot be exact stays unparsed (never a float); a lone "½" works; text such as "1 egg" or "2 1/2-inch pieces" must not be misparsed as a range or an amount-and-unit incorrectly ("2 1/2-inch" is amount 2 1/2 with unit text "inch" only if it does today; do not change today's outputs for inputs that parse); (4) tests (`services/main/tests/recipe-amount-parse.test.ts`, owner tier, no stack): the table of Unicode fractions, mixed numbers, ranges (or the documented unparsed outcome), verbatim lexical, bounds, and a regression table of today's inputs. Do not change any route, schema, migration or the model. The cookbook pin is `tests/fixtures/gutenberg/recipe-extract-pins.json` (Boston: 47 fractions, 2 ranges): add a test that runs the importer's amount parser over that pin's ingredient lines and asserts the numbers of lines parsed (all fractions parse; ranges per the decision above) and none is parsed wrongly.

Worktree discipline: relative paths only. QA discipline (Program): ONE goalctl test call per step; no reruns of passing files; fixtures grant access through grantFixtureAuthority. One session; commit and report with the counts.
