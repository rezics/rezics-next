## Outcome

Program's decision (2026-10-10): delete the Claim fold. Nothing is deployed and the charter forbids deploying, so no Claim population anywhere needs folding; dev and fixture data are rebuilt. The fold (`services/main/src/modules/verification/claim-fold.ts` 1,696 lines, `claim-fold-custody.ts` 191 lines) has no production caller, cannot complete (its `complete` and `release` phases are denied by the native policy until a reconciled inventory exists) and leaves a dataset-wide `rv:restoreHold` that nothing releases. Remove it: the TypeScript, the native `ClaimStatementFoldPolicy` and `ClaimFoldInventory` with their hooks in `CommandPolicy`, `CommandInvariant` and `CommandService`, their Java and TS tests, the guard `claim-fold-no-production-caller.test.ts`, the fold's use of `rv:restoreHold`, every reference in QA lists and the mechanism dependency file.
