## Outcome

Record §14 rows 3 and 4 (`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/arch-review-2/target-architecture.md`):
a new ordered vertical is configuration, and Recipe's transport converges on
the Composition owner without a larger runtime. Read the scout first:
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/kernel/scouts/K8.md` (part A).
Profiles are edited in place now (C0) and may be authored in Turtle through
`model/compiler/shacl.ts` (C2); extend the converter only if a construct you
need is missing, and only by that construct.

1. **ItemList.** Collection and Composition ordered membership is
   represented as `schema:ItemList` → `schema:ListItem` with `schema:item` and
   `schema:position` on the ListItem. Positions are parent-local Text derived
   from the existing order keys, so a reorder never renumbers the whole list
   (schema.org allows Text positions). Reuse the existing editing algorithms;
   groups, duplicates, pins, captures and historical revisions keep working.
   Normalize stored membership once (idempotent, receipt-backed, tested on a
   populated copy) and then remove the `rv:target` read fallback.
2. **A podcast playlist is configuration.** Prove it with
   `tests/qa/integration/podcast-playlist.test.ts`: a playlist of podcast
   episodes created, reordered and read through the existing Collection or
   Composition API, with no new profile family, route or handler.
3. **Episodic series.** `tests/qa/integration/episodic-structure.test.ts`: an
   anime series Work's Structure holds main episodes and a specials group;
   items are typed `schema:Episode` with `schema:episodeNumber`; they are
   created through the Composition API; the existing progress path
   (`structure.progress`) records episode 7 for a viewer and resumes it in a
   second session. If Structure restricts items to Post or Work, widen it to
   any admitted resource type. No new handler.
4. **Recipe on Composition.** `POST /v1/recipes`, `POST /v1/recipes/:id/changes`
   and `GET /v1/recipes/:id` go. Composition's request and response schemas
   admit Recipe's qualifiers (map `owner` to `work`), Recipe authorization and
   receipt policy stay as they are, `GET /v1/recipes/works/{id}` (the Recipe
   Work-page read the web uses) stays unless you list its web references under
   OWNER CHANGES, and the seed (`recipes-step.ts`) and tests
   move to the Composition operations. Nutrition, scaling, measures,
   import/export and the Recipe Work page stay. Composition create, change,
   restorations and reads stay public at launch (program matrix).

`zone/owner-create.ts` and `scripts/dev/seed/recipes-step*.ts` lie in the
launch Goal's area and are lent for this task only: change them only as far as
items 1 and 4 require.

Not to build: no composition capability framework, operation registry,
generic index service, new profile family, compatibility endpoints or
selectable ordering strategies. Discover effects and the physical chapter seek
are a later task.
