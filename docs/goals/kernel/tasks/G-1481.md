## Outcome

The worker-tick isolation task (`services/main/src/worker-tick.ts`, `runWorkerTick(name, fn)`, guard scan in `services/main/tests/worker-tick-isolation.test.ts`) left the template-directory recovery interval in `services/main/src/modules/query/seek-index.ts` (`startRecovery`, the `setInterval` calling `this.recover(epoch)` and the one-off `backfill` started from `services/main/src/index.ts`) outside the wrapper because the projector-progress task held that file; it no longer does. Run each recovery tick through `runWorkerTick` (name `main.template.recovery`), with the same behaviour otherwise (the `.catch` log, the `recoveryRun` bookkeeping, `stopRecovery` awaiting). Then move `services/main/src/modules/query/seek-index.ts` out of `HELD_SCHEDULERS` in the guard test so the scan covers it, and update that test's comment. `modules/access/realm-management-recovery.ts` stays on the held list (another task owns it); do not touch it.

Do not change what recovery reads or writes, and do not wrap anything else.
