## Outcome

Program decided (2026-10-10): unify Claims into Statements. Kernel's own finding (G-1573 handoff, evidence by file:line) is that a Claim and a Statement are ONE proposition in two representations: both are subject/referent, predicate, value, author (`statedBy` vs speaker), precision qualifiers and withdrawal; they differ only in what judges the proposition (a verification case: evidence, assessments, challenges, vs an acceptance decision). `ClaimRecord` already carries `representation: 'claim' | 'statement'` and reads a folded Statement back as a Claim. END STATE: ONE representation per proposition: a Statement (subject, predicate, relation definition, value, speaker, qualification, evidence references) plus the existing DecisionSlot. Evidence, assessments and challenges remain as a VERIFICATION CASE attached to a Statement. No compatibility layer: nothing is deployed and the web app does not call `/v1/claims`.
