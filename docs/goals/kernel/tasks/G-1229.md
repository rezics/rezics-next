## Outcome

Record §10 (`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/arch-review-2/target-architecture.md`):
legal data must not poison a shared pipeline; writers and projection consumers
share one definition of acceptable data, and work that cannot be projected yet
never blocks another target. A read-only scout re-verified the first-round
findings on current main with line evidence:
`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/kernel/scouts/K3.md`. Read it first.

1. **One definition of acceptable Content text and language.** Admission
   allows 65,536 UTF-16 units and 196,608 UTF-8 bytes, projection only 65,536
   bytes, so 30,000 CJK characters pass admission and then fail projection.
   Admission accepts BCP 47 extension tags that projection rejects. Make
   admission and both public and private projection use one executable
   definition, aligned with the match-unit shapes, keeping the admitted
   single-unit text budget.
2. **Tokenless bodies.** A punctuation-only body is legal; Java qualification
   and rebuild verification currently require analyzer tokens. Verify Lucene
   membership by document identity and stored fields instead.
3. **Organization descriptions.** `catalog-description-v1` is admitted and
   published but has no projection recipe, so it stalls the Content relay.
   Add an explicit, tested skip; do not make it searchable to acknowledge it.
4. **Failed-target isolation** for the Content projection relay and the
   read-ranking projection: a target that fails is retained durably with the
   scan's advance and retried in bounded batches on its own; other targets
   proceed. Same-target order is preserved or supersession proven; scan
   progress stays distinct from completed coverage, so readiness never passes
   while retained work is pending; lost work is never acknowledged. Malformed
   events are still rejected. Upgrade populated state with migrations from
   your ranges (applied Content migrations do not re-run).

Later, not here: public ranking's full-history rebuild and EventTime month
precision poisoning event generation (both stay on the population-scale list).

`CommandService.java` is now in the claim (after G-1228 merged its stream
changes): its guard at about lines 813–815 rejects admitted long CJK bodies;
allow 196,608 UTF-8 bytes while enforcing the admitted 65,536 UTF-16-unit
budget and keep the language equality check. `CommandPolicy.java` and
`CommandInvariant.java` stay out of the claim. The scout report and design
record are read by absolute path from the main checkout
(`/home/edge/projects/rezics/rezics-next/.temp/...`), not from your worktree.

Not to build: no new search contract, registry, generic projection layer,
chunking scheme, dead-letter service or retry options. Do not raise
population limits to hide a scan.
