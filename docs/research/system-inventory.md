## Shared patterns and suspected fragmentation

1. **Semantic identity is the common join.** Subjects, relations, classification values, catalogue adoption and Zone queries belong to the semantic owner; content is a separate owner linked by IDs and references. D accepted ADR-0012, ADR-0022 and ADR-0023 establish these boundaries. Do not replace a content item with a Subject merely to reuse a page, or make a Collection authoritative for a series.
2. **Access is a common decision, not one visibility flag per surface.** D ADR-0007 and ADR-0014 and [Access](../../services/kernel/src/access/README.md) support shared membership and bounded disclosure. Separate content and semantic moderation are visible in [content moderation](../../services/kernel/src/content/moderation.ts) and [semantic moderation](../../services/kernel/src/semantic/moderation.ts). Different owners are legitimate; duplicated case handling, policy decisions or inconsistent revocation across them are risks to investigate, not proven defects. Reuse an access decision and a case workflow while retaining each owner's authoritative security state.
3. **Lists already have competing shapes.** [Library](../../services/kernel/src/library/README.md) and [the Collection contract](../library-and-collections.md) expose a dedicated library subsystem while the contract calls for dynamic Collections, shared cursors, bulk and retention adapters. Treat the existing subsystem as a source of personal facts and an adapter to the one list contract; audit the actual algorithms before asserting code duplication. A Zone should consume the same saved query and Collection membership, with its own permission intersection.
4. **Feeds, search and notifications share selection and disclosure, not authority.** Search is implemented; follows exist; feed and delivery are not evidenced. Use replayable event/projection infrastructure and bounded access checks with purpose-specific adapters, preserving notification preferences and send-time revocation. Do not let a search document or a feed become the owner of a vote, follow or content revision.
5. **Web reuse is already present but needs integration.** [UI](../../packages/ui/README.md) specifies Cover, cards and progress; [document](../../packages/document/README.md), [definitions](../../packages/definitions/README.md), [filter](../../packages/filter/README.md), [composition](../../packages/composition/README.md), and [client](../../packages/client/README.md) provide common seams. Public pages, shelves, search and threads should consume the same summaries and disclosure rather than reconstruct them independently. Per-owner sitemap feeds in [SEO](../seo.md) are a legitimate boundary; canonicalization and publication rules should remain common.
6. **Import and ordinary editing should converge on the same facts.** D accepted ADR-0002 and ADR-0017 require source provenance and common adoption; [importer](../../services/importer/README.md) is a distinct pipeline. Audit whether its output and human edits share validation, citations and acceptance, rather than introducing a second catalogue authority.

## Recommended first-public-scope priorities

1. **Close disclosure and account lifecycle first.** Prove that public subject, search, media, review, thread, Collection and sitemap outputs honor the same current Access decisions, including revocation and deletion; cover cross-owner moderation and appeals before inviting public contributions. Owners: accounts, content, semantic, search and web.
2. **Make the core catalogue journey coherent.** Demonstrate import with provenance and rights, editorial adoption, identity resolution, subject page, search and accessible cover together. Confirm definitions and editor activation for the first supported verticals; avoid claiming provider breadth from a single bridge. Owners: imports, semantic, search, content and web.
3. **Finish the personal-to-public library journey.** Implement or demonstrate the documented library profiles, common Collection interface and safe dynamic membership, progress and privacy, then connect them to subject pages. Consolidate cursor, bulk and retention mechanisms through adapters and test across both curated and dynamic lists. Owners: accounts and web, with semantic.
4. **Define and deliver a minimal community loop.** Verify account/Agent identity, Realm/Space membership, boards, posts, reviews, reports and moderator resolution as one journey. Decide which parts of Zones and appeals must enter the first public scope; assign explicit owners. Owners: accounts, content, semantic and web.
5. **Assign discovery and delivery gaps.** If feeds, notifications or recommendations are in first-public scope, give each an owner and contract with preference, explainability, freshness, retry and send-time disclosure. Otherwise explicitly defer them in the owning product scope; a follow list is not sufficient coverage.
6. **Complete publication and transactions to the selected scope.** Verify SEO and sitemaps for every public page and locale, then exercise offers through order and entitlement and reconcile points if included. Partner API/export and hosted-work authoring need explicit ownership and scope decisions before being advertised.
