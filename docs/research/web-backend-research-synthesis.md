## Assessment and evidence boundary

The product has substantial generic backend machinery, but its ordinary default composition and its interface do
not yet form coherent user journeys. The frontend problem is substantive: incomplete reading hierarchy, broken
shared overlays, inconsistent cover defaults, missing scope registration and examples whose useful content is
overwhelmed by independent failures. Backend gaps include unavailable native producers/default dependencies,
algorithms below the required upper bounds, a shared snapshot violation and incomplete lifecycle custody.
Neither test volume nor a rendered fixture answers these problems.

Keep the modular monolith, existing owner authorities and mature PostgreSQL/OpenSearch/Better Auth/DBOS/editor
engines. Repair the missing ports and whole-owner algorithms, then bind truthful interfaces. Presentation repairs
that do not advertise absent capabilities can proceed independently after implementation is authorized.

All thirteen initial reports are inputs, including their limitations and independent disagreements. Their common
inspected product revision is `6d3f767467c85dbb9d3bb561d12af784407bd92c`. This synthesis also inspected decisive
source at `dev`/starting HEAD `173222cb1d800d2114ea5d8b26259eaad224362c`, where the thirteen reports are integrated.
That is not the public Storybook build. Source inspection, retained image review and primary-document reading
are the evidence here; no install, build, compiler, test, service, new capture campaign, gate or deployment ran.
Documentation-only changes affect no typed workspace. All original main/root, acceptance, native, privacy,
physical-erasure, browser, accessibility, growth and promotion obligations remain unproved and paused.

Use five independent capability columns: **D** specified/designed; **B** substantive backend source; **A** mounted
API, with grants/default dependencies/native producer readiness stated separately; **U** interface consumer;
**T** assertion source and what it actually protects. T never means an executed pass in this synthesis. A recording
protects a response consumer; a fake protects the constructed case; neither proves ordinary native production.
An unavailable dependency is different from an absent route, a closed grant, known empty or an unselected design.

## Frontend proposal

### Shared search and scopes

Use symmetric shell geometry so a field is centered in the viewport despite unequal side controls; the icon-only
variant has no artificial centering requirement. One surface contains the field, removable scope chip and trailing
right Filters action. Keep a visible accessible focus treatment while removing the accidental blue boundary/two
surface mismatch. The current isolated phone full-screen implementation is real; repair route integration and
focus entry/return rather than invent another search primitive. The custom portal's inline `max(triggerWidth,128)`
width conflicts with its 32rem form; fix that sizing owner, including Collection DynamicRule. A contained Library
bottom sheet is a different path. Opening, applying, clearing and resuming pagination must preserve scope/query,
layout and applicable sort; current filter submission loses grid mode while curated continuation preserves it.

`apps/web/src/search/header-search/{types,authority,suggestions}.ts`, `shell-search.tsx`,
`library/collection-filters.tsx` and `services/kernel/src/library/collection-routes.ts` show why CSS is insufficient:
Collection members accepts filter/sort but no text `q`, production scope/action registration is absent, and public
discovery rejects owner/saved domains. The resource operands for Collection/Realm/Zone are not admitted by the
current public grammar. Build one text/analyzer/order/intersection/currentness/cursor contract, not client-side
first-page intersection. Public indexes must not mirror private Library facts.

### Mixed Home and special homes

Current [scope](../scope.md) defers feeds and recommendations: Home is Continue plus catalogue rows. The four
Book/Anime/VisualNovel/Recipe TypeLists match that narrower configuration but fail the desired mixed outcome.
Recommend an explicit minimum amendment: one curated public heterogeneous discovery stream of Subjects,
reviews/discussions, Collections and Spaces, then an exact recent chronological Following stream. Personalized
recommendations, social progress publication and arbitrary historical reconstruction remain later. This is a
proposal for scope adoption, not permission to expose gated sources now.

Anonymous visitors receive useful public mixed discovery without private influence. Signed-in newcomers receive
the same public stream and optional skippable explicit interests. Established users get a bounded private Continue
region plus their selected Discover/Following mode. Membership is not follow or notification subscription.
Following must not silently backfill unfollowed sources. Quiet/preparing/unavailable are separate states.
At 300 Spaces/200,000 kinds/thousands of follows, retain the same geometry: small pinned subset plus searched,
paged scope/kind selectors and one stream; no block per kind, Realm, question or follow. Remove the tagline.

Candidate proposal from the independent home analysis: at most four shared decks, 2,000 recent references/deck,
128 examined references/page and 20 output cards. Following proposal: at most 2,000 references or 30 days per
viewer, resumable fanout batches and explicit coverage horizon. These are unadopted candidate budgets, not
measured throughput or existing source capability. Reconcile each physical owner debit with the common composition
ledger; do not multiply four independent full budgets. Preserve last examined continuation for sparse results.
Fanout-on-write still owes total subscriber delivery, expiry, revocation and cleanup work; it is not free.

Use explicit editorial eligibility/placement and native publication time for the first Discover ordering, with a
stable tie-break and explainable family diversity; do not claim personalized relevance. Following is exact recent
chronology over followed sources. Current mixed transport at page20 already has three rounds and at most123 raw
candidates (`min(600,6s+3)`), so the independent128-reference proposal is not a drop-in existing allowance. Reconcile
it with that owner contract and the shared ledger before adoption. A candidate recent inbox at one million viewers
×2,000 references×160 bytes is320 GB of payload before indexes/replicas. Five thousand follows×ten backfill keys
is50,000 keys per returning viewer; one million×5,000 follows is five billion edges. These desk envelopes require
global byte admission and resumable work. Shared reference decks plus read-time bounded merge are an alternative
with lower replication and more merge work; do not select per-viewer inbox storage before that owner tradeoff.

Bind continuation to viewer/scope/query/order version and source generation, with bounded expiry and stable session
order plus an explicit new-items action. Follow/unfollow, mute/block, visibility reduction, source withdrawal,
Realm revocation and identity merge require current eligibility checks; asynchronous cleanup is not permission to
serve stale reasons. Hard exclusions differ from later soft feedback. Public ranking must not use private progress,
inbox membership, sparse hidden population scores or withheld facts that final-card filtering cannot conceal.

Historical comparison needs a precise correction. Old REZICS at
`apps/web/features/explore/home.tsx` already used one `ApiFeedList`,20-item pages and sort/content/language/Realm/tag
URL state. Its Realm/tag controls only fetched50 choices without demonstrated continuation, and accumulated loaded
pages have no retained-page bound. The backend's latest32-placement window before eight visible context summaries
is bounded but not complete membership. `legacy-final:apps/web/features/home/home-page.tsx` also renders **one**
mixed `HomePostsFeedList`, not a separate stream for every Realm. `home/tabs.tsx` has More and
`work-page/scope-bar.tsx` has top-four-plus-selected with a paged other-community picker. Remaining limits include
`work-page/ratings.tsx` mapping every question Context, feed-control O(SR) selected-label lookup/O(R) options and
moderated-community rail entries with no local cap. The followed-concept page defaults20/max64 and the has-follow
probe asks for one; it is not an all-follows scan. Retain those genuine mitigations and fix the residual enumeration
class. Historical main-feed budgets excluded large Continue/rail budgets and are not current measurements or a
budget to import. Both [home](home-feed-current.md) [reports](home-feed-deep.md) provide exact source traces.

Space is a specialized native community/catalogue entry; Zone configures bounded shared views over authorized
sources. Books need edition/language/translation and actual reading-session continuation; anime needs schedule,
episode/season structure and spoiler controls; games need platform/release/edition and acquisition context.
These are complete vertical tasks over shared capabilities, not three backend forks. Static special homes can
improve information hierarchy independently; useful source-backed homes require deliberate Zone scope expansion.
Compare the [two](home-feed-current.md) [home reports](home-feed-deep.md), not just old screenshots.

### Subject, ratings and acquisition

Default order: identity/portrait cover and short creator facts; admitted tracking/rating/acquisition action;
bounded synopsis with accessible expansion; a small applicable edition/structure/credit preview; compact native
rating plus review/discussion previews and complete-view destinations. Detailed facts/provenance/sources, complete
credits, relations, editions and structural contents belong to appropriate finite views. Applicability comes from
the profile, not one tab per kind/Realm. Folds bound visible prose, not backend fetch or DOM cost.

`apps/web/src/features/pages/subject.tsx` positional `r0..r6` consumption disagrees with the read plan; the purchase
pattern casts a recorded raw page through `unknown as SubjectPageData`. Repair typed binding and the fixture
boundary before diagnosing their failed siblings as native Commerce absence. Required-view reads still need an
interim overview for tab availability; count-only availability is not implemented. Composition's 16 reads/128
slots excludes separate rating and offer work, which still needs page-wide admission.

Group optional failures by actual dependency and provide targeted recovery with useful siblings visible. Do not
change unavailable to empty or turn a denied target into a revealing diagnostic. Short human relation labels
replace raw property names. One native public figure shows mean/scale/count/population, own vote separately, and
an optional accessible distribution. Hide revision/internal-processing prose in details. Native aggregate,
imported VNDB statistic and own vote fail independently. Aggregate `asOf`/updating metadata exists but is omitted
by the view; public-figure and full aggregate DTOs have different state shapes. Do not invent freshness fields.

One searchable/paged population chooser replaces three fixture Realms or hundreds of cards. Population reads
admit 20 raw candidates and aggregate reads at most 20 selected populations; names/search and sparse continuation
still need a complete journey. Preserve the 100 counted-membership policy and explain asynchronous contribution;
do not confuse it with a maximum number of Subject Realm aggregates. The lack of a final rating revalidation
equivalent to Commerce is a static review gap, not a demonstrated exploit. A cursor not viewer-bound is not alone
an authorization bypass when each request rechecks rights.

Native offers already exist (10 default/20 maximum). Put one compact acquisition entry near identity; show real
edition/terms in a chooser or phone sheet, not ten hero offers. No invented cheapest/from price, complete count
or owned badge. Individual entitlements are real; subject-owned batch and creator-sales producer are separate gaps.
Payment acceptance must eventually include current use of the exact purchased Content edition, not a return URL.
See both [Subject](subject-page-ia-current.md) [investigations](subject-page-ia-deep.md).

### Collections, covers and Storybook

Readable members already render Cover/placeholders. Mixed Film/Recipe landscape and Album square defaults violate
the settled all-kind portrait policy; absence of a real image does not remove the frame. Use one shared portrait
token, including unknown kinds; preserve landscape art with contain in the reserved portrait slot, optional neutral

Configured Collection media and native FULL key facts are real; ordered safe member-cover fallback and complete
nonempty kind evidence remain missing. Current preview budget is 20 parents/100 raw candidates/three unique
subjects, not million-member enumeration. Old loaded-page dedup must not erase intentional occurrences.

Pages include the real shell only when the journey uses search/navigation/session context. Patterns isolate a
composable interaction with its minimum real router/query/session providers. A full shell is not a cure for missing
QueryClient. Separate canonical happy/empty cases, deliberate refusal/fault/preparing cases and native API-derived
recordings in navigation and evidence labels. Do not add more galleries to hide integration gaps. Ready examples
must not require a maintainer to understand internal build/revision machinery.

Rating deserves a separate genericity qualification. `rating/{votes,schemas,aggregate-read}.ts` names
work/release/episode/character grains and associated thresholds. The standard question and four initial grains
are selected product bounds, not evidence that every rating is missing or that arbitrary question authoring is
promised. Nevertheless, extensible semantic meanings must eventually be released descriptors consumed by one
algorithm; invariant privacy floors and exact aggregation stay executable behavior. Do not make the next vertical
a new kernel fork. Provider normalization is a legitimate edge adapter, while Library state names and cover maps
must not silently become cross-domain behavior or authority.

At 256 Subjects on different partitions in one database, summaries can issue at least 512 first-stage statements;
identity double-collection can issue up to 1,000 owner/data statements for 100 distinct source/control homes.
These are desk path counts excluding later work/transaction control, not measured latency. A pool of ten connections
does not make them cheap. Rating redirect recursion in `rating/subject-resolve-target.sql` has neither hop nor cycle
bound; final LIMIT 1 does not bound traversal. Graph output cap 500 coexists with up to 400 first-frontier neighbors
and 6,400 second-round descriptors; stop/debit expansion work even after the output budget is exhausted.

Collection commands admit only 5,000 occurrences; `readMemberOn` reads the owner and ordinal fallback respaces it.
Replace with exact full-key member lookup and bounded local order blocks or resumable generation maintenance,
preserving old readable order until atomic publication. A CRDT is unnecessary without offline concurrent editing.
Definitions currently cap types/properties/bytes at 1,024/4,096/4 MiB. Separate cold finite release validation from
keyed/paged immutable runtime delivery. Registry digest/map/sort, all-kind `terms` and cold Home naming must not
iterate the whole catalogue per request. OpenSearch's default terms limit is 65,536 **if** that representation is
used; it is not evidence that all kinds must be types or terms.

Current common composition admits 16 reads/128 slots, with documented aggregate limits including 6,784 Access
points, 14,080 examined rows, 72 statements/backend and 96 engine operations. Loose standalone Scope defaults
(up to 8,192 statements) do not prove composed consumers use that stricter ledger. Zone retained slots can admit
32×1 MiB per viewer/Zone: 300 such pairs have a 9.375 GiB arithmetic ceiling before overhead. Require aggregate
byte admission, per-viewer/global quota and expiry. This is not observed allocation. Events' 4,096-home loop and
unlimited hot-home draining need indexed pending scheduling and bounded fair turns, not another queue engine.
