A protected-target replay after visibility changes is not an actual Realm-ban transition; do not count it as a banned-member scenario. The acceptance result must identify any applicable banned or protected non-member assertions reached before claiming those personas covered.

## External conditions and ownership

- Notice transport, preferences at send time and delivery retries belong to the notification clause. Report, decision and appeal tests can prove committed notice effects; they do not by themselves prove production transport. Do not edit that driver's binding here.
- Production object storage and production moderation operations remain configured external services, as stated by [Content](../goals/content.md#external-conditions). Isolated native storage proves the repository lifecycle, not the production service configuration.
- Do not expand this work into a global adjudication workbench. The Global catalogue binding lists Subject reports only and still requires current Global authority and current native target checks.
