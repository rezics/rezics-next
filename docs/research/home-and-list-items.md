# Home and shared list items

Research date: 2026-11-01. The inventory remains historical supporting evidence. The migration and waiting
contracts below follow the [owning Web requirements](../goals/web.md#adopted-quality-round-three-requirements),
including the adopted [quality round three findings](quality-round3-backlog.md) and [core-page experience
requirements](../goals/web.md#adopted-core-page-experience-requirements); they do not create another product
authority.

## Recommendation

Make the existing `ListRow` interaction the foundation of one slotted content-item family. Evolve
`MemberListRow`, `ShelfListRow` and `CoverCard` into adapters over that family; migrate Home, discussion teasers and
other independently built listings to it. A content row has no divider, border or card background at rest. Its
rounded surface appears on hover, keyboard focus and press. Its title supplies the primary stretched link;
secondary links and actions remain independently operable. Subject items have a substantial cover on the left.
Accounts have avatars, and text-only discussions do not receive invented covers.

Home should start with a compact Home/Following switch, a bounded filter control and actual content. Keep public
discovery, Following and Continue interleaved in the one native stream. Give rows enough visual identity, context,
useful previews and actions to make this a reading destination. Remove the permanent retention paragraph and the
successful source-status ledger. Do not fill their space with another slogan, a tutorial, fabricated activity,
per-kind sections or an unimplemented recommendation algorithm.

The text problem extends beyond Home. Move implementation explanations to help, settings or an explicit details
disclosure. Keep real failure, incomplete-result, destructive-action, rights and purchase consequences beside the
affected action, written in reader terms. Removing the word “native” must never turn an unconfirmed result into
success or turn an unavailable source into an empty list.

## Rendering inventory and consolidation

### The existing common core

[`packages/ui/src/components/list-row.tsx`](../../packages/ui/src/components/list-row.tsx) already implements the
requested interaction: isolated group, rounded pseudo-element outside the content inset, no rest surface, hover
only on a capable pointer, focus-visible and active feedback, and a forced overlay for stories. `ListRowLink`
provides the primary anchor layer; `ListRowControl` raises independent controls above it. Reuse this behavior.

[`list-presentation.tsx`](../../packages/ui/src/components/list-presentation.tsx) provides `MemberListRow`, a useful
beginning of the shared anatomy. It adds cover, title, trailing controls and children, with portrait cover widths
72 px on phone and 96 px on desktop; landscape contexts use wider covers. It still leaves most metadata and layout
to callers. Its visible heading is currently `aria-hidden`; the family should expose a real named heading without
duplicating the primary link in the keyboard order.

[`shelf-list.tsx`](../../packages/ui/src/components/shelf-list.tsx) shares the interaction but owns a second subject
row anatomy, including progress, selection and actions. [`cover-card.tsx`](../../packages/ui/src/components/cover-card.tsx)
is an independent tile anatomy with a stretched title link, selection, metadata, status, progress and credits.
These are useful variants of one item, not reasons for unrelated title/metadata/interaction implementations.
[`mobile-feed-card.tsx`](../../packages/ui/src/components/mobile-feed-card.tsx) is a separate bordered article with
community, preview media, title-only navigation and reaction controls. No production consumer was found outside its
own component/story surface. Retire this parallel phone contract after its useful rich-content slots are represented.

### Content listings that should use the family

“Shared shell” means the hover primitive is reused but the body is still assembled locally. “Shared anatomy” means
`MemberListRow`, `ShelfListRow` or `CoverCard` is already used. Both need migration to a consistent slot contract;
they are not equally severe defects. No row gets a navigation link if its current purpose is only an action.

`apps/web/src/library/subject-row.tsx` (`SubjectIdentity`/`SubjectCover`) and
`collection-member-row.tsx` (`CollectionMemberDetails`) are additional shared partial anatomies. They are used by
Collection publication/editing, progress-session and log reading. Move their identity/fact parts into the family
adapters; preserve their supplied-facts and unavailable-subject rules. A single destination document such as a log
may reuse these parts without becoming a stretched-link content row.

### Shared patterns and disagreements

Across the sources, useful homes repeat object identity, a content-specific preview, human context and a small
action set. Feed intent and candidate controls stay near the top; storage/readiness explanations do not occupy a
permanent block before successful content. Strong object imagery helps cultural media, while discussion text and
account identity need a different body. A shared frame permits all three.

There is no universal winner between streams, thumbnail grids, masonry and horizontal shelves. Netflix and public
catalogue homes show that shelves can work; X/Mastodon show that heterogeneous activity can work in one stream.
REZICS has already chosen the latter. This report recommends improving that stream, not reopening its scope.
Likewise, history-based ranking, recommendation training, custom-feed infrastructure, autoplay and engagement
optimization are capabilities of some comparators, not implied REZICS requirements.

## Proposed shared item system

The proposed public name is `ContentItem`; naming is an implementation choice, not a new domain entity. Add one
family in `packages/ui/src/components/content-item.tsx`, built on the existing primitives. Preserve current
exports during migration. `MemberListRow`, `ShelfListRow` and `CoverCard` become compatibility/domain adapters;
they must not retain independent copies of title stretching, cover sizing, metadata rhythm and action layering.
If small internal parts need their own file, keep them owned by this family, not by Home.

### Anatomy and slots

| Slot               | Contract                                                                                                                            | Kind-specific content                                                                                                                |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Root/frame         | `row` in a semantic list, `tile` in a gallery, or a document composition using the same parts. One hover/focus/press implementation | No network, ranking, authorization or inferred entity identity in UI package                                                         |
| Leading visual     | Cover, avatar or absent; media is noninteractive inside the primary target unless it has a distinct purpose                         | Subject cover defaults to portrait and honors declared type presentation; account/Realm avatar; no generic fake poster for a thread  |
| Context/eyebrow    | Optional small human reason/source, one line that can wrap                                                                          | Author in Space, curated by an account, “Continue reading”; not a mandatory `discovery/following/continue` protocol label            |
| Title              | Required readable identity, correct language, real heading, one primary destination if available                                    | Work title; discussion title; Collection name; account display name/handle; explicit unavailable/unlabelled fallback                 |
| Metadata/byline    | A bounded inline flow with independent links and meaningful separators                                                              | Author/date, format, exact rating label and population, Space/board, ownership or purchase state when supplied                       |
| Preview/body       | Optional content-specific body; short previews clamp, do not force all kinds to identical height                                    | Description, discussion excerpt, review with cited object, Collection note or limited child preview already supplied by owner        |
| Progress/state     | Optional domain state with an accessible label; unknown is not zero                                                                 | Continue progress, current session, unavailable/withheld/deleted state, selected/checked state                                       |
| Footer actions     | Small set of actual current actions; independent focus targets raised above primary link                                            | Continue, Save/follow if supported, reply destination, overflow. A missing capability does not produce a decorative disabled promise |
| Trailing/selection | Optional selected checkbox, drag/move controls, price or dense management actions                                                   | Preserve pointer and keyboard reordering; no click interception around text selection                                                |

The row body is a two-column grid: visual and flexible content. Text-only items use the same content rhythm with
the visual column omitted rather than an empty 96 px hole. Context, title, metadata, preview and actions follow the
same order in every kind. A rich discussion can be taller than a label or transaction row. Consistency means stable
hierarchy and interaction, not padding every type to an arbitrary fixed height.

Recommended initial geometry: 96 px wide portrait cover at desktop, 80 px on phone, ratio from existing `Cover`
presentation; 16–20 px gap desktop and 12–16 px phone; roughly 12–16 px vertical breathing space per side; title
18–20 px desktop and 17–18 px phone; metadata 13–14 px. Most subject rows occupy approximately 160–210 px including
their cover. Text-only rows can be shorter. Keep descriptions to two or three lines; expand at the destination.
These are starting design values for adoption and visual review, not measurements claimed from competitors.

For `tile`, stack the same visual/title/metadata/body parts in existing `CoverGrid`/`CoverRow` containers. Maintain
their explicit presentation contexts and cover ratios. Tile selection and action layers use the family rules.
Do not wrap an article inside another `li` through accidental composition, or make a cover-grid tile carry row
dimensions. `ShelfTable` stays a table and uses compatible parts in cells.

### Interaction and accessibility invariants

- Keep a real anchor with a meaningful name and native open-in-new-tab behavior. Use a title-anchor pseudo-element
  to stretch the target, or explicitly associate the overlay anchor with the visible title; avoid a second tab stop
  for the same destination. Give the visible heading a readable accessible name instead of hiding it wholesale.
- Do not nest buttons/links inside the primary anchor. Put controls and secondary links in the existing raised
  control layer. Do not give every descendant a high z-index that shrinks the intended main hit target.
- Use the existing rounded overlay and visible focus outline. On touch, press feedback is available and all
  necessary actions remain visible or in a labelled overflow; nothing requires discovering hover. Selection has a
  persistent, separately identifiable state. Forced-colors and reduced-motion behavior remain usable.
- Keep ordinary `ul`/`ol` semantics for bounded pages. The [WAI-ARIA feed pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/feed/)
  adds a scrolling/focus interoperability contract; a marketing name “feed” does not require `role="feed"`.
- The [HTML anchor model](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/text-level-semantics.html#the-a-element) prohibits
  interactive descendants of an anchor. [WCAG target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html)
  specifies a 24 CSS px minimum with exceptions; use 44 px as the preferred REZICS action target, not as a claim
  about that AA minimum. Wrap/stack actions at narrow widths instead of shrinking them.
- A cover beside the same title can have empty alternative text; convey distinct information when needed. Never
  put the only title, rating meaning or completion state in an image, color or tooltip.
- Loading and recovery announcements should use a single affected-region status. [WCAG status messages](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html)
  supports announcing meaningful changes without moving focus; it does not call for three permanent “Ready” entries.

### Data boundary and cost

The item family accepts normalized presentation props and slots. Domain adapters live in `apps/web`: Home consumes
the actual `FeedItem` union, Search consumes its actual results, and Library keeps occurrence/session semantics.
Do not introduce a second Home DTO that can drift from `KernelClient`. Reuse `home-feed-destination.ts` for local
canonical navigation and the existing Continue destination; never stretch an untrusted source-supplied URL.

Each item must render in O(1) work relative to the returned summary and fixed preview limits. A 20-item Home page
has at most 20 row renderings and no per-row network hydration. No request fan-out to Subject/profile/thread pages,
no global dictionary of all kinds and no browser scan through source histories. A cover is an ordinary image
request under the established media policy, with intrinsic dimensions and appropriate lazy loading; it is not a
new read port for content qualification.

At 200,000 kinds, hundreds of Spaces and many Contexts, show the active filter names and bounded searchable choice
pages, never a tab/chip for every possible value. Preserve current directory pages of 20 plus selected metadata
lookup; keep the feed's page size 20. The current reader stores one displayed page and replaces it on Next. This
proposal preserves that boundary rather than silently turning it into unbounded infinite-scroll accumulation.
Account/viewer/CSRF, mode or selection changes retire rows, cursor and late responses together. No private summary
persists in a reusable component or cross-viewer cache.

Fields not established by the current union are optional future capability work: discussion excerpt/author/Space,
Collection curator/count/preview, question-specific rating metadata and declared cover presentation where absent.
Name the producer summary field, disclosure rule and maximum size before promising it. The first row migration is
landable with current summaries; it must not synthesize authors, counts, progress units or “because you liked” text.

## Concrete Home specification for adoption

### Desktop

Keep the existing shell navigation and search. Center one reading column of about 720–800 px within the content
area, with sensible outer margins. On a wide screen the remaining space is breathing room, not a reason to add a
new query-heavy right rail. Home has an accessible H1; the compact selected Home tab can supply the visible page
identity. No hero, marketing line, second repeated title or service-health block precedes the stream.

The first compact band contains Home and, for a signed-in reader, Following. Home means the existing native mixed
stream, including Continue where returned. Following means its existing following-only request mode, not a second
column. A second compact band contains one Filters trigger and the names of active selections, with clear/remove
controls. The expanded panel exposes Kind, Space and rating-question Context. Use ordinary labels and searchable,
paged choices. An unset selection is clearly “All”; active choices remain understandable when collapsed. Do not
add a separate sort or “Recommended” option whose backend semantics are absent.

Immediately below, show the ordered rows, in exactly the native order. A Subject row has its substantial left cover,
title, supplied description and meaningful metadata. A discussion has a readable title and, when the producer
supplies it, author/Space and a short excerpt. An account has avatar/name/handle and any disclosed summary. A
Collection uses its own identity and curator/description only when available. A Continue row uses the same work
identity, a human progress label and a distinct Continue link. No separate “Continue” shelf interrupts the stream.

For example, a returned book, a discussion and a resumed work should look like three members of the same list:
cover/title/description; discussion title/context/preview; cover/title/progress/Continue. A source reason is optional
small context, not a large heading before each kind. If the summary only contains a title, display that honest
compact row rather than filling its preview with a technical explanation. Richer native summaries are the route
to a richer Home, not more decorative empty containers.

Use restrained type hierarchy, predictable vertical rhythm, actual covers and concise previews. At an ordinary
desktop viewport the controls and the first few items, rather than an introductory essay, should dominate. Avoid
giant gaps between a one-line title and its description. An image failure should leave a proportional fallback and
the same title/content layout. Do not require hover to discover that the entire row navigates; title styling and
focus provide that cue.

The page ends with existing explicit Next navigation when a continuation exists. While advancing, label the actual
pending action and prevent duplicate requests. When the next page arrives, move focus/announce its content boundary
appropriately and position the reader at that boundary; do not leave focus on a removed row or unexpectedly steal
focus during background metadata refresh. Do not claim a global item total or “all caught up” from a sparse page
with a continuation. Full historical browsing remains at the source destination.

### Phone

Keep the shell's phone navigation. The content column becomes the viewport width with 16 px side padding and no
horizontal scrolling. The mode band remains compact. Filters collapse into a labelled trigger; active selections
wrap on a short summary line rather than squeezing three full selectors side by side. The filter panel/dialog
supports keyboard access, searchable pages, selected labels and an obvious clear action. Large result dictionaries
are never downloaded to populate it.

Use the same `ContentItem` adapter and DOM ordering. A work cover is about 80 px wide on the left, title and metadata
on the right, with a two/three-line preview. If a rich body needs the full width, let that slot span both columns
below the identity band. Accounts keep small avatars; text discussions use available width. Actions wrap below
content with touch-sized targets. Show Continue directly on Continue items; overflow remains visible without
hover. Long translated titles wrap, long handles break safely, and the primary title is not reduced to a single
truncated line solely to preserve a fixed height. Honor the existing display/suitability and language rules.

No phone-only `MobileFeedCard` business path, smaller-data fake feed, or hidden technical ledger is required. The
same failed, withheld and sparse states must read correctly at narrow widths and enlarged text.

## Technical and explanatory text relocation

### Placement principles

1. The ordinary page shows identity, content and actions. A sentence explaining transport, retention architecture,
   indexes, protocol bounds or service readiness needs a concrete user decision to earn that space.
2. Put a predictable limit beside the input that can reach it, or in validation/help. Put algorithm/sort windows
   behind the relevant information control. Put long-term retention policy in help/privacy and source-history
   navigation at the source. Do not prepend those explanations to every successful list.
3. Keep uncertainty, partial results, saved drafts and safe retry behavior in the affected error state. Explain
   what happened and the next action; keep request keys, heads, fences, capability names and receipts in collapsed
   diagnostics if they help support. Preserve the underlying retry/authorization behavior.
4. Keep rights, consent, exact edition, immutable publication, payment scope, restore deadlines and irreversible
   deletion consequences beside the corresponding decision. Those are product meaning, not clutter to erase.
5. Successful normal reads need no health announcement. Successful actions need a short human confirmation.
   Advanced editors, moderation and account erasure can have genuine progress/status pages; translate state names
   and distinguish recorded history from the current observed outcome.
6. A popover is for optional explanation, not the only place to learn that an action is destructive, a price is for
   one fixed edition, a result is incomplete or a save is unconfirmed. Help links must resolve to adopted content;
   do not ship a dead help destination merely to remove prose.

These recommendations accord with [Primer's content guidance](https://primer.style/product/getting-started/foundations/content/)
on useful, direct interface language, and with the source patterns above. They are not a claim that every technical
term is forbidden on an advanced management page.

## Decisions and limits of this report

The adopted contracts keep Home/Following as stream modes, Context as exact rating-question selection, a single
retained page and native qualification. Row/tile is one family; tables/forms/documents keep their semantics.
“Most content reuses it” means most content **listings**, not every settings card or authored list. Subjects use
substantial portraits by default; declared landscape presentation and avatars remain valid.

Richer summaries are bounded owner capabilities, not presumed installed backend work. Personalized
recommendations, custom feeds, autoplay and another Home rail remain outside scope. Existing source repairs,
including Home scaffolding and Realm identity, supersede historical inventory defects; current Progress source
already has a human missing-unit label, accessible enum details and honest unavailable historical meaning, so
its next index contract preserves that work while receiving the anchor obligation. No completed consumer is
reopened merely because publication is stale.

Research is linked evidence, the Web Goal owns requirements, and the slices above retain complete original
journeys and owner boundaries. Neither these documents nor folder observations establish all-state, keyboard,
native, full-suite, privacy, 320px or publication acceptance. Those require their own actual evidence; source
presence, integration, checks, native, visual and publication status must not be collapsed into one completion claim.
