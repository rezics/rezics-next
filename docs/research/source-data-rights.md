# Source data reuse under U.S. law

Checked 2026-09-24. REZICS is operated by a U.S. company and includes a
collaborative wiki. Assess the particular material and use; neither corporate
status nor a wiki label settles reuse rights. This research supports the
[source lifecycle](../contracts/source-lifecycle.md#basis-for-acquisition-and-reuse)
and [rights contract](../contracts/license-grants.md). It is not a legal opinion
clearing a particular import or publication.

## Facts, expression and fair use

In [Feist Publications v. Rural Telephone, 499 U.S. 340 (1991), pp. 347–351](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/service/ll/usrep/usrep499/usrep499340/usrep499340.pdf),
the Supreme Court distinguishes unprotected facts from original selection or
arrangement. [17 U.S.C. §§ 102–103](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#102)
protect qualifying expression and compilation authorship, not ownership of the
underlying facts. Collecting facts does not itself create copyright in them.
Thus recording an actual release date, identifier or bibliographic fact can have
a basis independent of a source's copyright license. A synopsis, translation,
illustration, creative annotation or original compilation needs separate analysis;
the word *metadata* does not settle protectability. Manual entry and automated
import are both subject to this distinction.

For protected material, [17 U.S.C. § 107](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#107)
requires considering purpose and character, nature of the work, amount and
substantiality, and market effect. Commercial character is one consideration,
not automatic disqualification. Conversely, an educational or community purpose
does not automatically establish fair use. The
[Copyright Office's explanation](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/)
expressly rejects both shortcuts.

[Andy Warhol Foundation v. Goldsmith (2023), opinion pp. 12–20](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/22pdf/21-869_87ad.pdf)
requires attention to the specific challenged use; a new meaning or beneficial
purpose alone is insufficient. For REZICS, a bounded quotation used to discuss a
work, a cover used to identify an edition, a full image download and a replacement
copy of a source database are different uses. None is cleared merely by calling
it wiki content, attribution, indexing or a thumbnail.

## NonCommercial and ShareAlike

The [Creative Commons NC explanation](https://creativecommons.org/faq/#does-my-use-violate-the-noncommercial-clause-of-the-licenses)
focuses on the use's purpose, not whether the user is a company or nonprofit.
For REZICS, examine the wiki activity's actual connection to advertising,
subscriptions, sales and other commercial benefits; free access alone does not
resolve that question. Do not infer tax-exempt or nonprofit status from the wiki.
NC compliance and statutory fair use are separate analyses.

Use the exact license version. For example,
[CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 §§ 2 and 4](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/3.0/legalcode.en)
preserves copyright exceptions and uses a commercial-purpose restriction, with
attribution and sharing conditions. A use outside copyright or covered by an
applicable exception need not rely on that license grant; record that basis
instead of pretending the licensor gave broader permission.

ShareAlike is not synonymous with NonCommercial:
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) allows commercial
reuse subject to its conditions. The
[ODbL §§ 3–4](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/)
likewise allows reuse with notice, share-alike and, where applicable, access to
the derivative database or alterations. A collection and a derivative database
have different obligations. Separate named graphs help track provenance; they
do not by themselves determine the legal scope of a combined database or export.

## Complaint handling and section 512

The maintainer selects broad intake and preservation with provenance, followed
by scoped action on concrete complaints. Incomplete license information alone
does not require rejection, quarantine or individual pre-clearance. This is an
operating policy, not a finding that every stored copy is lawful until challenged.

[17 U.S.C. § 512(c), (i), (m)](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap5.html#512)
does not impose a general monitoring obligation as a safe-harbor condition, but
§ 512(c) concerns storage at users' direction and requires action on qualifying
notices or actual/apparent infringement knowledge. Eligibility also involves
agent designation, a reasonably implemented repeat-infringer policy, standard
technical measures and limits concerning financial benefit/control. Do not assume
the same protection for REZICS-selected bulk imports.

The [Copyright Office's notice/counter-notice overview](https://www.copyright.gov/512/)
describes expeditious removal or access disabling, uploader notification and
counter-notices. Where that process applies, restoration follows the statutory
10–14 business-day window after a compliant counter-notice unless the claimant
notifies the provider of the specified court action. These are conditional
liability protections, not permission to copy. Agent registration and operational
compliance have not been verified for REZICS in this documentation review.

## Application and remaining uncertainty

A provider-wide commercial/noncommercial flag would reject permissible factual,
licensed or fair uses. Intake therefore keeps available evidence and uncertainty
at the material/use level; it does not stamp every wiki import as legally cleared.

Record whether the basis is original contribution, unprotected facts/public
domain, an applicable license or permission, or a reasoned statutory exception.
Record acquisition terms separately. A U.S. copyright conclusion does not alone
resolve contract formation/enforceability, a provider's governing-law clause or
applicable foreign rights. This review does not resolve those questions for an
unidentified transaction.

The actual monetization context, copied fields/assets, amount, presentation and
downstream exports remain necessary for individual NC or fair-use conclusions.
Targeted clarification or U.S. counsel's assessment can resolve a consequential
dispute or uncertain reuse; neither is a universal intake prerequisite. Ordinary
factual entry does not require blanket provider permission. The
[acceptance cases](../testing/source-conformance.md#field-and-workflow-coverage)
test preservation of these distinctions, not a software verdict on legality.
