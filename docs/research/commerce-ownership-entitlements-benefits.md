# What is bought, owned and used

Research accessed on 2026-10-31. This is evidence and a recommendation for Commerce to adopt, not a shared ABI or an implementation claim. The sole future financial-module map is [Commerce modules](../commerce-modules.md). Existing authority remains the [Commerce contract](../../services/kernel/src/commerce/README.md), [Commerce Goal](../goals/commerce.md), [scope](../scope.md), [Access](../../services/kernel/src/access/README.md) and [Library](../library-and-collections.md).

**Finding:** a public subject, a sale offer, evidence of payment, a licence or entitlement, an executable benefit and a person's Library facts need distinct identities. “Owned” is useful buyer language but does not establish transferability, perpetual availability or permission to retrieve bytes now. A future transferable item also needs an identity distinct from its holder's access rights.

## Evidence and limits

**Documented fact** below means a public product contract, official API/help page, or identified source behavior. **Inference** draws a design lesson from that evidence. **Recommendation** proposes REZICS behavior. **Unknown** means the sources read do not establish it; it is not a claim that a feature is absent. All linked external sources were accessed on the date above. Help pages describe public behavior, not private database schemas. Website material remains under its publisher's terms; no implementation or long passage is proposed for copying.

The source inspection is Ghost, pinned to `75f33df36d963327323f4af2c159dbf345e725ad`, [MIT licence](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/blob/75f33df36d963327323f4af2c159dbf345e725ad/LICENSE). Its reference path is `.reference/commerce-ownership/ghost/` in the main checkout, recorded in `.reference/INDEX.md`. It was read, never installed, built or run. Other products were researched through current official documentation; their private source was unavailable.

The current worker's Commerce types, routes, settlement and payout contracts and purchase UI were read. No payment-evidence/settlement research report was available in this base. Consequently this report does not resolve Stripe eligibility, fee/tax/FX allocation or the accounting sufficiency of stored evidence. The separate provider-evidence research remains the input for those decisions. No runtime, native, browser, database or performance experiment supports this report.

## Ten sale models

This table compares only the sale unit with the identity of the thing presented or used. The mechanics and qualifications follow.

| Product                    | Sale unit                                                                 | Content or benefit identity                                  |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Steam                      | Package licence, possibly containing multiple applications/depots         | AppID and depot contents                                     |
| Apple App Store / StoreKit | App purchase or in-app product, with product-type-specific lifetime       | App/product identifier and developer-delivered functionality |
| Kindle                     | Title licence, subscription access, or a prepaid gift copy                | Kindle content and supported reader                          |
| Bandcamp                   | Digital release/track purchase; some merchandise includes digital content | Release/track and downloadable files                         |
| itch.io                    | Project purchase, bundle or generated download key                        | Project files, sometimes individually priced                 |
| Gumroad                    | Product/version, including membership or licensed product                 | Files, posts or creator-integrated functionality             |
| Patreon                    | Membership tier or one-time offering                                      | Selected posts and creator-defined benefits                  |
| Ko-fi                      | Shop item or membership tier; a tip is another operation                  | Files/links or tier-restricted posts/rewards                 |
| Bilibili                   | Paid content/course or a distinct timed support programme                 | Content/lessons or programme-specific rewards                |
| Ghost                      | Publication tier and duration, including a gift subscription              | Posts selected for a membership audience                     |

### Steam: package licence is not a marketable item

**Fact.** A package groups applications and depots; buying it or activating its key determines which content an account can download and launch. Packages can share an AppID. Adding content to a package can benefit its existing owners; new multi-copy packages cannot simply be created like ordinary packages. Thus package identity is neither game identity nor a generic inventory-copy count. [Packages](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/application/packages)

For a backend, authenticating a session ticket establishes SteamID; ownership is a separate `CheckAppOwnership(AppID)` or publisher-ownership query. An OpenID login alone is not that check. This is a concrete benefit gate for, for example, a publisher's download or private forum. [Authentication and ownership](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/auth) The subscriber agreement describes licensed subscriptions and restricts transfer except where expressly permitted. Inventory separately marks item definitions `tradable` and `marketable`; a bought game licence is not thereby a resale item. [Agreement](https://store.steampowered.com/subscriber_agreement/), [inventory schema](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/inventory/schema)

The refund policy distinguishes playtime from the purchase date and covers gifts, with recipient participation for redeemed gifts and money returned to the purchaser. This is consumption affecting refund eligibility, not playtime creating ownership. [Refunds](https://store.steampowered.com/steam_refunds/) Partner gross sales/activation reports differ from monthly reports containing deductions and payable amounts. [Sales reporting](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/finance/payments_salesreporting)

**Unknown.** The sources do not expose Steam's private licence/revocation schema, wishlist/progress/cache joins, or a universal chargeback-recovery transition. **Inference:** keep acquisition evidence and current use checks separate; do not model all Steam purchases as transferable copies. Market and Wallet mechanics are treated separately in the module map.

### Apple: purchase evidence, current entitlement and developer authority

**Fact.** StoreKit distinguishes consumables, non-consumables, auto-renewing and non-renewing subscriptions. A product's configured type, rather than a content page, determines purchase semantics. [In-app products](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/configure-in-app-purchase-settings/overview-for-configuring-in-app-purchases/) App Store transactions are signed JWS information returned through `VerificationResult`; verified and unverified results must be distinguished. Transaction identifiers and purchase/revocation properties are evidence, not a developer's membership in an App Store Connect team. [Transaction](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/transaction)

`Transaction.currentEntitlements` includes non-consumables and applicable subscriptions, excludes refunded/revoked products and consumables, and includes the latest non-renewing subscription transaction even when finished: its validity still needs interpretation. This is not an all-purchases ledger or an app-specific count of unused consumables. [Current entitlements](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/transaction/currententitlements) Family Sharing can share eligible non-consumables/subscriptions; family access must stop when sharing ends or a relevant refund occurs. It is a defined access relationship, not resale of the original purchase. [Family Sharing](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/storekit/supporting-family-sharing-in-your-app)

A consumer can use the receipt to locate the purchasing account and request a refund; approval depends on circumstances and region. A receipt or downloaded app alone cannot establish every current in-app right. [Consumer refunds](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223) App Store Connect roles govern developer administrative privileges; the Account Holder signs agreements. Financial reports contain finalized proceeds and payments. Apple expressly warns against using the transaction payload's price/currency as revenue-reconciliation evidence. [Roles](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-your-team/overview-of-accounts-and-roles/), [payments](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/getting-paid/overview-of-receiving-payments/), [transaction payload](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/appstoreserverapi/jwstransactiondecodedpayload)

**Unknown.** App-specific use/download gates, wishlist/library presentation, progress, cache handling, consumable restoration and gifting/copy rules beyond the documented product types cannot be generalized. **Inference:** buyer rights, signed transaction evidence, consumption counters and seller administration are four authorities, even within one ecosystem.

### Kindle: a licence, reader state and a gift copy

**Fact.** Kindle terms license rather than sell digital content for personal use through supported applications/devices. Subscription content is available during the subscription; redistribution, resale and assignment are restricted except as expressly allowed. The terms describe reader/software restrictions, possible termination and collection of usage such as the last page read. Those are contractual and reader behaviors, not evidence of Amazon's internal entitlement tables. [Kindle Store terms](https://digprjsurvey.amazon.com/csad/help/node/201014950?theme=light)

Gift purchasing has distinct copy semantics: prepaid multi-copy purchases create a redemption link per copy; unredeemed copies can be re-gifted or refunded subject to marketplace-specific deadlines. This is not transfer of a redeemed personal licence. KDP also distinguishes royalty accrual for the gift options. [Buying and gifting](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200652260) The customer return policy permits eligible accidental ebook returns, whereas KDP royalties use specified price, territory, tax and delivery conditions. Neither reading progress nor a headline royalty percentage is a complete financial history. [Kindle returns](https://digprjsurvey.amazon.com/csad/help/node/GKM69DUUYKQWKWX7), [KDP royalties](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G200644210)

**Unknown.** The surveyed pages do not disclose a public authorization API, exact ordinary repeat-purchase cardinality, chargeback reinstatement, seller order-level visibility/payout detail beyond royalty rules, or relationships among wishlist, personal collection, receipt and downloaded-cache rows. **Inference:** distinguish one person's content licence from an unredeemed, separately identifiable gift claim; cached bytes and progress are not fresh proof of access.

### Bandcamp: receipt recovery and collection access

**Fact.** Purchases can be recovered through the collection or receipt/download email. Current re-download guidance asks ordinary buyers without an account to create one from the download link. The gift guidance separately says a gift recipient need not have an account; these are different documented flows, not a universal account requirement. Gifts can come from wishlists and can be sent to multiple recipients. [Re-downloads](https://get.bandcamp.help/en/articles/15263192-can-i-re-download-my-purchases), [gifts](https://get.bandcamp.help/en/articles/15263244-sending-gifts)

Digital purchase terms cover downloading and permitted streaming, but legal or contractual removal can remove previously bought material from collections. Proof of payment is relevant to support; continuing collection visibility is not an unconditional promise of perpetual hosting. A downloaded file does not make later hosting recoverable. [Terms](https://bandcamp.com/terms_of_use?from=footer) Sellers have release summaries, payout statements and raw data covering sales, payouts and reversals; gross/net summaries and payout grouping answer different questions. [Sales reports](https://get.bandcamp.help/en/articles/15263083-which-sales-report-should-i-use)

**Unknown.** Internal licence identities, ordinary duplicate-copy accounting, transfer of an already redeemed purchase, progress/cache enforcement and chargeback restoration are not documented in these sources. **Inference:** a private collection is a useful recovery surface; REZICS should still preserve immutable purchase history when delivery availability changes, and should not turn a wishlist gift into a generic transfer feature.

### itch.io: ownership keys and mutable file eligibility

**Fact.** Payment produces a transaction-specific download page, recoverable by purchase email; buying without an account is supported. Linking it to an account changes how the page is accessed. [Finding purchases](https://itch.io/docs/buying/already-bought) Download keys can also be generated by a creator or claimed in an eligible sale; downloading a free project does not itself create ownership. A linked key requires its owner's login. Key holders see the current uploaded file set, so a purchased project and its bytes are separate identities. [Download keys](https://itch.io/docs/creators/download-keys)

Changing the project's minimum price preserves existing ownership. Individually priced files are different: increasing their threshold can exclude a buyer whose historical payment no longer meets it. Early-access purchases include future updates. This is a documented, potentially surprising mutable-benefit policy; REZICS should choose and snapshot its own policy rather than assume all file access is perpetual. [Pricing](https://itch.io/docs/creators/pricing)

Direct payments and collected payments have different merchant, refund/chargeback and payout responsibilities; seller payment reports cannot be interpreted without that mode. External activation keys are also distinct benefits and may be delayed or exhausted. [Payments](https://itch.io/docs/creators/payments), [purchase recovery](https://itch.io/docs/buying/already-bought)

**Unknown.** These pages do not establish all gifting, repeated-purchase/copy, dispute-restoration or wishlist/progress/cache rules. A creator-generated key is not evidence of a sale or of permission to transfer an existing key. **Inference:** retain entitlement provenance independently of payment, and explicitly define whether a benefit promises a fixed version or an update channel.

### Gumroad: licence verification is an integration operation

**Fact.** A product may issue a licence key. The documented `/v2/licenses/verify` operation takes product/key evidence and can increment use count; callers can disable that increment for checks. Its response exposes refund/dispute and membership information. The creator's integration must interpret it to permit actual use; one verification count is not one financial purchase or necessarily one physical device. [Licence keys](https://gumroad.com/help/article/76-license-keys)

A purchase account is optional for many products; the Library groups purchases by account/email. Removing a Library card does not remove access through the original receipt/download link. The reader can retain a PDF/EPUB position. These are unusually explicit examples of presentation, progress and purchase access diverging. [Library](https://gumroad.com/help/article/198-your-gumroad-library) The sales dashboard supports buyer lookup, refunds, receipt resend, version and seat administration; changing seats or versions there does not automatically charge/refund the difference. [Sales dashboard](https://gumroad.com/help/article/268-customer-dashboard)

The payouts dashboard separately reports sales, deductions and payout amounts; chargebacks can affect the balance. Consumer refund guidance also distinguishes app-store purchases, whose refunds go through that store. [Payouts](https://gumroad.com/help/article/269-balance-page), [refunds](https://gumroad.com/help/article/190-how-do-i-get-a-refund)

**Unknown.** The gifting article could not be read beyond its title in this session; no gift-transfer protocol is inferred. General repeat-copy cardinality, file-cache revocation and exact rights recovery after a won dispute are not established by these pages. **Inference:** use a read-only authorization check for repeated use; consuming a licence seat must be a separate idempotent command.

### Patreon: a tier is a selection of current benefits

**Fact.** Post access depends on the tiers selected for that post, not merely paying a numerically higher amount. A custom pledge without tier membership may not unlock the post. Declined payments lock access while billing retries; cancellation access depends on the billing model. External rewards require the creator's corresponding delivery/integration. [Post access](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360026984152-Why-can-t-I-access-posts)

Patreon's terms cover memberships and one-time offerings and describe a personal, revocable, nontransferable licence. Eligible membership gifts specify tier, duration and a redemption window; they do not make every historical post a transferable owned copy. Refunds, store-billed purchases and chargebacks have separate consequences, including possible creator balance deductions/recovery. [Legal terms](https://www.patreon.com/policy/legal) Creator payout history has its own statuses and destination, while separate exports cover patron payments, refunds, earnings and taxes. A processed patron payment, current post access and completed creator withdrawal are different facts. [Payouts dashboard](https://support.patreon.com/hc/en-us/articles/360058429051-Payouts-Dashboard)

**Unknown.** A universal private entitlement schema, bookmark/progress/cache relationship, repeat one-time purchase cardinality and exact access restoration after each dispute outcome are not established. **Inference:** model a tier's benefit selection and term explicitly if subscriptions are later adopted; do not import a subscription model into today's one-time Commerce slice.

### Ko-fi: tips, shop purchases and memberships differ

**Fact.** A shop can deliver uploaded files or links through an order/receipt flow, with later access to updated digital items. Shop orders have a seller dashboard. A listed quantity is not a documented transferable-licence count. [Shop](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/360009712917-Ko-fi-Shop-Sell-digital-physical-products) Memberships unlock tier benefits and eligible restricted posts during their paid term; cancellation alone normally retains access until the term ends, whereas cancellation plus refund can end it immediately. A monthly tip is not automatically a tier membership. Discord-role delivery is a separate integration and retries can delay removal. [Memberships](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/4402945994001-Ko-fi-Memberships-and-Membership-Tiers)

Refund guidance distinguishes removal of digital re-download access from the membership term. Payments go directly through the creator's connected provider; Ko-fi is not the merchant of record for these creator sales and does not adjudicate provider disputes. Thus the creator's payments/order views and provider settlement are separate evidence surfaces. [Issuing refunds](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/7733731935773-How-to-issue-a-refund), [supporter refunds](https://help.ko-fi.com/hc/en-us/articles/28169910467869-How-can-a-supporter-get-a-refund)

**Unknown.** General gifts/transfers, duplicate-copy identity, chargeback reinstatement, wishlist/progress linkage and recall of already downloaded files are not established. **Inference:** a donation, purchased file and recurring membership should never share an implicit “supporter therefore owns content” rule.

### Bilibili: paid content and timed support are separate programmes

**Fact.** The paid-content agreement covers separately purchased video/text/image content, including preorder cases. It grants personal platform use rather than intellectual-property ownership or redistribution rights; copyright/region/time restrictions can affect availability. It restricts transfer and generally excludes voluntary refunds, with stated exceptions when content cannot be supplied. VIP status and its coupons are not interchangeable with this purchase. [Paid-content agreement](https://www.bilibili.com/blackboard/activity-y-SJTTYBp.html)

Classroom guidance distinguishes course validity lasting a number of months, a fixed date or a long-term period; changed validity rules apply to new purchases. Lessons, previews and the course sale are separate concepts. It describes complaint/refund handling and withholding creator revenue for relevant breaches. [Classroom cooperation help](https://www.bilibili.com/cheese/cooperate/help?active=6) Charging/support tiers instead describe timed rewards such as posts, videos and emotes. Their creator income/withdrawal rules are a separate programme and cannot establish classroom order settlement. [Charging agreement](https://www.bilibili.com/blackboard/activity-3URYtAAX2G.html), [charging income rules](https://www.bilibili.com/blackboard/activity-MCghHtgvEO.html)

**Unknown.** Public documentation read here does not disclose the internal receipt/entitlement schema or authorization API, complete classroom seller order/settlement fields, chargeback recovery, ordinary duplicate copies or wishlist/progress/cache authority. No download right is inferred from a viewing purchase. **Inference:** snapshot the bought term and benefit; a subject-level “paid” badge cannot describe all these programmes.

### Ghost: explicit audience gating with readable source

**Fact.** Tiers configure prices and described benefits; posts select their audience. Archiving a tier stops new acquisition without simply canceling existing subscribers. [Tiers](https://ghost.org/help/tiers/), [protected content](https://ghost.org/help/protected-content/) At the pinned source revision, `ghost/core/server/services/members/content-gating.js` builds an audience filter from post visibility and tier slugs; missing membership or an unusable tier filter denies access. Web and email segmentation reuse that decision path. This is source behavior, not proof of atomic consistency with all payment changes. [Source](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/blob/75f33df36d963327323f4af2c159dbf345e725ad/ghost/core/server/services/members/content-gating.js)

Gifts are one-time Stripe payments for a chosen tier/term with a later member redemption. The publisher is paid at purchase even if redemption never happens; active paid members cannot redeem them and gifts cannot stack. Stripe refunds update gift status; expiration does not automatically refund. An archived tier's purchased gift remains redeemable. Admin provides gift purchase/redemption notifications, member filtering and analytics. [Gift subscriptions](https://ghost.org/help/gift-subscriptions/) The inspected `ghost/core/server/services/members/members-api/services/payments-service.js` separately handles tier/cadence prices and checkout configuration. [Payment source](https://github.com/TryGhost/Ghost/blob/75f33df36d963327323f4af2c159dbf345e725ad/ghost/core/server/services/members/members-api/services/payments-service.js)

**Unknown.** The inspected files do not prove a full chargeback/recovery contract, downloaded-content recall, general transferable copies, reading-progress/wishlist integration or complete financial reporting. **Inference:** share access decisions across delivery surfaces, but keep purchase, gift claim and membership lifetimes separate. REZICS can implement that pattern using its own Access port; copying Ghost's schema or filter engine is unnecessary.

## Shared patterns and deliberate differences

**Inference from the survey:**

- A sale may bundle many benefits, and a benefit may be obtainable from several offers or a non-sale grant. Identity must survive repricing and delisting.
- Receipt recovery, an account library and a current authorization query solve different problems. The Gumroad removal behavior and itch.io free-download behavior make this distinction observable.
- Access is to an action on an actual target: launch an AppID, retrieve a project file, read a tier-selected post or watch a valid course. A subject page, receipt, successful redirect and installed file are inadequate substitutes.
- Version/update policy is a product promise. Steam package additions, itch.io file thresholds and Bilibili term snapshots make different promises; there is no universal perpetual-file rule to import.
- A gift claim, licence, inventory item and stored balance have different transfer rules. Refund processing, loss of future access and deletion of already delivered bytes are also different operations.
- Seller sales, provider evidence, financial reports and payout completion are separate projections. A purchase API response does not prove final net revenue.

## Current REZICS: reusable boundaries and missing links

**Existing facts.** [Types](../../services/kernel/src/commerce/model.ts) place offers in the creator account's partition; orders, entitlement and Stripe transaction evidence live in the buyer's partition. An offer has `targetSubjectId`, title, price/currency, tax code, royalty basis points, state and revision. An order captures those terms and has `pending | paid | failed | unknown` settlement plus Checkout navigation and observation time. An entitlement links buyer, order, offer and subject with grant/revocation times and reason. None identifies an actual benefit resource/action/version.

[Routes](../../services/kernel/src/commerce/routes.ts) already expose known-ID offers, order creation, private order history and entitlement read/list. [Offer UI](../../apps/web/src/checkout/offer-page.tsx) starts hosted Checkout through the web boundary; [order UI](../../apps/web/src/checkout/order-page.tsx) displays status and entitlement state. Polling settlement deliberately marks current entitlement information for refresh. The [subject page](../../apps/web/src/features/pages/subject.tsx) lacks the purchase entry; known-ID checkout pages do not complete subject discovery, owned-benefit navigation or actual use authorization.

The [settlement owner contract](../../services/kernel/src/commerce/README.md) retains the direct US account, hosted Checkout and Managed Payments baseline, subject to actual provider eligibility. REZICS remains offer authority. Native order creation precedes provider I/O; stable native receipts, provider idempotency and durable reconciliation have different lifetimes. Fresh provider reads, not redirect parameters or webhook payload fields, drive fulfilment. Payment and entitlement are distinct: a refunded order remains historically paid. Existing refund/dispute rules can revoke and later restore an entitlement when the blocking condition clears; terminal account lifecycle still forbids restoration of private relations. These are current owner rules, not findings that another product or Stripe supports every financial assumption.

[Payout recording](../../services/kernel/src/commerce/payouts.ts) accepts named evidence lines, deduplicates them and incrementally updates a creator-period statement. Late adjustments do not rewrite paid historical statements. `markPaid` records an external payment reference; it does not itself send a Stripe transfer. [Royalty calculation](../../services/kernel/src/commerce/royalty.ts) still requires the separate evidence audit for tax/FX/reversal assumptions. Existing account-role grants enabling creator statement access are not content-benefit entitlements.

[Library](../library-and-collections.md) independently owns private states, labels, progress and holdings/copies. A purchase must not create these facts or public activity silently. Library Undo must never undo payment, entitlement or future item ownership. Content delivery remains closed until its own owner contract and grants are ready.

## Recommended model for adoption

Start with one-time, account-bound benefits. Do not implement subscriptions, gifts, transfer, consumable seats, wallets, points, market or exchange in this slice. Keep a future transferable item separate as described in [the module map](../commerce-modules.md). The following types are design sketches, not new exported types or permission names:

```ts
type BenefitRef = { benefitId: string; version: number };
type BenefitDefinition = {
  id: string;
  creatorAccountId: string;
  targetSubjectId: string; // public identity, not the protected payload
  version: number;
  kind: 'read' | 'download' | 'stream' | 'use';
  target: { owner: string; resourceType: string; resourceId: string };
  action: string; // an action adopted by that target's existing Access owner
  deliveryPolicy: 'fixed-version' | 'update-channel';
  termsVersion: string;
};
type PurchaseLineSnapshot = {
  offerId: string;
  offerRevision: number;
  creatorAccountId: string;
  targetSubjectId: string;
  title: string;
  benefits: readonly BenefitDefinition[];
  currency: string;
  unitAmountMinor: number;
  taxCode: string;
  royaltyBasisPoints: number;
};
type BenefitEntitlement = {
  id: string;
  accountId: string;
  benefit: BenefitRef;
  source: { kind: 'purchase'; orderId: string };
  state: 'active' | 'revoked';
  revision: number;
};
```

Commerce owns the benefit catalogue in the creator partition alongside offers, avoiding a second catalogue. A benefit references an existing target owned by Content or the relevant use-feature owner. Publishing an offer must validate seller authority over that target through Access. A subject reference alone never proves the creator can sell it. Immutable benefit versions define what the order bought; mutable availability/takedown and current account eligibility remain separate. Prefer a fixed delivered edition for the first slice; adopt an update channel only with a named owner promise. Offer revision or delisting cannot silently edit purchased terms.

Initially cap an offer to one benefit and quantity one. This permits existing single-subject orders to evolve without inventing bundle accounting or partial-benefit refunds. Existing orders have **unknown benefit mapping**, not an invented licence for all subject content. Migration needs an explicit seller/Content-approved mapping or a truthful “purchase recorded; benefit unavailable” display. Future bundles require bounded line counts and a refund-allocation policy before adoption.

Separate state machines:

```text
offer:       active -> delisted                 (new acquisition only)
order:       unpaid observations reconcile among pending/unknown/failed
             any unpaid state -> paid; paid -> paid (refund is separate)
entitlement: absent -> active <-> revoked       (evidence + current lifecycle)
delivery:    available <-> unavailable          (target owner, independent)
use decision at request time:
  current authenticated/eligible account
  AND exact current entitlement for the bought benefit
  AND target owner's current Access decision and delivery availability
Library:     explicit personal edits only       (independent history)
```

The use conjunction is a proposed paid-benefit eligibility input **inside existing Access**, with Content retaining its resource/action decision. It is not a new policy engine or an `access:grant` escalation. Existing public/free routes keep their own rules. An active entitlement does not bypass moderation, suspension, takedown, geographic/term restrictions or a terminal account fence; revocation does not erase the order. A failed refund or won dispute can restore access only through existing authoritative reconciliation and only when no other current blocker remains.

### Revocation, retries and privacy

**Recommendation.** Current authorization is evaluated at each protected use, with the entitlement's buyer-home revision and target's current lifecycle checked through bounded owner ports. A copied entitlement response, a stale Access grant projection or a successful checkout redirect is insufficient. If a materialized Access grant is eventually introduced, its source identity and revocation generation must be checked against current entitlement evidence; it cannot be the sole stale authority.

No cross-home read gives simultaneous linearizability across buyer and Content homes. Define the decision instant and reject stale revisions when accepting a use; recheck before issuing a delivery token. For downloads/streams recommend a maximum 60-second token/renewal window, account and exact target/version binding, and fail closed when authoritative checks are unavailable. This bounds new delivery after a race; it cannot recall bytes already sent, offline files or a request admitted before revocation. A stricter promise would need an adopted shared fence/co-location design. The duration and delivery-origin capability are cross-owner adoption decisions, not existing guarantees.

Keep existing principal-scoped receipts and reauthorization before replay. Order-start retries retain their native order identity; provider timeouts stay unknown and reconcile through the existing durable runtime. A finite provider idempotency window does not authorize creating a second charge after native evidence expires. Evidence application deduplicates by bound provider account/mode/object and stable effect identity; reordered events cause fresh current reads, not state rollback. A read-only authorization request never consumes a copy. Unknown evidence must not grant, restore, claim a payout or become a guessed zero amount.

Orders, entitlements and use history are private. Creator sales need an order locator and accounting facts, not unrestricted buyer email, Library activity or reading progress. Public subject/offer caches must never contain private entitlement state. Audit/evidence retention follows purpose, legal need and existing terminal-account barriers; retain necessary financial facts with minimized identity, not perpetual content-consumption telemetry. Access and Library lifecycle rules remain authoritative.

## Meaningful later outcomes and remaining journey work

**Recommendation for focused product verification after adoption:** an anonymous viewer sees only disclosed subject/offer information and signs in to buy; an eligible buyer sees the exact bought edition/price/terms, returns to pending status until verified fulfilment, finds the benefit later and can actually use it. Another account cannot read that order or reuse its delivery token. A creator can manage only authorized targets/offers and sees sales versus statements with their distinct meanings. A suspended/terminal account, revoked buyer or unavailable target cannot retrieve protected bytes. A refunded buyer keeps truthful purchase history without a Library mutation. An active owner opening a benefit creates no new charge, grant, copy, progress or receipt mutation.

Repeat requests with the same semantic idempotency key return the original result after current authorization; a changed offer/benefit intent conflicts. Duplicate evidence/effects do not double-grant or double-record royalties; old events cannot restore a newer blocked state. No-effect denial and read-only use checks leave native state unchanged. These are proposed persona/replay outcomes, not tests executed or new test scaffolding in this research task.

The reusable slice is known-offer checkout, buyer order/evidence binding, durable reconciliation, entitlement history, private purchase APIs, ordinary Access and creator statement APIs. Missing pieces are a concrete benefit catalogue/snapshot, subject discovery entry, stale-price consent, duplicate-acquisition policy, owned-benefit web navigation, target delivery enforcement, bounded current eligibility, creator offer/sales pages and evidence enrichment. Keep these as later product slices with visible outcomes; do not use this research to declare implementation, payment qualification or integrated acceptance complete.
