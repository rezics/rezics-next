# Subject overview, destinations and progressive disclosure: current investigation

Research date: 2026-10-31. Status: evidence and recommendations for adoption, not a product contract or a delivery claim.

The subject page should answer four questions quickly: what is this, is it relevant to me, what can I do next, and where can I investigate further? Keep identity, a compact public rating, personal next actions and a short About section immediately useful. Follow with small, bounded previews of the work's structure and community; put exhaustive lists at real destinations. Fold long prose, secondary facts, edition metadata and rating methodology independently. A failed optional dependency must not turn every section into a repeated generic notice.

The current base contains substantial native capabilities, including guarded Realm-selected factual pages, selected-place community reads, public rating aggregates, personal rating writes and real subject-offer discovery. These are not equivalent to a finished subject experience. The rating Ready example is a constructed aggregate display, not a complete rating journey. The purchase-slot Empty example means no provider is mounted, not that Commerce returned no offers. Its raw page recording is also older than the current read plan. The principal defect classes are capability-state conflation, incomplete composition, missing disclosure design and evidence that protects individual pieces without judging the complete reader outcome.

### Shared patterns, disagreements and adaptation

The accessible work pages converge on recognizable identity, compact evaluative/social evidence, a personal action, short descriptive content and secondary routes for exhaustive information. Work/edition/release is repeatedly a useful distinction. Ratings are almost always more legible when count, source/population and user's own action are distinguishable. Purchase/preview/playback depend on availability rather than catalogue existence.

They disagree on density and emphasis. Bangumi and Douban tolerate more immediate metadata; Goodreads and Apple Books prioritize reading decisions; Steam foregrounds acquisition and separates recent reviews; Qidian's separate chapter directory reflects serial consumption. No single layout should be copied across all REZICS kinds. Use released definition/presentation configuration over the same subject/relation/content primitives. Per-kind route logic, separate rating stores and retailer-specific purchase truth would violate the repository architecture.

Classify the references before commissioning work:

| Reference capability                                                                             | REZICS classification                                                                       | Consequence                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Short summary, key facts, portrait cover, edition/credit/relation previews and destination links | Existing generic data and presentation configuration, with missing coherent folding/quality | Finish shared rendering and configuration before adding new backend engines.                                    |
| Global rating, thresholded distribution, own vote, source statistics                             | Implemented native primitives; incomplete integration/presentation                          | Preserve current algorithms and ordinary API; fix independent states and audience/grain labeling.               |
| Selected Realm facts and local community                                                         | Implemented guarded composition, partially exposed; some owner prose stale                  | Do not commission a duplicate Realm wire. Complete the specific missing rating/list/selection seams.            |
| Searchable choice among hundreds of rating populations, including honest counted status          | Missing polished generic read/selection capability                                          | Design bounded directory/metadata and state contracts first; no browser enumeration.                            |
| Native offer discovery and exact offer review                                                    | Implemented/exposed primitives and browser adapter; current evidence incomplete             | Finish honest subject placement and recorded/native journeys, not an unconditional unavailable adapter rewrite. |
| Already-owned/continue from subject                                                              | Missing bounded private subject-benefit lookup                                              | Keep the existing records link until Commerce owns a bounded exact-benefit answer.                              |
| Similar works/personal recommendations, following feed, Realm rating sort, serial subscriptions  | Explicit scope/adoption boundary                                                            | Do not turn competitor examples into silently required launch features. Curated Lists remain useful today.      |

### Composed page, sections and destinations

`apps/web/src/features/pages/subject.tsx::loadSubjectPage` calls the generated `pages.$read.post` with `{target:{subject,view?,realm?},languages,continuation?}`. It preserves native missing/redirect outcomes. `SubjectPageData` carries `profile`, `template`, `document`, optional `views`, optional selected `context`, optional continuation and a map of `{status,body}` reads. `subjectBody` resolves the actual planned subject descriptor rather than treating any JSON body as a subject.

`packages/composition/src/reads.ts` deduplicates canonical descriptors into positional `r0…` keys. Limits are 16 distinct reads, 128 logical returned-subject slots, 256 nodes, depth eight and a 65,536-byte canonical document. Descriptor budgets are useful admission limits; they do not prove all underlying permission, document, projection or database work. `services/kernel/src/presentation/reads.ts` and its README retain the separate native dispatch/control responsibilities, with four dispatch workers.

`services/kernel/src/presentation/selected-page.ts::selectPage/guardSelectedPage` actually read current Realm, publication and Context authority. A genuinely absent publication chooses independently authorized Global facts; a hidden/denied/banned Realm does not. Final guards discard changed output. `packages/composition/src/registry.ts::readSubjectThreads` uses the selected Realm population ID as Content place, or Global otherwise, with preview windows five and six respectively. `apps/web/src/features/pages/subject-community.ts::withComposedCommunity` consumes those original leaves and batches visible author summaries. The older compatibility helper hardcodes Global, but is explicitly not the actual composed query path.

`apps/web/src/features/pages/overview.tsx` already caps ordinary previews at 12. `Summary` renders the selected document or fallback description plus facts/tags; spoiler disclosure exists, but ordinary long-prose collapse does not. Spoiler reveal is not a substitute for Show more. `MoreStatements`/Details and full views exist, but that does not bound the height of prose rendered in About. Independent `Failed` components render the same generic message and whole-page reload link; repeated failures therefore create a wall by construction. RelationGroups has a partial-success path but still appends generic copy.

`apps/web/src/features/pages/views.ts::subjectTabs` derives destinations from configured nodes/read results. Overview and Details are stable when defined. Reviews and Discussion require an open successful native read, including successful empty. Small complete lists can fit entirely in the overview and omit their tab; direct URLs remain destinations. Missing reads and missing counts are not verified zero. Relation/edition/character/credit views use the same forward/inverse graph streams, not new per-kind stores. `view-tabs.tsx` scrolls the active entry into view and allows horizontal overflow on phones.

The standard scale stores integer 0…20, displayed as 0…10 in half steps. Zero is a valid saved vote and a valid published mean when its population is sufficient; null is unrated/withheld, never zero. Existing `hasPublicMean` checks a positive published count and accepts a mean of zero. Preserve it.

Privacy thresholds are five for work/release and ten for other grains in `aggregate-read.ts`; do not derive withheld means or histograms client-side. Public ordering uses the same-question, same-population Bayesian prior described in the owner README; no active prior means no score, not a fallback external/global score. The displayed mean and ordering score are different values. Do not pool episodes, releases or imported distributions into a work headline.

`voter-realms.ts` has `VOTER_REALM_CAP = 100`; later memberships can be recorded uncounted, with promotion when a counted membership leaves. A user in 300 Realms is therefore not necessarily counted in 300 populations. The current aggregate endpoint also deliberately returns count-zero/freshness for an unreadable requested Realm. This protects disclosure but cannot be presented as proof of an empty readable population. A polished owner contract must preserve concealment while separating what the UI is entitled to say; it must not expose a private Realm's existence or threshold deficit.

### Folding is not pagination

Use three explicit mechanisms:

1. **Prose disclosure:** Show more/Show less expands the same summary or review excerpt. Preserve paragraphs, language, spoiler gating and the reader's position. Short prose has no redundant control. Expansion is local state and causes no unrelated reads; loading an omitted large body requires its own truthful owner state.
2. **Secondary details disclosure:** Details and editions groups edition facts, identifiers and format choice below the key facts. Within it, keep selected edition facts separate from work-wide facts. Opening this group does not eagerly load every edition/credit or every Realm. The Details URL remains directly addressable and meaningful without JavaScript.
3. **Exhaustive destination:** All N editions, all credits, relations, contents, reviews or lists navigates to the existing view, where one opaque continuation is read per action. A fold with all rows hidden in the DOM is not bounded navigation. Do not hide purchase consent, saving errors or the only personal action behind a vague More link.

Native `<details>/<summary>` is suitable for secondary static content; a custom prose toggle must use a real button and `aria-expanded`/`aria-controls`. For ordinary rendered prose, CSS line clamping alone leaves hidden focusable children and ambiguous screen-reader content; render a safe excerpt and explicit expansion instead. Do not insert focusable links into visually clipped content. Preserve the expanded state across incidental personal-query updates; reset deliberately on subject, language or selected Context change.

### Destination rules

Keep stable names and canonical server-rendered URLs: Overview, Editions, Contents, Characters, Credits, Relations, Reviews, Discussion, Lists, Details; Works and Appearances for the applicable identity. Use the owner contract's omission of empty/small-complete structural tabs rather than adding one tab per property. Every preview still has a clear route when there is more; a direct empty full view gives an honest local empty state. Reviews/Discussion can remain destinations when their native read is open and empty, so readers can participate.

A destination is not a hidden accordion panel. Implement routed destinations as navigation links with `aria-current="page"`; reserve ARIA tab roles/arrow-key behavior for an actual same-page tabpanel model. The existing Base UI Tabs wrapper around anchors in `view-tabs.tsx` warrants a semantic review against that distinction, not a speculative declaration that every link currently fails accessibility. Keep horizontal phone scrolling, visible active position and usable focus without obscuring headings under sticky chrome. Counts are optional and must retain exact/at-least semantics. Do not display an unavailable count as zero, nor invent a total by draining cursors.

Detail-view navigation currently also reads overview for tabs/frame: `apps/web/README.md` documents up to two `pages:read` calls; a later compact view-count/frame contract could remove this duplication, but is not delivered merely by suggesting it.

Current native access remains authoritative at final output. Never cache selected protected Realm facts by subject ID alone; partition by the actual reader/credential and selected Realm where the owner requires it. Requested Realm selector is not proof of selected Context. A genuine fallback shows Global and an explicit deliberate return path, without unavailable Realm metadata. Do not turn denials, account suspension, bans, removal or platform closure into a Global/anonymous success to avoid an error.

Lifecycle scenarios for adoption must cover:

- Subject merge while viewing/paging: canonical survivor and original offer target remain distinct; restart stale continuations, retain no old permission or duplicate vote.
- Realm leave/ban/publication withdrawal/Context removal between initial read and interaction: current native guards conceal/discard stale data; no stale local label/count/body leaks, no automatic Global substitution after denial.
- Membership counting/promotion/erasure: own vote and eventual aggregates can temporarily differ; preserve native freshness and do not locally reconstruct withheld results.
- Sign-out/account switch/credential failure while own vote or offers are in flight: cancel/partition private results; never show previous actor's value/record and never silently retry anonymously.
- Offer repricing/delisting/captured edition change: current review and expected revision guard purchase; a prior subject card is discovery, not consent. Paid records retain historic truth after refund/revocation; current use is separately checked.
- Long-lived expanded details and page navigation: cancel stale requests, bound retained pages, reset selection deliberately, preserve appropriate focus/back state and no-JavaScript destinations.

## Accessibility and cross-surface consistency

Use the W3C [disclosure pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/) for custom buttons, the [tabs pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/tabs/) only for real tabpanels, and the [rating-slider example's cautions](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/slider/examples/slider-rating/) when evaluating the existing slider. APG examples are illustrative, not production certification. [WCAG 2.2 target-size guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/target-size-minimum.html) describes the 24 CSS-pixel AA criterion and exceptions; a 44-pixel product touch target is a recommendation, not a claim that WCAG universally mandates 44.

Keyboard/focus outcomes matter as much as attribute existence. Expand/collapse must retain a meaningful focus position; collapsing content containing focus must return it to its trigger. Destination navigation updates title/heading and active link predictably. A mobile chooser is a modal dialog/sheet with labeled search, close/back, focus containment and return. Tables/histograms need textual values, counts, scale and population; color alone cannot encode distribution. Pending save/error states must be announced once, without seven repeated status messages. Respect reduced motion and handle large text, long translated labels and mixed-script titles without clipped actions.

The same public mean/count/scale/state must appear on subject, search hit, Collection row and any configured Zone card that is permitted to show it; none may infer a score from imported data or private Realm figures. Default cover slots remain portrait everywhere, with landscape originals contained and available in the viewer. A missing cover uses a neutral identity-consistent placeholder, not a kind-specific landscape fallback. Personal Library state, follows, progress, own vote and entitlements remain separate controls/queries; a purchase must not silently mark read or add a copy. Subject-scoped actions must reuse the settled header search and Filters entry rather than add a second filter wall. This report does not assert backend support for every search scope; the independent search investigation owns that trace.

### Decisions resolved here and genuinely owner-level adoption

The following ambiguities were resolved as research recommendations from current contracts/source, without asking the maintainer:

- Overview is a curated bounded reading page, not a dump of every template read. Long prose, secondary details and exhaustive navigation are different mechanisms.
- Preserve the existing canonical destinations and omission rules for empty/small-complete structural tabs; Reviews/Discussion remain useful open participation destinations when empty.
- Global remains the public headline; selected Realm facts/community must be explicitly labeled, with no fabricated local ratings/lists and no automatic fallback on denial.
- Preserve standard scale, zero, thresholds, Bayesian ownership, imported separation, ordinary writes and existing idempotency. The problem is presentation/integration, not authority to redesign the algorithm.
- Routine empty purchase discovery should be quiet; provider absence is omitted; unavailable/preparing/denied are separate. The existing private records link remains until exact subject ownership is supported.
- Portrait slot with contained landscape original, no tagline and the settled shared header/search behavior are constraints, not options reopened by this report.

No new maintainer-only product decision is required to finish Global subject IA, folding, honest degradation, native rating controls or existing offer discovery. Managers/owners can adopt the concrete recommendations in their owning documents and reconcile the independent deep investigation. Two extensions need explicit product authority if desired: (a) ownership-aware subject Continue across multiple immutable editions—recommend a bounded private exact-benefit answer with explicit edition choice rather than a subject-wide ownership badge; (b) recommendations/feed/serial commerce or Realm rating sorting—recommend retaining their current scope boundaries rather than making them part of subject polish. The question of changing the 100 counted-Realm policy is also not a UI implementation choice; retain it and disclose the supported consequence, and seek an owner/maintainer decision only if changing that policy is proposed.
