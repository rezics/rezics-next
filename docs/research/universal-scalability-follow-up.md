## Adopted boundaries

Use a common meaning for source coverage, exact evidence, resumable progress and per-step limits.
Keep storage, transaction, authorization, lifecycle, completion and effects with the owning domain.
An incomplete observation does not establish absence. A page boundary, cursor, lease or artifact digest
does not prove a whole source set or a financial outcome. The [architecture owner](../architecture.md)
records the shared rule. The research does not justify a universal workflow table, one universal epoch,
or sharing an execution engine before two real consumers agree on the required invariants.

## Subsequent owner work

- **Prepared Search (Q07):** move accumulating output out of the control row into indexed rows or
  bounded segments while preserving atomic frontier updates, source identity, disclosure and erasure.
  Local custody expiry is established separately from physical PIT renewal, which must be verified
  on the installed engine. Preserve generation allocation and reader-retirement guards.
- **Library Export:** the current server already pages with a small control row; the browser accumulates
  the artifact. Consider renewable streaming and a bounded consumer sink after source-set proof. A
  durable immutable artifact or pinned cut is conditional on the required consistency and restart
  contract, rather than a prerequisite for the minimum fix.
- **Content revisions (M16):** design metadata-first enumeration and exact-body fetch for the first
  real consumer, with disclosure and early admission before bodies load. Keep compatible legacy
  reads. A count limit or declared encoded-byte threshold is not a measured heap bound.
- **Placement and throughput (Q02, Q03, R13):** preserve existing UUIDs and logical authority, measure
  the local gate, database fence and Search topology separately, and verify the complete movable
  transaction and copy closure before authorizing a home move. A routing-directory change does not
  alone split required write order or prove capacity.
- **Finance paging:** bounded provider pages and durable facts can support larger histories only
  with completion proof, replay identities, and migrated downstream royalty readers. Keep existing
  lifetime guards until consumers are verified; measured safe capacity and actual provider coverage
  remain open.

Do not raise numerical bounds or promote an exact, complete, moved or financially final result
until the owning native gate passes.
