For this focus, the owning contracts are [subject-page design][design], [slice handoffs][slices],
[SEO publication rules][seo] and [current public aggregate disclosure][aggregate]. Their requirements are:

1. A public feed includes only currently readable, visible, nonmerged Global subjects with a shown public fact
   beyond the title. It uses current public display labels, with actual language tags, independently of session
   or `Accept-Language`. Proposals, unselected/private facts and title-only subjects do not establish eligibility.
2. Public change time means the original authoritative time of a meaningful shown change, including removal
   while an older witness survives. Stable change identity deduplicates replay. Private/unshown activity,
   security counters, rebuild, compaction and worker clocks cannot advance it. Missing original evidence stays
   unknown and the field is omitted, including for legal old rows.
3. Current positive proof checks the original subject, Global Access, current selection/rank, moderation,
   provenance/fences and relation targets. D=8 cached witness identities are hints; exhausted uncertainty
   permits omission, then finite durable repair. Immediate complete foreign knowledge is not promised.
4. Source tuple routing and the retained `takenDownAt`/`takedownId` period belong to Content. Forward membership,
   reverse owner-home dependencies, version/tombstone replay and durable owner fanout must converge across
   restart and late membership without cross-home transactions or degree scans in native commands.
5. Repair/bootstrap/replay/fanout share at most 32 examined keys and **one total key-only sentinel** per step.
   Filtered keys count. Durable progress survives reconstruction; quiescence reaches repeated exact idle.
6. HTTP takes at most raw `limit+1` keys from one partition, proves only the candidates, resumes after the last
   examined key and does not consume the sentinel. Empty/short pages can continue. Signed global cursors bind
   purpose/version, partition/epoch, key and limit. GET never runs repair or scans for replacement eligible rows.
7. `createKernel` mounts one real publication runtime shared with omission scheduling. The real publication
   Relay and one default semantic WorkModule compose with Source services before one DBOS launch and drain
   through the existing lifecycle. A dormant accessor or custom test module list is insufficient.
8. Public numbers require current source-safe proof for the entire set for that viewer and Context. Otherwise
   omit the field/key, preserving rows only with their own current proof. Unknown is never zero, page length,
   an internal counter, stale `exact:false`, a lower bound or an alternate total-bearing flag.
9. All API, native/current-proof, foreign convergence, legal growth, generated-contract and Web outcomes remain
   mandatory. The separate Search four-case default-worker proof retains its own wire/count policy.

These rules serve different personas: anonymous feed readers receive public evidence only; authenticated
editors cannot personalize the feed; Context acceptors may alter private Context state without moving public
time; suspended accounts must retain normal denials. Aggregate proof is viewer/Context-specific, so an
anonymous example cannot establish the same result for every persona or private overlay.

## Shared source patterns, recommendation and limits

The owning documents and actual code share a consistent pattern: authority lives at native routed homes;
outbox/Relay delivery is durable and replayable; derived membership and counters are useful internal hints;
current positive disclosure is a separate bounded operation; original time/identity survives asynchronous
processing; and unknown public evidence is omitted rather than reconstructed from incidental state. The
candidate reader already follows raw keyset bounds, and Content already retains original tuple-period identity.
Neither primitive establishes the entire public outcome by itself.

Recommendation: adopt the evidence distinctions and existing owner chain above. Keep the current design and
acceptance scope; do not replace them with another algorithm or a narrower definition of completion. Review
the actual composed behavior after producers land, and record any adopted product decision in its owning
document. This report itself remains research evidence.

- The specific report-only instruction governs validation here; the generic worker root-typecheck rule does
  not authorize gates/native tests during this read-only audit.
- “Integrated” means the recorded merge is an ancestor of the captured integration ref. “Main merged” requires
  ancestry into captured main. Source/test presence is distinct from execution at that pin.
- Missing original time is compatible with a proven current public row; unknown current listability is safely
  omitted pending finite repair. These are different kinds of missing evidence.
- Missing aggregate fields/keys mean unknown, including command replies and Web. A conditional small-set
  assertion does not establish that production ever supplies a positively proved exact total.
- Source and Search dependencies remain separately owned. No retention, Source-access or count-policy
  research is reopened; no tests or authority are changed to resolve base defects.
