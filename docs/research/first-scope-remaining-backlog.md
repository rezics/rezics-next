## Finding

There is runnable product work. The strongest remaining gaps are a permanently unavailable creator-sales page,
selected Space Home requests that the native feed rejects, and the incomplete Subject rating-population journey.
Provider review still requires an editorial Context UUID despite an installed named Context directory. A native
rating-maintenance path also follows an unbounded redirect chain inside the voter's transaction. These are concrete
source defects or missing bodies, rather than requests for another gallery, contract, audit or proof campaign.

Five new complete journeys can be briefed now, subject to the ordinary ownership and risk declarations of their
implementation briefs. They are ordered within their owning Goals below. Two larger Library journeys remain
conditional because their complete native producer/composition boundaries are not installed or settled. Existing
reservations already cover question-filtered Home, community appearance selection and Auth-dependent erasure
retirement; create no competing assignment for them. Accounts, Content and Imports do not need invented new
feature work merely to populate all eight Goal lists.

The source is substantially newer than the review captures. Positional Subject reads, authored summaries,
Realm presentation selection, public-filter default composition, Collection ordering, financial receipts,
shared content rows, Settings navigation and many recovery/phone defects have received substantive corrections.
Reissuing those broad old recommendations would duplicate integrated work. Source presence still does not prove
native completion, browser quality, growth, physical erasure or release readiness.
