# Accounts, authentication, preferences and deletion: design status

Research evidence, 30 October 2026 UTC. This report traces the worker's integrated development source; it does not adopt policy or certify native outcomes. **Implemented** means a concrete source path exists, **proposed/adopted** means a contract without the complete runtime journey, and **outcome-proven** requires the owning delivery's actual outcome evidence. Existing landed delivery is trusted; no acceptance, native, browser or database campaign was repeated. The current minimal verification instruction defers this report's formatting acceptance until integration.

## Boundary and authority

The [scope](../scope.md) places accounts, authentication, preferences and deletion In, owned by Accounts. [Accounts](../accounts.md), [deletion](../deletion.md), [Auth](../../services/auth/README.md) and [Access](../../services/kernel/src/access/README.md) are the normative authorities; [the Goal](../goals/accounts.md) assigns Auth, Access and the shared auth contract. Web owns the main-site BFF and Auth pages; Kernel owns request/transaction machinery; the other erasure participants own their stores. Organization/Realm membership, verified public agents and platform gates cross those boundaries. Content, Commerce and Semantic retain authority over their records and permitted exceptions. This report cannot create an export service, new identity provider, legal retention schedule, account restoration or a competing deletion service.

The five concepts must stay separate:

| Concept    | Existing contract and consequence                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Account    | Immutable, non-reused ID; actor or organization. An organization cannot authenticate or share a password. A suspended/deleted identity is not an available login.                                                                                       |
| Operator   | An automated actor has exactly one human, unoperated actor or organization as operator. No recursive bot chain. Organization managers act as themselves; operator responsibility does not give their token the bot's identity.                          |
| Credential | Actor-owned password, passkey, intended TOTP/backup codes, or key used through `private_key_jwt`. A credential proves identity; it does not confer a resource role. Humans retain an interactive credential; personal access tokens are not the design. |
| Client     | Registered scope ceiling and issuer audience. First-party standing consent differs from explicit third-party consent. A client's existence does not grant account/resource permissions.                                                                 |
| Grant      | Current resource-scoped roles/actions and denials, with an optional immutable principal assignment for delegation. No token exchange is implied by delegation.                                                                                          |

`effective permission = actor grants ∩ current client-consented scopes ∩ principal grants (if delegated)`

The anonymous floor is evaluated separately. Kernel authenticates and authorizes at its trusted current **T**, including before an idempotency receipt is returned. The actor/client/`requested_by` tuple is restored for new workflow actions and reauthorized, rather than persisting bearer tokens. Mechanical cleanup uses a narrow already-committed obligation. Account/operator truth lives on the account anchor; organization membership on the organization anchor, with a member-side inverse; grants on the protected resource anchor. Handle allocation has a separate normalized-spelling authority. Cross-partition work uses resumable DBOS steps and receipts, not a distributed transaction or a connection held across a wait. See [native binding composition](../../services/kernel/src/access/native-account-bindings.ts) and [workflow approval resolution](../../services/kernel/src/access/workflow-approvals.ts).

## Deletion, privacy and operational gaps

Ordinary-record retention and account deletion are different entry conditions in [deletion](../deletion.md). An account stays irreversibly deleted while eligible published contributions can remain frozen and de-identified under their owner's contract. Private/unlisted/deleted collections, private activity and credentials are not granted a new retention exception. Financial exceptions require actual fields, purpose and expiry from the owning policy; this report invents none. Handle retirement prevents impersonation without requiring permanent former-owner provenance. The native erasure consumer must preserve an original registration/effect/responsibility result until every legitimate consumer has finished or transferred its proof, then minimize it; immutability is not permission to retain private history forever.

Participant version 1 is exactly **access, auth, library, commerce, content, community, semantic, search, imports, shared-history, delivery**. Ratings/notices, Zones and media/cache map to their actual owners rather than extra guessed participants. Keep five observable states separate: immediate authentication/permission refusal; participant-local native completion; actual shared-copy minimization; final eleven-owner live-store closure; backup/external-copy disposition and safe restore admission.
