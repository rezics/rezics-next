# Storybook catalogue

The catalogue presents route-level pages, shell-free patterns, and shared components. Page stories use recorded data; a
pattern isolates one connected region of a page. The [coverage map](../../../../docs/development/page-stories.md) tracks
implemented and pending outcomes.

- **Product pages and workflows** live under their product area and feature. Find the
  [Collection Editor](?path=/story/patterns-collection-editor--details) under Library → Collections.
- **Reusable components** live in the [UI Library](/ui/). Find [Button](/ui/?path=/story/actions-button--default)
  under Actions, [Cover](/ui/?path=/story/media-cover--states) under Media, and
  [Document Body](/ui/?path=/story/editor-reading-document-body--text-profile) under Editor → Reading.

Use the sidebar search to jump directly to a page, workflow or component by name.

Explore the complete [Book product](?path=/story/pages-library-books--rich-member) under Library → Books.
It includes direct entries for every Book tab and connected Edition and Series returns. The
[signed-out Book](?path=/story/pages-library-books--rich-signed-out) uses the same product and public reading routes.
Generic Subject stories remain under Subjects.

## Change the view

Use the Locale and Theme toolbars to switch language and light or dark theme. Choose a story and use the viewport
toolbar to inspect the same story ID at `desktop1280` or `phone390`. Width, locale and theme use globals rather than
separate behavior exports.
