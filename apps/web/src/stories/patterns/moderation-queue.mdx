# Moderation Queue

This shell-free review uses the delivered `BoardReportQueue`, including its form controller. The canonical reports
pages use that same component and the same contract-shaped report, current-target and reported-revision fixtures.
The queue keeps submitted evidence separate from current content and the exact reported revision. These fixtures
contain no reporter identity or moderator notes; they do not change Content's own-submission or appeal surfaces.

The plays reach pending, ambiguous retry and refused submissions through the actual reason-only form and its
`onAction` boundary. Both action buttons disable while pending. Retrying an unchanged command keeps its key;
editing the reason or switching actions creates another intent. Blank and whitespace-only reasons never reach the
callback, and the textarea caps input at 1,024 characters. Dismissal needs no reason.

Conflict (409), forbidden (403) and unavailable (503) callbacks all use the delivered generic failure message,
retain the draft and never show a successful disposition. No new error mapping or permission behavior is implied.
Resolved rows render supplied read outcomes, rather than assuming a denied or ambiguous command succeeded.
